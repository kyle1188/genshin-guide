# Genshin Impact Story Quests After 5.4: The Legend Key Rule Changed — Here's What's Actually Worth Doing

*The most-confused system in Genshin Impact right now isn't the gacha. It's the Story Quests — and specifically what happened to the Legend Key in version 5.4. Half the guides online still tell you to save keys for every character quest. That advice is now wrong. Here's the current state of play.*

---

A Story Quest is the single-character storyline for a five-star (usually Zhongli's "Salt-Stained Letters," Raiden's "The Sacred and the Profane," etc.). You get to try the character for free, earn about 60 primogems per act, and sometimes unlock a weekly boss. Sounds straightforward — until you've been burned by the Legend Key requirement and don't know which rule is current.

**The short version: since 5.4, opening a Story Quest no longer consumes a Legend Key at all.** You just meet the unlock requirements and start. The keys now matter for a completely different quest type.

## The 5.4 rule change, clearly

Before 5.4:
- Each Story Quest act cost Legend Keys to unlock and to replay.
- Players hoarded keys for weeks, running out at exactly the moment a new banner character's quest dropped.

Since 5.4:
- **Opening and replaying Story Quests is free.** No key cost, ever.
- **Legend Keys are now spent on Hangout Events** instead — 2 keys to start each Hangout act (after the first unlock it's free to replay different endings).

This single change is why the "I'll just save my keys for Zhongli's quest" strategy no longer works. There's nothing to save for on the Story side anymore.

## How Legend Keys actually work now

- **How to get them:** After Adventure Rank 26, every **8 Daily Commissions completed** grants 1 Legend Key — but you must claim it manually from the Legend Quests tab in the Quest menu. It does *not* auto-deposit.
- **Hold cap:** 3 keys max at once. But the progress bar keeps counting to 8, so if you spend one you can immediately claim the next.
- **What they cost:** 2 per Hangout Event act, first time only.

The keys are still worth something — just not for the quests most guides are still telling you to reserve them for.

## What Story Quests give you

Across the six main regions there are roughly **31 Story Quest acts total**:

| Region | Acts | Priority notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mondstadt | 7 | Venti, Jean, Diluc, Klee, Mona, Albedo, Eula |
| Liyue | 6 | Zhongli (2 acts), Ganyu, Xiao, Hu Tao, Yanfei, Baizhu |
| Inazuma | 5 | Raiden (2 acts), Ayaka, Kazuha, Sayu, Yae Miko |
| Sumeru | 5 | Nahida (2 acts), Alhaitham, Cyno (2 acts), Dehya, Nilou |
| Fontaine | 8+ | Furina, Neuvillette, Wriothesley, Navia, Clorinde, Sigewinne, Arlecchino, Chiori |
| Natlan | Ongoing | Tribal Tales format — Malanya, Kinich, Xilonen, Citlali, Mavuika, Chasca, etc. |

At ~60 primogems per act, that's **~1,860 primogems (≈ 11.6 pulls)** for the full set. Not life-changing, but it's free, it's repeatable once you've done it, and it gives you the character trial runs.

## The two quests that actually matter for combat

Most Story Quests are "60 gems + a story." Two are pure unlock gates:

1. **Zhongli, Act 2** — unlocks the **Azure Miroshidra** weekly boss. This is the source of Geo character ascension talent materials. If you're running Zhongli, Ningguang, or Arlecchino in any capacity, this is mandatory prep.
2. **Raiden Shogun, Act 2** — unlocks the **Sakura Ideala** weekly boss. Same deal for Electro character talent materials.

Everything else is nice-to-have. Do these two first, always.

## How to sequence them sensibly

The trap new players fall into is doing all the Story Quests out of order — clearing Sumeru before Inazuma, for instance — and either getting spoiled for the Archon Quests or missing character context.

The clean order:

1. **Push the Archon Quest for the current region** to the point where that region's Story Quests unlock.
2. **Clear that region's character quests** (Zhongli / Raiden Act 2s first if you want the weekly bosses).
3. **Move to the next region's Archon Quest.**
4. **Save the Natlan Tribal Tales for the current patch cycle** — they drop as the new characters launch, so doing them in the same version the character is live is the smoothest experience.

One more thing people miss: when you're **stuck on an Adventure Rank break**, a Story Quest is the cheapest experience source available. Two or three acts usually closes the gap faster than re-running weekly bosses, and each one pays out ~60 primogems on top.

## What's *not* worth doing

- **Hoarding Legend Keys for "later" Story Quests.** They don't cost keys anymore. Spend them on Hangout Events instead — or just let the progress bar run out and claim the free keys as they come.
- **Prioritizing Natlan quests over the two weekly-boss unlockers.** The tribal quests are great, but they don't gate anything as critical as the Zhongli / Raiden Act 2s do.
- **Doing a region's quests before its Archon Quest.** You'll miss context and occasionally hit a prerequisite wall you didn't need to hit.
