# Genshin Impact Friendship Levels: How to Actually Get There (Without Grinding for Months)

*There's a system in Genshin Impact that gives you a free namecard for every character, unlocks their full story, and costs you nothing in primogems — if you know where the EXP actually comes from. Most players either ignore it or grind it the slowest possible way. Here's the fast lane.*

---

Friendship level (also called Companionship level) is the heart icon on every character's profile. Ten levels total. The final reward at level 10 is that character's **exclusive namecard** — a permanent collectible — plus the "Unheard Tales" achievement progress. No gacha, no spending. Just time and the right setup.

Most people take 4–5 months per character. You can do it in 6–8 weeks if you understand how the EXP actually works.

## The one rule that changes everything

Friendship EXP is **shared with your entire active party** — and it's not split. When you gain Friendship EXP, *every character in your party gets the full amount*. Not divided by four. Not diluted. Each one gets 100% of whatever you just earned.

This means: **packing all four slots with characters you want to level is strictly better than running one at a time.** You get the same EXP for all of them at no extra cost.

The Traveler (your protagonist) doesn't participate. Characters already at max level 10 don't absorb EXP either. The EXP just goes to the other characters in the party.

## How much EXP is the whole thing

Level 1 → 10 requires **29,100 total Friendship EXP**. The last level (9 → 10) alone costs **5,650** — about a fifth of the total. That's why the final stretch feels so much slower.

| Level range | Total EXP required | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Early levels (1 → 4) | Cheapest stretch | Daily commissions alone can cover it quickly |
| Mid levels (4 → 7) | Heavier per level | Weekly bosses start to matter |
| Late levels (7 → 10) | Most EXP, slowest stretch | 9 → 10 alone is 5,650 — about a fifth of the whole; co-op or the Pot really helps here |

## Where the EXP actually comes from

Every source listed below is **doubled in co-op**. This is the single biggest lever.

| Source | EXP per instance | In co-op |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Commissions (4/day) | 25–60 each | **×2** |
| Commission delivery reward (Katheen) | 45–100/day | **×2** |
| Weekly Boss (Spiral Abyss-adjacent) | 55–70/fight | **×2** |
| World Boss (standard) | 30–45/fight | **×2** |
| Domain / resin-spent area | 10–20/fight | **×2** |
| Random world events | 10–15/each, 10/day cap | **×2** |
| Black Iron forging | 10/lump | — |
| Serenitea Pot idle (per hour, per character) | 2–5 | — |

**Solo baseline:** one full day of commissions + delivery + resin consumption = roughly **200–300 EXP**.
**Co-op baseline:** the same day = **400–600 EXP**.

## The Serenitea Pot: the idle engine most people never set up

This is the single highest-ROI setup for a passive player. After completing the "Serenitea Pot" world quest, you can **house up to 8 characters** in the pot simultaneously (full capacity unlocks at Trust Rank 9).

Housed characters accumulate Friendship EXP **while you're offline** — 2–5 EXP/hour depending on your pot's Serenitea energy level, capping at 500 EXP per "gift batch." You have to log in and collect the "Divine Divination Gift" for it to bank; it doesn't auto-deposit.

A pot with high Serenitea energy (above ~20,000) can produce a meaningful chunk of the total 29,100 just from overnight idle time. Set it up once; collect weekly.

## The daily routine that actually works

Five steps, roughly 10 minutes a day:

1. **Put your target characters in your active team.** All four slots if you're grinding multiple.
2. **Do your 4 Daily Commissions in co-op** (either host or join a friend's world). Commission rewards double, and delivery rewards double too.
3. **Spend your resin on weekly bosses first**, then world bosses, then domains — in that order, highest EXP density first.
4. **Clear any random events** while you're in the open world. Ten per day, ~100–150 EXP, easily skipped.
5. **Before you close the game, check your Serenitea Pot** and collect any pending Divine Divination Gifts.

Stick with that and you'll hit 0 → 10 in **6–8 weeks** for a co-op-active player, **2–3 months** for a solo daily-commissions-only player.

## What the levels actually unlock

Friendship rewards are pure collection content — no combat stats.

| Level | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 2 / 4 / 6 | Character Story chapters 1–5 (some require quest prerequisites) |
| 3 | Second Photo Mode emote for the character |
| 4 | Large batch of "About Other Characters" voice lines |
| 6 | Pot dialogue options expand |
| 8 | "Lantern Rite" challenge eligibility (where applicable) |
| 10 | **Exclusive namecard** + "Unheard Tales" achievement progress |

There's no reason to rush unless you're a completionist. But there also isn't a reason to ignore it — it's free, it's passive, and the namecard is genuinely the last piece of each character's collectible set.

## The mistakes people make

- **Thinking EXP is split four ways.** It isn't. Full amount to everyone in the party.
- **Forgetting to collect delivery rewards.** The 45–100 EXP from commission delivery is often left unclaimed.
- **Not collecting the Pot gift.** If you're not logging in for a week, your housed characters stop earning at the cap.
- **Running the Traveler as a party member for Friendship purposes.** He doesn't get EXP and doesn't block anyone else, but it's wasted thinking.
- **Neglecting the random events.** Ten per day, free, no resin cost.
