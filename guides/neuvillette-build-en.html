Neuvillette Build Guide 2026 — Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Neuvillette is the Hydro Sovereign who redefined "effortless DPS": hold one button, and the chamber empties. Here's how to build him in 2026 without wasting resin — best weapons, the one artifact set that matters, HP-scaling stat targets, and the teams that make him broken.

Sources: KQM theorycrafting, Game8, Genshin.gg, and community Abyss data. Patch numbers shift — in-game values are final.

The verdict in one line

4-piece Marechaussee Hunter, HP% Sands / Hydro DMG Goblet / CRIT DMG Circlet, Prototype Amber if F2P, pair with a shielder or Furina. The build hasn't changed since Fontaine — and it's still S-tier in 2026.

First, understand how he scales (this trips everyone up)

Neuvillette's Charged Attack — the giant water beam — scales exclusively off Max HP. This one fact drives every build decision:

If your Neuvillette is holding an ATK% Sands, swap it now — you'd be playing at roughly 40% of his potential damage. HP% is not negotiable.

Best artifacts — only one set matters

The consensus across every major build resource is unanimous: 4-piece Marechaussee Hunter.

  • 2pc: Normal & Charged Attack DMG +15% — direct buff to his beam.
  • 4pc: +12% CRIT Rate per HP change (max 3 stacks = +36% CRIT Rate). His beam constantly drains and heals HP, so this has near-100% uptime.
Resin-efficient farm: Marechaussee Hunter drops from the same Fontaine domain (Denouement of Sin) as Golden Troupe — which is Best-in-Slot for Furina and Fischl. Farming this one domain builds your whole team; off-pieces aren't wasted.

Temporary fallbacks (only until you have 4pc MH): 2pc Marechaussee + 2pc HP%, or 4pc Heart of Depth if you're AR 45–50. Don't farm Heart of Depth or Nymph's Dream specifically for him.

Talent priority

Normal Attack > Burst > Skill. His Charged Attack scales off the Normal Attack talent, so crown that first. Skill stays low. Aim for roughly 10 / 9 / 9 (NA / Burst / Skill).

Best team comps

Hypercarry / Mono Hydro

Neuvillette + Furina + Kazuha + flex

The meta core. Furina buffs the whole team and her HP fluctuation feeds his Marechaussee stacks; Kazuha shreds Hydro RES with Viridescent Venerer. Flex slot: Xilonen, Baizhu, or a shielder.

Electro-Charged

Neuvillette + Furina + Kazuha + Kuki Shinobu

Steady AoE. Kuki provides healing and Electro application for constant Electro-Charged reactions while Neuvillette beams everything.

Hyperbloom

Neuvillette + Furina + Nahida + Kuki Shinobu

High single-target. Nahida drives Dendro cores that Kuki triggers into Hyperbloom, with Neuvillette as the on-field Hydro enabler.

Comfort / shielder

Neuvillette + Zhongli + 2 flex

C0 Neuvillette can be interrupted mid-beam. A shielder (Zhongli, or Layla/Diona) keeps his channel running — often comfier than chasing C1.

The HP-fluctuation trick (and why Furina is his best partner)

Neuvillette's Charged Attack drains his own HP, and his Skill/Burst spawn "Sourcewater Droplets" that speed up his charge and heal him. This constant HP drain-and-heal is exactly what keeps Marechaussee Hunter's +36% CRIT Rate active — and it's also what fuels Furina's Fanfare stacks, letting her Burst hand the whole team a massive damage buff. They buff each other in a loop: that's why Neuvillette + Furina is the defining pairing of this era. All Hydro reactions except Vaporize also push his beam to 160% damage.

5 common mistakes

  1. Running ATK% Sands — the single biggest damage killer. HP% only.
  2. Farming Heart of Depth / Nymph's Dream for him — Marechaussee domain is strictly better and also builds Furina.
  3. Chasing his signature or C1 first — Prototype Amber R5 and a shielder cover most of it; build characters before weapons.
  4. Letting him get interrupted — C0 needs a shielder or careful positioning to keep the beam going.
  5. Skipping CRIT Rate because "MH gives it" — you still want ~40% base so you're not at 0% before stacks ramp.

Don't have Neuvillette yet?

He's a limited 5★. While you wait for a rerun, other strong on-field Hydro/HP-scaling options include Ayato, or building a reaction carry from our beginner roster. Prototype Amber and the Marechaussee domain are worth pre-farming either way, since they transfer to future HP-scalers.
