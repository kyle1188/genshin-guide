Genshin Impact Craftable Weapons Guide 2026 – All Forgeable Weapons & How to Get Them

Craftable weapons are free, reliable alternatives to banner weapons. This guide covers every forgeable 4★ and 5★ weapon, where to get the blueprints, what materials you need, which characters benefit most, and when forging is better than pulling.

🔨 Forge Alert: Version 7.0 added 5 new Snezhnaya forgeable 4★ weapons — one per weapon type, all reaction-focused and free to craft. See the Snezhnaya section below.

How Forging Works

To craft any weapon, you need three things:

  1. Blueprint (Diagram) — either auto-unlocked at certain ARs, from quests, or purchasable from a blacksmith NPC.
  2. Billets — Northlander Billets drop from weekly trounce domains (Stormterror, Andrius, Azhdaha, etc.). One billet per weapon forged.
  3. Ore Materials — White Iron Chunks (common), region-specific ores (Crystal Chunks, Amethyst Lumps, etc.).

You can also refine a crafted weapon by forging additional copies, up to R5. Higher refinement = stronger passive effects.

Billet Farming Priority

Billets are the bottleneck for forging. Prioritize these weekly bosses:

  • Stormterror Dvalin — drops Sword, Bow, Catalyst billets. First priority for new players.
  • Andrius — drops Claymore and Polearm billets.
  • Azhdaha — drops all billet types (best efficiency).

Farm one boss per week, rotate to collect all billet types over a month.

Common Mistakes

  • Forging before AR 20: You won't have the blast furnaces or quests unlocked yet. Wait until you've done the early Archon Quests.
  • Ignoring refinement: R2–R5 on a well-chosen 4★ weapon can match a low-refinement 5★. Don't stop at R1.
  • Forging the wrong weapon: Don't forge a weapon for a character you don't plan to use. Check each character's build page first.

快速问答

What is the best craftable weapon in Genshin Impact?

There is no single best weapon — it depends on your main DPS. For sword users: Iron Sting (EM builds) or Amenoma Kageuchi (energy). For bows: The Stringless is top-tier for Xiao/Fischl. For polearms: Dragon's Bane for Xiangling.

Are forgeable weapons worth upgrading to R5?

Yes, especially for F2P players. R5 Dragon's Bane or R5 The Stringless can outperform R1 5★ weapons on the right characters. Refinement material comes from duplicating the weapon.

How do I get Northlander Billets?

Farm weekly trounce domains: Stormterror (Mondstadt), Andrius (Dragonspine), Azhdaha (Sumeru), and the latest weekly boss. Each has a chance to drop Sword/Bow/Catalyst/Polearm/Claymore billets respectively.

What is the Snezhnaya forgeable weapons set?

Five new 4★ weapons added in 7.0: Emberwell (sword), Covenant of Frost and Snow (bow), Blade of Atonement (claymore), Echoes of the Heart (catalyst), Song of the Vigil (polearm). All scale with reaction and Stellar Glimmer damage.

Should I save primogems for 5★ weapons or characters?

Characters first. A good support like Kazuha or Bennett makes any DPS stronger. Only pull a 5★ weapon if your character is bottlenecked by their current weapon.
