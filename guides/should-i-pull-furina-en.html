Genshin: Is Furina Rerun Worth It? 7.2 Support Verdict

Verdict: if you don't own her and want to raise your team's ceiling, pull her; if you already have a buffer and are low on Primogems, wait for the next rerun. Furina is a 5-star Hydro support / sub-DPS whose Burst grants up to 75% team-wide damage (250 Mood cap), making her one of the strongest team lifters in the game. C0 is complete and clears the Abyss; her constellations add smooth damage rather than a hard gate. A 4★ dockhand sword reaches about 85% of her signature weapon. Two hard requirements: bring a healer (to stack Mood) and aim for 130–180% ER.

Quick Decisions

  • Worth it? No Furina yet + want a team lift → pull (the rerun is the ideal entry point); already run Xingqiu / Yelan / Xiangling → can wait.
  • Constellations? C0 is complete and clears the Abyss; constellations are smooth gains, not a gate — stay C0 on a budget.
  • F2P? A 5-star is steep; if you have a buffer, skip her; save enough and she's a big lift — she reruns often.
  • Key requirement: bring a healer + 130–180% ER, otherwise the 75% buff won't fill.

Worth it: what Furina actually adds

Extremely flexibleFits pure-damage, Vaporize, Freeze, Burgeon and Aggravate teams — the rerun is a rare shot for players who never got her

In one line: Furina is not a "strong or not" question (she's top tier) — it's "do you need a buffer right now." If you do, pull her; don't miss the rerun window.

Read it this way: unlike Mitya, Furina's value is fully realized at C0 (the 75% buff is not missing anything); her constellations are polish. For the vast majority of players, C0 is the optimal answer, and only heavy spenders should chase max constellations.

Key numbers: target 130–180% ER (180% as the solo Hydro, 130–150% with double Hydro); HP 36,000–42,000+; CRIT Rate 55–65% (69–73% when using the dockhand / Favonius sword). F2P players with Xingqiu can already run Vaporize, so Furina is "better with" rather than "can't play without."
