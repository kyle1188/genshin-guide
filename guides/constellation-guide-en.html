Genshin Constellation Priority 2026: C1 Value, Weapon vs Const & F2P Advice

Most characters are complete at C0. Constellations are a bonus, not a requirement — this guide helps you judge whether that first con is worth it, and how to rank weapon vs constellations.

Most characters are complete and playable at C0. Constellations are incremental, not a gate. Build the character you have first; think about cons later.

Bottom line: C0 is usually enough

The modern design trend is self-contained C0 kits. Abyss 36-star, open world, events — a C0R1 (C0 + signature) character with decent artifacts clears all of it. Spending primogems on "one more new character" usually widens your options more than "one more con on an old one."

The first constellation (C1) is usually best

C1 is the small-invest big-return breakpoint: most characters get +15–25% damage or a key mechanic fix (extra skill charge, RES shred, energy). But C1's value varies: Emilie's C1 beats her signature weapon, while Varka's signature R1 beats his C1. No universal rule.

Weapon vs Constellation: R1 or C1 first

Read the character

If the weapon is core to the kit (big ATK/crit/transform), take R1; if C1 is a mechanic transform (unlocks playstyle / big threshold drop), take C1.

General order

C0R1 → C1R1 → C2 (if strong) → stop, or push C6 (whale). Get the weapon before cons.

Versatility

A signature weapon carries to the next same-type character; a constellation is locked to this one unit. Weapons age better.

Which constellations are "transformative"

A few change how the character plays, not just add numbers: Faruzan C6 (Anemo support core, usable → essential), Xiao C6 (infinite E during burst), and some C2s that are huge jumps. But most C2s are just "a bit more" — not a transform. Don't assume C2 is always worth it.

F2P / low-spender: pull cons?

Generally no. Primogems are scarce; priority is always: main DPS C0R1 + good artifacts > constellations. Save cons for beloved units or team cores (e.g. a key support's C6 in your reaction team). A C1 from a standard-banner duplicate is a bonus, not a target.

C6 and common traps

  • C6 is whale territory: not worth it for most, unless it's a core main DPS you'll play for a long time.
  • C3 / C5 are just +3 talent levels: lowest value per pull; only grabbed on the way to C6, never as a target.
  • Chasing C1 on a limited banner and missing the next stronger DPS — the most common primogem waste.
  • C2 looks strong but isn't a transform — often the signature weapon is the better buy.

Quick Answers

What are constellations?

Constellations are enhancement tiers (C1–C6) auto-unlocked when you pull a duplicate of a character. Each duplicate raises one constellation, up to 6. Both limited and standard characters have them.

Should I pull constellations after the character?

Usually not required. C0 is complete and playable; cons are incremental. Build your C0R1 unit with good artifacts first, then consider cons.

Weapon or constellation first?

Most characters: take the signature weapon R1 first (base ATK + effect, carries across units). But if C1 is a mechanic transform (e.g. Emilie), take C1 first. It's character-specific.

Should F2P pull constellations?

Generally no. Primogems are limited; get main DPS to C0R1 + artifacts first. Save cons for beloved units or team cores (e.g. a key support's C6). A C1 from a standard duplicate is a bonus, not a goal.

Which constellations are most worth it?

Early transformative cons: Faruzan C6, Xiao C6, and similar; for most characters C1 has the best value. C3/C5 (+3 talent levels) are rarely worth targeting — only grabbed on the way to C6.
