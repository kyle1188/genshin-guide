Genshin Artifact Farming: Which Sets to Farm First & the Routes That Save Weeks

Artifacts make or break a build — and they are the single easiest place for new players to waste months of Resin. This guide shows when to start farming, which sets to prioritize for a free-to-play account, the resin route that actually pays off, and the mistakes that quietly stall your account.

Note: artifact sets and their bonuses are long-term stable game knowledge and do not change with patches. Exact drop rates and domain layouts may shift slightly, but the priority order below holds. In-game data is always final.

When to Actually Start Farming Artifacts

Before AR 45: do not farm artifact domains. Below AR 45 the gold-drop rate from domains is low, so most of your Resin returns 4-star pieces you will replace anyway. Use whatever 4-star pieces you pick up from quests and chests, and focus Resin on talent domains, weapon domains, and weekly bosses instead.

At AR 45: domains start dropping a guaranteed 5-star artifact per run. This is the moment artifact farming becomes efficient. From here, commit Resin to 1–2 specific domains, not a random spread.

Which Sets to Farm First (F2P Priority)

These sets cover the universally useful units from the beginner must-build list and stay relevant into late game. Farm them in roughly this order.

PyroEmblem of Severed Fate

Burst Sub-DPS & supports

The single highest-value set for a F2P account. 4-piece gives Energy Recharge scaling into burst damage — perfect for Xiangling, Xingqiu, Raiden, Yelan, and anyone living off their burst. One domain feeds half your roster.

Farm: Domain of the "Emblem / Shimenawa" pair · Priority: ★★★★★

GeoNoblesse Oblige

Support / buffer

4-piece grants a team-wide ATK buff on burst — the default for Bennett and any burst-support. One holder per team is enough; do not stack it on everyone.

Farm: Domain of the "Noblesse / Crimson Witch" pair · Priority: ★★★★

AnemoViridescent Venerer

Anemo supports

4-piece shreds enemy elemental RES and amplifies Swirl — the core set for Kazuha, Sucrose, Venti, and Anemo Traveler. Huge damage multiplier for the whole team, not just the wearer.

Farm: Domain of the "VV / Gladiator" pair · Priority: ★★★★

DendroDeepwood Memories

Dendro / Bloom teams

4-piece cuts enemy Dendro RES — mandatory in Nahida-led Spread/Bloom teams. One piece on the off-field holder is enough for the team benefit.

Farm: Domain of the "Deepwood / Gilded" pair · Priority: ★★★

PyroGladiator's Finale / Crimson Witch

Main DPS

Gladiator (18% ATK at 2-pc) drops from weekly bosses and is a safe generic main-DPS fill-in. Crimson Witch is the dedicated Vaporize/Melt Pyro set. Farm these once your supports are geared.

Gladiator: weekly bosses · Crimson Witch: Noblesse domain · Priority: ★★★

New SystemsArchaic Petra / Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City

Nod-Krai / Natlan teams

The "Scroll" set is a top pick for new-system carries and supports. Pull it in once you commit to a Nod-Krai / Natlan core; skip it early while your account is still generalist.

Farm: respective new-system domains · Priority: ★★ (late-game)

Best Resin Route (What to Spend On)

Resin is the only truly limited resource. Spend it in this priority so you never soft-lock your account:

PrioritySpend onWhy
1Weekly Bosses (3/week)Drop character ascension + talent gems + weapon billets, and 5-star artifact sets (Gladiator / Wanderer's Troupe) — best value per Resin.
2Talent DomainsTalents are permanent, account-wide progress and never get replaced.
3Weapon DomainsForge F2P weapons like The Catch; cheap and reusable across characters.
4Artifact Domains (post-AR45)Only after supports/weapons are handled; pick 1–2 domains and commit.

Save Fragile Resin until AR 45+ — spending it pre-45 on low gold-rate domains is the most common early waste. Bank it, then dump it on a domain you are committed to.

Efficient Farming Routes

  • Combo domains: most artifact domains share a building with a talent or weapon domain. Run your daily domains back-to-back in the same region to cut travel time.
  • Convert, don't trash: use the Artifact Transmuter (3-to-1) to turn dead 5-star pieces into a targeted set/main-stat. This is how you fix the last missing slot without 200 more runs.
  • Feed the dog: level useless 3–4 star artifacts as enhancement fodder instead of selling them — they are worth more as XP than as Mora.
  • Strongbox from Liyue: the Mystic Enhancement Ore and artifact strongbox convert duplicates into useful sets; keep spares.

Mistakes That Waste Weeks

  • Farming artifacts before AR 45. You will drown in 4-stars you replace at 45. Resin spent there is gone forever.
  • Chasing perfect double-crit on every slot. A usable piece (right main stat + one good substat) is 90% of the value. Stop at "good enough" and move on.
  • Spreading across 5 domains. One focused domain beats five half-farmed ones. Depth > breadth.
  • Stacking Noblesse on the whole team. The 4-piece buff doesn't stack — one holder only.
  • Burning Fragile Resin early. Save it for committed post-45 farming.
Bottom line: Don't touch artifact domains until AR 45. Then farm Emblem first (it feeds your whole burst-based core), add Noblesse + Viridescent Venerer for supports, and only after that chase main-DPS sets. Spend Resin on weekly bosses and talents first, bank Fragile Resin, and use the Transmuter to close gaps. Do this and your account compounds instead of stalling.
