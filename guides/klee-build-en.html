Genshin Klee Build & Teams: 5-Star Pyro Catalyst & Crimson Witch of Flames

Quick Answer

  • Klee is a 5★ Pyro catalyst from Mondstadt, an on-field main DPS whose damage comes from Jumpy Dumpty's constant Pyro application plus high-frequency Charged Attacks.
  • Core set: Crimson Witch of Flames 4pc (2pc +15% Pyro DMG; 4pc +15% Vaporize/Melt DMG and extra Pyro stacks after the Skill); swap to Marechaussee Hunter 4pc with Furina.
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Pyro DMG Goblet / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: Crits > ATK > EM (reaction teams) > ER (Burst costs only 60, 110–130% is enough).
  • Key: almost all her damage is Pyro; Vaporize teams are most consistent, Melt leans on Cryo application, Mono-Pyro just stacks Crits.
  • Best teams: Klee + Xingqiu/Yelan + Bennett + Kazuha (Vaporize); Klee + Diona/Kaeya + Bennett + Kazuha (Melt).
  • Weapon: Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds is the most stable (CRIT Rate + move speed + stacking Elemental DMG); F2P uses Dodoco Tales or Solar Pearl.

How Klee Works

Her loop is "constant Pyro application + on-field burst":

  • Skill (Jumpy Dumpty): bounces 3 times and drops 8 mines, applying Pyro constantly — the engine for Crimson Witch stacks and Vaporize/Melt triggers.
  • Burst (Sparks 'n' Splash): 10s of auto-targeting sparks that also grant +15% team Pyro DMG; costs only 60 energy, so her rotation is easy.
  • A4 Passive (Pounding Surprise): hits from Jumpy Dumpty or Normal Attacks have a 50% chance to grant an Explosive Spark that makes the next Charged Attack cost no Stamina and deal +50% DMG — Charged Attacks are her main damage.
  • So she runs ATK + Crits + a Pyro Goblet; reaction teams keep a little EM.

Team Comps

Vaporize Team (most consistent)

Klee + Xingqiu/Yelan (Hydro) + Bennett (ATK + heal) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Xingqiu/Yelan keep Hydro up; Klee's Charged Attacks trigger Vaporize; Kazuha shreds Pyro/Hydro RES.
  • Bennett's Burst zone gives the ATK buff Klee's window sits inside.

Melt Team (higher multiplier)

Klee + Diona/Kaeya (Cryo) + Bennett + Kazuha

  • Off-field Cryo + Klee's Pyro → Melt; good against Cryo-immune or single targets.

Mono-Pyro Team

Klee + Xiangling + Bennett + Kazuha

  • Double Pyro resonance + Xiangling's off-field Pyro; just stack Crits.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: Jumpy Dumpty and mines reduce enemy DEF by 23% for 12s — the biggest spike and best stopping point.
  • C4: after the Burst, party ATK SPD and move SPD up — more flexible swaps.
  • C6: constant Pyro + self-energy; a whale goal.

FAQ

Is Klee still worth building?

Yes. Vaporize is still solid, and pairing her with Furina + Marechaussee Hunter raises her ceiling; her only downsides are a squishy frame and long animations, so she wants a shielder/healer.

What is her best-in-slot weapon?

Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds (CRIT Rate + move speed + stacking Elemental DMG). Without 5-stars: The Widsith (R5 bursts hard) or the event weapon Dodoco Tales.

Vaporize or Melt?

Vaporize is more consistent (Xingqiu/Yelan keep Hydro up); Melt has a higher multiplier but relies on off-field Cryo and favors single targets.

How much Energy Recharge does Klee need?

Her Burst costs only 60. Around 110–130% ER with Bennett is enough — no need to over-invest.

Is C2 worth chasing?

Yes — C2's 23% DEF shred is a flat, always-on damage gain and the best value constellation; C2 is a good stopping point.
