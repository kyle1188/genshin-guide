Genshin Tighnari Build & Teams: 5-Star Dendro Bow DPS · Spread Quick-Swap · Deepwood Memories

Quick Answer

  • Tighnari is a 5★ Dendro Bow (Sumeru Forest Watcher) quick-swap DPS; core = Skill cuts charge time then fires Clusterbloom Wreath Arrows.
  • Core artifacts: Deepwood Memories 4pc (team Dendro RES −30%) or Gilded Dreams 4pc (EM + ATK); Wanderer's Troupe as generic.
  • Main stats: EM or ATK% Sands / Dendro DMG Goblet / CRIT Circlet.
  • Substats: CRIT > EM (300–500) > ATK% > ER (40-cost Burst).
  • Key: ASC gives Dendro DMG and his A4 scales off EM — EM is the stat right after CRIT.
  • Best teams: Tighnari + Yae Miko/Fischl + Nahida + Zhongli (Aggravate), Tighnari + Fischl + Kuki + Kazuha (Hyperbloom).
  • Weapon "Hunter's Path" fits best; F2P use "The Stringless" or 3★ "Slingshot".

Role & Kit

Tighnari's damage comes from charged shots and a quick-swap loop:

  • Charged Shot "Clusterbloom Wreath Arrow": at full charge fires a Wreath Arrow that spawns 4 homing Clusterbloom arrows — his main damage.
  • Skill "Vijnana-Phala Mine": throws a taunting field and grants "Vijnana Suffusion" that cuts charge time by 2.4s — the key to his 3-shot quick-swap.
  • Burst "Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft": fires 6 arrows, then 6 more on hit, covering Oz/Electro for Aggravate.
  • Prioritize EM and CRIT; his 40-cost Burst is easy to upkeep.

FAQ

Is Tighnari still worth building?

Yes. C0 is complete and he is easy to get from the standard banner; his Aggravate teams handle both single and group targets with relaxed play.

Should I stack EM on Tighnari?

Yes. Spread scales directly with EM; aim for 300–500, then top up CRIT.

How much Energy Recharge?

His Burst costs 40; with dual Dendro ~130–140%, as the only Dendro ~150–160%.

What is his best weapon?

Hunter's Path fits best; without a 5★ use Aqua Simulacra or Polar Star, F2P use The Stringless or R5 Slingshot.

What teams is he good in?

Spread/Quicken is core; he also fits Hyperbloom and dual-Dendro quick-swap.
