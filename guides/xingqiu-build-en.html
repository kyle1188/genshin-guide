Genshin Xingqiu Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 6.7 Phase 2 (live through Aug 11, 2026)

Raiden Shogun is rerunning now — and Xingqiu is the missing 4th piece of Raiden National (Raiden + Xingqiu + Xiangling + Bennett), the free team that clears Spiral Abyss far above its cost. If you're pulling Raiden, you need Xingqiu built right: enough ER to ult every rotation, the Sacrificial Sword trick, and the C2 breakpoint. This guide covers exactly that.

Xingqiu — the rain-calling bookworm of Liyue — is the single best Hydro enabler in Genshin Impact. His Burst rains swords that apply Hydro off-field, cut incoming damage by 15%, and keep your carry alive, all while he sits in the backline doing zero on-field work. He is also permanent and farmable (wishes + the Starglitter shop). This guide covers his best weapon (Sacrificial Sword vs Favonius/Wolf-Fang), the 4pc Emblem of Severed Fate set, the ~200% Energy Recharge breakpoint, why C2 is the real upgrade, and teams with Raiden / Hu Tao / Neuvillette / Bennett.

Note: Xingqiu's kit, his Sacrificial Sword default, the 4pc Emblem preference, and the ~200% ER requirement are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 6.7 Raiden rerun above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of banner.

Why Xingqiu Is Worth Building

No other Hydro unit delivers off-field application, team survivability, and reaction enabler in one cheap package. If you build one support besides Bennett, make it him.

  • Off-field Hydro engine: his Q (Raincutter) rains swords that hit enemies automatically, enabling Vape/Melt on every carry swing without him taking the field.
  • Built-in 15% damage cut: the Rain Swords shatter to reduce incoming damage to the active character — a permanent mini-shield that makes squishy carries survive.
  • Minor heal + C6 ATK buff: shattered swords heal a little; at C6 the field also grants +15% ATK and interruption resistance to your on-fielder.
  • Permanent and accessible: obtainable from any standard/wuxu banner and the Starglitter shop, so he is never gated behind a specific rerun.
A correctly built Xingqiu turns almost any on-fielder into a Vape/Melt monster and cuts team deaths by a third. For a free unit, that is absurd value.

Best Weapons

Xingqiu wants Energy Recharge (to Burst every rotation) and likes CRIT / HP% for personal damage. He uses a sword.

BiS ★Sacrificial Sword

Best-in-slot · 4★ sword (R5 ideal)

The default for a reason: its passive lets his Elemental Skill recast, generating a second wave of energy particles and doubling Hydro application. R5 is the dream; even R1 plus C2 enables the double-E combo. This is what makes National tick.

Obtain: forge / wishes

4★Favonius Sword

Team-energy tool

Generates CRIT-based particles on hit, solving energy for the whole team when Xingqiu can't ER his own Burst. Best when nobody else on the team has a battery. Pair with some CRIT rate.

Obtain: wishes

5★Wolf-Fang / Primordial Jade Cutter

Personal-damage options

Wolf-Fang (battle pass) gives CRIT rate and ramps his own Skill/Burst damage — strong if you value his hits. Primordial Jade Cutter converts HP into CRIT and ATK for a beefy, hard-hitting Xingqiu. Use when ER is already covered.

Obtain: battle pass / weapon banner

AltHaran / Key of Khaj-Nisut

Premium personal-dmg

If Xingqiu is your personal-damage piece (rare but valid in quickswap shells), Haran or Key push his numbers hard. Overkill for a pure enabler — only when ER and support duties are solved.

Obtain: weapon banner

Stat Targets — ER First, Always

Xingqiu's entire value is his Burst. If Q is down, he contributes almost nothing. So ER is non-negotiable: hit the breakpoint, then stack CRIT/HP% for personal damage.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — his Skill feeds energy and his Burst is his damage:

  1. Elemental Skill (Guhua Sword) → 8–13. Generates his particles and, with Sacrificial Sword, enables the double-cast that solves energy. Crown this first if you can.
  2. Elemental Burst (Raincutter) → 8–13. The Rain Sword damage and Hydro application scale here. Level it right after the Skill.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. He barely swings his sword outside quickswap memes. Leave it low.

Constellations — C2 Is the Real Upgrade

Xingqiu's constellation curve is front-loaded: one constellation changes everything, then it is smooth sailing.

  • C1 (Crushing Rain): small — increases Rain Sword damage and extends their duration slightly. Nice, not essential.
  • C2 (Soothing Droplet): adds a 3rd Rain Sword and cuts his Skill cooldown by 3s. This is the big one: with R1 Sacrificial Sword the -3s lets him double-cast the Skill, doubling Hydro application and energy. Hugely better uptime and Vape consistency.
  • C4: extends Q duration and makes more Rain Swords — more uptime and damage. A clear step up.
  • C6 (Hydropathic Absolution): the on-field character in his Q gains +15% ATK and interruption resistance. A free team buff that caps his value. Worth it if you main him.
Key rule: Get C2 and stop. C2 is where Xingqiu goes from "good" to "best Hydro enabler in the game." C6 is a luxury, not a requirement.

Common Mistakes

  • Too little Energy Recharge. The #1 Xingqiu killer. Sub-180% ER without a battery means a dead Burst and a team that loses all its Vape and shielding.
  • Building him as a main DPS. His job is the rain, not the sword. Stacking raw ATK/DMG on him wastes the slot — ER and CRIT/HP% win.
  • Skipping Sacrificial Sword. Without the double-E, his energy and Hydro application both suffer. It is the single highest-impact weapon for him.
  • Wrong artifact set. Random 2pc+2pc without Emblem leaves ER and Burst damage on the table. Emblem 4pc is the default for a reason.
  • Forcing him where Yelan is better. For pure single-target personal damage, Yelan out-scales him — but she gives no particles and no damage cut, so Xingqiu still wins for battery-needing and survivability-focused teams.

Don't Have Xingqiu Yet?

He is permanent: pull him from any standard banner or grab copies via the Starglitter shop over time. While you wait, Barbara or Candace can supply off-field Hydro in a pinch, and Yelan covers single-target Vape — but neither gives Xingqiu's rain of swords plus the 15% damage cut plus his own battery. Build him alongside Bennett and Xiangling; together they are the National core that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they're the backbone of every Raiden National clear during the 6.7 rerun.
