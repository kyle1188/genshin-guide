Genshin Nahida Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Nahida — the Dendro Archon of Sumeru — is the backbone of every Bloom, Quicken, and Hyperbloom team in Genshin Impact. Her Elemental Skill marks enemies and triggers coordinated Dendro attacks that scale off Elemental Mastery, and her Burst turns her personal EM into team-wide damage and Dendro RES shred. This guide covers weapons, the Gilded Dreams and Deepwood Memories sets, EM stacking, Bloom/Quicken team comps, and the Dendro engine behind the strongest reactions in the game.

Note: Nahida's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Nahida Is Worth Building

She is the most efficient Dendro enabler in the game, and Dendro reactions (Bloom, Aggravate, Spread) are among the highest-damage, lowest-investment setups available:

  • Off-field Dendro application: her Skill's Tri-Karma Purification marks up to 8 enemies; any on-field attack triggers a coordinated Dendro hit — she applies Dendro without being on field.
  • Elemental Mastery scaling: both her Skill damage and her Burst's team buff scale off her own EM, so the stat that powers her also powers the whole reaction team.
  • Burst utility: Illusory Heart converts her EM into a team Dendro DMG bonus, extends mark duration, and adds Dendro RES shred when a second Dendro teammate is present.
  • Universal fit: she slots into Bloom, Hyperbloom, Quicken/Aggravate, and Spread teams with minimal rebuild — the definition of evergreen value for a Dendro account.
In a Bloom or Aggravate team, Nahida's EM scaling plus her Burst buff typically adds +20–35% team DPS while also being the team's Dendro driver. That is why she is on almost every Dendro wishlist.

Best Weapons

Nahida wants Elemental Mastery and a catalyst. Because her damage scales off EM, an EM weapon beats a higher-ATK one for most builds.

5★ BiSStaff of the Scarlet Sands

Signature · EM from HP

Her tailor-made weapon: grants a huge Elemental Mastery bonus based on her Max HP, pushing EM past 1,000 easily. The clear best-in-slot, but the gap to F2P is small enough that you can skip it.

Obtain: weapon banner

5★ altWandering Evenstar

EM + team buff

A clean alternative that gives EM and a small party-wide DMG buff after her Burst. Comfortable if you pulled it elsewhere and lack the signature.

Obtain: weapon banner

F2P ★Magic Guide

Top free-to-play pick

A 3-star EM catalyst that, at Refinement 5, rivals 5-star options because Nahida's kit rewards raw EM. The default recommendation for a F2P Nahida — keep it R5.

Obtain: shop / drops (R5 craftable via catalyst wish)

F2PFruit of Fulfillment / Mappa Mare

Craftable EM alternatives

Fruit of Fulfillment gives massive EM (with an ATK penalty that barely matters off-field). Mappa Mare (craftable) adds EM and reaction DMG. Both are strong, free, and easy to refine.

Obtain: blacksmith (crafting)

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Normal Attack is the least of her worries:

  1. Elemental Skill (Tri-Karma Purification) → 8–10. The mark and coordinated attack are her entire kit; leveling it raises both damage and application.
  2. Elemental Burst (Illusory Heart) → 8. Converts EM into team Dendro DMG and adds RES shred; level for comfort.
  3. Normal Attack → 1 (or 6 if you run her as a dedicated on-field driver). Ignore otherwise.

How the Dendro Engine Works

Nahida's value is in enabling reactions, not in the hit itself. Master this and she out-values most 5-stars:

  • Cast Skill: she marks enemies; any on-field attack (yours or a teammate's) triggers a coordinated Dendro strike that scales off her EM and Talent level.
  • Apply the right reaction: pair her with Hydro for Bloom, Electro for Aggravate/Hyperbloom, or pure Dendro for Spread. The reaction you enable decides the team's damage type.
  • Burst EM share: her Burst turns her own EM into a team Dendro DMG bonus — more EM = bigger team buff, which is why EM is her #1 stat.
Key rule: a Nahida who never triggers her coordinated attack (e.g. no on-field attacker) wastes most of her kit. Always pair her with an active driver.

Common Mistakes

  • Building ATK instead of EM. Her damage and team buff scale off EM. An ATK-sponge Nahida buffs almost nobody.
  • Skipping Deepwood Memories. If no one else holds it, Nahida should — without the -30% Dendro RES shred the team loses a huge chunk of damage.
  • Running Deepwood on both Nahida and a teammate. The set effect does not stack. Give it to one holder and Gilded to the other.
  • No on-field driver. Her coordinated attack needs someone attacking. A pure-buffer Nahida with no attacker does almost nothing.
  • Over-leveling her Normal Attack. It barely matters. Spend the books on Skill/Burst.

Don't Have Nahida Yet?

She is a limited 5-star, so you may not own her. For Dendro application, Dendro Traveler (free, holds Deepwood well) or Collei (a 4-star on the beginner radar) are the best stand-ins — both apply Dendro off-field and can hold Deepwood Memories. Build them while you wait for her banner; they cover most Bloom/Quicken teams until she arrives.
