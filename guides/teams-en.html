Genshin Team Comps 2026: Best Teams by Reaction Archetype

A strong Genshin team is never "one broken character" — it is one elemental reaction with four roles filled correctly: a Main DPS, an enabler that supplies the second element, a buffer, and a healer or shielder. This guide organizes the best teams in 2026 by their reaction archetype — Vaporize, Hyperbloom, Aggravate, Freeze, and the new Lunar (Moonsign) meta — then hands you copyable F2P starter teams. Every character links to its full build page.

Note: team archetypes and role logic are long-term stable meta knowledge and do not shift with every patch. Specific unit rankings and banner availability change over time, but the reaction-first framework below holds. In-game data is always final.

The Role Framework

Almost every good team is built from four jobs. Pour your resources into the Main DPS first; a single well-built carry plus a healer clears the story and most mid-game content. Four half-built characters clear nothing.

The one rule that beats any tier list: think in pairs of elements. A perfectly built carry with no enabler is a worse team than two average units that vaporize on every hit.

Vaporize Teams

Vaporize multiplies a Pyro or Hydro hit by up to 2×, which is why Pyro carries want a Hydro applier and Hydro carries want a Pyro. The classic enabler is Xingqiu; Yelan is his stronger, off-field upgrade.

VaporizeHu Tao Vape

Premium Pyro carry core

Hu Tao on-field into Vaporize windows, Yelan/Xingqiu (see beginner list) for Hydro, a shielder (Zhongli) or healer, and a flex buffer. High ceiling, accessible, and one of the most popular teams in 2026.

Core: Hu Tao + Yelan / Xingqiu

VaporizeNeuvillette + Pyro

Self-sufficient Hydro carry

Neuvillette is a self-sufficient Hydro carry who barely needs supports; pair him with a Pyro unit (or just double Hydro) and a buffer. Extremely future-proof for a 2026 start.

Core: Neuvillette + flex

Hyperbloom Teams

Hydro + Dendro seeds Bloom, then an Electro trigger detonates the cores into homing damage that ignores enemy DEF and scales off Elemental Mastery. Cheap to build and dominant in multi-wave Abyss.

HyperbloomNahida Core

The Dendro backbone

Nahida applies Dendro and shares EM; add a Hydro (Yelan/Xingqiu), an Electro trigger (Raiden or Kuki Shinobu), and a shielder/heal. Scales off EM, not the carry — the most F2P-friendly meta team.

Core: Nahida + Yelan + Raiden

HyperbloomRaiden Trigger

Off-field EM trigger

Raiden as a full-EM trigger generates cores on nearly every off-field Electro instance — almost every Bloom core pops within ~1.5s. Best used in current-cycle Floor 12.

Build Raiden full EM (1000+), no CRIT needed

Aggravate Teams

Electro + Dendro (Quicken) buffs every Electro hit. Nahida enables it; an on-field Electro carry reaps the boosted damage. Lower investment than Hyperbloom for single-target.

AggravateNahida + Electro

Dendro-empowered Electro

Nahida's Tri-Karma mark enables Aggravate; pair with an on-field Electro carry (Raiden, Keqing, Alhaitham) plus a buffer. Great single-target, rewards EM investment on the trigger.

Core: Nahida + Raiden

AggravateSpread (Quicken)

Dendro-on-Electro variant

The mirror of Aggravate — Dendro hits get buffed instead. Same Nahida core; swap the on-field unit to a Dendro carry. Flexible for whatever you pulled.

Same support core, different carry

Freeze Teams

Cryo + Hydro locks enemies in place, denying turns while your carry tees off. An Anemo unit holding Viridescent Venerer (VV) shreds resistance and amplifies the lockdown.

FreezeFurina Freeze

Premium crowd-control core

A Cryo carry (Ayaka/Wriothesley/Skirk/Ganyu) + Furina off-field Hydro + Kazuha (VV) + healer. Reliable CC and big Furina buffs. Note: Furina is incompatible with the newer Lunar comps, which keeps her a mainstay here.

Core: Furina + Kazuha + Cryo carry

FreezeKazuha VV Holder

Universal resistance shred

Kazuha swirls the enemy element and shreds resistance for the whole team — the single most flexible support in Freeze and nearly every other archetype.

Slot Kazuha into almost any team

Lunar (Moonsign) Teams

The newest reaction family (version 6.x, around 6.5–6.7). Lunar teams reward stacking specific characters who feed one Moonsign reaction — Lunar-Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Crystallize — rather than chasing classic element pairs. They currently top the Abyss meta but are more roster-dependent.

LunarLunar-Bloom

Top AoE damage (6.7)

Centered on Nefer + Lauma with Columbina and Nahida as the Dendro core. Excels against multi-enemy rooms with frequent, powerful AoE. Needs the newer 5-star linchpins to hit its ceiling.

Core: Nefer + Lauma + Nahida

LunarLunar-Charged

Flins + Ineffa core

Flins deals huge AoE when supported by Moonsign characters (Columbina, Sucrose flex). High ceiling, but Columbina is effectively required for the top tier.

Core: Flins + Ineffa + Columbina
Roster reality: Lunar teams are the strongest in 2026 but demand recent 5-stars. If you do not own the linchpins, the four older archetypes above will clear everything — build those first.

Best Starter Teams (F2P-Friendly)

If you are new or have a shallow roster, these two cores are the shortest path from zero to Abyss 12 clear. Both are 100% free-to-play.

OverworldKazuha + Bennett + Xingqiu + Flex

Universal exploration core

Covers healing, buffs, Hydro reactions, and Anemo utility for puzzles. Drop in any Main DPS you own. Handles every overworld challenge with no perfect rotation.

Core: Kazuha + Bennett + Xingqiu + DPS

AbyssRaiden National

100% F2P, 36-star capable

Raiden + Xiangling + Xingqiu + Bennett. Meta-stable since 2.1, lowest-investment team in the game, and clears all 36 Abyss stars with zero 5★ weapons. Build National first regardless of roster.

Core: Raiden + Xiangling + Xingqiu + Bennett

Common Mistakes When Building Teams

  • Two carries of the same element. They fight over the on-field slot and never trigger a reaction. Run one carry per team.
  • No healer or shielder. Four damage dealers struggle with survivability. At minimum, one character should heal or shield.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge. A team whose Bursts never come up is not dealing its full damage. Check ER on every unit.
  • Stacking buffs that don't apply to the Main DPS. Bennett's ATK circle only helps units inside it — off-field carries like Xiangling still benefit, but a frequently-leaving carry like Hu Tao may miss it.
  • Building EM on the wrong unit. In Hyperbloom, the Electro trigger needs EM, not the Dendro applicator. Stacking EM on Nahida in a Hyperbloom team is a common error.
  • Four different elements. No resonance bonus. Running two of the same element (especially Hydro or Pyro) gives a meaningful passive.
  • Chasing the newest banner blindly. Many new 5-stars are sideways upgrades. If your current team clears comfortably, spend resin on artifacts, not the latest pull.
