Genshin Kazuha vs Xiao: Which to Build? 2026

Kazuha (Support (VV shred)) and Xiao (Plunge DPS) are often compared. Before you wish, which is the better investment? This guide contrasts them across element, weapon, role, best weapon and difficulty, then gives beginner advice and a team-building verdict.

Note: this comparison is based on the two characters' long-term stable roles and mechanics, not version-specific. In-game data is final.

Who Is More Worth Building?

Both are Anemo but different slots: Kazuha is a RES-shred supporter, Xiao is a plunge DPS. Kazuha fits any team; Xiao needs field time and a shield. Need a universal support — Kazuha; want an Anemo carry — Xiao. They can co-team (Xiao + Kazuha plunge comp).

They co-team well — Xiao + Kazuha plunge comp is classic; Kazuha's shred and grouping directly amplify Xiao's plunges. Partners, not rivals.
