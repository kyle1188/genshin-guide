Character Progress Guide

Core Elements of Character Development

In Genshin Impact, character development is one of the core gameplay elements. Proper planning can make your gaming experience smoother.

### Main Development Dimensions

DimensionDescriptionImportance
LevelBase attribute improvementHigh
AscensionUnlock higher level capsHigh
TalentSkill level improvementHigh
WeaponAttribute bonusMedium
ArtifactsSet effectsVery High
ConstellationCharacter transformationAs needed

Development Priority Suggestions

### Phase 1: Beginner Stage (Adventure Rank 1-20)

Focus on main DPS characters:

  • Prioritize leveling main DPS to 60
  • Collect basic artifacts
  • Obtain free weapons

    • ### Phase 2: Growth Stage (Adventure Rank 20-40)

    Comprehensive team development:

  • Level main DPS to 80
  • Level support characters to 60
  • Start collecting artifact sets

    • ### Phase 3: Stable Stage (Adventure Rank 40-55)

    Refined development:

  • Level main DPS to 90
  • Level all characters to 80
  • Optimize artifact main stats

    • Resource Allocation Strategy

    ### Mora Usage Suggestions

  • Prioritize character level upgrades
  • Weapon enhancement is secondary
  • Artifact enhancement can be considered later

    • ### Experience Book Selection

  • Use universal experience books for main characters
  • Special experience books for specific characters
  • Don't waste on backup characters

    • Common Mistakes to Avoid

    ### Mistake 1: Max Everyone

  • Resources are limited, focus on core characters
  • Efficiency decreases above level 60

    • ### Mistake 2: Neglect Supports

  • Support characters also need some development
  • Ensure overall team strength

    • ### Mistake 3: Blind Pulling

  • Plan resin usage rationally
  • Wait for character banner events

    • Summary

    Reasonable character development planning is key to success in Genshin Impact. Create a suitable development plan based on your resources and situation for a more enjoyable gaming experience.
