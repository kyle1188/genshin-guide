Genshin Skirk Build: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: On-field Cryo DPS using Serpent Subtlety instead of normal Energy.
  • Best Weapon: Signature Azurelight (CRIT Rate sub), Mistsplitter second.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Finale of the Deep Galleries (best) or Marechaussee Hunter (with Furina).
  • Main Stats: ATK% Sands / Cryo DMG Goblet / CRIT circlet.
  • Key mechanic: Serpent Subtlety provides i-frames; C0 fully viable.
