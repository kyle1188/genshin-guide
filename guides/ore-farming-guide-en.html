Genshin Impact Ore Farming Guide: Respawn Times, Best Routes & Forging Uses

Quick Answer
  • Respawn cycles: Iron Chunk 24 hours; White Iron Chunk and Starsilver 48 hours; Crystal Chunk, Amethyst Lump, Condessence Crystal, Rainbowdrop Crystal and Magical Crystal Chunk 72 hours.
  • Core recipes: 4 Crystal Chunks + 50 Mora = 1 Mystic Enhancement Ore (10,000 weapon EXP); 3 Magical Crystal Chunks + 10 Original Resin = 6 Mystic Enhancement Ore.
  • Ask the blacksmith daily to mark 3 mining outcrops (unlocked by reaching Reputation level 2 in Mondstadt or Liyue).
  • Fastest ore breaking: Zhongli's held Elemental Skill, then Claymore Charged Attacks.

1. Ore Types and Respawn Times

Ore is the foundation of weapon progression. Weapon levels are fed by enhancement ore, and all enhancement ore comes from mining. When you are stuck on a weapon level, the bottleneck is usually ore rather than Mora. Memorize the three respawn tiers first: 24 / 48 / 72 hours. They define your farming rhythm.

One mechanic matters more than the numbers: the timer starts when you break the node, not at a global reset. Inside a single mine, one Crystal Chunk may regrow today and another tomorrow, so do not camp a single spot.

Two official rules deserve their own line: forging has a daily cap of 300,000 weapon EXP (about 30 Mystic Enhancement Ore), and forging with Magical Crystal Chunks ignores that cap. So Magical Crystal Chunks are not just ore savings, they are a way to break the daily ceiling.

Treasure Compasses (Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro and Pyro) each cost 50 Crystal Chunks plus a regional specialty and Gold Raven Insignias. Crystal Chunks are permanently in demand, so stockpile every one you mine.

3. Best Mining Characters and Techniques

  • Zhongli: his held Elemental Skill destroys ore nodes outright with no attack animation, making him the fastest miner in the game.
  • Claymore users: Razor, Noelle, Eula and Diluc break clustered nodes with a single Charged Attack, a very reliable fallback.
  • Ningguang: her exploration talent marks nearby ore on the minimap, ideal for scouting unfamiliar areas.
  • Yanfei: the same type of talent, so she complements Ningguang when searching.
  • Mobility and stamina: bring a NRE and stamina food. Travel time usually exceeds mining time.
  • Unlocking outcrops: raise Mondstadt or Liyue Reputation to level 2 for Mining Outcrop Search, then talk to the blacksmith (Wagner or Iron Shoulder Zhang) daily to mark 3 nodes on your map.

Route design principle: loop between teleport waypoints and Statues of the Seven instead of clearing the map. A loop covering four to six dense clusters is enough; overreaching wastes more time than it earns.

5. Three Farming Rhythms

  • Daily (Iron): sweep two or three Iron nodes when you log in. Almost free, and it covers day-to-day needs.
  • Every other day (White Iron and Starsilver): the 48-hour tier, used to top up Fine Enhancement Ore.
  • Every third day (Crystal, Amethyst, Condessence, Rainbowdrop, Magical): the main event. One high-value loop typically beats the other two runs combined.

Short on ore and out of time? Go co-op. Ore nodes are independent per player, so gathering in a friend's world does not reduce their ore. Ask first, though.

6. Common Mistakes

  • Burning Crystal Chunks as EXP. Check whether you still owe someone a Treasure Compass, which costs 50 chunks each.
  • Farming one mine only. A 72-hour respawn means each cluster yields once every three days, so rotate between regions.
  • Ignoring the daily forging cap. Standard forging only accepts 300,000 weapon EXP per day. Magical Crystal Chunks are how you go faster.
  • Bringing no ore-breaker. Chipping nodes with a sword or polearm character is several times slower than using Zhongli.

Bottom Line

Ore farming comes down to three respawn tiers and one fixed loop. Remember 24 / 48 / 72 hours, string four to six high-value clusters into a circuit, and run it every third day. Fill gaps through co-op, and use Magical Crystal Chunks when you need to beat the daily forging cap. The ore is not hard to find, only hard to keep farming.
