Genshin Venti Build & Teams: 5-Star Anemo Bow & Viridescent Venerer

Quick Answer

  • Venti is a 5★ Anemo (wind) Bow user — Mondstadt's Anemo Archon, Barbatos — a crowd-control support / sub-DPS whose core is his Burst "Wind's Grand Ode" grouping enemies + Swirl RES shred.
  • Core artifact: Viridescent Venerer 4pc (the "VV" set; 40% Swirl RES shred is his support backbone).
  • Main stats: ER Sands → EM Sands once ER is met; EM Goblet / EM Circlet for pure support; substats ER > EM > ATK / CRIT.
  • ER threshold: 160–200% to keep Burst up every rotation (at C0).
  • Signature Elegy for the End; strong 4★: The Stringless, Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow, Windblume Ode.
  • Best teams: Freeze (Ayaka + Shenhe + Kokomi), International (Childe + Xiangling + Bennett), Hyperbloom (Nahida + Alhaitham + Kuki).
  • C0 is already complete — no constellation investment needed for F2P.

Character Mechanics

Venti's value is top-tier group crowd control + Swirl RES shred:

  • Elemental Skill "Skyward Sonnet": tap summons a wind domain that lifts small enemies and generates particles; hold creates a larger AoE and lets Venti ride the wind upward (a god-tier overworld exploration tool).
  • Elemental Burst "Wind's Grand Ode": fires a storm that continuously pulls nearby enemies in; on contact with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro it gains that element and deals bonus damage — the engine of his grouping and Swirl.
  • Passive "Stormeye": after the Burst ends, Venti recovers 15 energy (and the corresponding element's characters recover 15 if a conversion occurred), easing his ER needs.
  • So Venti prioritizes Energy Recharge to guarantee per-rotation uptime, then shifts to Elemental Mastery to amplify Swirl once ER is satisfied.

FAQ

Is Venti still worth building?

Yes. C0 is already complete, and his grouping + Swirl RES shred is extremely universal; he's also T0 for overworld exploration. The only downsides: large enemies (Ruin Guards, shielded Fatui) can't be grouped, and he's weaker against single targets.

What is his best artifact set?

Viridescent Venerer 4pc (the 'VV' set) — the 40% Swirl RES shred is his support core. Once ER is met, swap Sands/Goblet/Circlet to Elemental Mastery. Use 2+2 if you lack good VV pieces.

How much Energy Recharge does Venti need?

Around 160–200% to guarantee Burst every rotation. After that, prioritize Elemental Mastery to amplify Swirl — no need to stack CRIT.

Is his signature weapon worth pulling?

Elegy for the End fits his support role (team EM + ATK), but 4★ The Stringless / Favonius Warbow are already enough for Spiral Abyss and daily play — not a must-pull.

Venti or Kazuha?

Pick Venti for group crowd control; pick Kazuha for single-target buffing and flexibility. They fill different niches and can coexist in one box.
