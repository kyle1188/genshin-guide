Spiral Abyss Speedrun Techniques

The Spiral Abyss is the ultimate test of character strength. This guide teaches you how to use proper team composition and rotation sequences to complete Floor 12 in under 3 minutes.

Abyss Structure Review

The Spiral Abyss has two chambers, each with 2-3 waves of enemies. Total time limit is 3 minutes on Floor 12.

----------------------------------

Floors 7-12Elite + Boss + mechanics2:30-3:00

SupportBuff/debuffKazuha/Bennett/Zhongli
Healer/ShielderSurvivalBarbara/Ningguang/Beidou

Key pairing rules:

1. 1 high-frequency elemental apply (Xingqiu/Kuki Shinobu/Fischl)

2. 1 reaction trigger (Dendro/Vaporize/Melt)

3. 1 damage buffer (Kazuha/Bennett)

4. 1 survival slot (shield or heal)

---

Chamber 1: Speedrun Teams

Recommended lineup:

  • Main DPS: Raiden Shogun / Hu Tao / Yelan
  • Sub DPS: Xingqiu / Xiangling / Kuki Shinobu
  • Support: Kazuha / Bennett
  • Healer: Barbara / Ningguang

Rotation sequence (Raiden example):


①\ Kazuha\ E\ group\ →\ ②\ Xingqiu\ E\ water\ →\ ③\ Xiangling\ E\ pyro\ →\ ④\ Bennett\ burst\ →\ ⑤\ Raiden\ burst\ →\ ⑥\ Normal\ attacks

Time control:

  • Group clearing phase: under 15 seconds for mobs
  • Boss phase: under 45 seconds

---

Chamber 2: High Pressure Strategy

Multi-target encounters:

  • Use AoE characters (Yelan/AYaka/Hu Tao)
  • Prioritize ranged enemies
  • Use elemental sight to mark targets

High defense enemies:

  • Use shield pierce characters (Sayu/Zhongli)
  • Elemental reactions bypass defense (Superconduct/Vaporize)
  • Avoid pure physical DPS

Time management tips:

1. Burst on opening — use elemental bursts immediately

2. Quick swap — switch in and use skill instantly

3. Dodge efficiently — reduce healing pressure, save resin

FAQ

Q: What team do I need for chamber 1?

A: A high-frequency app (Xingqiu/Kuki) + reaction trigger (dendro/vaporize) + support (Kazuha/Bennett) + healer.

Q: Running out of time?

A: Prioritize chamber 1 clear; use quick-swap technique for chamber 2; use fragile resin for extra attempts.

Q: Which elemental resonance to pick?

A: Double Anemo (resistance shred) + Double Hydro/Dendro (stable damage). Avoid Double Pyro (overloaded risk).

Q: Tips for Floor 12?

A: Use elemental sight for weaknesses; clear minions first before boss; position off-field characters early.
