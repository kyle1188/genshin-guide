Genshin Talent Priority: How to Level Talents by Role

Quick Answers

  • Core principle: a main DPS maxes its key burst; a support only reaches the level that matters.
  • Value inflection point: level 6 on one talent is the best cost-to-benefit level; cost climbs steeply after that.
  • Crown strategy: do not spread Crowns evenly; concentrate them on one or two core characters.

Per-Character Advice

Priority (highest value)

  • Bennett: Burst (ATK buff) → Skill (heal) → no Normal Attacks
  • Xingqiu: Skill (sword) → Burst → no Normal Attacks
  • Xiangling: Burst (Guoba) → Skill → no Normal Attacks
  • Fischl: Burst (Oz) → Skill → no Normal Attacks

Adjust to your playstyle

  • Hu Tao: Burst > Normal > Skill (charged builds also raise Normal)
  • Raiden Shogun: Burst > Skill > Normal (charged benefits during the burst)
  • Nahida: Skill (seed reactions) ≈ Burst > Normal

Crown of Insight Strategy

Crowns are extremely scarce, usually one or two per version. Allocation rules:

  1. Only for a core or favorite: a character you will keep long-term justifies a triple-crown.
  2. Secure the base levels first: get the three talents at least to 7 before touching a Crown.
  3. Do not spread them thin: two Crowns on one character beats one Crown on three.
Golden rule: a Crown is the final investment. First raise your core talents to 9-10 with normal upgrades, then use remaining resin and Mora to sprint toward a Crown.

Common Questions

What talent level is the best value?

For supports/sub-DPS, levels 6-8 offer the best value; for a main DPS's key talent aim for 9-10. One talent from 1 to 6 costs only about 120k Mora, and the cost doubles from level 7 onward.

Who should get a Crown of Insight?

Only long-term core characters. If you are not sure who you will keep, level the other talents to 9-10 first and hold the Crown.

When should I farm talent books?

Each talent book opens on fixed weekdays. Check the domain schedule and plan your resin in advance; you can also craft lower-tier books into higher ones to fill gaps.

Do I need to max all three talents?

No. For most characters, two key talents at 9-10 are enough, and leaving the third at a low level saves resources.
