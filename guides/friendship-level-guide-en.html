Genshin Friendship Level Guide: Fastest Way to Level 10 & All Rewards

Friendship Level is every character's bond meter: level 10 unlocks their exclusive namecard plus every character story and voice line. It costs no Primogems — only "screen time" — but co-op doubling and Serenitea Pot idle gains can halve the grind. This guide covers every EXP source, the optimal daily loop, and what each level pays.

  • What does Friendship do? Unlocks character stories (1-5), "More About" voice lines, Photo Mode expressions, and the level-10 exclusive namecard. Purely collectible — no combat stats.
  • How is EXP shared? Every point of Companionship EXP goes in full to all four party members (it multiplies, it never divides). The Traveler and level-10 characters don't participate.
  • Fastest sources? Daily commissions + Katheryne turn-in in co-op (×2 everything); then weekly bosses (55-70 each) and the Serenitea Pot (2-5/hour idle, 500 per batch cap).
  • How does the Teapot work? Finish "Idle Teapot Talk" to unlock companion invitations; housed characters passively accumulate EXP by Adeptal Energy — claim the Realm Bounty from Tubby periodically.

How the system works

Friendship is the heart-shaped bar on each character's profile, fed by Companionship EXP to a cap of level 10 (29,100 total EXP). Three rules govern everything:

  • Full value to everyone: EXP awards each party member the full amount — not split by four. Whoever you want to level, keep them in the active party.
  • Traveler excluded: the main character has no friendship track; level-10 characters stop earning, effectively donating their share.
  • Co-op doubles: commissions, bosses and domains run in co-op award ×2 Companionship EXP — the single biggest legal accelerator in the game.

Reward pacing: stories and voice lines unlock at 2/4/6, a second Photo Mode expression at 3, the Envisaged Echoes challenge at 8 (where available), and the exclusive namecard at 10 plus progress toward the "Hitherto Unknown" achievement.

All of the above double in co-op. A normal day (4 commissions + turn-in + resin spent) yields roughly 200-300 EXP solo, 400-600 in co-op.

The optimal daily loop

  1. Load the target characters: fill your party with whoever needs friendship — the full-value rule makes 4 targets at once optimal.
  2. Co-op the commissions: enter a friend's world (or host them) so all four commissions plus the turn-in double.
  3. Spend resin on weekly bosses first (best EXP per resin), then normal bosses and ley lines.
  4. Clear random events: grab the NPC pop-up events while traveling — 10 per day is a steady 100-150 EXP.
  5. Claim the Realm Bounty nightly: park up to 8 characters in the pot (Trust Rank 9) and collect from Tubby when you log in.

At this pace a character goes 0 → 10 in about 2-3 months solo, or 6-8 weeks with regular co-op.

Serenitea Pot deep dive

The pot is the only offline friendship source — set it once and it works forever:

  • Unlock: the world quest "Idle Teapot Talk" grants the Realm Dispatch, enabling companion invitations.
  • Mechanics: housed companions earn 2-5 EXP/hour based on Adeptal Energy, banking up to 500 per batch (Trust Rank 10) — you must enter the pot and claim the Realm Bounty from Tubby to bank it.
  • Capacity: Trust Rank gates companion slots up to 8 at rank 9 — park every "no time to play but want the namecard" character there.
  • Swapping: housed characters accumulate while you're offline; earned EXP stays with each character when swapped out.

At 20,000+ Adeptal Energy the passive income is substantial (see our Serenitea Pot guide) — the casual player's friendship engine.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming EXP splits by four: it doesn't — every member gets the full value, so more targets in party is pure profit.
  • Skipping the turn-in: the 45-100 EXP bonus at Katheryne is missed as often as the Primogems.
  • Letting Realm Bounty cap sit: unclaimed pot EXP stalls at the batch cap — collect when you log in.
  • Party slots wasted on the Traveler: the Traveler earns nothing; fill those slots with real targets.
  • Ignoring random events: 10 per day is a guaranteed 100-150 EXP for tasks that take seconds.

Summary

Friendship is a "time for collectibles" system: the full-value rule makes 4-target parties optimal, co-op doubles everything, and the pot keeps earning while you sleep. Keep targets in your party, never skip the turn-in, claim the Realm Bounty daily — one namecard every 2-3 months at zero Primogem cost. Hand the idle time to the pot and the rushed time to co-op; that's the entire strategy.
