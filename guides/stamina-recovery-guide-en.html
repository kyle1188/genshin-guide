Stamina & Recovery Guide

Understanding the Stamina System

Stamina is one of the most important resources in Genshin Impact. It affects your character's movement, combat, and exploration abilities.

### Basic Mechanics

  • Base stamina cap: 100 points
  • Sprinting consumes: ~6 points per second
  • Climbing consumes: ~3 points per second
  • Plunge attack consumes: 14-27 points

    • ### Recovery Methods

    MethodRecovery AmountNotes
    -------------------------------
    Standing still1 point/secFastest recovery
    Consuming foodInstantRequires consumption
    TeleportingFull restoreEvery teleport

    Efficient Stamina Management Tips

    ### 1. Plan Routes Carefully

  • Avoid unnecessary climbing and sprinting
  • Use waypoints for quick travel
  • Pre-plan collection routes

    • ### 2. Choose Suitable Characters

  • Anemo characters can glide to save stamina
  • Hydro characters can swim to reduce stamina drain
  • Geo characters can create platforms to reduce climbing

    • Stamina Challenges by Region

    ### Mondstadt

  • Relatively flat terrain
  • Suitable for beginners to practice stamina management

    • ### Liyue

  • Mountainous terrain requires more climbing
  • Special stamina recovery mechanics available

    • ### Inazuma

  • Electro element affects stamina recovery
  • Special Sacred Sakura trees for restoration

    • ### Sumeru

  • Complex rainforest terrain
  • Rich elemental reactions

    • Summary

    Proper stamina management is key to improving your gaming experience. By understanding the mechanics, choosing suitable characters and items, exploration becomes more relaxed and efficient.
