Daily Resin Efficiency Guide

Resin is one of the most precious resources in Genshin. The right daily routine and material farming strategy can double your progression efficiency.

Standard Daily Resin Flow

Goal: Complete all dailies in 30-45 minutes with zero resin waste.

--------------------------------

Daily Commission Rewards

Complete 4 commissions to receive:

  • 2,000 Mora
  • Adventure EXP ×10
  • Random materials ×1 (4 types total)

Resin cost: 0 (perfect!)

> Tip: Do commissions first thing every day. Don't delay.

Zero-Waste Resin Principles

PrincipleDescription
------------------------
**Clear resin before sleep**Ensure resin is zero or converted to fragile resin before bed
**Don't farm overflowing mats**Stop farming when you have excess materials
**Prioritize current character**Spend resin on your active team, not spreading thin
**Weekend bonus farming**Farm extra on weekends to avoid weekday shortages

FAQ

Q: What's the relationship between commissions and resin?

A: Daily commissions cost zero resin — they're free daily rewards giving EXP books and materials.

Q: Can I save resin?

A: Yes, max storage is 160. Excess overflows and is lost. Clear before sleeping or convert to fragile resin.

Q: Not enough weekly boss materials?

A: Farm once each on Mon/Thu/Sun to cover the weekly cycle. Prioritize materials for your current character.

Q: When's the best time to farm event materials?

A: Event materials have expiration dates. Start farming immediately when an event launches to avoid running out.
