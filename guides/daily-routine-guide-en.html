Genshin Impact Daily Routine Checklist: The 15-Minute Loop and Weekly Reset Priorities

Genshin Impact does not reward grinding harder — it rewards never missing something that resets. Commissions reset daily, weekly bosses reset on Monday, and Resin stops regenerating entirely once it caps at 200. Missed time is never refunded. This guide turns the daily and weekly essentials into one executable checklist, with compressed versions for busy days.

  • What is mandatory daily? Four commissions plus the Katheryne turn-in (60 Primogems), then spend your Resin so it never caps.
  • When does everything reset? Daily and weekly both reset at 4:00 AM server time; the shop resets on the 1st at 4:00 AM, and the Spiral Abyss on the 16th.
  • What is the Resin cap? 200 (since 5.0), regenerating 1 point every 8 minutes — about 180 per day, and roughly 26h40m for a full refill.
  • Short on time? With 2 minutes, just check in and spend Resin. With 5 minutes, add commissions. Only 15 minutes gives you the full loop.

Order matters: do commissions before spending Resin, because commissions carry you across the map and naturally cover the regions you need. Leave expeditions and the teapot for last — they cost no stamina, so forgetting them only costs you a small amount.

Resin: the only resource you can waste

  • Cap: 200 (raised from 160 in 5.0). Regenerates 1 point every 8 minutes — about 180 per day, and roughly 26h40m from empty to full.
  • Once it hits 200, regeneration stops completely. This is the one resource in the game you lose by not playing.
  • Condensed Resin: 40 Original Resin + 1 Crystal Core + 100 Mora, hold up to 5, doubles domain or Ley Line rewards. It cannot be used on weekly bosses.
  • Fragile / Transient Resin: each restores 60 and can push you past the 200 cap. Save Fragile Resin until Adventure Rank 45, when 5-star artifacts become farmable.
  • Spending Resin also grants Adventure EXP (10 Resin = 50 EXP), so clearing it is progression in itself.

Common mistakes

  • Letting Resin sit at 200: every capped hour costs you about 7.5 Resin of regeneration.
  • Leaving weekly bosses until Sunday night: they reset Monday 4:00 AM, and last-day rushes are where drops get missed.
  • Assuming each region has its own reputation weeklies: the 3+3 is shared across all regions.
  • Burning Fragile Resin early: artifact farming before AR45 is inefficient, so save it.
  • Buying Resin with Primogems: 50 Primogems for 60 Resin is far worse value than saving for wishes.

Summary

The daily routine is not about doing more — it is about never missing something that resets. Make commissions plus Resin spending muscle memory, treat Monday as your weekly cleanup day, and let expeditions, the teapot and the Battle Pass come along for free. Consistency beats the occasional binge grind every single time.
