Is a Genshin Signature Weapon Worth Pulling: R1 to R5 Refinement, 4-star Fallbacks

"Is a signature weapon worth pulling?" has a two-part answer: worth it for R1, not worth it for R5. An R1 already captures 85-95% of the weapon's total value and its base stats plus sub-stat crush any 4-star; but R1 to R5 only adds 8-18% real DPS at the cost of one to two 5-star characters in pulls. This page turns the refinement curve, the 4-star 85% fallback threshold, and the weapon-banner 75/25 Epitomized math into one pull-decision table.

  • Verdict: pull a signature weapon to R1 and stop; R5 is only for two outliers (Wolf's Gravestone / Fishing Rod on a free path).
  • Fallback threshold: a 4-star R5 hits 90-109% of an R1 signature weapon's performance - the 85% line is "good enough".
  • Cost basis: weapon banner hard-pities at 80; worst case 160; soft pity from ~62-65.

The R1 to R5 Refinement Curve

Refinement only raises the passive's rank (R0x1 to R5x6); base stats never move. The passive's share of your damage sets how much refinement is worth - high passive-growth weapons bank the full refinement bonus, stat-stacking weapons (attack/crit) barely do:

The quantified basis: R1 to R5 lifts passive numbers 15-25%, but real DPS only usually rises 8-18% (depending on weapon type and team). By comparison, the same pulls buying a new 5-star character almost always give your account the bigger gain.

Important: a fallback only means "good enough", not "optimal". The signature weapon's base stats plus sub-stat still hold a 10-15% edge, so "pull R1 and stop, use a 4-star R5 if you miss" has a higher expected return than "chase full R5 by force".

Weapon-Banner Cost: 75/25 Epitomized Math

The weapon banner splits pulls across a 75% Epitomized weapon / 25% elemental-weapon pool; hard pity at 80, soft pity from ~62-65, and pity carries over between weapon banners. Worst case (two 80-pull misses) = 160 pulls. The weapon banner's 5-star rate is 0.8% per pull, so the expected time to one 5-star is 125 pulls (unlike the character banner, where pulls still follow the 80 hard-pity). Chasing R5 means getting the same 5-star four extra times - 500+ pulls in expectation, well past most accounts' weapon budget.

The 3 Checks Before Pulling

  1. Is this weapon's passive a big share of your DPS with high uptime? (If not, stop at R1.)
  2. Are your artifacts, talents, and team already done? (If not, do characters first - refinement is the last optimization.)
  3. Do you already own every 5-star you want? (If not, a new character > weapon refinement.)

Only when all three are "yes" should you consider R5; if any is "no", invest the resources elsewhere.

Bottom Line

In one line: a signature weapon is worth pulling for R1, not for R5 - R1 captures 85-95% of the value, R5 only adds 8-18% DPS at four times the pulls; only the Fishing Rod (free) and Wolf's Gravestone are exceptions. If you miss it, use a 4-star R5 (inside the 90-109% band) and send the saved pulls to your next 5-star.
