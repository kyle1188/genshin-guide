FAQ

Q: Do I need to pull every T0 character?

A: No, but they drastically reduce build difficulty. T0 characters' real value is "versatility" — they appear in many team compositions.

Q: Are 5-star characters always better than 4-stars?

A: Not necessarily. Xingqiu, Bennett, Fischl, and Xiangling (the "Four Divine Generals") are all 4-stars that outperform many 5-stars.

Q: Will character tiers change with new versions?

A: Yes. New character releases, artifact set changes, and weapon banners can shift tier rankings. This list reflects the 5.0+ metasphere.

Q: Who should F2P players prioritize?

A: Xingqiu → Bennett → Fischl → Xiangling → Kuki Shinobu → Sucrose. These six form the foundation of the strongest F2P roster.
