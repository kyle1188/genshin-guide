Genshin Impact Nilou Build & Teams: 5-Star Hydro Polearm Bloom Enabler

Quick Answer

  • Nilou is a 5-star Hydro Polearm (Sumeru dancer), a Bloom enabler/support who deals almost no personal damage.
  • Core passive "Court of Dancing Petals": with a Hydro+Dendro-only party, normal Dendro Cores become Bountiful Cores — fast-bursting, wide-radius, and CRIT-immune.
  • Artifacts: 2pc Tenacity of the Millelith + 2pc Vourukasha's Glow (best mixed set); main stats HP% Sands / HP% Goblet / HP% Circlet ("triple HP").
  • Weapon: signature Key of Khaj-Nisut (HP converted to team-wide EM); F2P Iron Sting or Xiphos' Moonlight.
  • Talent priority: Elemental Skill > Elemental Burst >> Normal Attack (skip NA).
  • HP stacks: aim for 74,444 HP to cap her A1 (every 1,000 HP above 30,000 adds 9% Bountiful Core DMG, capped at 400%).
  • Best team: Nilou + Nahida + Kokomi + Collei (pure Hydro-Dendro Bloom); C0 is already complete.

Role Mechanics

Nilou doesn't deal personal damage — she turns the whole team into a Bloom engine:

  • Elemental Skill "Dance of Haftkarsvar": Tap (Sword Dance) applies Hydro, Hold (Whirling Steps) deploys a Hydro ring. Three E casts set up max Hydro application — the core of Bloom triggering.
  • Core passive "Court of Dancing Petals": With a Hydro+Dendro-only party, normal Dendro Cores upgrade to Bountiful Cores — they burst faster, hit a wider radius, ignore CRIT, and cannot trigger Hyperbloom/Burgeon.
  • A1 passive "Dreamy Dance of Aeons": Every 1,000 Max HP above 30,000 adds 9% Bountiful Core DMG, capped at 400% — so everything revolves around HP.
  • Elemental Burst "Dance of Abzendegi": AoE Hydro DMG scaling off HP with sustained application; only 60 energy, easy to rotate.

FAQ

Must Nilou be Hydro+Dendro only?

Yes. Court of Dancing Petals requires a party of only Hydro and Dendro characters; any Pyro/Electro/Cryo/Anemo teammate disables the passive and reverts cores to normal.

Best Nilou weapon?

Key of Khaj-Nisut is best-in-slot (HP converted to team EM). Without it, Iron Sting (craftable, EM) or Xiphos' Moonlight work well.

Does Nilou need CRIT?

No. Bountiful Cores ignore CRIT; stack HP% and a little EM. CRIT substats are wasted.

How much HP for Nilou?

Target 74,444 to max her A1 passive (9% per 1,000 HP over 30,000, cap 400%); stay above 50,000 at minimum.

Which constellations matter?

C0 is complete; C2 adds Hydro/Dendro RES shred (big spike); C6 opens a direct-damage playstyle but is costly.
