Genshin Crit Rate vs Crit DMG 2026: The 1:2 Ratio & Target Panel

Everyone starts at 5% Rate / 50% DMG. Pushing both to ~1:2 is the highest-value panel move you can make — here's the math and the practice.

Every character starts with 5% Crit Rate / 50% Crit DMG. Gear must raise both, near a 1:2 ratio — the highest-value panel move you can make.

Why 1:2

The crit multiplier on average damage is 1 + Crit Rate × Crit DMG. +1% Rate adds your current DMG; +1% DMG adds your current Rate. Because one artifact substat line of DMG (≈7.8%) is worth about twice a line of Rate (≈3.9%), pushing DMG to ~2× your Rate balances the two — the mathematical optimum.

Counter-example: 100% Rate + 50% DMG = 1+1×0.5 = 1.5; but 50% Rate + 150% DMG = 1+0.5×1.5 = 1.75, same total invest, far higher. Lopsided hurts more than low.

Crit Value: Score Any Piece

The community grades a piece with CV = Crit DMG% + Crit Rate%×2 (Rate doubled for the 1:2 value). Roughly: CV 20+ usable, 30+ good, 40+ god-tier. CV counts substats only, not base or weapon.

Which Circlet Main Stat

Circlet offers only Crit Rate (cap ≈31.1%) or Crit DMG (cap ≈62.2%). Pick the one you lack: a weapon already pouring DMG → Crit Rate circlet, and vice versa. Sands/Cup follow the kit (ATK%/element DMG%/HP%).

Where Crit Comes From

Circlet main

Biggest single lever; directly fixes your unbalanced side.

Weapon substat

Many 5★/4★ weapons carry crit; dictates circlet compensation.

Ascension bonus

Some characters ascend into crit, quietly solving half the ratio.

Artifact substats

Double-crit rolls are the endgame; keep pieces with both.

Common Mistakes

  • DMG only: too little Rate makes DMG worthless; average stays low.
  • Rate to 100%: sacrifices DMG; total multiplier drops unless forced by kit.
  • Forgetting ascension/resonance: Cryo Resonance +15% Rate and ascension crit are free — count them before choosing circlet.

Quick Answers

What are Crit Rate and Crit DMG?

Crit Rate is the chance a hit crits; Crit DMG is the bonus multiplier on a crit. Everyone starts at 5% Rate / 50% DMG; gear must raise both together.

Why aim for a 1:2 ratio?

Average damage multiplier is 1 + Rate×DMG. One artifact line of DMG (≈7.8%) is worth ~twice a line of Rate (≈3.9%), so pushing DMG to ~2× your Rate balances them — the optimum. Stray far and the over-weighted stat's marginal gain drops.

How do I calculate Crit Value?

CV = Crit DMG% + Crit Rate%×2. It grades a piece's crit quality: 20+ usable, 30+ good, 40+ god-tier. Substats only, not base or weapon.

Crit Rate or Crit DMG circlet?

Pick the one you lack. If your weapon already gives lots of DMG, take a Rate circlet, and vice versa. Cryo gets +15% Rate from resonance and some characters ascend into crit — count those before choosing.
