Genshin Impact Shenhe Build & Teams: 5-Star Cryo Polearm Icy Quill Buffer

Quick Answer

  • Shenhe is a 5-star Cryo Polearm (Liyue "Lonesome Transcendence"), a Cryo-specific buffer / sub-DPS whose core is Icy Quill — a rare independent multiplier that adds flat Cryo DMG based on her own ATK.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Noblesse Oblige (Burst grants team +20% ATK); main stats ATK% Sands / ATK% Goblet / ATK% Circlet.
  • Substats: Energy Recharge > ATK% > CRIT; aim for 180–220% ER to Burst every rotation.
  • Weapon: signature Calamity Queller (high base ATK + ATK%); F2P Favonius Lance (particles ease ER) or The Catch (fishing).
  • Talent priority: Elemental Skill > Elemental Burst >> Normal Attack.
  • Mechanic: Skill Press gives 3 Icy Quills, Hold gives 5–7; Hold lasts longer with more triggers — Ayaka's Burst consumes them all.
  • Best team: Shenhe-Ayaka-Kazuha-Kokomi (classic Freeze core); also Ganyu Freeze and Melt-Ganyu.

Role Mechanics

Shenhe is a Cryo-specific buffer who turns her ATK into an independent damage multiplier for the team:

  • Elemental Skill "Spring Spirit Summoning": Grants the team Icy Quills — Cryo DMG gains extra scaling based on Shenhe's ATK (an independent multiplier that conflicts with nothing). Press gives 3 Quills, Hold gives 5–7, with Hold lasting longer and triggering more times.
  • Elemental Burst "Divine Maiden's Deliverance": A field dealing periodic Cryo DMG that shreds enemy Cryo and Physical RES, plus +15% Cryo DMG to the team.
  • Passive "Spirit Communion Seal": After Skill, grants the team +15% corresponding DMG (Hold = NA/CA/Plunge, Press = Skill/Burst) for 10–15s.

FAQ

Support or sub-DPS?

Primarily a Cryo damage amplifier; C0 is pure support, C6 can go sub-DPS (4pc Blizzard Strayer + CRIT).

Best Shenhe weapon?

Calamity Queller fits best (high base ATK + ATK%). Without 5★, Favonius Lance (eases ER) or The Catch (free fishing weapon).

Does Shenhe need CRIT Rate?

Only if using Favonius Lance — then ~40–60% CRIT to proc its passive. Other weapons don't need it.

How much ER for Shenhe?

180–220% (lower with Favonius); her Burst (80 cost) also shreds Cryo/Physical RES.

Which constellations?

C0 is complete; C1 adds extra Quill charges and eases rotation; C6 makes NA/CA not consume Quills — sub-DPS breakpoint.
