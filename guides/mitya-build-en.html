Genshin Mitya Build: 7.2 Electro Catalyst DPS

Quick Answer

  • Role: On-field DPS or off-field support, built around Stellar-Conduct reaction.
  • Weapons: Starsealed Edict (5★ signature) > Winter's Heavy Heart (4★) > Wandering Evenstar.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (on-field) or Heart of the Furnace / Tenacity (support).
  • Stats: EM sands + EM goblet + CRIT circlet. ER target 120-140% for on-field.
  • Teams: Mitya + Odette + Yae Miko + Valeriy (needs Cryo teammate).

Who Is Mitya?

Mitya is a 5★ Electro catalyst character released in Version 7.2 (November 4, 2026). His kit revolves around Stellar-Conduct—a customized version of Superconduct that requires both Cryo and Electro elements.
