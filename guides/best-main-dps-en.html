How to Choose / Team Up

Build order: pour resources into one main DPS to completion (level 90, key talent 9, double-CRIT artifacts) before a second. One finished carry beats several half-built ones.

Quick Answers

Which main DPS should a beginner build first?

Prioritize ones that work at C0 without a specific team: Neuvillette, Arlecchino, or Hu Tao (with Xingqiu) are stable; Ganyu/Xiao/Wanderer want more investment. On a tight budget, a Hydro or Pyro carry is most universal.

How do I pair a main DPS with sub-DPS?

The main DPS stays on-field; off-field sub-DPS (Yelan/Xingqiu/Xiangling/Fischl) add damage; supports (Kazuha/Zhongli/Bennett) shred/shield/buff. One main DPS + one off-field sub-DPS + two supports is the universal frame.

Are these main DPS still worth pulling?

All are long-term strong with stable kits that don't get overhauled by versions. Fill the element/playstyle you lack; don't chase new over proven.
