Cyno Build & Teams: 5-Star Electro Polearm Carry & Aggravate Core

Quick Answer

  • Cyno is a 5★ Electro polearm on-field carry. His Burst enters an empowered "Initiator's Oath" state where Normal/Charged Attacks deal Electro; core play = Aggravate + Quickbloom.
  • Core sets: Gilded Dreams 4pc (+80 EM, EM to same-element / ATK to different-element teammates) or Thundering Fury 4pc (Electro reaction route, E CD −1s).
  • Main stats: EM Sands (reaction) / ATK%, Electro Cup, Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: ER (keep Burst cycling) > Crits > EM > ATK.
  • Best teams: Cyno + Nahida + Fischl + Kazuha/Baizhu (Aggravate); Cyno + Nahida + Xingqiu + Raiden/Kuki Shinobu (Quickbloom).
  • Signature weapon: Staff of the Scarlet Sands converts EM into damage.

How Cyno Works

Cyno is a "long on-field + fastest melee attack speed" carry:

  • Burst enters Initiator's Oath: Normal/Charged/Plunging all become un-overridable Electro, lasting ~18s, can't swap out during it.
  • Empowered state grants knockback immunity + Electro-Charged immunity, making on-field safer.
  • Very high attack frequency → triggers Aggravate with Dendro + Electro application, or Quickbloom with Hydro.
  • The Skill can be cast mid-Burst when the purple prompt appears to land the empowered multi-hit — his main output input.

Team Comps

Aggravate Team (most reliable)

Cyno (DPS) + Nahida (Dendro/EM) + Fischl (off-field Electro) + Kazuha/Baizhu (grouping/shield)

  • Nahida applies Dendro + Fischl's Oz applies Electro → target stays Quicken'd.
  • Cyno's on-field hits trigger Aggravate constantly; Gilded Dreams amplifies it.
  • Baizhu's shield resists interruption; Kazuha swirls and shreds Electro RES.

Quickbloom Team (best single-target)

Cyno (DPS) + Nahida (Dendro) + Xingqiu (Hydro) + Raiden/Kuki Shinobu (trigger/ER)

  • Xingqiu applies Hydro + Nahida applies Dendro → Dendro Cores spawn.
  • Raiden/Kuki Shinobu apply Electro → Cores turn Quickbloom.
  • Cyno's on-field window cashes in both Quickbloom and his own Aggravate.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: Normal hits stack +10% Electro DMG (max 5 stacks) — the biggest on-field upgrade, top value.
  • C1: +20% ATK Speed in the empowered state, higher reaction trigger rate.

FAQ

Is Cyno Electro or Dendro?

His element is Electro (Electro Resonance; can trigger Charged/Superconduct/Aggravate). He's a Sumeru character but since 4.x plays as an Electro polearm carry. Teams revolve around "he applies Electro + teammates apply Dendro/Hydro."

Is Cyno worth pulling now?

Yes — he has the fastest melee attack speed in the game, scaling both Aggravate and Quickbloom. The tradeoff is needing a Dendro + Electro (or Hydro) setup plus interruption resistance.

Should Cyno prioritize EM or Crits?

Crits are the on-field damage engine; EM amplifies reactions. Main EM Sands preferred (even better with his signature weapon), Crit Circlet, Crit substats to finish.

Why can't Cyno swap out after his Burst?

The empowered state lasts ~18s; swapping cuts the reaction chain. Run persistent off-field abilities (Fischl's Oz, Xingqiu's Rain, Nahida's field), especially in multi-wave content.

How much Energy Recharge does Cyno need?

His Burst costs 80 energy. Solo Electro needs 120–140%; dual-Electro Resonance drops it to ~120%. Serpent Spine / Kitain Cross Spear ER substats help.
