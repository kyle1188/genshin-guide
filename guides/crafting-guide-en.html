Genshin Crafting Bench Guide: Condensed Resin, Alchemy & Artifact Strongbox

The Alchemy Bench is the most underused efficiency tool in Teyvat. It converts idle materials into the ones you actually need, banks Resin that would otherwise overflow, and turns three junk artifacts into a set you want. Here is everything it does, plus the recipes and the limits.

Quick Answer
  • Location: every major city has one (Mondstadt near the teleport waypoint, Liyue Harbor, Inazuma City, Sumeru City, Fontaine, Natlan, the Dragonspine camp). You can also build one in the Serenitea Pot.
  • Three functions: Crafting, Conversion and the Artifact Strongbox (unlocks at AR45).
  • Condensed Resin recipe: 60 Original Resin + 1 Crystal Core + 100 Mora, claims three reward sets at once, maximum of 5 held.
  • Where it does not work: Trounce Blossoms for weekly or normal bosses, and most Resin-costing time-limited events.
  • Strongbox: 3 five-star artifacts become 1 five-star artifact of a chosen set, with a 34% chance of four starting substats versus 20% from domains.

1. Where to Find the Crafting Bench

Every major city has an Alchemy Bench: Mondstadt’s sits below the waypoint next to Timaeus, Liyue Harbor’s is beside the waypoint, Inazuma City’s is by the bridge on the way to the Adventurers’ Guild, and Sumeru City, Fontaine, Natlan and the Dragonspine camp each have one.

The convenient option is building your own in the Serenitea Pot: finish every task in “Adepti Mirror: Part III” to get the blueprint, spend 20 White Iron Chunks, and place it next to your teleport point.

3. Function Two: Conversion

Conversion exists to fix uneven drops. Two different consumables handle two different problems:

  • Dust of Azoth — converts ascension gems between the seven elements. Pick the gem you want at the bench, then use “Change Material” in the top right to select any other element as the source. It is not limited to Pyro-based conversions.
  • Dream Solvent — converts the three materials dropped by the same weekly boss into each other, so being one Dvalin Claw short is no longer a dead end.

Dust of Azoth consumption scales with material rarity:

Gem tierRecipeDust of Azoth
Sliver (2☆)2 slivers → 1 target sliver1
Fragment (3☆)1 fragment → 1 target fragment3
Chunk (4☆)1 chunk → 1 target chunk9
Gemstone (5☆)1 gemstone → 1 target gemstone27

Two things to remember. First, sliver conversion runs at 2:1 and loses value, so start at Fragment tier or above. Second, Dust of Azoth comes from the Stardust Exchange in Paimon’s Bargains at 5 Stardust for 10 Dust, capped at 100 packs (1,000 Dust) per month with a 2,000 inventory limit — buy only what you need rather than clearing the shelf.

4. Function Three: Artifact Strongbox (AR45)

At Adventure Rank 45 a new tab with a triangle icon appears on the crafting bench:

  • Rule: 3 five-star artifacts become 1 five-star artifact of a set you choose. Slot and stats stay random.
  • Bulk: you can feed up to 39 artifacts at once for 13 results.
  • Hidden upside: Strongbox artifacts have a 34% chance to start with four substats, against 20% for domain drops. Four starting substats means one extra upgrade roll, which compounds into better odds of a strong piece.
  • Inventory cap: artifacts are capped at 1,800, so clear junk regularly — any unwanted four-star or five-star can be fed in.

Before you trade in, lock the pieces worth keeping: elemental damage goblets, CRIT Rate/CRIT DMG circlets and Elemental Mastery sands, goblets and circlets. Otherwise auto-fill will happily eat them. Note also that some newer sets (Golden Troupe, Marechaussee Hunter, Nymph’s Dream and others) are still not available from the Strongbox, so they must be farmed directly.

5. Condensed Resin: Recipe, Cap and Limits

Condensed Resin is the most-used recipe on the bench and the only way to save Resin that is about to overflow.

ItemDetail
BlueprintReach Liyue Reputation Level 3 and claim “Instructions: Condensed Resin” (or buy it from the blacksmith in Mondstadt and Liyue). Use it from the Precious Items tab to unlock the recipe permanently.
Recipe60 Original Resin + 1 Crystal Core + 100 Mora = 1 Condensed Resin
Cap5 held at once (that is 300 Original Resin banked). Event-granted Resin does not count against the cap.
Where it worksLey Line Blossoms (Blossoms of Wealth and Revelation) and Petrified Trees in domains — three reward sets per use
Where it failsTrounce Blossoms for weekly and normal bosses, and most Resin-costing events unless stated
Other rulesUse grants 300 Adventure EXP. Crafting counts toward the Battle Pass “use Original Resin” mission; spending it does not.

One easily missed detail: the “Bounty of the Earth” achievement tracks 800 Ley Line Blossom claims, and Condensed Resin only adds 1 to the counter rather than 3.

6. Where to Farm Crystal Cores

The bottleneck for Condensed Resin was never Resin — it is Crystal Cores, which only come from catching Crystalflies in the open world. Reliable clusters:

  • Mondstadt: around the Dawn Winery Statue of the Seven, Windrise and Windwail Highland
  • Liyue: Mt. Tianheng, Qingce Village and The Chasm
  • Sumeru, Fontaine and later regions each have their own fixed spawn clusters

Spawn density is higher at early morning and dusk in-game. Grab a lap while running dailies and three minutes will net you a dozen. Do not wait until your Resin is about to cap to discover you have none left.

7. Daily Resin Planning

  • Original Resin regenerates 1 point every 8 minutes, caps at 200, roughly 180 points of natural regeneration per day.
  • When Resin approaches the cap, craft Condensed Resin and bank it instead of letting it overflow.
  • Hold Fragile Resin until after AR45 — that is when 5-star artifact domains make each point worth far more.
  • Do not convert everything: keep some Original Resin for small 20-point spends.

8. FAQ

Can I use Condensed Resin in the Imaginarium Theater?

No. The Imaginarium Theater, weekly bosses and normal world bosses do not accept Condensed Resin.

Are crafting Mora costs significant?

Not really. A 5-star gem synthesis at 2,700 Mora is the priciest recipe on the bench, which is trivial next to leveling equipment.

Does the Strongbox cover new sets?

Some of them, and the list changes with updates. Sets such as Golden Troupe, Marechaussee Hunter and Nymph’s Dream are still excluded and must be farmed from their domains.

Summary

The bench is not about what it can make — it is about converting what you have into the one piece you are missing. Craft Condensed Resin before Resin caps, swap in a bonus character when synthesizing materials, and feed junk artifacts into the Strongbox after every AR45 domain run. Those three habits take under a minute a day and save a noticeable amount of Resin and Mora over a month.
