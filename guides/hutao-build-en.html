Genshin Hu Tao Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hu Tao — the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor — is the premier Pyro DPS in Genshin Impact for single-target content. Her Elemental Skill consumes HP to convert her Normal and Charged Attacks into Pyro damage scaling off her Max HP, and her passive rewards keeping her below half health. This guide covers weapons (Staff of Homa to F2P picks), the Crimson Witch of Flames set, her HP-scaling engine, the Vape team with Yelan / Xingqiu / Zhongli, and the half-HP trick that unlocks her true damage.

Note: Hu Tao's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Hu Tao Is Worth Building

She is the benchmark single-target Pyro carry: her damage scales off HP (a stat that also boosts her tankiness) and she runs one of the highest-damage Vape setups in the game.

  • HP-scaled Pyro: her Skill (Paramita Papilio) turns her attacks into Pyro and scales them off Max HP — building HP raises both damage and survivability.
  • Half-HP passive ("Flutter By"): when her HP is below 50%, her Pyro DMG Bonus jumps by up to +33%. Mastering the low-HP window is the core of her kit.
  • Vape monster: her frequent Charged Attacks pair perfectly with a Hydro unit for reverse Vaporize, multiplying her already-high single-target damage.
  • Self-sufficient DPS: she does not need a second Pyro unit for resonance, freeing her team slot for shields, an Anemo shredder, or a second Hydro.
In a clean Vape rotation, Hu Tao's Charged Attacks typically deal 2–4× the damage of most other single-target carries. She is the gold standard for boss DPS — at the cost of tighter HP management.

Best Weapons

Hu Tao wants CRIT and a polearm. HP weapons are ideal because they amplify her scaling, but any high-CRIT polearm works well.

5★ BiSStaff of Homa

Signature · HP% + CRIT DMG

Her tailor-made weapon: grants a huge HP% bonus (boosting her scaling) and a CRIT DMG spike, plus an ATK buff when below half HP. The clear best-in-slot for both damage and comfort.

Obtain: weapon banner

5★ altStaff of the Scarlet Sands

HP-based CRIT alternative

Converts her Max HP into CRIT Rate, easing the substat chase. Strong if you already own it and lack Homa — but it gives no HP%, so lean on artifacts for HP.

Obtain: weapon banner

F2P ★Deathmatch

Top free-to-play pick

A battle-pass polearm that grants CRIT Rate and a big ATK/DEF buff. It is the best non-5★ option and dramatically smooths her CRIT stat. Widely considered the F2P gold standard.

Obtain: Gnostic Hymn (battle pass)

F2PWhite Tassel / Lithic Spear

Craftable / 4★ alternatives

White Tassel (a 3-star from Liyue shops) gives CRIT Rate and a Normal Attack boost — surprisingly good. Lithic Spear adds CRIT and ATK if your party is Liyue-heavy. Both are free and easy to refine.

Obtain: shop (Mora) / Liyue banner

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Charged Attack through the Skill is everything:

  1. Elemental Skill (Paramita Papilio) → 8–10. This is the source of her Pyro-converted, HP-scaled attacks. Leveling it raises nearly all of her damage.
  2. Normal Attack → 8–9. Her Charged Attacks (fired through the Skill) are the actual hit; leveling NA raises them directly.
  3. Elemental Burst → 6–8. Useful for healing (via her passive) and AoE, but lower priority than Skill/NA.

The Half-HP Trick (Her A4 Passive)

Hu Tao's damage ceiling lives below 50% HP. Manage it on purpose, not by accident:

  • Stay under 50% HP in combat: her "Flutter By" passive grants up to +33% Pyro DMG Bonus while below half health — this is where her real numbers come from.
  • Use a shield: Zhongli (or Diona / Layla) lets her sit at low HP without dying. No shield = constant death risk at the exact moment she is strongest.
  • Burst to heal: her Elemental Burst heals her based on the number of enemies hit — a panic button to climb back above the danger line, then drop again.
  • Don't over-stack HP: enough HP to survive under a shield is plenty. Past ~38k HP you get more value from CRIT and EM instead.
Key rule: a Hu Tao sitting at full HP is a fraction of her potential. Embrace the low-HP window — with a shield, it is safe and dramatically stronger.

Common Mistakes

  • Keeping her at full HP. The half-HP passive is her biggest damage source. Running her topped-off throws away up to +33% Pyro DMG.
  • No shield. Low-HP play without a shielder (Zhongli / Diona) is a death spiral. A shield is not optional for her best numbers.
  • Building ATK instead of HP. Her Skill-scaled attacks scale off Max HP (and Pyro/CRIT). A pure-ATK Hu Tao underperforms badly.
  • Forgetting Elemental Mastery for Vape. In a Vape team, ~100–180 EM noticeably lifts reaction damage. Zero EM leaves damage on the table.
  • Spamming her Burst. Her Q is for healing/AoE, not her main rotation. Over-relying on it wastes the Skill-Charged-Attack loop that is her real DPS.

Don't Have Hu Tao Yet?

She is a limited 5-star, so you may not own her. For Pyro single-target DPS, Diluc (a free 4-star from the starter and shop) is the classic stand-in, and Yoimiya (a 4-star on banners) covers single-target Pyro via Normal Attacks. For a free Vape driver, Xiangling (from Spiral Abyss and the shop) is excellent and pairs with the same Xingqiu / Yelan / Zhongli shells. Build any of these while you wait for her banner; they cover most Pyro teams until she arrives.
