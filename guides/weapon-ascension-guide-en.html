Genshin Weapon Ascension Guide: Domain Schedule & Material Requirements

Weapon ascension comes down to two constraints: the weekday and the quantity. Materials only drop on certain days, and maxing a 5-star weapon takes dozens of domain runs. This guide covers the Domain of Forgery schedule, the full material requirements by weapon rarity, and crafting tricks that can save over a thousand resin.

  • When do weapon materials drop? Each Domain of Forgery rotates three series: one on Mon/Thu, one on Tue/Fri, one on Wed/Sat. All three open on Sunday.
  • What does a 5-star weapon need? Domain mats at 6/14/14/6 across four tiers, plus 91 elite drops, 65 common drops, and 225,000 Mora.
  • How rare are gold materials? Under a 5% natural drop rate, so they mostly come from the crafting bench at a three-to-one ratio.
  • How do you save resin? Bring crafting passive characters like Albedo or Alhaitham for double or refunded materials.

Crafting Bench: Where Gold Materials Come From

Gold materials drop at under 5% naturally, so farming them directly is inefficient. The right approach is to farm blue and purple, then craft gold:

  • 3 blue = 1 purple
  • 3 purple = 1 gold
  • Bring crafting passives: Albedo and Alhaitham give a 10% chance to double the result, while Eula, Layla, and Ayaka refund lower-tier materials 25% of the time.
  • Long-term payoff: across several weapons, these chances save the equivalent of over a thousand resin.

Enemy Drop Traps

  • Sumeru Fungi: killed with Pyro or Electro they become Activated or Scorched and drop the wrong spores. Use Hydro, Cryo, or Anemo to get standard spores.
  • Inazuma Nobushi (Handguards): low drop rate and scattered spawns, one of the most tedious farms in the game.
  • Fatui and Hilichurls: easier to farm in dense camps, good for a fixed route.

Suggested Farming Order

  • Confirm which material series your target weapon uses and write it down.
  • Farm on the open days, putting all resin into one series at a time.
  • Craft gold materials instead of grinding for them.
  • Weave enemy drops into your normal exploration routes.

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing up the days: showing up on Tuesday for a Monday material wastes resin.
  • Never crafting: gold materials never accumulate.
  • Skipping crafting passives: giving up free probability gains.
  • Ignoring enemy drops: getting blocked by mob materials after the domain mats are done.
  • Wrong element on Fungi: bringing home a pile of unusable spores.

Bottom Line

Weapon ascension comes down to a calendar and a crafting bench. Materials rotate by weekday and all open on Sunday. A 5-star weapon needs 6/14/14/6 domain mats plus elite and common drops and 225,000 Mora, and gold materials should be crafted three-to-one rather than farmed. Bringing crafting passive characters like Albedo or Alhaitham saves a lot of resin over time, and transitional weapons can stay at level 80.
