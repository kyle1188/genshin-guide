Genshin Ayaka Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 7.0 Snezhnaya (launches Aug 12, 2026)

Version 7.0 reworks Cryo with the new Stellar Reactions and debuts Odette, so Cryo units are the talk of the patch. Ayaka stays the smoothest f2p-friendly Cryo main DPS, and her Freeze shell only gets stronger alongside the new reactions. Build her (Mistsplitter or the craftable Amenoma, 4pc Blizzard Strayer, C2 if you can) and your Cryo core is 7.0-ready from day one. This guide covers exactly that.

Ayaka — the frost princess of Inazuma — is the game's cleanest Cryo main DPS. Her kit is built around Normal and Charged Attacks (her Skill dashes and infuses Cryo; her Burst floods the field with Cryo blades), so she thrives in Freeze teams where enemies can never move. She is the Cryo counterpart to Hu Tao: a dedicated on-fielder who rewards good artifacts and a tight rotation. This guide covers her best weapon (Mistsplitter Reforged vs the craftable Amenoma Kageuchi), the 4pc Blizzard Strayer set, the ATK% / Cryo DMG / CRIT plan, why C2 is the key constellation, and teams with Ganyu / Kazuha / Furina / Shenhe.

Note: Ayaka's kit, her Blizzard Strayer preference, and the C2 breakpoint are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 7.0 banner note above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of version.

Why Ayaka Is Worth Building

Among Cryo carries, Ayaka is the most self-contained: she needs no field-buffing support to function and she hard-counters any enemy that can be frozen.

  • On-field Cryo DPS: her infused Normal/Charged Attacks plus her Burst deal consistent Cryo damage with no dependency on off-field applicators.
  • Freeze lock: pair her with Hydro and she freezes entire groups, removing enemy actions entirely — the safest way to play the Abyss.
  • F2p-friendly: her best craftable (Amenoma Kageuchi) is forgeable, so she performs without a 5★ weapon.
  • 7.0-ready: with the Cryo rework adding Stellar Reactions, her Freeze core gains new team options without changing her build.
A correctly built Ayaka is the Cryo answer to Hu Tao: a dedicated on-fielder who, once her artifacts click, clears the Abyss with zero drama. If you want one reliable Cryo carry, she is the pick.

Best Weapons

Ayaka wants CRIT, ATK% and a high base-attack sword. She uses a sword.

BiS ★Mistsplitter Reforged

Best-in-slot · 5★ sword (her sig)

Her signature: huge base ATK, CRIT DMG substat, and a stacking buff (ATK% + Elemental DMG) that rewards her infusion playstyle. The clean "equip and forget" option.

Obtain: weapon banner

4★Amenoma Kageuchi

Craftable f2p best

Free from the Inazuma forge: its passive refunds Energy on skill casts, smoothing her Burst uptime, and it has clean ATK%/CRIT-friendly stats. The best weapon you can make yourself.

Obtain: forge (Inazuma)

5★Haran Geppaku Futsu

Alternative sig-tier sword

High base ATK plus a Normal Attack damage buff after her Skill — strong if you already own it from another character's banner. Close second to Mistsplitter.

Obtain: weapon banner

AltThe Black Sword / Finale of the Deep

F2P Battle Pass / forge options

The Black Sword (Battle Pass) gives CRIT + a self-heal on NA/CA — great sustain. Finale of the Deep (forgeable) offers ATK% and a stacking buff. Both solid if Amenoma is taken.

Obtain: BP / forge

Stat Targets — CRIT First, Then ATK% / Cryo

With 4pc Blizzard Strayer you already get +40% CRIT Rate from Freeze, so push CRIT DMG hard and fill the rest with ATK% / Cryo DMG.

SandsATK% (never EM on a carry)CRIT DMG > ATK% > CRIT Rate (to ~70% with set) > ER% (only if Burst drops)
GobletCryo DMG% (always over ATK% on her)

ER note: Ayaka wants her Burst up every rotation. With Amenoma's refund you usually hit ~110–130% ER comfortably; with Mistsplitter you may need a touch more from substats. Miss the Burst and her damage floor drops sharply.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her attacks are the damage, her Skill the dash/infusion, her Burst the nuke:

  1. Normal Attack (Kamisato Art — Kabuki) → 9–13. Her infused NA/CA are the core damage. Crown this first if you can.
  2. Elemental Burst (Kamisato Art — Soumetsu) → 9–13. The Cryo-blade flood is her biggest single hit. Level right after NA.
  3. Elemental Skill (Kamisato Art — Hyouka) → 6–9. Mostly a dash + small AoE; level last.

Constellations — C2 Is the Key Upgrade

Ayaka's curve is smooth, but one constellation stands out as the real value stop.

  • C1 (Snowswept Sacrifice): her Burst gains +1 extra blade wave and a small DMG boost. Nice, not essential.
  • C2 (Blizzard Blade Seki no To): the key one — her Burst's blades deal +20% more damage to enemies below 50% HP and hit a wider area. C2 roughly doubles her burst-clear value. Worth chasing.
  • C4 (Crystal Divine Body): after her Skill, her NA/CA gain +20% DMG for a while. A clear step up.
  • C6 (Frostflake Iris): her Burst gains another blade wave and her infused attacks deal extra Cryo. Luxury cap.
Key rule: C0 is already a top-tier Cryo carry; get C2 and stop. C2 is where her clear speed jumps from "good" to "elite." C6 is a luxury.

Common Mistakes

  • No freeze setup. Ayaka without Hydro loses the Blizzard Strayer CRIT bonus and most of her comfort. Always pair her with a Hydro applier.
  • Wrong Goblet. An ATK% Goblet on her is a damage loss — Cryo DMG% is strictly better once she has base ATK from weapons/artifacts.
  • Skipping CRIT DMG. Because Blizzard Strayer hands her CRIT Rate, stacking CRIT DMG is how she actually scales. A 70/140 split is the floor.
  • Burst downtime. Her floor drops hard without her Burst. Watch ER (Amenoma helps) and her rotation so Soumetsu is up every cycle.

Don't Have Ayaka Yet?

She is a limited 5★, so you pull her on a rerun — and with 7.0 Snezhnaya spotlighting Cryo via the Stellar Reactions rework and Odette, building your Cryo core now pays off. While you wait, Ganyu can supply Cryo main-DPS in a pinch (her Bloom/Aim style is different but effective), and Kaeya is a free Cryo option for Superconduct. Build her alongside Kazuha and Furina; together they are the Cryo core that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they are the safest evergreen pickup before the 7.0 shift.

Quick Answers

What is Ayaka's best build?

Ayaka is a Cryo burst/charged-attack sword DPS. Use 4-piece Blizzard Strayer (ATK% sands, Cryo DMG goblet, CRIT circlet) for Freeze, or 2-piece Blizzard + 2-piece ATK for flexibility. Aim CRIT Rate 40-50% (Blizzard gives +40% vs Frozen) and 200%+ CRIT DMG. Signature is Mistsplitter Reforged; best 4-stars are Amenoma Kageuchi and Blackcliff Longsword.

Why does Ayaka rely on Freeze?

Her kit (fast application, easy Cryo) pairs perfectly with Blizzard Strayer, which grants +40% CRIT Rate against Frozen enemies and +20% Cryo DMG. Freeze lets her skip building CRIT Rate and stack CRIT DMG instead.

What stats should I prioritize on Ayaka?

CRIT DMG 200%+, ATK 2000+, CRIT Rate 40-50% in Freeze (or 70%+ otherwise), and about 120% Energy Recharge for burst uptime. Elemental Mastery matters only in Melt teams.
