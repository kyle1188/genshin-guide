Genshin Wanderer Build Guide 2026: Weapons, Artifacts, Teams & Flight Mechanic

Wanderer (Scaramouche) is an Anemo catalyst on-field DPS from Inazuma. His Elemental Skill lifts him into the air so he can fire off rapid Anemo-imbued normal attacks — one of the few characters who can dodge melee hits while dealing damage. This guide covers weapons, artifacts, stat targets, team comps, and his synergy with Faruzan.

Note: Wanderer's kit, stat priorities, and artifact choice are long-term stable core gameplay and do not change much between versions. Constellations and banner schedules shift, but the build logic below holds. In-game data is final.

Is Wanderer Worth Building?

Wanderer earns his spot as one of the most comfortable Anemo on-field DPS thanks to four traits:

  • Fast Anemo normal attacks: while floating, all his normals become Anemo, with high attack speed that feels smooth against both single targets and groups.
  • Resistance shred through teammates: paired with Faruzan he shreds enemy Anemo RES and gains a key damage multiplier — the core of his output.
  • Damage while dodging: the floating state avoids melee lock-on, giving him better survivability than most on-field carries.
  • Flexible reactions: his Skill grants buffs when it contacts Pyro / Hydro / Electro / Cryo, letting him slot into Vaporize, Electro-Charged, and Freeze teams.
Wanderer's constellation gains are modest: he is complete at C0 and very cost-efficient. Only C6 is a real spike (greatly boosting normal and charged attacks while lowering floating stamina drain); C1 and C2 are minor. Most players do not need to force constellations.

Wanderer scales with ATK, CRIT, and normal-attack damage, so CRIT catalysts suit him best. A 4-star can reach about 85% of his signature's performance.

Best Team Comps

The soul of a Wanderer team is Faruzan — she shreds Anemo RES and buffs Anemo DPS; without her the damage drops sharply. Bennett is the second core, giving ATK, healing, and interruption resistance. Recommended pairings:

ClassicWanderer + Faruzan + Bennett + Xiangling

Most stable all-rounder

Faruzan shreds and buffs, Bennett gives ATK plus healing and interruption resistance, Xiangling adds off-field Pyro. Wanderer's Skill contacting Pyro also boosts his ATK and floating cap — smooth and group-strong.

The most recommended Wanderer team for newcomers

Talent Priority

Wanderer's normal attacks and Skill are his main damage; level those two first:

  1. Normal Attack (Yuuban Meigen) → 9 or crown. The rapid floating normals are the bulk of his damage.
  2. Elemental Skill (Hanega: Song of the Wind) → 8-9. Enters the floating state, grants buffs, and resets his attack rhythm.
  3. Elemental Burst (Kyougen: Five Ceremonial Plays) → 8. Mainly for grouping and supplemental damage, not the rotation core.

Floating & Elemental Buff Mechanic

Wanderer's core: his Skill lifts him into the air (floating state) and turns his attacks Anemo; the element his Skill contacts grants a corresponding buff. Understanding this is what makes his damage spike.

  • Skill (floating): enters the hovering state; normals / charged / plunging all become Anemo and can briefly dodge melee hits.
  • Element-contact buff: while floating, if his Skill touches Pyro (Bennett) or Hydro (Yelan / Xingqiu), it raises his ATK by about 30% and increases his floating stamina cap. So Bennett / Yelan are not just supports — they are his damage amplifiers.
  • Faruzan's VV effect: her burst grants the team Anemo DMG and shreds enemy Anemo RES — the single most important damage multiplier for Wanderer. At C6 she adds CRIT DMG.
Key rule: Wanderer is weak to interruption — getting hit drops him out of the air and breaks his rotation. Bennett's interruption resistance or a shield character (Zhongli) drastically improves his output environment. Never float naked.

Common Mistakes

  • Skipping Faruzan. Without RES shred, enemy Anemo RES eats a huge chunk of his damage. Faruzan is the core of a Wanderer team, not optional.
  • Building him like a pure ATK carry. He needs ATK and CRIT, but ER should not be too high; the more common mistake is weak CRIT so his normals never crit.
  • Ignoring interruption resistance. Getting knocked out of the air stops his damage. Bennett or a shield fixes this — do not go unprotected.
  • Forcing the weapon banner for his signature. The Widsith already hits about 85% of the signature's level; save primos for constellations or other characters.
  • Putting Viridescent Venerer on Wanderer himself. The RES-shred set belongs on Faruzan to be useful; Wanderer wearing VV is worse than Desert Pavilion. Don't mix them up.

No Wanderer Yet?

Wanderer is a limited 5-star. If you need an Anemo on-field option, Kazuha is a top-tier Anemo support and Xiao is another Anemo on-field DPS (scaling on attack speed and ER) covering different needs. Build Faruzan first and wait for his rerun.

Quick Answers

What is the best Wanderer build?

Wanderer plays as an Anemo on-field DPS. His best artifact set is 4-piece Desert Pavilion Chronicle, with ATK% sands, Anemo DMG goblet, and CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG circlet, aiming for about 2000 ATK, 70-80% CRIT Rate, and 160%+ CRIT DMG. For weapons, his signature Tulaytullah's Remembrance is best, while the 4-star The Widsith is the top F2P pick and Mappa Mare is a craftable alternative.

Why does Wanderer need Faruzan?

Faruzan is currently the only Anemo support who sharply lowers enemy Anemo RES and buffs Anemo DPS. Without her, a large chunk of Wanderer's Anemo damage is eaten by enemy resistance and he loses a key damage multiplier. At C6 Faruzan also adds CRIT DMG, making her the core piece of any Wanderer team.

How much Energy Recharge does Wanderer need?

Wanderer is the on-field DPS and picks up most of the elemental particles himself, so his burst demand is low. Around 100%-130% Energy Recharge is enough to open his burst every rotation; if the team has no second Anemo character to share particles, push it closer to 130%.
