Genshin Fischl Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 7.0 Snezhnaya (launches Aug 12, 2026)

Version 7.0 reworks Cryo and adds the free Electro unit Alyosha plus the new Stellar Conduct reactions, so Electro teams are squarely back in the spotlight. Fischl — the free 4★ who fights through her raven Oz — is still the most f2p-friendly Electro enabler you can build: she drops into Aggravate, Electro-Charged and Raiden cores without spending a single Primogem. Build her right (The Stringless R5, Golden Troupe, C6 if you can) and she carries your Electro teams from day one of 7.0. This guide covers exactly that.

Fischl is the game's best free off-field Electro unit. Her Elemental Skill summons Oz, a raven that stays on the field and fires coordinated Electro attacks while you use your main carry — zero field time, permanent off-field damage and Electro application. She is the Electro answer to Xiangling: a must-build 4★ that slots into almost any team. This guide covers her best weapon (The Stringless R5 vs Skyward Harp / Alley Hunter), the 4pc Golden Troupe set, the ATK% / Electro DMG stat plan, why C6 is the game-changer, and teams with Nahida / Raiden / Xiangling / Bennett.

Note: Fischl's kit, her Golden Troupe preference, and the C6 breakpoint are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 7.0 banner note above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of version.

Why Fischl Is Worth Building

Among Electro units, only Fischl gives you set-and-forget off-field Electro with no field time and at zero cost (she is a standard-banner 4★ you will own at C6 eventually).

  • Off-field Electro engine: Oz applies Electro continuously, feeding Aggravate, Electro-Charged, Superconduct and Overload without a swap.
  • Free and farmable: she sits in the standard wish pool, so most accounts hit C6 within a few banners — no Primogem commitment.
  • Massive team flexibility: she fits Aggravate, Taser, Raiden cores, Mono Electro and even physical Superconduct teams.
  • Quiet DPS: at C6 her coordinated attacks hit hard, turning her from "enabler" into a real damage source.
A correctly built Fischl is the Electro version of Xiangling: the free, no-swap, ever-present sub-DPS that every account should own. If you only build one Electro support, make it her.

Best Weapons

Fischl wants ATK% and Elemental Mastery (for reaction teams) and loves anything that boosts her Skill. She uses a bow.

BiS ★The Stringless (R5)

Best-in-slot · 4★ bow (f2p)

Her default: Elemental Mastery plus a passive that boosts Elemental Skill and Burst damage by up to 24% at R5. Free to refine, no contest for a sub-DPS Fischl. The craftable, farmable, perfect pick.

Obtain: standard wishes (refine to R5)

5★Skyward Harp

Highest raw ATK · crit

Big base ATK and a crit-rate substat with a physical-proc passive. Best when you are not running a reaction team and just want her personal damage maxed.

Obtain: weapon banner

4★Alley Hunter

Off-field damage boost

Gains up to 12% DMG per second off-field (stacks to 60% after 5s), which is exactly how Fischl plays. Strong alternative to The Stringless if you have it refined.

Obtain: Battle Pass

AltMitternachts Waltz / Chain-Breaker

Reaction- or forge-based

Mitternachts Waltz boosts Normal Attack and Skill damage for physical/Aggravate shells; Chain-Breaker (forgeable) gives EM + a team reaction buff. Use when your team is a specific reaction build.

Obtain: event / forge

Stat Targets — ATK%, Then Crit, Then EM

Fischl's Oz damage scales on ATK% and her personal hits want CRIT. EM matters only in Aggravate / Electro-Charged teams.

SlotMain statSubstat priority
SandsATK% (never EM/ER on a sub-DPS build)CRIT (rate/dmg) > ATK% > EM (Aggravate/EC teams) > ER% (only if you Burst often)
GobletElectro DMG% (or ATK% if no good Electro piece)
CircletCRIT Rate / CRIT DMG (balance to ~70/140)
ER note: Fischl rarely needs Energy Recharge — Oz's recasts from C1/C6 and her low Burst cost mean ATK%/CRIT win. Only add ER substats if you find her Burst missing often (e.g. in a no-battery Electro-Charged team).

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Skill drives Oz, her Burst the Nightrider:

  1. Elemental Skill (Nightrider) → 8–13. Oz's damage and duration live here. Crown this first.
  2. Elemental Burst (Midnight Phantasmagoria) → 6–10. Refreshes Oz and deals burst damage; level it after the Skill.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. She barely attacks outside meme on-field builds. Leave it low.

Constellations — C6 Is the Game-Changer

Fischl's constellation curve is front-loaded: a couple make her better, then C6 flips her into a damage dealer.

  • C1 (Gaze of the Deep): extends Oz's duration and lets Fischl's own attacks summon a joint attack. Nice quality-of-life.
  • C2 (Devourer of All Sins): Oz's joint attacks lower Electro RES — a clean team damage buff.
  • C4 (Her Pilgrimage of Bleak): her Burst's coordinated attacks gain extra DMG based on ATK. A clear step up.
  • C6 (Eulogist of the Setting Sun): the big one — while Oz is out, all of Fischl's attacks (and Oz's) gain +60% damage, and her Normal Attacks summon Oz. C6 is where Fischl stops being "support" and becomes a genuine carry.
Key rule: C0 is already a top-tier off-field Electro; get to C6 and stop. Because she is a standard-banner 4★, C6 will happen on its own — just keep pulling the blue banner.

Common Mistakes

  • Building her as a physical carry. Fischl's real value is Oz's off-field Electro. Stacking physical DMG wastes her kit — go ATK% / Electro / CRIT.
  • Wrong artifact set. Random 2pc+2pc without Golden Troupe leaves the +70% Skill damage on the table. Golden Troupe 4pc is the default for a reason.
  • Dumping Energy Recharge. She rarely needs ER; over-investing steals substats from CRIT/ATK%. Only add ER if her Burst actually drops.
  • Skipping her for a 5★ Electro. Raiden / Yae Miko hit harder in spots, but neither is free nor as universally slot-able. Fischl stays the most efficient Electro you will ever own.

Don't Have Fischl Yet?

She is a standard-banner 4★, so you will pull her on any wish — and with 7.0 Snezhnaya spotlighting Electro via the free Alyosha and Stellar Conduct, building your Electro core now pays off. While you wait, Raiden or Kuki Shinobu can supply off-field Electro in a pinch, but neither gives Fischl's free, no-swap Oz convenience. Build her alongside Nahida and Raiden; together they are the Electro-Dendro core that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they are the safest evergreen pickup before the 7.0 shift.

Quick Answers

What is Fischl's best build?

Fischl is an off-field Electro applicator/sub-DPS. Use 4-piece Golden Troupe or 2-piece ATK + 2-piece Electro (ATK%/EM sands, Electro goblet, CRIT circlet). CRIT 70%+, ATK 1800+. Signature is Skyward Harp; best F2P is The Stringless (R5) or Mitternachts Waltz.

Why is Fischl so versatile?

Oz summons deal off-field Electro application that enables Aggravate, Hyperbloom, and Overload with zero field time. She slots into almost any team needing consistent Electro.

What stats should I prioritize on Fischl?

CRIT Rate/DMG first (70%/140%+), ATK 1800+, then Elemental Mastery 100-200 in Aggravate teams. Energy Recharge about 120% since Oz generates particles.
