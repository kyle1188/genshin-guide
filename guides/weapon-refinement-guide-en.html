Complete Weapon Refinement Guide: From F2P to Full Refinement

Weapon refinement enhances a weapon's secondary stats by duplicating it. R5 requires 4 copies — expensive. This guide explains the mechanics, Weapon Pledge rules, and smart F2P choices.

What Is Weapon Refinement

Weapon refinement (精炼) duplicates a weapon to enhance its secondary stat (e.g. CRIT DMG, Elemental Mastery). Each weapon has 5 refinement levels from R1 (base) to R5 (max).

Refinement LevelDescription
-------------------------------
R1Base version, lowest stats
R2Refined once, ~20% stat increase
R3Refined twice, ~40% stat increase
R4Refined thrice, ~60% stat increase
R5Refined four times, 100% stat increase (max)

Refinement Resources

Duplicate weapons: Obtained through Wishes or exchanged via the Weapon Pledge system using Intertwined Fates.

Mora: Each refinement costs increasing amounts of Mora.

Refinement StepMaterials RequiredMora Cost (approx.)
---------------------------------------------------------
R1→R21 duplicate weapon40,000
R2→R31 duplicate weapon100,000
R3→R41 duplicate weapon200,000
R4→R51 duplicate weapon400,000

> Note: Mora values are estimates and may change with game updates.

Weapon Pledge (武器池) Mechanics

The Weapon Pledge is part of the Wish system. Players can spend 160 Intertwined Fates to enter the pledge and choose 1 of 3 featured 5-star weapons, receiving a "refinement +1" fragment.

Activation conditions:

  • Pity count: obtain the featured weapon 6 times (including duplicates)
  • Or spend 160 Intertwined Fates directly

F2P Refinement Strategy

F2P players should NOT pursue R5 — the cost-to-benefit ratio is terrible. Recommended priorities:

Weapon TypeRecommended LevelReason
---------------------------------------

FAQ

Q: How much does refinement actually improve a weapon?

A: Each refinement level boosts the secondary effect by ~20%. Significant for 4-star weapons, marginal for 5-star signature weapons.

Q: Is full refinement worth it for F2P?

A: No. F2P players should prioritize wishing for characters; R1 or R2 weapons are already fully viable.

Q: What's the difference between the Weapon Pledge and regular banners?

A: The Weapon Pledge grants "refinement fragments" as a bonus reward, ideal for players pursuing R5 signature weapons. Regular character banners only grant characters and constellations.

Q: How many Primogems for a full R5 5-star weapon?

A: Theoretically ~64,000 primogems (640 Intertwined Fates), but actual costs vary significantly due to pity mechanics — budget at least 80,000 primogems as a safe ceiling.
