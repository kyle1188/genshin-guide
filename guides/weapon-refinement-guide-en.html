Complete Weapon Refinement Guide

A max-refined 5-star weapon can provide 20-40% more stats than its base version. But refinement costs are steep — knowing which weapons to refine and which to skip is essential skill for every traveler.

Refinement Level Mechanics

What is weapon refinement?

Refinement upgrades a weapon's passive skill by duplicating it.

Refinement LevelSymbolPassive Skill Change
------------------------------------------------
R0×1Base level
R1×2+1 level
R2×3+2 levels
R3×4+3 levels
R4×5+4 levels
R5×6+5 levels

Refinement cost (duplicate weapon conversion):

  • Convert duplicate weapons into refinement materials
  • Material requirements increase per refinement level

Refinement Material Sources

1. Weapon Banner (Most Direct)

  • Hard pity at 90 pulls
  • Soft pity starts around 74 pulls

- 50-50 mechanic: If you lose the 50-50, next guaranteed

2. Forging (Free)

  • 4-star forged weapons: Free with ore materials
  • 5-star forged weapons (e.g., Mistsplitter): Requires world quest unlock

- Advantage: Never expires, can forge anytime

3. Battle Pass

  • 1680 primogems for BP access
  • BP weapons (e.g., Favonius Codex) available via weapon banner

4. Event Rewards

  • Holiday events, version events sometimes give free 5-star weapons
  • Watch official announcements for free weapon opportunities

---

Refinement Decision Framework

Ask yourself three questions:

1. Is this weapon the character's signature?

  • - Yes → Prioritize refining to R1
  • - No → Consider universal weapons

2. Does R1 bring qualitative change?

  • - Yes (e.g., Engulfing Lightning R3) → Worth investing
  • - No (e.g., normal DMG bonus) → R1 is sufficient

3. Do I have stable income sources?

  • - Monthly card + BP → Can consider R2-R3
  • - Pure F2P → Cap at R1, don't over-invest

FAQ

Q: How many primogems to refine a 5-star weapon to R1?

A: About 90 pulls (14,400 primogems), realistically 70-90 pulls with soft pity.

Q: Do refinement materials expire?

A: No. Refinement materials are permanent and always usable.

Q: Is it worth refining 4-star weapons?

A: Festering Desire series (sword/greatsword/bow) worth R2; other 4-stars at R1 is enough.

Q: Should I refine weapons or level talents first?

A: Talents first. Talents are direct multipliers in the damage formula with more stable returns.
