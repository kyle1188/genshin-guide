Genshin Story Quest Guide: Unlock Conditions, Best Order & Story Keys

Story Quests are single-character storylines in Genshin Impact, most with a character trial, steady Primogems, and a few that unlock weekly bosses. Story Keys confuse a lot of players — and the rules changed in Version 5.4. This guide covers the current unlock mechanics and the best order to play them.

  • Do story quests still need Story Keys? No — since Version 5.4, story quest acts open freely once requirements are met. Keys are now mainly spent on Hangout Events (2 per act).
  • How do I earn Story Keys? After Adventure Rank 26, complete 8 Daily Commissions per key. Claim them manually in the Quest menu; you can hold up to 3 (progress still banks toward 8).
  • Which quests first? The weekly-boss unlockers: Zhongli Act II (Azhdaha) and Raiden Shogun Act II (Magatsu Mitake Narukami). Then clear by region after finishing its Archon Quest.
  • What are the rewards? Each act gives ~60 Primogems plus character materials and Adventure EXP; a few acts are prerequisites for main story or weekly bosses.

What is a Story Quest?

Story Quests are dedicated storylines for a single playable character, one act per character, most letting you trial that character even if you do not own them. They sit alongside two similar systems:

  • Story Quests: usually 5★ characters' single-path stories, named after the character's constellation (e.g. Zhongli's "Historia Antiqua").
  • Hangout Events: multi-ending branch stories for 4★ characters — see our Hangout Event guide.
  • Tribal Chronicles: Natlan characters' storylines told per tribe, mechanically similar but unlocked through tribe reputation and main-story progress.

Most acts reward ~60 Primogems plus character materials when completed.

Unlock conditions & the Story Key system

The Story Quest system opens at Adventure Rank 26 after finishing Prologue Act III; each act has its own prerequisites too (usually AR 40 plus the relevant region's Archon Quest). Current key rules:

  • Earning keys: after AR 26, every 8 Daily Commissions grants 1 Story Key — but you must claim it manually in the Quest menu's Story Quest tab.
  • Cap: hold up to 3 keys at once; the progress bar still accumulates to 8, so spending one lets you claim the next immediately.
  • The 5.4 change: since Version 5.4, opening story quest acts no longer costs Story Keys; keys now go mainly to Hangout Events (2 per act).
  • Tribal Chronicles: cost no keys and unlock through Natlan tribe progression.

Where keys go now: Hangout Events

Keys now exist almost solely for Hangout Events: each hangout act costs 2 Story Keys to start, then is free forever to replay for different endings. Good habits:

  • Keep clearing Daily Commissions and claim keys on time — overflow progress is not wasted while capped at 3.
  • Spend every 2 keys on a hangout character you want; full endings pay Adventure EXP, Primogems and specialty dishes.
  • Story fans pick by interest; meta players prioritize hangouts whose characters need rare materials (e.g. Thoma, Kuki Shinobu).

Story quests worth prioritizing

Most acts are simply "60 Primogems plus a good story", but a few carry real unlock value:

  • Zhongli Act II: unlocks the Azhdaha weekly boss — a must-do prerequisite for Geo character talents.
  • Raiden Shogun Act II: unlocks the Magatsu Mitake Narukami weekly boss, source of Raiden and related talent materials.
  • Some acts gate the main story: if an Archon Quest is locked, check whether a character story quest is unfinished.
  • Stuck below an AR cap: acts grant lots of Adventure EXP — perfect "exp packs" before ascension quests.
  • Test a character before pulling: use the trial to check feel and teams (see our build guides for details).

Efficiency tips

  • Archon first, story second: finish the current main story, then mop up regional quests to keep the narrative straight.
  • Never stop banking keys: with keys now spent only on hangouts, the 2-days-per-key rhythm matters more.
  • Check your EXP gap before ascension: when stuck on an AR cap, clearing 2–3 story acts usually closes it.
  • Do Tribal Chronicles with each patch: new Natlan characters arrive with their chronicle in the same version — follow the patch calendar.

Summary

The rules changed — keys no longer gate story quests since 5.4, so bank your keys for Hangout Events instead. Play main story first, clean up regions in order, and prioritize the weekly-boss acts. Story quests then become a dependable stream of story, Primogems and Adventure EXP.
