Genshin Mona Build & Teams: 5-Star Hydro Catalyst & Omen Damage Amp

Quick Answer

  • Mona is a 5★ Hydro catalyst (Mondstadt astrologer), a Support / Sub-DPS; her signature mechanic is the Omen damage-amp debuff.
  • Core set: Noblesse Oblige 4pc (+20% team ATK after the Burst) or Emblem of Severed Fate 4pc (ER + Burst DMG, when recharge is short).
  • Main stats: ER Sands / Hydro or ATK Goblet / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stats: ER > Crits > ATK (on-field ~150-180% ER; off-field catching own particles ~230-300%).
  • Key: Skill applies Hydro + taunts; Burst (Illusory Torrent) traps enemies and applies Omen; Freeze extends the Omen window; A4 converts ER into Hydro DMG.
  • Best teams: Mona + Ganyu/Yun Jin + Xingqiu (Freeze); Mona + Hu Tao/Yoimiya + Bennett + Xiangling (Vaporize).
  • Weapon: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers (3★ support T0, +24% ATK on the next character, +48% at R5).

How Mona Works

Mona is a classic "Burst damage-amplifier support":

  • Omen: her Burst traps enemies and applies Omen, increasing the damage they take (scales with Burst talent), lasting up to 5s; Freeze extends the Omen window.
  • Phantom: her Skill spawns a decoy that taunts and applies Hydro, exploding when it expires or is destroyed.
  • A4: converts 20% of your Energy Recharge into Hydro DMG — so ER is a core sub-stat, not wasted on her.

Team Comps

Freeze Team (most common)

Mona + Ganyu/Yun Jin + Xingqiu + Diona/Baizhu

  • Mona applies Hydro + the Cryo user applies Cryo → Freeze; the Freeze extends the Omen window, so the carry eats the full buff.
  • Ganyu/Yun Jin carry; Xingqiu adds Hydro uptime; Diona/Baizhu add shred + damage reduction.

Vaporize Nuke Team

Mona + Hu Tao/Yoimiya + Bennett + Xiangling

  • Mona applies Hydro and buffs; Bennett provides Pyro + healing; Hu Tao / Yoimiya nuke with Vaporize.

Constellation Priority

  • C1: +15% Hydro-based reaction damage on Omen-marked targets (the Freeze/Vaporize game-changer, highest value).
  • C2: chance to auto-Charged Attack, plus an auto-Charged Attack after the Burst that grants the whole team +80 Elemental Mastery.
  • C4: +15% Crit Rate on Omen-marked targets (a rare team-wide Crit buff).

FAQ

What artifacts does Mona use?

For support, run Noblesse Oblige 4pc (team ATK); when recharge is short or as a sub-DPS, run Emblem of Severed Fate 4pc; ER Sands + Hydro/ATK Goblet + Crit Circlet.

No signature weapon — what next?

Mona has no 5-star signature. The 3-star Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers is her T0 support pick (ATK to the next character); Favonius Codex for ER; Goldendew to add a healer.

Who should team with Mona?

Freeze (Ganyu / Yun Jin + Xingqiu) is the most stable; Vaporize (Hu Tao / Yoimiya + Bennett + Xiangling) hits harder.

How much ER does Mona need?

On-field ~150-180%; off-field catching her own particles ~230-300%. Favonius Codex, Noblesse or Emblem ease the requirement.

Which Mona constellation matters most?

C1 (+15% Hydro reaction damage) is the most valuable — target it for Freeze/Vaporize teams; C4 (team-wide Crit) is also worth having.
