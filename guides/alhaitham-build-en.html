Genshin Alhaitham Build Guide 2026: Weapons, Artifacts, Teams & EM Mechanic

Alhaitham is the scribe of the Sumeru Akademiya and currently the most reliable Dendro on-field DPS. He uses Chisel-Light Mirrors to turn his attacks Dendro and fires off Projection Attacks, while scaling heavily on Elemental Mastery. This guide covers weapons, artifacts, stat targets, team comps, and his synergy with Nahida.

Note: Alhaitham's mirror mechanic, stat priorities, and artifact choice are long-term stable core gameplay and do not change much between versions. Constellations and banner schedules shift, but the build logic below holds. In-game data is final.

Is Alhaitham Worth Building?

Alhaitham earns his spot as the benchmark of Dendro damage thanks to four traits:

  • Stable Dendro on-field: his Skill spawns Chisel-Light Mirrors that convert his normals / charged / plunging attacks to Dendro, sustaining damage against both single targets and groups.
  • Mirror projection mechanic: each mirror triggers a Projection Attack, and more mirrors mean more total damage, with a smooth rotation.
  • EM pays off: his A4 passive turns Elemental Mastery directly into direct-damage bonus, so stacking EM serves two purposes at once.
  • Reaction-team core: whether Spread (Aggravate) or Hyperbloom, he is the most stable Dendro main DPS, with very flexible teambuilding.
Alhaitham is complete at C0 and very cost-efficient. C2 noticeably boosts him (each mirror generated adds 50 EM, up to 4 stacks) and is the best value investment; C6 is the real spike (greatly boosting Projection and burst damage). Most players do not need high constellations.

Nahida applies Dendro, Fischl adds Electro for Aggravate, Zhongli shields for survival. Alhaitham on-field — stable damage, excellent single-target. The most recommended Alhaitham team for newcomers.

Talent Priority

Alhaitham's normal attack is his main damage source; level it first:

  1. Normal Attack (Abductive Reasoning) → 9 or crown. Every normal after the Dendro conversion feeds mirror projections — the bulk of his damage.
  2. Elemental Skill (Universality: An Elaboration on Form) → 8-9. Spawns / refreshes Chisel-Light Mirrors, the key to starting the rotation.
  3. Elemental Burst (Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena) → 8. Supplements damage and refreshes mirrors; not the rotation core.

Mirror & Elemental Mastery Mechanic

Alhaitham's core: his Skill creates Chisel-Light Mirrors that turn his attacks Dendro, and each mirror triggers a Projection Attack; more mirrors means more total damage. Understanding this is what makes his damage spike.

  • Skill (mirrors): spawns mirrors (up to 3 on field); normals / charged / plunging all become Dendro and trigger Projection Attacks on hit.
  • A4 passive "Mysteries Laid Bare": each point of his EM raises Projection Attack and burst damage by 0.1%, capped at 100% (reached at about 1000 EM). So EM is both a reaction need and a direct-damage multiplier.
  • Nahida's buff: Nahida not only applies Dendro but also grants the team EM based on a teammate's EM, directly amplifying Alhaitham's projections and reactions — an indispensable Dendro base.
Key rule: Alhaitham's output drops sharply with no mirrors — keep mirrors up via Skill / burst, and do not swap off-field too long or they vanish. Also, only one unit should wear Deepwood Memories (usually Nahida or Baizhu); two wearers waste the set.

Common Mistakes

  • Stacking CRIT before enough EM. His passive turns EM into direct damage; 300-400 EM is the baseline — meet it before balancing CRIT.
  • Wearing Deepwood himself while a teammate also wears it. Deepwood's RES shred is needed only once; duplicate is wasted. Let Nahida / Baizhu wear it and use Gilded Dreams yourself for higher damage.
  • Skipping Nahida. Without a stable Dendro base, the Spread / Hyperbloom rhythm breaks and both damage and coverage fall. She is his core piece.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge. When he is the only Dendro, ER demand hits 175-220%; otherwise his burst lags and mirrors are not refreshed. Adjust by Dendro count.
  • Building him like a pure ATK carry. Alhaitham wants ATK and CRIT, but EM is his unique multiplier; ATK-only building lowers both projections and reaction damage.

No Alhaitham Yet?

Alhaitham is a limited 5-star. To experience Dendro reactions first, Nahida is the engine of any Dendro team and Baizhu provides a Dendro shield and healing — both let you build the Dendro framework first and add the main DPS when he reruns. For an Electro trigger, see Fischl.

Quick Answers

What is the best Alhaitham build?

Alhaitham plays as a Dendro on-field DPS. His best artifact set is 4-piece Gilded Dreams, with EM or ATK% sands, Dendro DMG goblet, and CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG circlet, aiming for 300-400 Elemental Mastery, around 70% CRIT Rate, and 140%+ CRIT DMG. For weapons, his signature Light of Foliar Incision is best, while the 4-star Xiphos' Moonlight and craftable Iron Sting work well, and the 3-star Harbinger of Dawn is surprisingly strong in a healthy team.

Why does Alhaitham need Nahida?

Nahida provides steady Dendro application and an Elemental Mastery buff, serving both as Alhaitham's Dendro enabler (so Spread / Hyperbloom trigger reliably) and as the EM he lacks. Without Nahida, Alhaitham's Dendro-reaction rhythm breaks and both his damage and reaction coverage drop noticeably.

How much Elemental Mastery does Alhaitham need?

A typical target is 300-400 Elemental Mastery. His A4 passive, Mysteries Laid Bare, converts each point of EM into +0.1% damage for his Projection Attacks and Elemental Burst, capped at 100% (reached at about 1000 EM). So EM is both a reaction requirement and a direct-damage multiplier, but do not sacrifice CRIT balance just to max it out.
