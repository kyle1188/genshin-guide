Genshin Ayato Build & Teams: 5-Star Hydro Sword & Echoes of an Offering

Quick Answer

  • Ayato is a 5★ Hydro sword from Inazuma and head of the Kamisato Clan (Yashiro Commission). His loop is his Skill's Takimeguri Kanka state, which turns Normal Attacks into AoE Hydro and grants Namisen stacks (DMG scaling off Max HP).
  • Core set: Echoes of an Offering 4pc (chance to deal full Normal-Attack DMG) — best for his attack-string style; alternatives are Heart of Depth 4pc or Gladiator's Finale 4pc.
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Hydro DMG Goblet / Crit Circlet (HP% substats also help, since Namisen scales on Max HP).
  • Sub-stat priority: Crits > ATK > HP% > ER (Burst costs 80, 120–140%).
  • Key: inside the Skill state he cannot Charged/Plunge — it's all Normal-Attack strings; Vaporize (with Xiangling) and Freeze teams are most consistent.
  • Best teams: Ayato + Xiangling + Bennett + Kazuha (Vaporize); Ayato + Ayaka/Ganyu + Venti/Kazuha + Diona (Freeze).
  • Weapon: Haran Geppaku Futsu is his signature; swaps are Mistsplitter Reforged or Primordial Jade Cutter, and 4★ The Black Sword is strong.

How Ayato Works

His loop is "cast Skill → Takimeguri Kanka string":

  • Skill (Kamisato Art: Kyouka): dash-leaves a water illusion and enters Takimeguri Kanka, converting Normal Attacks into un-overridable AoE Hydro. Hits grant Namisen (DMG bonus scaling off current Max HP; starts at 4 max stacks, 1 every 0.1s).
  • Burst (Suiyuu): unfolds a garden that rains Hydro blades and boosts party Normal-Attack DMG (80 energy).
  • A4 Passive: when off-field and below 40 energy he regens 2 energy/s, easing his uptime; the Skill state also raises interruption resistance.
  • So he runs ATK + Crits + a Hydro Goblet; since Namisen scales on HP, a little HP% substat helps.

Team Comps

Vaporize Team (most consistent)

Ayato + Xiangling (Pyro) + Bennett (ATK + heal) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Xiangling's off-field Pyro + Ayato's Hydro strings → frequent Vaporize; Kazuha shreds Hydro/Pyro RES and Bennett buffs ATK.

Freeze Team

Ayato + Ayaka/Ganyu (Cryo) + Venti/Kazuha (grouping) + Diona (shield/heal)

  • Hydro + Cryo Freeze locks groups in place while Ayato strings on-field — great against mobs.

Double-Hydro / Hyperbloom

Ayato + Yelan + Kazuha or Ayato + Nahida + Kuki Shinobu

  • Double-Hydro resonance boosts HP; Hyperbloom uses Nahida's Dendro + Kuki's trigger.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: Namisen max stacks +1 (to 5) and +50% Max HP at 3+ stacks — his signature constellation, directly amplifying the core DMG mechanic.
  • C1: +40% Shunsuiken DMG against foes below 50% HP — stronger finisher.
  • C6: next Shunsuiken spawns 2 extra strikes (450% ATK each) — whale goal.

FAQ

Is Ayato still strong?

Yes. His Hydro attack-string kit is simple and stable, fitting both Vaporize and Freeze cores; his only weakness is on-field reliance and interruption, so he wants grouping and a shielder.

What is his best-in-slot weapon?

His signature Haran Geppaku Futsu is the best fit; without it use Mistsplitter Reforged or Primordial Jade Cutter, and 4★ The Black Sword (Battle Pass) is a strong budget pick.

Vaporize or Freeze?

Vaporize (with Xiangling) gives the steadiest, highest damage; Freeze is better for crowd control against groups — both are great.

How much Energy Recharge does Ayato need?

His Burst costs 80, but his A4 regens energy when off-field and below 40, so ~120–140% ER is enough.

Is C2 worth chasing?

Yes — C2 is his signature constellation: +1 Namisen stack and +50% Max HP at 3+ stacks, directly amplifying his core DMG mechanic.
