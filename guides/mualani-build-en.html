Genshin Mualani Build: 5.0 Hydro Catalyst DPS

  • Weapons: Surf's Up (5★ signature) > Sacrificial Jade / The Catch (4★) > Ring of Yaxche (craftable).

Who Is Mualani?

Mualani is a 5★ Hydro catalyst character released in Version 5.0, the renowned surf instructor from Natlan. Her damage scales entirely off HP, and she enters "Nightsoul's Blessing" state after using her Elemental Skill to boost her Normal Attack damage significantly.
