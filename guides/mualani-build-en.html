Genshin Mualani Build: 5.0 Hydro Catalyst DPS

Mualani (v5.0) is a 5★ Hydro catalyst main DPS built for the forward Vaporize system — every hit scales off HP. Endgame targets: 300,000+ max HP, CRIT Rate/DMG at ~1:2 (prioritize CRIT DMG); BiS is her signature Surf's Up (542 ATK, 88.2% CRIT DMG substat) + Obsidian Codex 4pc (+40% CRIT Rate in Nightsoul). C0 is complete; C1/C2 are the step-ups.

Quick Answer

  • Role: On-field main DPS, HP-scaling Hydro Vaporize specialist.
  • Weapons: Surf's Up (5★ signature) > Sacrificial Jade / The Catch (4★) > Ring of Yaxche (craftable).
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex 4pc (best) / Heart of Depth 4pc /mixed sets.
  • Stats: HP sands + Hydro DMG goblet + CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: Mualani + Xiangling + Kazuha/Faruzan + Zhongli/Bennett.

Who Is Mualani?

Mualani is a 5★ Hydro catalyst character released in Version 5.0, the renowned surf instructor from Natlan. Her damage scales entirely off HP (a high max-HP base plus ascension CRIT bonuses), and she enters "Nightsoul's Blessing" after using her Elemental Skill to boost Normal Attack damage significantly. The core loop is consuming Nightsoul points (lost on each Normal Attack) into Vaporize reactions.

Talent Priority

Normal Attack ≥ Skill > Burst. Mualani's core damage comes from her enhanced Normal Attacks (the shark-bite bursts) during Nightsoul's Blessing — level those two to 9/10 first; the Burst has a lower multiplier and can wait.
