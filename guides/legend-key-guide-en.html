Genshin Legend Key: How to Get It and What It Unlocks Now

Quick Answer

  • Biggest change: since Version 5.4 Story Quests no longer consume Legend Keys. Meet the requirements and they unlock for free.
  • What keys are for now: Hangout Events — 2 keys per act. Unlocking an act once makes all its endings permanently replayable.
  • How to earn: from AR26, every 8 daily commissions gives 1 key — but you must claim it manually on the Story Quest tab.
  • Cap: hold at most 3; progress keeps banking up to 8, so spending a key lets you claim another immediately.
  • Not needed for: Tribal Chronicles. Those unlock through Natlan reputation and the main story.

The Short Answer

If you are here because a guide told you to save Legend Keys for a character’s Story Quest, that advice is out of date. Since Version 5.4, Story Quests cost nothing. Their gating is now purely: prerequisite Archon Quest completed, Adventure Rank reached, and for some acts, reputation or world-quest requirements.

Legend Keys still exist and still accumulate. They are spent on Hangout Events — the 4-star character storylines with multiple branching endings. Ignoring keys does not break anything, but it does mean sitting on unspent Hangout content.

The manual-claim part is where most players lose keys. If you never open that tab, completed commissions pile up as invisible progress and you wonder why the count never moves.

The 3-Key Cap and Cached Progress

The cap is 3 held keys, not 3 earned keys. Progress behind the counter keeps ticking up to 8 commissions, so the moment you spend a key, you can claim the next one right away.

Practical result: you are never punished for capping out, as long as you spend keys reasonably often. What does get wasted is time — if you sit at 3 keys for two weeks, you have effectively stopped earning.

Rule of thumb: whenever your keys hit 3, go spend 2 on a Hangout act.

Common Mistakes

  • Saving keys for a Story Quest. Not needed since 5.4. Story Quests open on requirements alone.
  • Expecting keys to arrive automatically. They must be claimed on the Story Quest tab. Unclaimed progress does not show as keys.
  • Sitting at 3 keys for weeks. The cap blocks new earning. Spend 2 on a Hangout whenever you hit 3.
  • Re-spending keys on a cleared Hangout. Once an act is unlocked, every ending is permanently replayable — no extra keys.

FAQ

Do I still need Legend Keys to unlock Story Quests?

No. Since Version 5.4 Story Quests no longer consume Legend Keys - you only need the prerequisite quests and Adventure Rank. Any guide saying otherwise predates the 5.4 change.

How do I get Legend Keys fast?

Complete daily commissions: 8 commissions equal 1 key. The catch is that keys must be claimed manually on the Story Quest tab, so check it regularly - otherwise the progress never turns into a usable key.

Does the 3-key cap waste progress?

No. Earned progress keeps banking up to 8 commissions, so once you spend a key you can claim another immediately. What you lose by sitting at the cap is time, not progress.

What do Legend Keys unlock now?

Hangout Events - 2 keys per act. Story Quests are free, and Tribal Chronicles in Natlan use reputation and story progress instead of keys.

How many Legend Keys does a Hangout need?

2 keys per act. Once unlocked, all 5-6 endings can be replayed for free forever, so you only pay once per character.
