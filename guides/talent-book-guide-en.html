Genshin Talent Books Guide: Domain Schedule & Farming Plan

The trap with talent books is that they are locked to weekdays - which book your character needs decides whether you can farm on Monday or Wednesday. This guide covers the full regional domain schedule, the complete material checklist for a maxed talent, and a farming plan that does not fight the event calendar.

  • When do talent books drop? Each region has three books on rotation: one on Mon/Thu, one on Tue/Fri, one on Wed/Sat. Everything is open on Sunday.
  • How many books for one maxed talent? 3 Teachings, 21 Guides, and 38 Philosophies, plus enemy drops, weekly boss materials, and 1 Crown of Insight.
  • How much Mora does it cost? About 1.65 million for one talent to level 10, and roughly 4.95 million for all three.
  • Which book first? List which series your 2-4 target characters need and farm on the day that covers the most of them.

What Talent Books Are

Each region has a set of three talent book series, one per character group. Which book a character uses depends on their region, not their element - a Pyro character from Mondstadt uses the Freedom series, while a Pyro character from Liyue uses Prosperity.

Talent books carry a talent from level 2 all the way to 10 and are central to a character's damage. But the three series do not all drop every day; they rotate by weekday, which is why talent farming needs a calendar.

Farming Plan: Work From a List

  • Make a list first: write out which book series your 2-4 target characters need and find the day that covers the most.
  • Farm in one session: dumping all your resin into one series on one day beats scattering runs across series.
  • Use Sunday for gaps: if you are only a few books short, Sunday fills it without another week of waiting.
  • Prioritize the key talent: a main DPS rarely needs all three talents maxed. Level the damage skill first.

Mora and Crowns: The Hidden Cost

Talent books are never the whole bill: one talent to level 10 costs about 1.65 million Mora, and all three run close to 4.95 million. The harder limit is Crowns of Insight - each level 10 talent needs one, and Crowns come only from limited events, regional offerings, and a few quests. They cannot be farmed.

Treat triple-crowning as a long-term goal. Crown your long-term core characters and do not spread Crowns across every character you level.

Efficiency Tips

  • Use the crafting bench: convert lower-tier books three-to-one into higher tiers to cover a Philosophies shortage.
  • Bring crafting bonus characters: some characters refund or double talent materials when crafting.
  • Run the highest difficulty: higher domain levels give better drops, so push to the top tier once your team can handle it.
  • Do not let resin cap: farming in focused sessions beats tiny daily runs.

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing up the days: farming Tuesday's book on a Monday wastes resin.
  • Ignoring Sunday: waiting a full week for the last few books.
  • Stockpiling books but not Mora: having the books ready but no Mora to spend.
  • Wasting Crowns: spending the rarest resource on a temporary character.
  • Forgetting enemy drops: getting blocked by mob materials after the books are done.

Bottom Line

Talent books come down to a calendar and a checklist. Confirm which series your characters use, farm on the open days, and fill gaps on Sunday. One maxed talent needs 3/21/38 books, 1.65 million Mora, and a Crown, while all three run near 4.95 million Mora plus 3 Crowns. Crowns cannot be farmed, so save them for your long-term core team.
