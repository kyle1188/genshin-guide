Genshin Damage Formula Guide 2026: Multipliers & Finding Your Damage Gap

Damage is just several multipliers stacked. Fix the smallest one — this guide opens the formula so you stop blindly stacking ATK and start pinpointing the gap.

Final Damage = Base × (1+DMG Bonus%) × Crit × DEF mult × RES mult × Reaction. All six layers multiply, so "fix the smallest layer" — find the weakest multiplier, that's where your next roll pays most.

The Six Multiplier Layers

① Base

ATK (or HP/DEF/EM, per kit) × talent multiplier. Your ATK panel is the floor.

② DMG Bonus

Elemental/Physical DMG% (Goblet + set + weapon + buffs), summed then multiplied.

③ Crit

1 + Crit Rate × Crit DMG. See Crit Rate vs DMG.

④ DEF mult

(CharLvl+100)/((CharLvl+100)+(EnemyLvl+100)). Same-level 90 ≈ 0.5; shreddable via DEF reduction/ignore.

⑤ RES mult

RES ≥0: 1-RES; RES <0: 1-RES/2 (halved). Viridescent -40%, Superconduct -40%, Zhongli -20% all hit this.

⑥ Reaction

Amplifying (Vape/Melt) multiplies this hit; Transformative (Overload etc.) scales off EM independently, ignores crit.

Key: every layer is multiplicative, not additive. +10% ATK and +10% DMG Bonus each net exactly +10% — but filling the lowest layer first wins.

DEF Multiplier

Same level Lv90 vs Lv90: 190/(190+190)=0.5 — you only deal half your "theoretical" damage to a same-level boss, which is why Spiral Abyss bosses feel so tanky. DEF reduction (Raiden C2 -60%, Zhongli 0%) and RES shred are the main late-game levers.

Amplifying vs Transformative: Don't Mix

Amplifying (Vaporize/Melt) doesn't deal its own damage — it multiplies your hit by ×1.5 or ×2 (by element order), so it still benefits from ATK/Crit/DMG%, with only a small EM bonus (2.78×EM/(EM+1400)). Transformative (Overload/Swirl/Bloom...) damage is fully independent, scales off EM, and ignores crit — the most common "why is my damage low" mix-up.

Finding Your Damage Gap

  1. Main stats first: is Goblet/Sands/Cup right (element/ATK)? One wrong piece drops a 46% layer.
  2. Then crit: below 70/140, crit is the cheapest lever.
  3. Then set + talents: 4-pc effect and talent levels are baseline multipliers.
  4. Last, shred RES/DEF: VV, Zhongli, Raiden C2 lift the RES/DEF layers.

Common Mistakes

  • ATK only: ATK sits in the base layer; more is diluted by later layers. Crit and DMG% are usually what's missing.
  • Amplifying treated as transformative: a Vape team stacking EM instead of crit wastes the build.
  • Ignoring RES shred: vs high-RES foes (grass slime, cryo shield) without shred, damage is halved.

Quick Answers

What is Genshin's damage formula?

Final Damage = Base (ATK × talent multiplier) × (1+DMG Bonus%) × Crit (1+Rate×DMG) × DEF mult × RES mult × Reaction. Six multiplicative layers — fix the lowest one first.

My ATK is high but damage is low — why?

ATK only sits in the base layer and gets diluted by every later layer. Common causes: crit below 70/140, wrong Goblet/Cup main stat, or fighting high-RES foes without shred. Find the smallest layer.

How do I shred DEF and RES?

DEF via DEF reduction/ignore (Raiden C2, Zhongli); RES via Viridescent -40%, Superconduct -40%, Zhongli -20%. Both recover 'lost' damage and are the main late-game levers.

Amplifying vs Transformative reactions?

Amplifying (Vape/Melt) multiplies your hit and still benefits from ATK/crit/DMG%; Transformative (Overload/Swirl/Bloom) scales off EM independently and ignores crit entirely. Stacking EM on a Vape team is the classic mistake.
