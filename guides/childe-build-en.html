Genshin Tartaglia (Childe) Build & Teams: 5-Star Hydro Bow DPS

Quick Answer

  • Tartaglia (Childe) is a 5★ Hydro Bow DPS with a dual stance — ranged Hydro application ↔ melee "Foul Legacy" for rapid Hydro strikes + Riptide.
  • Core artifact: Nymph's Dream 4pc (best) or Heart of Depth 4pc (classic); 2+2 is a fine stopgap.
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Hydro DMG Goblet / CRIT Circlet; substats CRIT > ATK% > EM > ER (~110–120%).
  • Signature Polar Star; alternatives Aqua Simulacra, Thundering Pulse, Skyward Harp; F2P Rust (R5).
  • Best teams: International (Xiangling + Bennett + Kazuha), Taser/Electro-Charged (Beidou + Fischl + Kazuha), Furina buff team.
  • Key play: stay in melee only 6–8 seconds then swap out — overstaying triggers the long melee cooldown.

Character Mechanics

Childe's power comes from his stance swap and Riptide:

  • Elemental Skill "Foul Legacy: Riptide": swaps to melee stance where Normal/Charged Attacks deal Hydro and apply Riptide; swapping back to ranged or using his Burst detonates Riptide for AoE Hydro. The melee stance has its own cooldown that grows the longer you stay in it.
  • Elemental Burst "Havoc: Obliteration": in ranged stance it deals big AoE Hydro; in melee stance the nuke has higher multipliers, detonates Riptide, and (in melee) refunds part of the cooldown.
  • Riptide: enemies marked by Riptide take AoE Hydro damage (counted as Normal Attack damage) when hit — his core AoE tool. This makes him a premier Vaporize / Electro-Charged driver.

Teams

International (top tier)

Childe + Xiangling + Bennett + Kaedehara Kazuha

  • Childe's rapid Hydro application + Xiangling's Pyronado Vaporizes everything; Kazuha groups and shreds RES while Bennett buffs and heals — top-tier both single and AoE.

Childe Taser (Electro-Charged, new-player friendly)

Childe + Beidou + Fischl + Kazuha / Sucrose

  • Electro-Charged damage, works at low investment — great for players without Xiangling/Kazuha.

Furina buff team

Childe + Furina + Kaedehara Kazuha + Bennett

  • Furina's massive damage amp raises Childe's burst ceiling with smoother rotations.

Constellations

  • C0: already a competent main carry — great value.
  • C1: reduces melee stance cooldown, smoother rotations.
  • C6: melee Charged Attacks have no cooldown, enabling a pure on-field carry — for whales.

FAQ

Is Childe still worth building?

Yes. International remains a top AoE team, his dual-stance kit is unique and versatile; the only downside is the melee cooldown penalty, which demands good play to manage stance uptime.

What is his best artifact set?

Nymph's Dream 4pc is currently best; without it use Heart of Depth 4pc, or 2pc Nymph's + 2pc Heart as a stopgap. Main stats (ATK% / Hydro / CRIT) stay the same.

How much Energy Recharge does Childe need?

With Bennett/Xiangling carrying ER, ~110–120% on Childe himself is enough to rotate — no need to over-invest and waste substats.

Is his signature weapon necessary?

Polar Star is best-in-slot, but 4★ The Stringless / Rust (R5) already clear International comfortably — not a must-pull, lower priority than constellations or key teammates.

Is Childe really bad against single targets?

He's weaker 1-on-1 than top Hydro carries like Neuvillette, but International is still first-tier AoE and enables Xiangling's Vaporize — a high-value DPS regardless.
