Genshin World Boss Guide: The Best Way to Spend 40 Resin

Quick Answer
  • Each claim costs 40 Original Resin — open the Trounce Blossom that spawns after the kill.
  • A character needs 46 of their boss's exclusive material from level 1 to 90, split 0/2/4/8/12/20 across phases.
  • At World Level 8 a run drops 2-3, so 46 takes roughly 15-23 kills — about 720-760 Resin in total.
  • Gold Gemstones drop at only about 2% per run and Phase 6 needs 6 — craft them or take them from weekly bosses.
  • Weekly bosses cost 30 Resin for the first 3 each week and reset Monday 4 AM; World Bosses respawn right after claiming.

What World Bosses Are

World Bosses (Normal Bosses) are the powerful overworld enemies stationed across Teyvat — the Hypostasis family, Cryo and Pyro Regisvines, Oceanid, Primo Geovishap, Maguu Kenki, Jadeplume Terrorshroom, Icewind Suite and many more. Defeating one spawns a Trounce Blossom; opening it costs 40 Original Resin. No Resin, no reward — that is the key difference between a World Boss and an ordinary elite enemy.

They drop two things that matter: a unique Character Ascension material (one specific to each boss, such as a Hurricane Seed or Cleansing Heart) and elemental gems for the matching element (Sliver / Fragment / Chunk / Gemstone). When a character is stuck at the 20/40/50/60/70/80 level caps, these are the two things you are missing.

Note that gold Gemstone rate. The final ascension alone needs 6 Gemstones, and farming them directly from World Bosses is not realistic — convert lower-tier gems upward at the crafting bench, or take them from weekly bosses instead.

How Many Runs: the 46-Material Goal

Every character, from level 1 to level 90, needs a total of 46 of their boss's unique material. The number is fixed regardless of rarity or region:

The takeaway: Phase 6 alone costs 20 materials — 43% of the total. At World Level 8 a run drops 2–3, so 46 materials takes roughly 15–23 kills. At 40 Resin each, the full process (gems included) runs about 720–760 Resin — roughly four days of natural regeneration.

That is why rushing a brand-new pull to level 90 immediately is a bad idea: funding one character from zero eats most of a week's Resin.

Characters of the same element share gems, so when building several characters of one element, batch the same boss. Surplus gems are never wasted — a future character of that element will use them.

Respawning and Chain Farming

  • Normal World Bosses respawn shortly after you claim the reward (Version 5.3 shortened this dramatically from the old 3-minute wait). If the boss has not returned, you are still inside the arena cell — teleport to the nearest Statue or Waypoint and come back to force the refresh.
  • Not every boss can simply be attacked on sight: Icewind Suite requires speaking to an NPC in Fontaine first and choosing between two variants (Dirge of Coppelia / Nemesis of Coppelius). The two variants drop different exclusive materials, so pick carefully.
  • World Bosses can be fought in co-op, with enemy HP scaling up accordingly — but each player spends their own 40 Resin to claim. There is no way to freeload off someone else's Resin.

Weekly bosses give one talent-material claim per boss per week, and their gold Gemstone efficiency beats World Bosses. So once you are pushing 80 → 90, clearing all three discounted weekly bosses is the highest-value action available.

Also worth knowing: Dream Solvent converts unwanted weekly boss materials into the type you need at the crafting bench — a lifesaver when building several characters at once. It cannot convert World Boss materials.

Efficiency Tips

  • Decide which boss the character needs first, then point your Resin there. Boss materials have no substitute source.
  • Plan ahead: pre-farm materials before pulling (World Boss materials can be pre-farmed; materials from a boss that does not exist yet cannot).
  • When building multiple characters, prioritise those sharing the same boss or element so nothing is wasted.
  • Bring double Anemo resonance (−15% stamina, +10% movement speed) plus mobility characters (Kazuha / Wanderer / Yelan / Sayu) — travel time drops by half.
  • When Resin is tight, secure the three discounted weekly bosses and your current main DPS's ascension materials before anything else.
