Genshin Talent Leveling Priority 2026: Which Skill to Upgrade First

Talents cap a character's damage. Wrong order wastes Mora and Resin — knowing who to level and how far matters more than blind triple-crowning.

Level 6 is the value cliff: one talent from 1 to 6 costs only ~122.5k Mora and a few green/blue books; past level 7 costs explode (7: 120k, 8: 260k, 9: 450k, 10: 700k). Supports and sub-DPS are usually fine at 6-8 — save Crowns and Resin for your main DPS.

Leveling Order by Role

Main DPS

Burst > Skill > Normal. Exceptions: Hu Tao, Keqing (charged), Ayaka, Ganyu (aimed) need high Normal Attack too; NA-focused DPS prioritize it.

Sub-DPS / Quickswap

Only Burst and Skill to 8-10; skip Normal. Xiangling, Xingqiu, Yelan live on their Burst — max it.

Support / Healer

Level only the buffing skills to 6-8. Bennett maxes his Burst (heal + ATK buff); his Normal is dead weight.

Who Gets a Crown

Crowns are scarce: they come only from limited flagship events or permanent trees (Sacred Sakura, Frostbearing). You get maybe one or two per version. Reserve them for your favorite or core main DPS. A triple crown costs 3 Crowns + ~4.96M Mora — an endgame commitment.

Common Mistakes

  • Blind triple-crowning: not every unit deserves it. Push your main DPS key talent to 9-10; park supports at 6-8.
  • Leveling support Normal Attack: Bennett and Xingqiu Normal contributes almost no damage — pure waste.
  • Ignoring talent-book days: each book series is open only 3 days a week; use the alchemy bench to transmute lower books upward.
  • Forgetting character level: talents cap at ascension; an un-ascended Lv80 unit is stuck. Only HP/DEF/EM scalers need Lv90.

Quick Answers

Which talent should I level first?

For a main DPS, usually Burst > Skill > Normal; NA-focused units (Hu Tao, Ayaka, Ganyu, Keqing charged) also need high Normal. Sub-DPS only level Burst and Skill; supports only their buffing skills to 6-8.

How high should I level talents?

Supports and sub-DPS are fine at 6-8 — best value. Main DPS key talent wants 9-10. One talent 1 to 6 costs only ~122k Mora; past level 7 the cost explodes.

Who deserves a Crown?

Only your favorite or core main DPS. Crowns come only from limited events and permanent trees, maybe one or two per version. A triple crown needs 3 Crowns plus ~4.96M Mora — an endgame sink.

How do I farm talent books efficiently?

Each book series is open only 3 days a week — plan Resin around it. Use the alchemy bench to transmute 3 lower books into 1 higher. Weekly boss mats are a hard time-gate; stock up early.
