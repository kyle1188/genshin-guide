Genshin Chasca Build: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: On-field Anemo Main DPS powered by the Nightsoul system.
  • Best Weapon: Signature Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage; 2nd choice is The First Great Magic.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Obsidian Codex (best) or 4pc Viridescent Venerer (for Swirl teams).
  • Main Stats: ATK% Sands / Anemo DMG Goblet / CRIT circlet.
  • Key mechanic: Consuming Nightsoul points while buffed grants +40% CRIT Rate for 6s.

Character Overview

Chasca is a 5-star Anemo bow on-field Main DPS from Natlan. Her kit revolves around the Nightsoul system—when her Nightsoul gauge is active, consuming points triggers powerful effects including a critical +40% CRIT Rate bonus that makes her damage output exceptional.

Most of Chasca's damage comes from the elemental bullets fired during her Elemental Skill, making it the highest priority talent to level.

Talent Priority

Elemental Skill (E) > Elemental Burst (Q) > Normal Attack (A)

Chasca's Elemental Skill fires the bulk of her damage through sustained anemo arrows—level this first. Burst provides AoE grouping and secondary damage, so level it second.

  • When Nightsoul gauge is active, prioritize E skill shots for maximum DPS
  • Manage Nightsoul points carefully to trigger the +40% CR window at optimal moments
  • For Swirl teams using Viridescent Venerer, prioritize EM over pure ATK
  • Works best alongside Nightsoul-giving teammates like Mavuika
