Beidou Build & Teams: 4-Star Electro On-Field Applicator & Battery

Quick Answer

  • Beidou is a 4★ Electro claymore, an on-field high-frequency Electro applicator + battery; her Skill empowers Normal/Charged Attacks with continuous Electro.
  • Core set: Thundering Fury 4pc (+40% Overload/Aggravate/Superconduct, E CD −1s) or Instrument of the Fortified Will 4pc (battery).
  • Main stats: ATK%/ER Sands, Electro Cup, Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: Crits > ATK > ER > EM.
  • Best teams: Beidou + Fischl + Xingqiu + Sucrose/Kazuha (Charged); Beidou + Fischl + Nahida + Yaoyao (Aggravate).

How Beidou Works

Beidou is the classic "on-field sub-DPS + battery":

  • Skill "Lightning Tidecaller": while empowered, Normal/Charged Attacks deal Electro, apply it continuously, and proc Energy particles on hit.
  • Empowered hits trigger Electro-Charged/Aggravate at high frequency (multi-hit).
  • She's both a damage source (on-field Aggravate) and the team's Electro ER core — dual role.

Team Comps

Charged / Taser Team (classic)

Beidou (on-field Electro) + Fischl (off-field Electro) + Xingqiu (Hydro/Sub-DPS) + Sucrose/Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Electro Resonance + Xingqiu's Hydro → constant Electro-Charged.
  • Beidou's empowered multi-hit fires at high frequency; Sucrose groups + shreds Electro RES.

Aggravate Team

Beidou + Fischl + Nahida (Dendro/EM) + Yaoyao/Baizhu (healer)

  • Nahida applies Dendro + Beidou/Fischl apply Electro → target stays Quicken'd.
  • Beidou's on-field Normal Attacks trigger Aggravate; Thundering Fury 4pc amplifies it.

Superconduct Physical Team

Beidou (Electro) + Eula/Razor (Physical DPS) + Zhongli (shield/shred) + Diona/Rosaria (Cryo)

  • Cryo + Electro trigger Superconduct, shredding 40% Physical RES for the physical carry.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: extends the empowered "Lightning Tidecaller" window — the biggest on-field damage upgrade, top value.
  • C1: ER/shield after Burst, smoother battery play.

FAQ

Is Beidou a main DPS or sub-DPS?

She's an on-field sub-DPS and battery. She's both a high-frequency Aggravate source and the team's ER core, so she does more than a single-role carry.

Beidou or Fischl?

Fischl is more hands-off (Oz applies Electro off-field); Beidou is stronger on-field (multi-hit + ER). They can even run together. Use Beidou on-field if you lack Fischl, and vice versa.

What set does Beidou use?

Thundering Fury 4pc for on-field Aggravate (reactions +40%). For a pure battery slot, use Instrument of the Fortified Will 4pc. Main stat ATK vs ER follows her role.

No 5-star weapon — what's the F2P pick?

Prototype Archaic (craftable ATK%) > Favonius Greatsword (ER) > Whiteblind/Sacrificial. Forge Prototype Archaic for now.

Can Beidou clear the Spiral Abyss?

Yes. Aggravate/Charged teams are her home turf. Pair her with Nahida, Raiden and Yaoyao for a F2P-friendly 12-star Electro core.
