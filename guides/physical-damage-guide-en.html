Physical Damage Teams: Superconduct Shred, Eula & Razor Builds

Quick Answer

  • Superconduct (Cryo + Electro) shreds 40% Physical RES for 12 s — essential for any physical DPS team.
  • Only two top-tier physical carries exist: Eula (heavy-hit burst) and Razor (high-frequency normal attacks).
  • Key supports: Mika C6 (Crit DMG + Phys DMG + up to 25% ATK SPD), Yun Jin C6 (+12% ATK SPD team-wide), Rosaria C6 (+20% Phys RES shred, stacks with Zhongli C6 to 40%).
  • Pale Fire 4pc: +40% Phys DMG when target Phys RES < 20%.
  • Physical Elemental Resonance: team-wide +15% DMG.

Why Superconduct Is Non-Negotiable

Physical damage (Normal/Charged/Burst) is reduced by the enemy's Physical RES. Superconduct is the only systematic way to shred that stat:

  • Cryo + Electro on the same target → Superconduct fires
  • Affected enemies take 40% reduced Physical RES for 12 s (refreshes, does not stack)
  • The reaction itself deals no damage — pure debuff

Key Support Characters

Mika

  • After Burst: Active character's Physical DMG +25%
  • C6: Additional Crit DMG buff + ATK Speed (up to 25% ATK SPD)
  • Cryo applicator — triggers Superconduct while buffing the carry
  • Provides shield + healing for survivability

Yun Jin

  • Team-wide Normal/Charged DMG bonus
  • C6: +12% ATK Speed for the whole team — massive for Razor's fast hit rate
  • Not a physical DPS herself, but her buff covers the entire team

Rosaria

  • Off-field Electro application → maintains Superconduct
  • C6: +20% Phys RES shred (stacks with Zhongli C6 to 40%)
  • Sustained healing for team survivability

Zhongli

  • Geo Resonance: +15% team DMG while shielded
  • C6: +20% Phys RES shred
  • Best shield in the game — keeps physical carries alive during their burst window

Team Compositions

Eula Top-Tier

Eula (DPS) + Raiden Shogun (Electro + energy) + Mika C6 (Phys DMG + ATK SPD) + Zhongli (shield + Phys RES shred)

  • Rotation: Zhongli E (shield) → Raiden E (Electro) → Mika E (Cryo + Phys buff) → Eula on-field E + Burst
  • Raiden + Mika trigger Superconduct (40% shred); Zhongli C6 adds 20% more
  • Physical Resonance (+15%) if all 4 are Physical/Geo

Razor Sustained

Razor (DPS) + Rosaria C6 (Electro + Phys shred) + Yun Jin C6 (ATK SPD + DMG) + Zhongli (shield)

  • Razor E → normal-attack rotation → Rosaria Q maintains Superconduct off-field
  • Yun Jin C6: +12% ATK SPD; Rosaria C6 + Zhongli C6: up to 40% Phys RES shred
  • Higher ATK SPD = more normal attacks = more damage; prioritize ATK SPD sub-stats

FAQ

How long does Superconduct last?

12 seconds. Refreshing it resets the timer; it does not stack layers.

Do Superconduct and Zhongli C6 shred stack?

Yes. Superconduct (40%) + Zhongli C6 (20%) = up to 60% total Phys RES reduction (effective cap usually around 40–50%).

How do you get Physical Resonance?

Include a Physical-type or Geo-type character + a physical DPS in the team → team DMG +15%.

What if I don't have Mika C6?

Mika without C6 is still strong (E → +25% Phys DMG + Cryo application). Use Yun Jin C6 for ATK SPD, or Zhongli C6 for additional Phys RES shred.

Can physical carries trigger Vaporize or Melt?

No. Physical DMG from Normal/Charged attacks is not elemental — it cannot trigger Amplifying reactions. Physical teams rely on Superconduct shred + ATK SPD + Phys DMG cup for damage scaling.
