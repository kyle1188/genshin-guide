Crimson Witch of Flames: 2/4-Piece Bonuses & Best Users

Crimson Witch of Flames is the workhorse set for Pyro damage dealers: 2-piece grants +15% Pyro DMG, and 4-piece boosts Overloaded, Burning and Burgeon by 40% plus Vaporize and Melt by 15%, with a 3-stack Elemental Skill amplifier on the 2-piece bonus. This page lists the set bonuses, the stacking mechanic, tiered best users, main-substat priorities, and the farming domain so you can decide "who gets this set and how many pieces to farm."

  • 2-piece: +15% Pyro DMG Bonus (flat, applies to any Pyro character).
  • 4-piece: +40% Overloaded / Burning / Burgeon; +15% Vaporize / Melt.
  • Stacking: each Elemental Skill raises the 2-piece bonus by 50% for 10s, max 3 stacks.
  • Best users: Diluc, Klee, Yanfei, Yoimiya, Hu Tao, Xiangling, Mavuika.
  • Farm it: "Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula", Bishui Plain, Liyue (AR30, 20 resin).

Set bonuses: what 2-piece and 4-piece actually add

Note the 40% covers Overloaded, Burning and Burgeon; Vaporize and Melt only get 15% - so Vaporize/Melt teams earn less from the 4-piece than Overload/Burgeon teams. See the elemental reactions guide for exact coefficients.

The stack mechanic: when to hit your E

The stack is the part most players waste: each Elemental Skill cast raises the 2-piece Pyro DMG bonus by 50% (so 15% -> 22.5% per stack), lasting 10s, up to 3 stacks. The correct rotation is to cap all 3 stacks with your E right before your main damage window. The 10s duration is a timing constraint, not a permanent buff.

Bottom line

One line: Crimson Witch = 2-piece +15% Pyro DMG + 4-piece reaction bonus (Overloaded/Burning/Burgeon +40%, Vaporize/Melt +15%) + 3 E-skill stacks. First pick for Pyro mains that trigger reactions constantly (Diluc, Klee, Yanfei, Yoimiya, Hu Tao, Mavuika); farm it in Bishui Plain, Liyue. For Vaporize/Melt teams the 4-piece payoff is low - reach for the other Pyro set.
