Genshin Mavuika Build: Weapons, Artifacts, Fighting Spirit and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: 5-star Pyro claymore, top-tier on-field main DPS or a strong off-field Pyro applier. Her Burst uses Fighting Spirit, not energy.
  • Best weapon: A Thousand Blazing Suns (signature). Budget: Serpent Spine (needs a shield) or Beacon of the Reed Sea.
  • Best artifacts: 4pc Obsidian Codex as main DPS; 4pc Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City when played off-field.
  • Stat rule: ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT. Never build Energy Recharge — she does not use it.
  • Team requirement: 1–2 Natlan teammates to generate Fighting Spirit. Xilonen and Citlali are the gold standard.

Why Mavuika Builds Differently

Mavuika is the Pyro Archon of Natlan (released in 5.3) and one of the strongest damage dealers in the game. What separates her from every other Pyro character is her resource: her Burst does not consume energy at all. It runs on Fighting Spirit, generated by your team.

That single fact invalidates half the usual build advice. Energy Recharge is a dead stat on her. No ER sands, no ER weapons, no Favonius weapons — they do nothing for her Burst cycle.

Her ascension stat is CRIT DMG (+38.4% at Lv.90), which is why her builds lean on CRIT Rate from the circlet and weapon rather than chasing CRIT DMG.

Fighting Spirit: How the Burst Actually Works

Teammates generate Fighting Spirit by consuming Nightsoul points or by landing Normal Attacks. That is the entire reason she needs Natlan teammates: characters with a Nightsoul mechanic feed her meter far faster than anyone else.

Talent Priority and Constellations

Burst and Skill first, Normal Attack effectively irrelevant. Her motorcycle attacks scale off the Skill and Burst multipliers, not the Normal Attack talent — that catches a lot of players out. If you play her off-field, level Skill and Burst roughly together.

C1 raises the Fighting Spirit cap and generation plus an ATK buff. C2 is the big one: a large Base ATK increase, and in off-field mode the Pyro ring shreds enemy DEF by 20%, which pushes her firmly into real sub-DPS territory. C6 massively upgrades both modes.

At C0 she is already a top-tier Pyro option. C2 is where most spenders should stop.

Best Teams

On-field carry

  • Mavuika + Bennett + Citlali + Xilonen — the reference Melt team. Citlali enables Melt and shreds Pyro RES; Bennett and Xilonen buff.
  • Mavuika + Furina + Citlali + Xilonen/Bennett — Freeze-flavoured variant with Furina’s damage buff.
  • Mavuika + Furina + Xilonen + Bennett — Vaporize; Furina covers both Normal and Charged attacks, which Xingqiu and Yelan do not.

Off-field / sub-DPS

  • Mavuika + Mualani + Kazuha + Xilonen — Mualani Vaporize.
  • Mavuika + Kinich + Emilie + Bennett — Kinich Burning.
  • Mavuika + Clorinde + Ororon + Chevreuse — Overload.

Every one of these needs a Natlan unit to keep Fighting Spirit flowing. Drop the Natlan slot and the rotation stalls.

FAQ

Does Mavuika need Energy Recharge?

No. Her Burst runs on Fighting Spirit, generated by teammates consuming Nightsoul points or landing Normal Attacks. Energy Recharge does literally nothing for her - no ER sands, no ER weapons.

How much Fighting Spirit should I have before casting the Burst?

Cast at the cap of 200 whenever you can. Damage scales with the amount consumed, so firing at the 100 minimum throws away a large chunk of the Burst.

Which artifact set, Obsidian Codex or Cinder City?

Obsidian Codex if she is your on-field carry - the 4pc gives +40% CRIT Rate while consuming Nightsoul points. Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City if you play her off-field and nobody else wears it.

Does Mavuika need Natlan teammates?

Practically yes. You need 1-2 Natlan characters (Xilonen, Citlali, Kachina, Pyro Traveler) to generate Fighting Spirit at a usable rate. Without them the Burst cycle falls apart.

Is Mavuika better as a main DPS or sub-DPS?

Both work at a high level. On-field with Obsidian Codex she is one of the strongest Pyro carries in the game; off-field with Cinder City she is arguably the best Xiangling replacement for teams that need reliable Pyro application.
