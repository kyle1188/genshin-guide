Genshin Swirl Reaction Explained: Damage Formula, Double Swirl & 4VV

Quick answer
  • Swirl happens when Anemo hits an enemy already affected by Hydro, Pyro, Cryo or Electro. The damage is fixed: 0.6 × level multiplier, about 868 at level 90 with 0 EM.
  • It scales only with the Anemo trigger's level and Elemental Mastery, plus the target's matching elemental RES. It ignores ATK, cannot crit, gets no elemental DMG bonus, and ignores DEF.
  • Swirl spreads the aura to every enemy within 6 m except the one that triggered it. That spread, not the 868 damage, is the real value.
  • Swirl is a weak interaction: it consumes roughly half the aura, so the original element usually survives and can react again.

1. Trigger rules and spread radius

There is exactly one way to trigger Swirl: deal Anemo damage to an enemy with a Hydro, Pyro, Cryo or Electro aura. Anemo cannot exist as an aura, so nothing ever "has Anemo on it".

Hydro is the sole exception. It still spreads Wet (which is why Hydro Swirl sets up Freeze), but the damage only lands on the enemy that triggered it. This is why Hydro-infused Anemo bursts feel like they do nothing.

Keep two things separate: Swirl damage and elemental absorption (infusion) damage are different pipelines. Kazuha's absorbed Pyro damage from his burst is normal damage — it crits, scales with ATK and DMG bonus. Only the Swirl tick itself runs on level and EM.

2. The damage formula and level table

Swirl DMG = 0.6 × Level Multiplier × (1 + EM Bonus) × RES Multiplier

The EM bonus is 16 × EM ÷ (EM + 2000), which has heavy diminishing returns: 200 EM gives +145%, 1000 EM gives +533%, and it flattens after that.

One practical conclusion: level your Anemo support to 90. Going from 80 to 90 raises Swirl damage by about 34%. This is one of the few places in the game where character level is literally damage.

3. Four hidden Swirl rules

It only consumes about half the aura

Swirl's reaction coefficient is 0.5 (some measurement frameworks use 0.625). It is a weak interaction, so 1U of Pyro hit by 1U of Anemo keeps roughly 0.5U Pyro afterwards. Anemo does not wreck your elemental setup — it extends and copies it.

The same target has a damage ceiling

One attacker can only deal 2 Swirl instances per 0.5 s to the same target, and multiple Swirls triggered on one target within 0.1 s produce only one instance of damage and one application. Fast multi-hit Anemo skills do not mean one Swirl per hit.

Swirl triggers secondary reactions

The spread element reacts again on contact, and that reaction's damage is calculated from the Anemo character's level and EM. Enemy A has Pyro, enemy B has Electro: Anemo on A swirls Pyro onto B, triggering Overload — and the Overload damage uses the Anemo character's stats, not a Pyro character's.

Swirled auras are thicker than the original

The applied aura is not a copy. It is recalculated from the smaller of the Anemo gauge and the existing aura, then scaled up (1U of Anemo produces an aura with roughly a 2.25U decay profile). Swirled elements therefore last longer than you would expect.

4. Double Swirl and why it matters

Double Swirl means triggering Swirl on two different elements inside one rotation, so Viridescent Venerer's −40% RES covers both. Your main DPS element and your sub-DPS element both get shredded.

RouteExecutionBest for
Two-element routeApply element A → stand in element B (Bennett burst field) → skill/burst absorbs B → plunging or lingering Anemo swirls AInternational (Tartaglia / Xiangling / Bennett / Kazuha)
Electro-Charged routeWith Hydro and Electro coexisting, use a held skill (2U Anemo) to swirl both at onceTaser (Raiden / Xingqiu / Kazuha)

The Electro-Charged route is far more reliable because both auras already coexist. The two-element route requires a strong original aura and precise absorption timing — one early Bennett field and it collapses into a single Swirl.

Three common failures:

  • Using tap skill (1U Anemo) on a 2U aura — not enough gauge, the second element never gets swirled. Use the held version.
  • Elemental absorption stealing priority — when the absorbed element hits an enemy with an existing aura, non-Swirl reactions resolve first and Swirl can be skipped.
  • Environmental auras — burning grass, water, Cryo flowers. Clear the field before you start the setup.

5. Why 4-piece Viridescent Venerer is non-negotiable

4VV gives +60% Swirl DMG and −40% elemental RES to the swirled element for 10 s. Resistance shred is a separate multiplier from DMG bonus, which is exactly why it is so strong.

FAQ

Can Swirl crit?

No. Transformative reactions cannot crit, gain no elemental DMG bonus and ignore DEF. They scale with level, EM and the target's RES only.

Is Swirl the same as Anemo infusion?

No. Swirl is a reaction with fixed damage. Elemental absorption is a skill effect that deals normal damage, crits, and scales with ATK.

Why level an Anemo support to 90?

Swirl = 0.6 × level multiplier. The multiplier goes from 1,077 at 80 to 1,447 at 90 — roughly 34% more Swirl damage, for free.

Do two 4VV users stack the shred?

No. Same-named effects do not stack on one enemy; the duration just refreshes. Two Anemo units in 4VV is still −40%.

Can Swirl trigger Hyperbloom?

Not directly. Swirl can carry Hydro or Electro onto a Dendro Core's vicinity and set off the explosion indirectly, and that explosion uses the triggering character's stats.

What if I do not have Kazuha?

Sucrose is the direct replacement (EM buff plus 4VV), followed by Sayu or Lynette in 4VV. What matters is that someone wears the 4-piece set and personally triggers the Swirl.
