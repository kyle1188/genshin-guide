Genshin Kinich Build Guide: 5.0 Dendro Claymore Burning DPS

Kinich is a 5-star Dendro Claymore on-field main DPS released in version 5.0. His damage scales off ATK and CRIT. His Elemental Skill grapples into Nightsoul's Blessing, where his Normal Attacks become Loop Shots (settled as Skill DMG and restoring 3 Nightsoul points). Keeping enemies in Burning or Burgeon states charges Nightsoul points fastest and powers his Scalespiker Cannon. Target cap panel: ATK 2000+, CRIT Rate ~60% (Obsidian Codex grants +40% on its own), CRIT DMG 200%+, ER 130%. Usable at C0; C1/C2 are quality-of-life jumps (+12.6% / +32% team damage).

Quick Answers

  • Role: On-field main DPS; Skill + Scalespiker Cannon under Nightsoul's Blessing, charged fastest via Burning / Burgeon.
  • Weapon: Fang of the Mountain King (5★ signature) > Beacon of the Reed Sea / A Thousand Blazing Suns (5★) > Serpent Spine / Earth Shaker (4★ F2P).
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex 4pc (first choice) / Unfinished Reverie 4pc (Burning teams).
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands + Dendro DMG Goblet + CRIT Circlet.
  • Team: Kinich + Xiangling + Bennett + Xilonen / Furina (Burning).

Who is Kinich

Kinich is a 5-star Dendro Claymore character released in version 5.0, a Huitztlan Saurian Hunter from Natlan with the title "Turnfire Hunt." His Elemental Skill fires a grappling hook to enter Nightsoul's Blessing; in this state his Normal Attacks become Loop Shots that scale with his Skill and restore 3 Nightsoul points, and once points fill he unleashes the Scalespiker Cannon for a high-multiplier Dendro hit. His ascension passive (A1, "The Price of Desolation") makes enemies affected by Burning or Burgeon restore 7 Nightsoul points every 0.8s — which is why an off-field Pyro or Hydro teammate charges him the fastest.

Main stats: ATK% Sands / Dendro DMG Goblet / CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG Circlet. Substat priority: CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Energy Recharge. Because Obsidian Codex already grants +40% CRIT Rate, favor CRIT DMG on the substats to avoid overflow.

Talent Priority

Elemental Skill > Elemental Burst > Normal Attack. Kinich's core damage comes from the Scalespiker Cannon and Loop Shots under Nightsoul's Blessing (both settle as Skill / Burst multipliers), so level the Skill first, then the Burst; Normal Attack multipliers are low and last.

Constellation Analysis

  • C0: Fully viable; the Nightsoul kit is intact, and a C0 Burning team clears Spiral Abyss
  • C1: +30% movement SPD after landing from the grapple, +100% CRIT DMG on Scalespiker Cannon — a big mobility and ceiling jump
  • C2: Skill hit shreds Dendro RES by 30%, first Cannon gains +100% DMG and wider AoE — team-defining (+32%)
  • C6: Endgame investment, sharply raises Cannon damage (up to +92% team ceiling)
