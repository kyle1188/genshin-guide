Genshin Enemy Farming Guide: Drops, Daily Caps & Efficient Routes

Character ascension and talent leveling both run on enemy drops, yet most players never check the rules behind them. Common enemies, elite enemies and bosses use completely different drop systems, respawn timers and daily caps. This guide lays out all three, the real quantities you need, and the routes that cut farming time in half.

  • How many enemy tiers? Common (slimes, Hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders), elite (high-HP rare-material carriers), and bosses (resin-costing world and weekly bosses).
  • Is there an elite cap? Yes. Elite enemies stop giving drops after 400 per day, resetting with the server daily reset. Past that, kills give no EXP and no loot.
  • How much for ascension? Level 1 to 90 needs 18 / 30 / 36 of the three common-material tiers. Maxing one talent adds 6 / 22 / 31.
  • Do kills cost resin? No. Common and elite drops are fully resin-free; only world bosses and weekly bosses cost resin.
  • Can I farm in co-op? Yes. Drops and caps are tracked separately for each player, so farming a friend's world costs them nothing.

The Three Enemy Tiers

Get the categories straight first, or you will farm the wrong target:

The key split: common and elite enemies are resin-free, limited only by respawns and the daily cap. Bosses are the resin sink. Missing common materials and missing boss materials are two different problems solved two different ways.

Elites: The 400-Per-Day Cap Matters

Elite enemies are your main weapon-ascension source, and the easiest thing to waste:

  • 400 drops per day, hard cap. Past it, elite kills award no EXP and no loot, regardless of world level.
  • Daily reset. It resets with the server daily reset, not on a 48-hour cycle.
  • Mora output. Roughly 5 Mora per enemy level: weak elites (Mitachurls) cap around 200, mid-tier (adult Rifthounds) around 400, strong elites (Lawachurls) around 600.
  • Character EXP. Elites grant about 3x the character EXP of common enemies in the same level bracket, so elite routes double as leveling.

Practical takeaway: fold elite routes into your daily routine rather than looping one point. After 400 drops you are just sightseeing.

Higher tiers are rarer, so do not only farm high-tier enemies: crafting merges low tiers upward, but there is no reverse conversion.

Efficient Routes and Methods

  • Batch your demand first. One enemy family usually feeds several characters, so a single route levels multiple units.
  • Plan with an interactive map. Route by dense clusters, not by whatever you pass. A tight elite loop can hit 20+ kills.
  • Bring mobility and AoE. Kazuha, Yelan or Wanderer for travel, plus an AoE carry to clear packs fast.
  • Use co-op to top up. Hit your daily cap? Farm a friend's world. Caps are per-player.
  • Farm passively. Clear elites along your commission, ley line and artifact routes for free progress.
  • Never spend resin on common mats. Craft spare low tiers upward instead of re-farming.

Common Pitfalls

  • Treating elites as infinite. After 400 drops per day you gain nothing. Check before looping.
  • Chasing only high tiers. Low tiers are the bottleneck, and crafting only merges upward.
  • Confusing material lines. Common drops, boss drops and artifacts are three separate tracks.
  • Forgetting co-op. When you are short a few dozen, a friend's world beats waiting.
  • Farming bosses casually. Bosses cost resin, so plan them rather than treating them like overworld mobs.

Final Thoughts

Enemy farming comes down to knowing the tiers and respecting the caps. Common and elite drops are resin-free, elites stop at 400 drops per day, and bosses are the only resin sink. Ascension needs 18/30/36, but maxing three talents pushes it to 36/96/129, so tally your real demand first, clear it in one route, and use co-op for the shortfall.
