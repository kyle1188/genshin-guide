Genshin Gacha Odds: Full Probability Table & Pull Math

"How many pulls until I get the character?" is the core arithmetic of every pull decision: the official consolidated 5-star rate is 1.6% (one 5-star every ~62.5 pulls on average), but the expected cost of a specific limited character is ~91 pulls, worst case 180 (28,800 Primogems). This page lists the official rate tables, the community soft-pity curve, the weapon-banner 75/25 split, and the 6.7 Lightrace Wish in one place; the expected-pull and cost math is compiled by Teyvat Starter Guide and matches the site-wide pity-page figures (160 Primogems = 1 wish).

  • Official consolidated rates: 5-star base 0.6% -> consolidated 1.6% (avg 62.5 pulls); 4-star base 5.1% -> 13%, with at least one 4-star per 10 pulls.
  • Soft pity: ramps from pull 74 (~6.6%), +6% per pull, 100% at pull 90 (community-measured; not officially published).
  • Specific UP: since Capturing Radiance (v5.0) the consolidated featured rate is 55%; expected ~91 pulls, worst 180 = 28,800 Primogems.
  • Weapon banner: consolidated 1.85%, 75/25 split, soft 63 / hard 80; Epitomized Path locks your weapon within 160 pulls (25,600 worst).
  • Lightrace Wish (new, 6.7): curated pool of returning 5-stars, shares pity with the Standard/Chronicled banner.

Two things people mix up: base rate is the per-pull hit chance with no pity involved; the consolidated rate is the long-run average after soft/hard pity. A 10-pull has roughly a 15-18% expected share of 5-stars, and after a 5-star lands the remaining pulls in that batch keep judging independently - so two 5-stars in one 10-pull is possible.

Implication: the average 5-star lands around pull 62.5 (1 / 1.6%), but most players hit it between pulls 75-85, because the curve is flat before 74 and steep after. This site budgets a soft-pity average of ~76 pulls = 12,160 Primogems, consistent with that curve.

Getting the specific UP: ~91 pulls expected, 180 worst

A 5-star drop is not an UP. Before v5.0 it was a pure 50/50; since Capturing Radiance, HoYoverse states the consolidated featured rate is 55% (a hidden counter makes three consecutive losses impossible). At that 55% figure:

The 6.7 Lightrace Wish plugs returning-character pools into that system, so F2P players chasing an old character have options beyond waiting - check the rerun schedule before banking.

Common misreads

  • "1.6% means one 5-star per ~10-pull set" - 16% is an expected share, not a guarantee; most 10-pulls come back with zero 5-stars.
  • "Losing the 50/50 is pure loss" - a loss arms a guaranteed featured next 5-star; the worst case caps at 180 pulls and the expectation stays ~91.
  • "Soft pity is an official mechanic" - the 74-pull ramp is community consensus; the only published guarantees are the 90-pull 5-star pity and the 10-pull 4-star pity.
