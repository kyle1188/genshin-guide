Genshin Impact Fishing Guide: The Catch, Bait Recipes & Best Spots

Fishing is one of Genshin's most underrated free-resource systems — it hands you The Catch, still a top 4-star polearm for Xiangling and Raiden Shogun, in exchange for a few casual fishing rounds. This guide covers the unlock chain, bait-to-fish matching, the 72-hour respawn rule, and an efficient route to R5.

  • Unlock? Finish the Serenitea Pot quest, then do the fishing world quest and land your first catch at Cider Lake, Mondstadt.
  • R5 The Catch costs? Raimei×18, Golden Koi×20, Rusty Koi×20, Pufferfish×40, Bitter Pufferfish×40.
  • Raimei Angelfish? Tatarasuna waters, Inazuma — 18:00–06:00 in-game, False Worm Bait.
  • Respawn? Every 72 real hours at 04:00; day and night counts separately; refill in friends' worlds.

How to Unlock Fishing

Fishing arrived in update 2.1. The unlock chain: reach the Adventure Rank requirement, finish Archon Quest Chapter I Act III "A New Star Approaches", complete the world quest "A Teapot to Call Home" for the Serenitea Pot, then the fishing world quest "Catch and Cook" triggers automatically. Follow Katherine and Nantuck's guidance and complete your first catch at Cider Lake in Mondstadt — fishing is now open.

Your first rod, the Wilderness Rod, comes from that very quest, along with the Fruit Paste bait recipe. Bait recipes for Fake Fly Bait, False Worm Bait and Redrot Bait can be bought from the Mondstadt Fishing Association for 3 Medaka each.

How Fishing Works: Three Phases

  • Cast: hold the cast button to aim and drop the hook 0.5–2 fish-lengths from the school. Too close scares the fish; too far gets no interest. Dropping into the red warning zone auto-reels.
  • Bite: interested fish test the bait 1–4 times before biting. The moment "Bite!" appears, press the reel key — hesitation loses the fish. If you do not pull within 5 seconds of the hook, the fish escapes and eats your bait.
  • Tension: hold/release to keep the white pointer inside the yellow optimal zone and fill the catch bar. If it empties for 2 seconds the fish breaks free. Rare fish (Raimei Angelfish, Moonfin, Snowstrider...) struggle far harder.

Pro tip: bring Thoma to your party — fishing in Inazuma has a 20% chance of double yield. You cannot switch bait mid-cast; reel in first.

Matching matters: Raimei Angelfish only takes False Worm Bait; koi and pufferfish only take Fake Fly Bait. Wrong bait, no bites. Before grinding The Catch, craft roughly 20 False Worm and 130 Fake Fly bait to avoid repeated crafting trips.

Respawn Rules & Day/Night Fish

  • Respawn: every fishing spot refreshes every 72 real hours (3 days) at 04:00. Each spot can be fished once by day and once by night.
  • Time gating: some fish appear only by day or night. Raimei Angelfish spawns only between 18:00 and 06:00 in-game. Koi are available all day.
  • Co-op fishing: once your world's spots are drained, join a friend's world to keep fishing the same species — the key trick to cut the grind.

The Catch: Materials & Efficient Route

The Catch is the star reward of fishing — a free 4-star polearm with ER substat plus burst DMG and CRIT Rate bonuses, a top F2P pick for Xiangling and Raiden Shogun. Trade with Kujirai Momiji at the Inazuma Fishing Association.

  • Raimei Angelfish: waters around Tatarasuna, Inazuma — night only, False Worm Bait; about 3–5 per world. The bottleneck of the whole grind.
  • Golden / Rusty Koi: dense spots at Liyue's Dihua Marsh, Wangshu Inn, Guili Plains — all day with Fake Fly Bait.
  • Pufferfish / Bitter Pufferfish: Cider Lake & Dawn Winery (Mondstadt), Qingce Village pond (Liyue), etc. — Fake Fly Bait.

Route: hit Raimei first each reset (night-only, few spots), then rotate koi and pufferfish Liyue → Mondstadt → Inazuma. Want R5 faster? Chain friends' worlds.

Summary

Fishing is a hidden free-weapon goldmine. Fish a quick loop every few days, hit Raimei at night and rotate koi/pufferfish spots across the 3-day reset, and R5 The Catch in two or three cycles for free. Bring Thoma, use the right bait, and raid friends' worlds.
