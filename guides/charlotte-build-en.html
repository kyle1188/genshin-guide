Genshin Charlotte Healing Formula & Build: 4-star Cryo Catalyst Healer

Quick Answer

  • Role: 4-star Cryo catalyst whose healing comes entirely from her Elemental Burst; it scales off ATK, not HP.
  • Healing formula: Heal = (ATK x talent multiplier + flat) x (1 + Healing Bonus). Burst Lv.10 = 461.8% ATK + 3539.
  • Best weapon: Prototype Amber (craftable: party heal + energy on Burst) > Favonius Codex > Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Song of Days Past (best overall) / Ocean-Hued Clam / Noblesse Oblige. ER sands + ATK goblet + Healing Bonus circlet.
  • Energy: her Burst costs 80. About 150-185% ER with Prototype Amber, 200-230% with other catalysts.
  • Best home: Furina teams - one Burst instantly tops up all four members and maxes Fanfare stacks.

Who Is Charlotte? 4-star Cryo Catalyst Healer

Charlotte is the Steambird reporter from Fontaine, and in combat she is a Burst-driven Cryo healer for the whole party. She is not a slow channel healer: she dumps a large heal on every party member the moment she casts her Burst, then keeps applying Cryo and light healing through a 4-second field.

Charlotte Healing Formula (The Core Answer)

This section exists for everyone searching how Charlotte's healing is calculated. The complete expression is:

Heal = (ATK x talent multiplier + flat value) x (1 + Healing Bonus)

  • ATK = base 173 + weapon base ATK + ATK% + flat ATK. Not HP.
  • Multiplier and flat value scale only with the Elemental Burst talent level. Skill and Normal Attack levels are irrelevant.
  • Healing Bonus comes from the circlet main stat, 2-piece set bonuses, and Diversified Investigation. All sources add together and act as one separate multiplier.

4. The Kamera heal is small

During the 4-second Newsflash Field the Kamera keeps healing the active character at 16.6% ATK + 126 (Lv.10). At the mid tier above that is about 705 per tick. Do not expect it to hold against sustained damage: her job is the big instant heal, not a heal-over-time.

Five Common Mistakes

  1. Stacking HP. Wrong - her healing scales off ATK.
  2. Leveling the Elemental Skill. Wrong - the Skill heals nothing. Only the Burst level counts.
  3. Using a Cryo DMG goblet. Unless you build sub-DPS, use ATK%.
  4. Treating her as sustained healing. The field lasts 4 seconds and ticks for a few hundred. Her strength is the instant full-party heal.
  5. Ignoring energy. An 80-cost Burst means poor ER equals zero healing.

FAQ

How is Charlotte's healing calculated?

Heal = (ATK x talent multiplier + flat value) x (1 + Healing Bonus). At Burst Lv.10 the cast heal is 461.8% ATK + 3539 and it applies to all four party members at once. At 1,800 ATK with 65.9% Healing Bonus that is roughly 19,700 HP per member.

Should Charlotte build ATK or HP?

ATK. Her healing scales entirely off ATK, and her ascension bonus is +24% ATK. Stacking HP does nothing for her healing - it is the single most common mistake.

How much Energy Recharge does Charlotte need?

Her Burst costs 80 energy. With Prototype Amber, roughly 185% solo Cryo, 160% with two Cryo units and 150% with three. Other catalysts need 30-45% more, so about 200-230%. Build toward 200% first.

Which talents should I level on Charlotte?

Only the Elemental Burst. Healing depends purely on the Burst level; the Skill affects only her own Cryo damage and mark frequency, and Normal Attacks are irrelevant. Leave both at level 1 if resources are tight.

Is Charlotte worth building, and how does she compare to Kokomi or Baizhu?

Yes, especially for Furina teams - Furina wants a healer who refills the whole party in one cast, and Charlotte is the cheapest 4-star answer at C0. If you need long sustained on-field healing, Kokomi or Baizhu fit better.

What is Charlotte's best artifact set and weapon?

Artifacts: 4pc Song of Days Past is best overall (Healing Bonus plus converting overflow healing into damage); 4pc Ocean-Hued Clam for a pure healer; 4pc Noblesse Oblige if nobody else runs it. Weapon: craftable Prototype Amber first, then Favonius Codex, or Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers in hyper-carry teams.
