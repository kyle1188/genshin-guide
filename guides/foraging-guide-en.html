Genshin Foraging Guide: Respawn Times, Elemental Gathering & Routes

Quick Answer
  • Four respawn tiers: 12 hours (Sweet Flower, Mint, Crystal Core, fish, Iron Chunk), 24 hours (Mushroom, Pinecone, Bird Egg, Carrot, White Iron Chunk), 48 hours (all regional specialties, Flaming Flower Stamen, Mist Flower Corolla, Electric Crystal), 72 hours (Crystal Chunk, fishing spots).
  • Some collectibles require an elemental attack: Anemo for Dandelion Seeds, Pyro for Electric Crystals, Electro for Naku Weed, Hydro for Lumidouce Bells.
  • Wood: each tree drops up to 3 pieces, and chopping the 11th tree resets the 1st, so a loop of 11 or more non-repeating trees can be farmed forever.
  • Daily investigation spots drop low-tier artifacts, about 60,000 artifact EXP per day, the equivalent of 80 Resin.

1. Collectible Respawn Time Table

Respawn times scale with rarity: the rarer the item, the longer the wait. Note that each node's timer starts the moment you gather it and runs independently, so there is no single global reset to camp.

One caveat: some ingredients and artifact fodder were changed in later versions to reset on the daily server cycle around 4:00 AM, so community tables and in-game testing occasionally disagree. In practice you only need two rules: schedule specialties and high-tier ore on 48 and 72-hour loops, and gather ordinary ingredients whenever you pass them.

2. Collectibles That Need an Elemental Attack

These are the most commonly overlooked items, because without the right element you can never pick them up and it looks like a respawn problem.

A useful habit: keep one character who can apply multiple elements in the party, or swap quickly when you reach the node. Dandelion Seeds in particular are worth planning for, since Anemo characters need them for ascension.

3. Ingredient Types and Uses

  • General ingredients (Mint, Sweet Flower, Mushroom, Raspberry, Apple, Sunsettia, Carrot, Radish, Bird Egg): used in cooking as the base for buffs and healing dishes.
  • Region-specific ingredients (Sea Ganoderma, Lavender Melon, Rukkhashava Mushroom, Spice, Sunsettia-like fruits, Zaytun Peach, Bulle Fruit, Romaritime Flower): needed only for that nation's recipe list.
  • Fish: used in cooking and some quests; fishing spots refresh every 72 hours.
  • Regional specialties: fixed 48-hour respawn, used for character ascension, and best planned separately from cooking.
  • Crystal Cores: 12-hour respawn and the key material for Condensed Resin, so they are worth far more than ordinary ingredients.

If what you actually need are ascension materials, that is a different gathering logic covered in the local specialty guide. For cooking, you only need to keep a few core ingredients stocked.

4. Wood Farming and the Infinite Loop

Wood is used for Serenitea Pot furnishings, and its rules are unusual:

  • You must deal physical damage with a sword, claymore or polearm. Elementally infused attacks do not count.
  • Each tree gives up to 3 pieces of wood.
  • The game only tracks the last 10 trees chopped: chopping the 11th tree resets the 1st.

That allows an infinite chopping loop: find a grove of 11 or more trees with as few repeated species as possible, then chop in a fixed order. By the time you return to the start, the first tree has reset. It is the most efficient wood-farming pattern in the game.

5. Daily Investigation Spots

The open world is dotted with shining investigation points that yield 1-star and 2-star artifacts. A full daily sweep produces roughly 20x 2-star and 100x 1-star artifacts, about 60,000 artifact EXP, equal to four domain runs and roughly 80 Resin saved.

Run this route merged with gathering: clear ingredients, specialties and investigation points in the same area rather than running three separate routes.

6. Habits That Speed Up Gathering

  • Let the party handle travel: bring a mobility character plus an NRE and stamina food. Travel time usually exceeds gathering time.
  • Use exploration talents: characters such as Ningguang and Yanfei mark nearby ore and collectibles on the minimap.
  • Fix your routes: string four to six high-value areas into a loop and only use your own marked nodes instead of sweeping the whole map.
  • Fill gaps in co-op: collectibles are tracked per player, so gathering in a friend's world costs them nothing and beats waiting for a respawn.
  • Use expeditions: dispatch expeditions to bring back ore and materials for free, small but free.

7. Common Mistakes

  • Attacking Dandelion Seeds or Electric Crystals with normal attacks: without the element, the trip is wasted.
  • Treating collectibles as a daily reset: high-tier ore and specialties run on independent 48 and 72-hour timers.
  • Chopping trees with infused attacks: wood only drops from physical damage.
  • Skipping investigation spots: that abandons about 80 Resin of artifact EXP every day, the easiest value to reclaim in long-term progression.

Bottom Line

Foraging is about tiered scheduling and one merged route. Remember the 12 / 24 / 48 / 72-hour tiers, put specialties and high-tier ore on a 48 to 72-hour loop, sweep ingredients and investigation points along the way, swap characters for the elemental nodes, and use the 11-tree loop for wood. Gathering is not hard, it just rewards a fixed route repeated over time.
