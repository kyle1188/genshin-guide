FAQ

Q: Which forge weapon should I prioritize?

A: Prioritize weapons for your main team members, like Sacrificial Sword for Xingqiu or Dragon's Bane for Kazuha.

Q: Are Battle Pass weapons worth it?

A: Yes, Aqua Simulacra and Sacrificial Fragments are highly recommended for their value.

Q: Should I level up 4-star weapons?

A: Not necessary during early game. Wait until you get 5-star weapons before investing heavily.
