Complete Character Building Guide

The core of character building is not "max everything" but "spend where it matters most". This article gives you a clear priority roadmap to build the strongest characters with minimal resin and resources.

Upgrade Priority Overview

The order of resource investment determines ROI. Each resource spend must have a clear purpose and measurable return.

PriorityItemInvestmentReturn
------------------------------------
**1**TalentsMora + books + crowns+30-40% damage
**2**WeaponsPrimogems / forge / BP+15-25% damage
**3**ArtifactsResin+20-40% damage (late game)
**4**ConstellationsPrimogems5-100% depending on C-level
**5**LevelEXP booksUnlock skills, base stats

> Golden rule: Talents ≥ Weapons ≈ Artifacts > Constellations > Level (late game)

---

Step 1: Talents (Highest Priority)

Why talents matter most?

  • Skill multipliers are direct multipliers in the damage formula
  • Lv.6 → Lv.9 increases skill multiplier by ~25-35%
  • Maxing talents costs zero resin

Upgrade order:

1. Elemental Burst (Q) — primary DPS source for most characters

2. Elemental Skill (E) — sustained elemental application/support

3. Normal Attack — only for main DPS who rely on normal attacks (Razor/Noelle/Diluc)

Cost reference (2026 version):

  • Lv.1→Lv.10 full books: ~1.65M mora + 3x1, 21x2, 38x3
  • Crown of Insight: max 2 per character (Q+E each)

Recommendation: Main DPS and core supports at least Lv.8; minimum Lv.6 if budget is tight.

---

Step 2: Weapon Selection

Free options first:

1. Forged weapons — Whiteblind, Verdict, Rainslasher, etc. free upgrade to 5-star

2. Battle Pass — 1680 primogems for 4-star premium weapons

3. Event freebies — e.g., Freedom-Sworn (available free in some versions)

Primogem priority (F2P-friendly):

  • 5-star signature weapons only when absolutely necessary (e.g., Engulfing Lightning for Raiden)
  • 4-star weapons are completely viable (Whiteblind, Prototype series, Iron Sting)

See [Best F2P Weapons Guide](best-f2p-weapons.html)

Farming strategy:

- Prioritize current event domains (easier, better drop rates)

  • Resin: allocate 1-2 runs × 8 resin × 2 times/week = 16-32 resin for artifacts

- Don't farm artifacts for max-level characters — save resin for new characters

---

Step 4: Constellations (Optional)

Worth pulling:

  • C1 brings qualitative change (Nahida C2: AoE expanded)
  • Already have signature weapon but need more damage
  • Stable income (petroleum card + BP + primogem reserves)

Not worth it:

  • C6 only for cosmetics/effects
  • Character has excellent alternatives
  • Current banner strength is average

Step 5: Resin Allocation (Daily)

Daily resin priority:

1. World Level 1-3: Gather materials (zero resin cost)

2. World Level 4+: Farm boss materials for current roles

3. Weekly bosses: Once each on Mon/Thu/Sun (30 resin each)

4. Artifact domains: Based on which characters you're currently leveling

Quick Start Roadmap

New player (AR40-):


①\ Pick\ 1\ main\ DPS\ \(Keqing/Raiden/Hu\ Tao\)
②\ Pick\ 1\ support\ \(Xingqiu/Bennett\)
③\ Prioritize\ talents\ Q\+E\ to\ Lv\.8
④\ Use\ forged/BP\ weapons
⑤\ Farm\ universal\ sets\ \(Thundersoother/Verdant/Gladiator\)

Mid game (AR40-55):


①\ Main\ DPS\ talents\ Lv\.10
②\ Signature\ weapon\ upgrade\ \(if\ budget\ allows\)
③\ Artifacts:\ correct\ main\ stats,\ optimize\ sub\-stats
④\ Consider\ second\ main\ DPS

Late game (AR55+):


①\ All\ characters\ max\ level\ \+\ max\ talents
②\ Artifacts:\ pursue\ extreme\ sub\-stats
③\ Constellations\ as\ needed
④\ Spiral\ Abyss\ 12\-floor\ clear\ teams

FAQ

Q: What's the first character to level?

A: Recommend Raiden Shogun or Keqing (main DPS) + Xingqiu/Bennett (support). These two form the backbone of most team compositions.

Q: How many artifact runs before swapping?

A: Wrong main stat = swap immediately. Correct main but poor sub-stats = keep farming up to ~120 runs, then reassess.

Q: How many times per week should I do weekly bosses?

A: Once each on Monday/Thursday/Sunday (3 times total), covering the weekly refresh cycle.

Q: Does resin expire?

A: Yes. Max storage is 160 resin; excess overflows and is lost. Spend before bed or save fragile resin to condense after AR45.
