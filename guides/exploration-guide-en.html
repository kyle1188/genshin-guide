Genshin Exploration & Chest Guide 2026: How to Hit 100% in Every Region

Exploration progress isn't just about chests — puzzles, waypoints, Oculi, and challenges all count. This guide teaches you the fastest path to 100% in every region.

Exploration progress is a per-region percentage — zoom out on the world map to see it. It increases by opening chests, collecting Oculi, unlocking waypoints and Statues, and completing challenges/puzzles. Hitting 100% requires the right tools.

Tools for 100% exploration

  • Elemental Resonance Stone: craft and use to reveal the approximate area of uncollected Oculi on the map. One per element.
  • Treasure Compass: unlocked at Region Reputation Level 6. Use to show the direction of nearby chests — essential for finding the last few.
  • Interactive Map: third-party tools (GameWith/Game8/HoYoLAB Map) mark every chest and Oculus location; pair with a chest tracker for systematic clearing.

Priority order for 100%

  1. Unlock Statues + Waypoints first: reveals the map, and chest/Oculus icons appear automatically.
  2. Collect Oculi and offer to Statues: unlocks the Resonance Stone, your core clearing tool.
  3. Main quests + World quests: many unlock hidden areas or chest clusters.
  4. Sweep with Treasure Compass: use the compass region by region; usually pushes 80%+ to 95%+.
  5. Fill gaps with Interactive Map: the last 5% requires checking each missed item against the map.

Common traps

  • Thinking chests alone are enough: Oculi, waypoints, challenges, and puzzles all count — chests alone won't reach 100%.
  • Not using Resonance Stones and Compasses: manually hunting the last few chests takes hours; the tools save you that.
  • Ignoring new patches: version updates sometimes add new chests/puzzles to old regions, dropping your 100% to 99%.
  • Skipping hidden quests: some chests only spawn after completing specific World Quests.

Quick Answers

How does exploration progress increase?

Primarily by opening chests, collecting Oculi, unlocking waypoints and Statues, and completing challenges/puzzles. Chests contribute the most, but chests alone won't reach 100% — you need Oculi and puzzles too.

Do chests respawn?

No. Chests are permanent collectibles — once opened, they're gone. But many only appear after meeting a condition (defeat enemies, solve a puzzle, complete a challenge).

How do I find the last few chests?

Use the Treasure Compass (unlocked at Region Reputation Level 6) — it shows the direction of nearby chests. Pair with an interactive map to cross-check; this usually pushes you from 80%+ to 100%.

What are Oculi used for?

Offer them to the corresponding region's Statue of The Seven for Primogems (10-20 per level), Mora, and EXP books. Excess Oculi after max level can be traded for souvenirs. Resonance Stones are your core clearing tool.

Can a new patch drop my exploration %?

Yes. Version updates sometimes add new chests/puzzles to old regions, which can drop a 100% area to 99%. Just go back and clear the new content.
