Genshin Defense & Resistance Guide: Why Half Your Damage Disappears

Plenty of players reroll artifacts for a week and barely gain damage, because the real tax is hidden in DEF and RES, two numbers the game never shows you. This guide covers both multipliers, the sources and caps of shred, enemy base resistances, and which shred to pick for your team.

  • How is the DEF multiplier calculated? (Char Level + 100) ÷ [(Char Level + 100) + (Enemy Level + 100) × (1 − DEF Reduction) × (1 − DEF Ignored)].
  • What does same-level look like? Against an equal-level enemy with no shred, you keep only about 16.7% of your damage (Lv90 enemies have roughly 950 DEF).
  • Is DEF shred capped? Yes, a hard 90% cap. Stacking past it does nothing.
  • How does RES work? Three brackets: RES<0 gives 1−RES/2; 0≤RES<75% gives 1−RES; RES≥75% gives 1÷(4×RES+1).
  • Is more shred always better? Below zero it only counts at half value. Superconduct's −40% against a 10% RES enemy ends up as about 27.8% more damage.

The Defense Multiplier: 80% of Your Damage Goes Missing

The DEF multiplier is its own box in the damage formula:

DEF Multiplier = (Char Level + 100) ÷ [ (Char Level + 100) + (Enemy Level + 100) × k ], where k = (1 − DEF Reduction) × (1 − DEF Ignored).

  • Enemy DEF = 5 × Enemy Level + 500, so a Lv90 enemy sits around 950 DEF.
  • With no shred, Lv90 vs Lv90: 190 ÷ (190+950) ≈ 16.7%, meaning your sheet damage is cut to under a fifth.
  • The multiplier scales with level, not HP, which is why level-gated content feels so punishing.

Key point: DEF Reduction shrinks k, raising your damage. DEF Ignore is a separate layer that multiplies with DEF Reduction rather than adding to it, so having both is stronger than either alone.

Resistance (RES): Three Brackets and the Negative Trap

RES is tracked separately per damage type (Physical, Pyro, Hydro, Electro, Cryo, Anemo, Geo, Dendro):

Total RES = Base RES + Bonus RES − Debuffs

RangeMultiplierNotes
RES < 01 − RES/2Negative RES still helps, but only at half value
0 ≤ RES < 75%1 − RESThe normal bracket: one point of RES is one point of damage
RES ≥ 75%1 ÷ (4×RES + 1)Heavy diminishing returns

Classic example: a 10% Physical RES enemy hit by Superconduct (−40% Physical RES) lands at −30%, so the multiplier is 1−(−0.30/2) = 1.15. It was 0.90 before, so the real gain is about 27.8%, not the 40% the tooltip suggests.

That is what halved negative RES means: shred always helps, but each extra point past zero is worth less.

Real setup (physical team): Superconduct −40% Physical + Zhongli shield −20% universal → a 10% RES enemy drops to −50%, a 1.25x multiplier. That is why physical carries almost always run a Cryo/Electro applier plus Zhongli.

Enemy Base RES Cheat Sheet

  • Most common enemies: about 10% per element (e.g. standard Hilichurls).
  • Mitachurls: roughly 30% Physical RES but only ~10% elemental RES, which is why physical teams feel weak against them.
  • Abyss Heralds and Lectors: very high RES to their own element (often 50%+), and can go immune while shielded. Break the shield first.
  • Hypostases, slimes and floating spirits: immune or heavily resistant to their own element. Do not brute-force with the same element.
  • Primo Geovishaps lock in a random elemental RES at the start of the fight and lose it when staggered.
  • Spiral Abyss and event bosses layer bonus RES on top of base values, which is exactly why shred units spike in hard content.

Rule of thumb: the tankier a target feels relative to your sheet damage, the more you are losing to hidden resistance, and the more a single shredder is worth.

Practical advice:

  • Most teams need one strong shredder (Anemo VV for 40%, or Zhongli for 20%) and nothing more.
  • Mono-element carries: prioritize VV 4pc. A free 40% is the best value in the game.
  • Physical teams: Superconduct is near-mandatory; add Zhongli to reach −50%.
  • Reaction teams: transformative reactions ignore DEF but are still reduced by RES, so shred matters more.
  • Hard Abyss: enemies stack both DEF and RES, so combining both shred types has the highest value.

Common Pitfalls

  • Expecting shred to add its full tooltip. In negative RES only half counts, so −40% Physical becomes roughly 27.8% more damage.
  • Stacking shred on already-high RES. The formula is not linear, and each point matters more in the high-RES bracket.
  • Ignoring DEF Ignore. It multiplies with DEF Reduction, so combining both beats either alone.
  • Using the same element on immune enemies. Hypostases and floating spirits shrug it off; switch element or character.
  • Piling DEF shred without RES shred. Against high-RES targets in mono-element teams, RES shred usually wins.

Final Thoughts

Low damage is not always a build problem; often it is a defense and resistance tax. Same-level enemies cut you to ~16.7%, DEF shred caps at 90%, and negative RES only counts at half value. One VV 4pc or Zhongli covers most teams, physical teams add Superconduct, and reaction teams lean on RES shred. Understand these two boxes and the same stats suddenly hit very differently.
