Genshin Shield Breaking Guide: Counter Chart and Gauge Units

What actually stalls an Abyss run is rarely low damage — it is an enemy parked behind a shield. Shield breaking runs on element type and gauge units, not on your stats: a completely un-invested character breaks shields exactly as fast as a fully built main DPS. This guide covers the mechanics, the full counter chart, and the best breakers per element.

  • Does shield breaking need damage? No. Elemental shields, elemental armor and physical shields all ignore your stats — only the element type and gauge units matter, so a completely un-invested character breaks them just as fast.
  • How does elemental efficiency work? Three tiers: the countering element runs at 2x, the same element or a transformative reaction at 1x, and any other element at 0.5x.
  • Does Elemental Mastery help? No. EM does not affect shield-breaking speed, so never build EM for shields.
  • Which element for which shield? Hydro for Pyro (Vaporize), Pyro for Cryo (Melt), Cryo or Pyro or Dendro for Electro, Dendro or Cryo or Electro for Hydro, and Geo attacks or claymore hits for Geo.

The three-tier efficiency rule

Shield-breaking speed is governed by elemental efficiency, which comes in three tiers:

  • Countering element — 2x: the opposing element on the wheel, such as Hydro against Pyro (Vaporize) or Pyro against Cryo (Melt).
  • Same element or transformative reaction — 1x: same-attribute hits, Swirl, Crystallize.
  • Any other element — 0.5x: the slow fallback.

Against a Cryo Abyss Mage, a Pyro character therefore breaks the shield twice as fast as Electro or Geo.

Internal Cooldown: fast hits are not fast breaks

Elemental application runs on a hidden timer: most abilities apply their element only once every 2.5 seconds or every 3 hits. Fast Normal Attacks therefore do not necessarily break faster. Ways around it:

  • Swap characters — switching resets the ICD, so rotating through your team beats one character mashing.
  • Charged and Plunging Attacks — these have no ICD and apply directly.
  • High-gauge skills — Bennett’s tapped Skill applies 2U while the held version applies only 1U, so the tap is the better shield-breaker.

Practical tips

  1. Do not burn your Bursts on the shield — save them for the damage window once it is down.
  2. Cover at least two elements in your team so no single shield type walls you out.
  3. Kill Fatui and Abyss Mages first; their shields are the most disruptive.
  4. Shield breaking is pure mechanics, so un-invested characters are fine as long as they survive.

Bottom line

Shield breaking is a gauge-unit race, not a damage race. Learn the three efficiency tiers (2x / 1x / 0.5x), bring the right elements, and use swaps and Charged Attacks to bypass ICD — and Abyss shield enemies go from a slog to a free win. Under-geared? Bring a dedicated breaker anyway; their stats do not matter.
