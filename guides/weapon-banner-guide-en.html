Genshin Weapon Banner Guide: Epitomized Path, Fate Points & Pity

The Weapon Event Wish (Epitome Invocation) is the most expensive and most misunderstood banner in Genshin. There is no 50/50 like on the character banner — it runs a 75/25 split, hard pity drops from 90 to 80 pulls, and the thing that actually decides whether you get that one weapon is the Epitomized Path. Here is the whole system, cleanly.

Quick answer
  • Hard pity is 80 pulls (character banner: 90); base 5★ rate is 0.7%
  • Every 5★ is 75% one of the two featured weapons, 25% a standard weapon
  • Epitomized Path: chart a course first, then any non-chosen 5★ weapon grants 1 Fate Point; since 5.0, a single point guarantees your chosen weapon on the next 5★
  • Worst case 160 pulls = 25,600 Primogems (240 pulls before Version 5.0)
  • Wish pity carries between weapon banners; Fate Points do not — they reset on banner end, course change, or cancel
  • If you cannot reach 160 pulls, skip the banner

Weapon banner vs character banner

They are easy to confuse because both guarantee via accumulated wish count, but the rules are different. Side by side:

In short: every 5★ on the weapon banner is a 75/25, so you can land the other featured weapon or a standard one — and the Epitomized Path turns that “other featured weapon” into “the next 5★ is guaranteed.”

How Epitomized Path works

  1. Open the weapon banner and tap the Epitomized Path button (between the two featured weapons).
  2. Select the weapon you want and confirm. Without charting a course there is no guarantee at all and no Fate Points accumulate.
  3. Wish as normal: any 5★ that is not your chosen weapon grants 1 Fate Point.
  4. Holding 1 Fate Point, your next 5★ is 100% your chosen weapon.

Key nuance: only pulling the other featured weapon grants a Fate Point. Pulling a standard 5★ weapon from the 25% pool leaves your Fate Point count unchanged — it neither adds nor resets. Many players assume “any off-target gives a point”; only the featured off-target does.

Fate Points are not pity — the biggest mix-up

These are two separate counters:

  • Wish pity: pulls since your last 5★. Carries across banners, never resets on a banner change.
  • Fate Points: valid only on the current banner, used to lock which weapon. They reset the moment the banner ends.

That is how you end up with “pity intact, Fate Points gone”: you bank 1 Fate Point on banner A but run out of pulls, the banner rotates, the point is wiped, yet your accumulated pity stays. It is the weapon banner’s biggest trap — banking a Fate Point you can’t finish is a straight loss.

Pull or skip?

Reasonable to pull

  • You have 160+ pulls saved and this is the weapon you want;
  • You already hold 1 Fate Point and the banner is still live — finishing is usually correct;
  • The signature weapon is a real upgrade for your main DPS (passive/main stat fit).

Better to skip

  • You cannot cover the full 160 pulls — stopping halfway wastes the Fate Point;
  • New F2P account: use crafted / Battle Pass / event 4★ weapons and put resources into characters;
  • “Just a couple of pulls” — soft pity starts around 62, so a 5★ can drop unexpectedly.

Rule of thumb: a signature weapon is typically a single-digit to low-teens percentage total damage gain over a free 4★, at roughly the cost of a full character. Characters always come before weapons.

Checking your pity and Fate Points

  • In game: Wish → History, filter by banner and count manually (no built-in counter).
  • Third party: paimon.moe and similar can import your history and track pity and Fate Points.
  • The Epitomized Path screen shows your current Fate Point count directly.
