Keqing Build & Teams: Electro Heavy-Attack Carry & Aggravate Core

Quick Answer

  • Keqing is a 5★ Electro sword. Her burst is her Charged Attack "Yuheng Swordplay"; core play = on-field Aggravate triggers.
  • Core set: Thundering Fury 4pc (+20% Aggravate, +40% Overload/Charged/Superconduct, E CD −1s).
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / EM (Aggravate teams), Electro Cup, Crit Rate/DMG Circlet.
  • Best teams: Keqing + Fischl + Nahida + Zhongli (Aggravate); Keqing + Fischl + Kazuha/Kokomi (pure Electro).
  • C6 is the game-changer: every attack hit adds +6% Electro DMG (stacks infinitely).

How Keqing Works

Keqing's output centers on her Charged Attacks:

  • Skill dash + Charged Attack = rapid multi-hit Electro DMG with enhanced "Swordplay" phases.
  • Each Charged hit on a Quicken'd target triggers Aggravate: +1.15× Level Multiplier (L90 = 1664 flat DMG).
  • Higher attack frequency = more Aggravate triggers. Keqing is one of the best Aggravate carries.

Team Comps

Aggravate Team (optimal)

Keqing (DPS) + Fischl (off-field Electro) + Nahida (Dendro/EM) + Zhongli (shield/shred)

  • Fischl's Oz applies Electro + Nahida applies Dendro → target stays Quicken'd.
  • Keqing's Charged hits on the Quicken target trigger Aggravate throughout.
  • Zhongli's shield keeps her safe on-field; C6 shred amplifies the whole rotation.

Pure Electro Team

Keqing + Fischl + Kujou Sara (C6: team Electro Crit DMG) + Kokomi (ER + healing)

  • Electro Resonance: Electro reactions drop Electro particles → fast ER.
  • Sara's C6 gives team-wide Electro Crit DMG; Keqing's Charged attacks cash in.

Constellation Priority

  • C6: Every Normal/Charged/Skill hit adds +6% Electro DMG (stacks infinitely) — the game-changer, top priority.
  • C4: After triggering an Electro reaction, ATK +25% — very strong in Aggravate teams.

FAQ

Is Keqing still viable now?

Yes. Aggravate brings her back to strength — the flat DMG bonus plus her high Charged frequency make her a strong F2P/low-cost Electro carry. Ceiling is lower than Raiden/Yae, but she's far easier to build.

Kazuha or Sucrose with Keqing?

Kazuha is better (shred + grouping) but rarer. Sucrose is the F2P equivalent — her 4pc Viridescent Venerer shred is the same, and she adds Swirl + particle generation.

Keqing on-field or off-field?

Strictly on-field. She relies on Charged attacks, so off-field use is pointless. Build the rest of the team to enable her on-field window.

Does Keqing need Energy Recharge?

Her Skill CD is 12s; Thundering Fury 4pc reduces it by 1s per reaction. Prioritize Crits; add ER from substats only as needed.

No Mistsplitter — what weapon?

Skyblade (Crit DMG) > Royal Longsword > 4★ Lion's Roar (DMG buff). F2P: forge Royal Longsword or use event weapons.
