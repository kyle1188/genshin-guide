Genshin Columbina Build: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: Lunar Reaction enabler/support, scales with HP and Elemental Mastery.
  • Best Weapon: Nocturne's Curtain Call (signature); 2nd choice is Reliquary of Truth.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Silken Moon's Serenade (best) or 4pc Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (if Silken occupied).
  • Main Stats: ER% Sands / HP% Goblet / CRIT circlet.
  • Key stat: Aim for 30,000~35,000 HP to max out her buff caps; ER 160~180% for consistent burst.

Character Overview

Columbina is a 5-star Hydro catalyst Sub-DPS and support from Natlan. She is the core enabler of Lunar Reaction teams, with her passive Gravity Ripple significantly boosting Lunar DMG for the entire party. Both her damage and her support scaling scale off HP, making HP stacking the number one priority.

Playstyle Tips

  • Prioritize reaching the 30,000+ HP threshold before optimizing other stats
  • Use E skill first to apply Hydro aura for the on-field DPS
  • Follow up with Burst to trigger team-wide Lunar buff
  • When switching off-field with Silken Moon equipped, Gleaming Moon effect activates automatically
