Which Genshin Characters Should Beginners Build First? A F2P Must-Build List & Team Guide

Just landed in Teyvat with resin and Mora tighter than your schedule? This guide gives new and free-to-play players a proven list of must-build 4-star characters, with how to obtain them, budget builds, and one universal team that carries you for a long time. Build these and your early game basically can't go wrong.

Note: character strength shifts slightly from patch to patch, but the 4-stars below have been the most universally useful "F2P generals" since launch — usable at C0, easy to obtain, and a far better value than most standard 5-stars. Newer limited 5-stars are stronger but harder to pull and are out of scope here.

Two Beginner Myths to Drop First

Myth 1: 5-stars are always stronger. Plenty of 4-stars outclass many standard 5-stars in both utility and damage, and full-4-star teams clearing Spiral Abyss Floor 12 are common. Don't quit just because you didn't pull a limited 5-star.

Myth 2: The Traveler is useless. Anemo Traveler's E groups enemies and is insanely efficient for early farming; Geo Traveler builds bridges and reaches chests. Leveling them to Ascension 70 is absolutely worth it. Don't bench the Traveler early.

Must-Build F2P 4-Stars (by Role)

These characters are cheap to obtain and fight-ready at C0 — the core you should prioritize first. Each lists its role, why it's worth building, how to get it, and a "budget build" (a low-cost setup that works without perfect gear).

PyroBennett

All-round buffer + healer

Nicknamed the "6-star" unit. His burst grants whole-team ATK and healing, and slots into almost any team. C0 already gives the full ATK buff; C1 removing the HP cap is a huge spike.

Get: standard/event banners, Stardust shop · Budget: Favonius Sword + Noblesse Oblige 4pc (ER/HP)

PyroXiangling

Off-field Pyro sub-DPS

Her burst "Pyronado" deals off-field, high-frequency Pyro with top-tier scalings — the core of Vaporize/Melt teams. Given free for clearing Abyss 3-3; extremely cheap to build.

Get: Abyss 3-3 (free) · Budget: The Catch (fishing) + Emblem of Severed Fate 4pc (ER/crit)

HydroXingqiu

Back-row Hydro enabler

Rain Sword gives DMG reduction, interruption resistance, and steady Hydro application; his coordinated bursts make him key to Vape and Freeze teams. Usable at C0, useful for life.

Get: Lantern Rite choose-one, banners · Budget: Sacrificial Sword + Emblem of Severed Fate 4pc (ER ≥ 180%)

ElectroFischl

Off-field Electro sub-DPS

Oz applies Electro off-field with solid coordinated damage at C0; great in Overload, Electro-Charged, and Aggravate teams. Frequently requested across events and comps.

Get: standard/event banners · Budget: The Stringless / Alley Hunter + Thundering Fury 4pc or TF 2pc + ATK 2pc

AnemoSucrose

Crowd control + team EM

"Mini-Kazuha." Her burst groups enemies and grants whole-team Elemental Mastery; core of Swirl teams. C6 adds an elemental DMG bonus. Cheap and stable output.

Get: standard/event banners · Budget: Sacrificial Fragments + Viridescent Venerer 4pc (EM/ER)

DendroKuki Shinobu

Hyperbloom trigger + healer

Her E continuously applies Electro to detonate Dendro cores; damage scales only with EM and level, so she's dirt cheap to build — the core of Bloom teams.

Get: event banners · Budget: Iron Sting + Gilded Dreams 4pc / Deepwood Memories 4pc (EM ≥ 900)

GeoNoelle

Shield + self-heal (beginner-banner guarantee)

Guaranteed from the beginner banner. A thick shield and self-heal mean you won't die inside it; built up she can even be a main DPS. Great early-game experience.

Get: beginner banner guarantee · Budget: Whiteblind (craft) + Husk of Opulent Dreams 4pc (DEF)

HydroBarbara

Early-game healer

Gifted from early quests and easy to C6; whole-team sustained healing. One E refills HP, saving tons of food resources while you're still learning.

Get: story quest · Budget: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers + Maiden Beloved 4pc / Ocean-Hued Clam 4pc (HP)

How to Pick Your Main DPS

Your main DPS holds the field and deals damage. Before you pull a limited 5-star DPS, these are comfortable transition picks:

  • Ningguang (Geo): a very comfy Geo main DPS for the early game; at C6 she rivals 5-stars and conveniently mines ore for you. A crit catalyst like Solar Pearl works fine.
  • Razor (Physical): the most hassle-free physical main DPS for the early game; slap on a physical build and he clears the map. Great transition option if you have no 5-star DPS.
  • A pulled limited DPS: if you're lucky enough to get the current banner DPS, build it first — it's usually stronger than the two above.

One Universal Team: National Team Variant

Combine the units above and you get the low-cost, high-power "National Team" idea. The two most useful versions for beginners:

SlotUnitRole
Buffer / HealBennettBurst ATK buff + heal, double Pyro resonance DMG
HydroXingqiuSteady off-field Hydro, Vaporize with Pyro DPS
Off-field PyroXianglingPyronado off-field Pyro, Vaporize core DPS
On-field DPS / ShieldNingguang / Razor / NoelleOn-field DPS — use whoever's handy early

This comp clears most world bosses and world quests early on; once you pull a stronger DPS, just swap the on-field slot. The supports (Bennett / Xingqiu / Xiangling) stay useful all the way to late game.

Resource priority: level Bennett, Xingqiu, and Xiangling to 80 and max their bursts (Q) first, then level your main DPS to 80. These three are "universal support/sub-DPS" with the highest return on investment; keep the Traveler, Noelle, and Barbara at Ascension 70. When talent materials are tight, prioritize Q on supports and skip normal attacks.

Most Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Hoarding resin waiting for a "perfect character" and still unable to beat a world boss after two weeks — early resin should go to character ascensions and key talents.
  • Looking down on 4-stars and running a team of unbuilt 5-stars that can't deal damage — build the 4-stars above first.
  • Spreading resources across many characters so none are maxed — focusing to form one complete team is far more efficient than raising ten halfway.

Summary

New and F2P players, remember one line: max Bennett, Xingqiu, and Xiangling first; don't bench the Traveler and Noelle; use Ningguang / Razor as your DPS transition. This setup gives a smooth early game, a workable Abyss mid-game, and supports that keep working late-game — maximum value. After that, once you understand teambuilding and save up for a limited character you like, fill in the rest slowly.
