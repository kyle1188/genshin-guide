Best 4-Star Characters in Genshin 2026: The Core Four + System Pillars

4-stars don't need hard pity — they show up in the standard banner, events and shops, and can hit C6 cheaply. Build these first and even a free-to-play account clears the Abyss and open world.

4-stars are free: they come from the standard banner, events and shops, and you can crown them at C6 for almost nothing. They are the foundation of any account — build these first.

Where to Spend Resources

Order: Bennett (ATK buff + heal, top priority) → Xingqiu / Xiangling off-field sub-DPS → Fischl → then the system pillars. Push the Core Four to key constellations (Bennett C1, Xiangling C4, Fischl C6 is a leap). Supports don't need crit — stack their core stat (ER, EM, HP).

Common Mistakes

  • Leveling every talent: some 4-star supports scale off an innate passive; their combat talents at level 1 are enough (e.g. Chevreuse) — don't waste talent books.
  • Main-DPS-first trap: in early game, off-field sub-DPS / supports (Bennett, Xingqiu) pay off more than forcing a 4-star main DPS.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge: Xiangling and Xingqiu live on their bursts; low ER breaks the rotation and tanks damage.

Quick Answers

Which four 4-stars are must-builds?

Bennett, Xingqiu, Xiangling and Fischl. Strong at C0 and universally useful — the foundation of every team.

Can you clear the Abyss without 5-stars?

Yes. The Core Four plus system pillars form National, Aggravate and Hyperbloom teams that fully clear endgame on a free-to-play account.

Which 4-star main DPS is worth it?

Razor (early-game on-fielder) and Yanfei (Pyro catalyst) work, but they rank below off-field sub-DPS and supports in priority.

Where should a beginner spend resources first?

Bennett first (ATK buff + heal), then off-field sub-DPS like Xingqiu and Xiangling.
