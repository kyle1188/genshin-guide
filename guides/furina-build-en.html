Genshin Furina Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Furina — the Hydro Archon of Fontaine — is widely regarded as the most account-defining 5-star since Kazuha. Her Elemental Burst hands the entire team up to +75% damage, she applies Hydro off-field, and she deals real damage herself. This guide covers how to build her: weapons, the Golden Troupe artifact set, stat targets, team comps, and the HP-fluctuation mechanic that ties it all together.

Note: Furina's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta knowledge and do not change with patches. Constellation and banner availability shift over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Furina Is Worth Building

Furina's value comes from four layered effects that stack into one of the strongest buffs in the game:

  • Universal team damage: her Burst grants up to +75% team-wide DMG (capped at 250 Fanfare stacks) for ~18s of a 30s window — and it is multiplicative with ATK buffs, VV shred, and reaction multipliers.
  • Reliable off-field Hydro: her Salon Members apply Hydro every ~2.5s, enabling Vaporize, Freeze, and Bloom teams without needing the on-field character to Normal Attack.
  • Self-sustaining HP mechanic: with a healer on the team, her HP-draining Skill becomes a fast fanfare engine instead of a liability.
  • Her own damage: the summoned Salon Members scale off her Max HP, so she is never "just a buffer."
In a Neuvillette Hypercarry team, Furina alone adds roughly +50% team DPS compared to any other support in her slot. That is why she is the #1 pull on most meta wishlists.

Best Weapons

Furina scales off Max HP, CRIT, and Energy Recharge — her weapon choice is unusually flexible, and the F2P option reaches ~85% of her signature's value.

Signature · highest ceiling

Her tailor-made weapon: 88.2% CRIT DMG plus Skill DMG and Max HP boosts that mirror her kit. The clear best-in-slot, but pulling the weapon banner just for her is generally not recommended — the gap to F2P is only ~15%.

Obtain: Furina weapon banner

CRIT Rate + HP-based ATK

High CRIT Rate and an ATK bonus scaled off HP — great if you already own it. Simplifies substat hunting.

Obtain: standard banner / Chronicled

F2P ★Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Top free-to-play pick

From Fontaine fishing. Gives Energy Recharge and Skill CRIT Rate — solves both uptime and crit needs at zero cost. The default recommendation for a F2P Furina.

Obtain: Fontaine Fishing Association

F2PFavonius Sword / Wolf-Fang

ER battery / Battle Pass

Favonius Sword generates particles for team burst uptime (build some CRIT Rate to proc it). Wolf-Fang (Battle Pass) gives CRIT Rate plus Skill/Burst DMG and stacks off-field.

Obtain: all banners / Gnostic Hymn

BudgetHarbinger of Dawn / Dockhand's Assistant

3★ / craftable fill-ins

Harbinger of Dawn (3★) gives big CRIT DMG and a CRIT passive if HP stays above 90% — strong on a shielded team. The Dockhand's Assistant (craftable, R5) supplies HP% and ER on heal ticks.

Obtain: chests / blacksmith

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Normal Attack barely matters:

  1. Elemental Burst (Let the People Rejoice) → 9 or Crown. The Fanfare buff scales with talent level; this is where her value lives.
  2. Elemental Skill (Salon Solitaire) → 8–9. The Salon Members deal damage and generate the HP fluctuations that feed fanfare.
  3. Normal Attack → 1. Ignore unless you run a C6 on-field build.

How the HP-Fluctuation Engine Works

Furina's kit is unique: her Burst records every HP gain or loss across the team and turns it into damage. Master this and she out-values any other support.

  • Cast Skill (Ousia): three Salon Members drain party HP over time and deal off-field Hydro. Swap to Pneuma (via Charged Attack) for a stationary healer instead.
  • Cast Burst: activates Universal Revelry for 30s. Every HP change (up or down) adds Fanfare stacks → +0.15% DMG per stack, capped at +75% (250 stacks).
  • The rotation: Furina E → Q → swap to healer (HP recovers, generating stacks) → swap to DPS and unload during the peak fanfare window. A healer makes stacks climb at maximum rate.
Key rule: without a healer, your team's HP just drops and fanfare stalls. Always bring a healer — that is what turns her "downside" into her biggest upside.

Common Mistakes

  • No healer on the team. Her HP drain never recovers, fanfare never stacks, and you lose most of her buff. The #1 error.
  • Building her like a normal DPS. She wants HP% and ER, not raw ATK. Ignoring HP leaves both her damage and healing weak.
  • Skipping Energy Recharge. A dead Burst = no team buff. Aim for 130–180% ER so it's up every rotation.
  • Using her as a pure healer. Her buff needs HP fluctuation, not a static full bar. Pure-heal playstyle wastes her kit.
  • Pulling her signature weapon on impulse. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman already gets ~85% of the value. Spend those primogems on constellations or other units.

Don't Have Furina Yet?

She is a limited 5-star, so you may not own her. For off-field Hydro application, Xingqiu and Yelan (both on the beginner must-build list) cover most teams — they just don't bring her team-wide damage buff. Build them while you wait for her banner.
