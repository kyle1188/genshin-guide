Genshin Baizhu Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 6.7 Phase 2 (live through Aug 11, 2026)

This is the last banner window before 7.0 Snezhnaya (drops Aug 12) — Raiden Shogun and Columbina are rerunning now. If you are rounding out a roster that survives the endgame, the evergreen Dendro core is Nahida + Baizhu: Baizhu is the shield-and-heal engine that lets Nahida's Bloom and Aggravate teams run without a single swap. Build him right — enough HP to shield, enough ER to Burst on cooldown, and the Deepwood set — and he anchors your Dendro teams for years. This guide covers exactly that.

Baizhu — the walking pharmacy of Liyue — is the game's most comfortable Dendro healer and shielder. His Skill drops a shield that follows your active character and heals on a timer; his Burst fires coordinated Dendro attacks that also top everyone up. He needs no field time, which makes him the easiest sustain to slot into Bloom, Hyperbloom, and Aggravate cores. This guide covers his best weapon (Jadefall's Splendor vs Prototype Amber / Thrilling Tales), the 4pc Deepwood Memories set, the ~130% Energy Recharge target, why C2 is the real upgrade, and teams with Nahida / Xingqiu / Raiden / Bennett.

Note: Baizhu's kit, his Deepwood preference, and the ~130% ER target are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 6.7 banner window above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of banner.

Why Baizhu Is Worth Building

Among Dendro supports, only Baizhu gives you shield + heal in one passive package with zero on-field time. If you run Nahida teams, he is the default comfort pick.

  • Universal shield + heal: his Skill (Universal Medicine) summons a Seamless Shield that trails the active character and heals whenever it pulses — protection against chip damage and corrosion in the Abyss.
  • No field time needed: everything he does is off-field, so he never steals seconds from your carry's rotation. Drop him in and forget him.
  • Dendro enabler: his Burst applies Dendro through coordinated attacks, feeding Bloom / Hyperbloom / Aggravate / Spread without a swap.
  • Team-wide HP scaling: at C2 his healing scales with max HP and his Burst also buffs the team, making him a quiet force multiplier.
A correctly built Baizhu turns a squishy Nahida Bloom team into a self-sustaining engine. For Dendro cores, he is the lowest-effort sustain in the game.

Best Weapons

Baizhu wants HP% (for shield/heal strength) and likes Energy Recharge if his weapon does not provide it. He uses a catalyst.

BiS ★Jadefall's Splendor

Best-in-slot · 5★ catalyst (his sig)

His signature: HP% main stat plus a passive that grants ER after using his Skill and ramps his own damage. The cleanest "equip and forget" option — solves ER and scaling at once.

Obtain: weapon banner

4★Prototype Amber

Craftable ER + heal tool

Free from the forge: its passive returns energy and heals the team when Baizhu uses his Burst, which neatly solves his ER and adds a second heal layer. The best f2p weapon for him by a mile.

Obtain: forge (Coral branches)

3★Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers

Budget buffer pick

TTDS (R5) grants a big ATK% buff to the next character after Baizhu swaps — strong if you only use him as a pure enabler and want to boost your carry. No ER, so watch his recharge.

Obtain: wishes (cheap)

AltHakushin Ring / Sacrificial Fragments

Reaction-focused options

Hakushin Ring boosts Electro/Dendro DMG for Aggravate/Quicken teams; Sacrificial Fragments gives EM for Bloom/Spread. Use these only when your team is a specific reaction shell and you already have ER covered.

Obtain: forge / wishes

Stat Targets — HP First, Then ER

Baizhu's shield and heal scale on max HP, so HP% dominates his gear. ER is the only other thing to watch: hit the target, then pile on HP%.

Best Team Comps

Baizhu's teams share one rule: pair him with a Dendro driver (Nahida) and a reaction trigger, then let the cores do the work.

Nahida + Baizhu + Xingqiu (Hydro) + flex (Kuki/Raiden for Hyperbloom). Baizhu shields the team from Bloom self-damage and keeps everyone alive while Nahida triggers the cores. The flagship Dendro team.

Best all-around Dendro shell

Talent Priority

Level in this order — his Skill drives the shield/heal, his Burst the Dendro + heal:

  1. Elemental Skill (Universal Medicine) → 8–13. The Seamless Shield strength and heal scale here. Crown this first if you can.
  2. Elemental Burst (Holistic Revivification) → 8–13. The coordinated Dendro attacks and the team heal live here. Level it right after the Skill.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. He barely attacks outside meme quickswaps. Leave it low.

Constellations — C2 Is the Real Upgrade

Baizhu's constellation curve is front-loaded: one constellation makes him noticeably better, then it is smooth sailing.

  • C1 (The Curve of Health): his coordinated attacks fire one extra time and the initial shield is stronger. Nice, not essential.
  • C2 (The Shackles of Life): healing scales with his max HP and his Burst also buffs the team — this is the big one. C2 roughly doubles his value as both a sustain and a quiet buffer. Worth chasing if you main Dendro.
  • C4: extends the Seamless Shield duration and adds a small coordinated-attack DMG bonus. A clear step up.
  • C6 (At What Cost): the character buffed by his Burst deals extra Dendro DMG — caps his carry-support value. Luxury, not a requirement.
Key rule: C0 is already a top-tier sustain; get C2 and stop. C2 is where Baizhu goes from "good healer" to "best Dendro enabler in the game." C6 is a luxury.

Common Mistakes

  • Too little Energy Recharge. The #1 Baizhu killer. Sub-120% ER without an ER weapon means a dead Burst — no coordinated Dendro, no team heal, and Bloom teams lose their trigger.
  • Building him as a damage dealer. His job is shield + heal + off-field Dendro. Stacking raw DMG/CRIT on him wastes the slot — HP% and ER win.
  • Wrong artifact set. Random 2pc+2pc without Deepwood leaves Dendro RES shred and HP% on the table. Deepwood 4pc is the default for a reason.
  • Skipping him for a pure healer. Barbara / Qiqi heal more raw numbers but give no shield and need field time. Baizhu's shield + zero-swap convenience is why Nahida teams prefer him.

Don't Have Baizhu Yet?

He is a limited 5★, so you pull him on a rerun — and the 6.7 Phase 2 window is your last chance before 7.0 Snezhnaya reshapes the banner calendar. While you wait, Yaoyao or Kirara can supply off-field Dendro + heal in a pinch, and Nahida alone can still drive Bloom — but neither gives Baizhu's Seamless Shield plus his no-swap convenience. Build him alongside Nahida and Xingqiu; together they are the Dendro core that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they are the safest evergreen pickup before the 7.0 shift.
