Genshin Chest Guide: All Types, Rewards and Respawn Rules

Chests are the steadiest source of Primogems, Mora and artifacts in Genshin Impact — and “do chests respawn” is the single most common misconception among new players. This guide covers all five chest tiers, the exact reward table, the region-scaling rule most people miss, and the tools that make hunting efficient.

  • Do chests respawn? No. Opened chests never come back and there is no respawn cycle. More chests only appear when a version adds a new region or a limited event.
  • How many types are there? Five tiers: Common, Exquisite, Precious, Luxurious and Remarkable, plus Shrine of Depths, Primal Obelisk and event chests.
  • Which is worth the most? Luxurious chests give 10 Primogems; Shrine of Depths chests give 40 each (80 in regions released since 5.0).
  • Does World Level affect rewards? No. Only the chest tier and the region it sits in matter, so holding your World Level to farm chests does nothing.

Remarkable chests are a special case: they only drop Serenitea Pot furnishing blueprints. In Tsurumi Island that is the sole reward; in other regions they also give 5 Primogems. No Mora, no sigils.

Region scaling: the same chest can pay double

This is the mechanic most players never notice. Primogems from Common, Exquisite and Precious chests depend on where the chest is:

  • Lowest bracket: low Zone Level areas of Mondstadt and Liyue — 0 / 2 / 5 Primogems.
  • Highest bracket: Dragonspine and Stormbearer Mountains, Guyun Stone Forest, Lisha and The Chasm in Liyue, plus every nation other than Mondstadt and Liyue — 2 / 5 / 10 Primogems.

In practice a Precious chest is worth 10 Primogems in Fontaine and only 5 in the Mondstadt starter zone. Early on, do not grind the low-bracket Mondstadt and Liyue chests — push into newer regions instead.

What actually drops from chests

Beyond Primogems and Mora, chests contain:

  • Sigils — Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro and Pyro Sigils, used in each region offering system.
  • EXP and enhancement materials — Character EXP, weapon enhancement ore and 2–3★ talent books.
  • Weapons — 1–2★ from Common and Exquisite, 3★ from Exquisite and above.
  • Artifacts — 1–3★, always from one of six fixed sets: Adventurer, Traveling Doctor, Lucky Dog, Berserker, Instructor, The Exile.

One hard rule: chest rewards do not scale with World Level. Open them as you find them.

Buried chests usually have a tell — a shovel, a pile of rocks, a signboard, or Treasure Hoarders loitering nearby. Walking a Seelie back to its court also always spawns a chest.

Tools that find chests for you

  • Treasure Compass — blueprints come from reputation: Reputation 6 in Mondstadt and Liyue, Reputation 9 in Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine, and max reputation with three tribes in Natlan.
  • Compasses have limits: the Anemo Compass does not work in Dragonspine, and each compass only covers its own region.
  • Interactive maps — community maps mark the vast majority of chest spawns, ideal for mopping up.

Chests and 100% exploration

Exploration percentage does not mean "open every chest". It accumulates from chests, oculi, time trials, puzzles, Seelies and regional quests, so you can usually hit 100% without opening every chest. Chest counts are tracked separately by region in the Achievements tab.

A sensible farming order

  1. Do world quests first — many Precious and Luxurious chests are quest rewards you get for free.
  2. Then puzzles and time trials — the highest yield per minute.
  3. Finally use a Treasure Compass plus an interactive map to sweep scattered world spawns.
  4. Prioritise Shrines of Depths: 40 Primogems each (80 in newer regions) is the best chest value in the game.

Bottom line

Three rules cover almost everything: chests never respawn, rewards ignore World Level, and Primogem value depends on the region. Rather than re-sweeping the Mondstadt starter zone, push world quests and newer regions, then mop up with a Treasure Compass — that is the order that maximises chest income.
