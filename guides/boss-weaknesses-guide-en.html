Complete Boss Weakness Guide: Elemental Counters, Shield Breaking & Team Comp

Every boss has unique elemental weaknesses and shield mechanics. Understanding these helps you pick the right team and farm materials efficiently.

  • What is Electro weak to? Pyro (Overload) and Cryo (Superconduct). Geo only triggers Crystallize, which leaves the Electro Hypostasis prisms standing.
  • Which bosses have no elemental weakness? Anemo Hypostasis (immune to Anemo, 10% to everything else), Maguu Kenki (a flat 10% to all elements) and Andrius (immune to Cryo and Anemo). Against these you just need your strongest team.
  • How do I break a boss shield? Match the counter element instead of forcing raw damage: a Cryo shield melts to Pyro, a Pyro shield dies to Hydro, and Geo shields need a Claymore, a plunge attack or Geo damage.
  • Which boss drops talent materials? Only weekly bosses drop talent Lv.7+ materials, at 30 Resin for the first three clears each week and 60 after. World bosses drop character ascension materials instead.
  • How do I survive a Hypostasis revival phase? Stop hitting the shell. Knock the Cryo Frostfruits back with Charged Attacks, snuff the Pyro Fire Seeds with Hydro, and break the Electro prisms with any non-Electro hit.

What Is Electro Weak To in Genshin Impact?

Pyro and Cryo. The Electro Hypostasis is immune to Electro itself, and the two elements that hurt it most are Pyro (Overload) and Cryo (Superconduct). In its revival phase it splits into three prisms - any non-Electro damage destroys them, while Geo only triggers Crystallize and leaves them standing.

That answer is boss-specific, not a global rule. Genshin has no element-versus-element weakness table: every boss carries its own resistance profile plus a mechanic that decides how fast it dies. The one universal rule is that elemental life forms are immune to their own element - Electro Hypostasis to Electro, Cryo Regisvine to Cryo, and so on.

Boss Types and What They Drop

Mixing these three up is the most expensive resin mistake in the game - they share almost no drops.

One-line rule: world bosses ascend characters, weekly bosses level talents past 6, domains ascend weapons. A Hypostasis never drops talent books, and a weekly boss does not hand out ascension gems as its main reward.

Shield Breaking Rules

Shields are not one mechanic. There are four families and each answers to a different input.

ShieldBreak it withWaste of time
Elemental shield (Abyss Mage, Herald)Its counter element - a Cryo shield melts to Pyro, a Pyro shield dies to HydroPhysical hits, wrong element
Geo shield or constructsClaymore, plunge attacks, Geo damageSingle-target bow spam
Hypostasis shellThe revival trick: Frostfruits, Fire Seeds, prisms, Anemo sigilsRaw DPS on the shell
Azhdaha's absorbed shellFast multi-hit elemental application plus reactionsSlow single big hits, or the absorbed element

Important: Geo never breaks an Electro prism - it only crystalizes. The same trap appears on every non-Geo shield: bringing a Claymore is not a universal answer.

Which Boss Should You Farm?

  • Weekly bosses first, but only the three discounted claims. Each week the first three claims cost 30 Resin instead of 60. Spend all three on the boss your carry needs.
  • World bosses only for a character you are ascending today. 40 Resin per run, 2-3 boss cores per claim, and roughly 46 cores for a full ascension chain.
  • Dream Solvent fixes a bad weekly roll. One solvent converts one weekly material into another from the same boss - use it when RNG keeps handing you the wrong piece.
  • Never farm a boss "for later". Boss materials are character-bound; unclaimed cores sit in your bag doing nothing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a global elemental weakness chart in Genshin Impact?

No. Genshin has no element-versus-element weakness table. Each boss has its own resistances plus a mechanic that decides how fast it dies. The only universal rule is that elemental life forms are immune to their own element.

Why does my attack do zero damage to a boss?

Either the boss is immune to that element or it is still shielded. The Pyro Hypostasis is immune to Pyro and opens with a shield, and the Electro Hypostasis is immune to Electro - only its prisms break to non-Electro damage.

Does Geo break Electro shields?

No. Geo only triggers Crystallize on an Electro prism and leaves it standing. Any non-Electro elemental hit destroys it.

How many times should I farm a weekly boss?

Claim the three discounted runs (30 Resin each) on the boss your character needs, then stop. Extra runs cost 60 Resin and only pay off when you are converting drops with Dream Solvent.
