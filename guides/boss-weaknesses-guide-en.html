Shield Breaking Mechanics

Different colored shields require specific elements to break:

- Physical shield: Heavy attack or elemental attacks

- Elemental shield: Counter-element breaks fast

- Geo shield (Azhdaha): Dendro or Electro pierces through

- Electro shield (Electro Hypostasis): Geo is most effective

- Pyro shield (Pyro Hypostasis): Hydro is most effective

Weekly Boss Priority for F2P

With limited resin, prioritize these weekly bosses:

1. Azhdaha — Geo character ascension

2. Wolf of the North — Cryo character ascension

3. Cryo Hypostasis — Cryo talent books

4. Pyro Hypostasis — Pyro talent books

5. Maguu Kenki — Electro talent books

6. Hypostases — Pick based on current needs

FAQ

Q: Why does my elemental attack do zero damage to the boss?

A: The boss may be immune (e.g., Pyro tree's ignition phase). Watch for immunity states and use counter-elements to break shields first.

Q: Not enough weekly boss materials?

A: Focus on the boss drops for the character you're currently leveling. Don't spread resin too thin.

Q: What's the difference between World Bosses and Weekly Bosses?

A: World Bosses are infinite challenges dropping talent/weapon mats. Weekly Bosses have weekly limits and drop character ascension materials.
