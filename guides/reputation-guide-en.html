Genshin Reputation System Guide: Unlock, Weekly Cap & Best Rewards

Reputation controls some of Genshin's most practical tool blueprints — Condensed Resin, the Portable Waypoint, treasure compasses, the Wind Catcher. This guide covers unlock requirements, the four EXP sources, how to spend the shared weekly cap, and which rewards to prioritize.

  • Unlock? Adventure Rank 25 + the region's prerequisite quests, then talk to the local NPC (Hertha, Ms. Yu...).
  • Weekly intake? 3 bounties + 3 requests shared across all regions, refreshed Monday 04:00; requests pay 80 EXP + 20k Mora each.
  • First goal? Liyue reputation 3 for the Condensed Resin blueprint — double your resin efficiency.
  • Wind gliders? Max reputation in each region (8 in Mondstadt/Liyue, 10 in Inazuma/Sumeru/Fontaine).

How to Unlock Reputation

City Reputation is a progression system tied to each nation. The baseline is Adventure Rank 25 plus the region's prerequisite quests — then talk to the local Reputation NPC:

Four Sources of Reputation EXP

  • Regional quests: Archon quest acts give 200 EXP each; world quests give 100. Your main early-game source — just play the story.
  • World exploration: claim 200 EXP + 20,000 Mora at 20% / 40% / 60% exploration in each region. Chests, Oculi and Statue offerings all count.
  • Bounties (Rep level 2): accept from the NPC, track the target with Elemental Sight, defeat the elite. Up to 3 per week before your region maxes.
  • Requests (Rep level 2): deliver the requested items or clear a camp — 80 EXP + 20,000 Mora each.

The weekly cap is the whole game: bounties and requests are each limited to 3 per week and shared across every region, refreshing Monday 04:00. Spend those 3+3 slots on the region you are pushing.

A 3-Minute Weekly Routine

  • After Monday reset, grab 3 bounties (re-roll to the highest tier) and 3 requests from your target NPC.
  • Use Elemental Sight to track bounties fast; deliver materials for requests instead of fighting camps.
  • Check exploration milestones — claim the 20/40/60% EXP + Mora whenever you hit one.

Summary

Reputation is a toolbox, not a stat. Grab Condensed Resin (Liyue 3) and the Portable Waypoint (Mondstadt 6) early, then feed your main region with the weekly 3+3 cap until the glider. Put it in your weekly routine and it pays forever.
