Genshin All Character Birthdays & Ages: Complete List

This page lists Genshin character birthdays and official ages, grouped by month. Log in on a character's birthday to collect 20 Primogems plus a gift-preference hint via mail. Most characters have no confirmed age — only the Archons and Adepti do (Zhongli is 6,000+ years old).

Quick Answer

  • How to check: Browse by month or search character name.
  • Birthday rewards: Login on their birthday to receive 20 Primogems + gift preference list via mail.
  • Age notes: Most characters have no official age—only gods and adepti have confirmed ages.
  • Traveler: You can select your own birthday date.
  • Special case: Bennett's birthday is Feb 29—rewards sent Feb 28 in non-leap years.

How to Check Character Birthdays

View each character's profile page to see their birthday. On their birthday, log in to receive 20 Primogems and a special gift notification via mail.

Traveler Birthday

The Traveler's birthday is selected during account creation. The selection window resets annually on September 28 at 00:00 server time. On your birthday, you receive a special cake mail containing 2 Fragile Resin and a collectible cake.

Birthday Reward Rules

  • Login on the character's birthday to receive rewards via mail
  • Reward content: 20 Primogems + character-specific gift preference hint
  • Rewards are only available on the birthday itself—no makeups for missing it
  • Some characters have special animation or mail styling for birthday messages
