Genshin Eula Build & Teams: 5-Star Cryo Claymore & Physical DPS Lightfall Sword

Quick Answer

  • Eula is a 5★ Cryo claymore (Mondstadt, Captain of the Favonius Reconnaissance), an on-field Physical DPS; her signature mechanic is the Lightfall Sword.
  • Core set: Pale Flame 4pc (2pc +25% Phys DMG; +25% more after consuming Grimheart) or Pale Flame 2 + Bloodstained Chivalry 2 (50% Phys DMG total, easier to farm).
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Physical DMG Goblet / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stats: Crits > ATK > ER (two-Cryo teams ~110-125%; 130-140% with Razor/Kazuha as the extra Cryo).
  • Key: her damage is Physical (no Elemental Mastery needed); the team uses Superconduct (Cryo+Electro) to shred 25% Physical RES.
  • Best teams: Eula + Fischl/Razor (Electro) + Kazuha (grouping/shred) + Bennett/Xingqiu.
  • Signature weapon: Song of Broken Pines (Phys DMG substat + ATK SPD); F2P uses Blackcliff Longsword / Prototype Archaic.

How Eula Works

Unlike most Cryo characters, Eula's main damage is Physical — Cryo is only there to trigger reactions.

  • Lightfall Sword: her Burst spawns a sword that lasts 7s; Normal/Skill/Burst hits stack energy layers (one per 0.1s). When it detonates, it deals Physical damage scaled by stack count — the more stacks, the bigger the hit. At C6 she can easily reach 25 stacks.
  • Grimheart: tapping her Skill builds up to 2 stacks; holding consumes all of them, lowering nearby enemies' Physical + Cryo RES and dealing Cryo DMG in return.
  • So she runs ATK + Crits and a Physical DMG Goblet; Pale Flame 4pc adds another +25% Phys DMG on every Grimheart consumption.

Team Comps

Superconduct Physical Team (most common)

Eula + Fischl/Razor (Electro) + Kazuha (grouping/shred) + Bennett/Xingqiu

  • Fischl's off-field Electro + Eula's Cryo attacks trigger Superconduct (25% Physical RES shred) — the core of her physical rotation.
  • Kazuha groups + shreds RES; Bennett/Xingqiu set up the damage environment while Eula detonates the Lightfall Sword.

Dendro Variant (Cryo does not react with Dendro)

Eula + Baizhu/Nahida + Furina/Xingqiu

  • Because Cryo and Dendro don't react, Eula fills on-field Physical output while her teammates handle Dendro reactions (Aggravate / Dendro-Blitz).

Constellation Priority

  • C1: increased Physical DMG while consuming Grimheart — a direct Lightfall buff.
  • C2: casting the Burst resets the Skill CD and grants a Grimheart stack — keeps Pale Flame 4pc cycling, best value.
  • C6: Lightfall Sword scales dramatically; stacking also has a 50% chance to add an extra layer, making 25 stacks easy — the physical nuke ceiling.

FAQ

Why is Eula Physical if she's Cryo?

Her element is Cryo (used to trigger Superconduct), but the Lightfall Sword and most of her damage are calculated as Physical — so she uses a Physical DMG Goblet and physical teams, and needs no Elemental Mastery.

No signature weapon — what next?

F2P: craft Blackcliff Longsword (Crit DMG substat) or Prototype Archaic as a transition; a 4-star is fully playable. For a finished build, run Pale Flame 4pc + refined Blackcliff / Archaic.

Can Eula team with Fischl?

Yes — that is the Superconduct core: Fischl's off-field Electro + Eula's Cryo hits shred 25% Physical RES. It is the most common physical comp.

Is Eula's Energy Recharge demand high?

Her Burst costs 80 energy. Two-Cryo teams need ~110-125%; with Razor or Kazuha as the extra Cryo it can reach 130-140%. Skyward Pride eases the recharge.

Which Eula constellation matters most?

C2 is the game-changer (resets the Skill CD and keeps Pale Flame 4pc cycling); chase C6 for the nuke. At C0 she is already a strong burst option.
