Genshin Kazuha Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Kazuha — the wandering samurai of Inazuma — is the single most flexible support in Genshin Impact. His Elemental Skill pulls enemies together, his Viridescent Venerer set shreds 40% elemental RES, and his passive turns his own Elemental Mastery into team-wide damage. This guide covers weapons, the VV artifact set, EM stacking, team comps, and the swirl engine that makes him a staple in nearly every endgame comp.

Note: Kazuha's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Kazuha Is Worth Building

Four effects stack into one of the best "just throw him in" supports in the game:

  • 40% elemental RES shred: the 4-piece Viridescent Venerer set cuts the swirled element's RES by 40% for everyone — this alone boosts team damage more than most units' entire kit.
  • Elemental Mastery share: his passive Poetics of Fuubutsu gives the whole party a damage bonus equal to 0.04% of his EM per point (capped at +40% for Anemo/Hydro/Pyro/Cryo). Stack EM and the whole team hits harder.
  • Grouping + interruption: his Skill plunge lifts and clusters small enemies, and his Burst's field deals continuous Anemo and enables clean swirls.
  • Universal fit: he drops into Vaporize, Freeze, Taser, Bloom, and mono-element teams with zero rebuild — the definition of evergreen value.
In a Freeze or Vaporize team, Kazuha's VV shred plus EM share typically adds +25–35% team DPS on top of his own swirl damage. That is why he is on almost every meta wishlist.

Best Weapons

Kazuha wants Elemental Mastery above all — his damage and team buff both scale off EM. A high-EM weapon beats a higher-ATK one.

5★ BiSFreedom-Sworn

Signature · EM + team buff

His tailor-made weapon: big Elemental Mastery plus a party-wide ATK/DMG buff after triggering a swirl. The clear best-in-slot, but the gap to F2P is only ~10–15%.

Obtain: weapon banner

F2P ★Iron Sting

Top free-to-play pick

Craftable (crafting bench, Forgiving smithing) EM sword. Maximum EM at zero cost. The default recommendation for a F2P Kazuha — it reaches most of Freedom-Sworn's value.

Obtain: blacksmith (Mora + crystal cores)

F2PKagotsurubeissou

Event ATK% alternative

A free event polearm-style sword giving ATK%. Decent if you have no EM weapon, but you'll want EM substats to compensate. Still a clean step up from nothing.

Obtain: limited-time event

F2PFavonius Sword / Toukabou Shigure

ER battery / event EM

Favonius Sword generates particles for team burst uptime (build some CRIT Rate to proc it). Toukabou Shigure (event) gives EM and Skill DMG — a strong F2P pick if you own it.

Obtain: all banners / event

Talent Priority

Level in this order — his Normal Attack is the least of his worries:

  1. Elemental Skill (Chihayaburu) → 8–9. The plunge is his main damage and swirl trigger; leveling it raises swirl and plunge damage.
  2. Elemental Burst (Kazuha Slash) → 8. The field enables continuous swirls and grouping; level for comfort, not priority.
  3. Normal Attack → 1. Ignore unless you run a dedicated on-field DPS build.

How the Swirl Engine Works

Kazuha's value is in the swirl, not the hit. Master this and he out-values most 5-stars:

  • Cast Skill (hold): he lifts into the air and plunges, dealing AoE Anemo and absorbing an element he touches — that element becomes the swirl.
  • Swirl the right element: if your carry deals Pyro, make sure Kazuha's plunge passes through Pyro before landing, so VV shreds Pyro RES, not Anemo.
  • Passive share: his EM converts to a team damage bonus the moment he swirls — more EM = bigger team buff, which is why EM is his #1 stat.
Key rule: a Kazuha who never swirls the carry's element wastes 40% of his value. Time the plunge through the right aura.

Common Mistakes

  • Building ATK instead of EM. His buff and swirl scale off EM. An ATK-sponge Kazuha buffs almost nobody.
  • Skipping Viridescent Venerer. Without 4pc VV there is no RES shred — he becomes a weak Anemo plunger. The set is the point.
  • Swirling the wrong element. Swirling Anemo shreds Anemo RES (useless). Swirl the carry's element for the -40% shred.
  • Low CRIT on Favonius. If you run Favonius Sword, you need ~30%+ CRIT to proc its particle generation, or the team's bursts stall.
  • Over-leveling his Normal Attack. It barely matters. Spend the books on Skill/Burst.

Don't Have Kazuha Yet?

He is a limited 5-star, so you may not own him. For grouping and Anemo utility, Sucrose (a 4-star on the beginner list) is the best F2P stand-in — she also shares EM and can hold Viridescent Venerer. Venti groups larger crowds but lacks the RES shred; Lynette is a budget third option. Build them while you wait for his banner.
