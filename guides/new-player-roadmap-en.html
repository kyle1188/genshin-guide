Genshin Impact New Player Roadmap 2026 – First 30 Days to AR45

Starting Genshin can feel overwhelming — dozens of systems, characters, and resource currencies. This roadmap strips away the noise. Follow the 4-phase plan below and you'll reach AR45 (the artifact farming milestone) with a solid team and zero wasted resin.

📅 Version Note: Roadmap based on Version 7.0 Snezhnaya (August 2026). New regions and characters may shift some priorities — core principles remain the same.

Phase 1: Days 1–7 (AR 1–20) — The Foundation

Goal: Clear the Prologue Archon Quest and unlock all core systems.

  • Follow the Archon Quest — this is your main priority. It gives massive AR EXP and unlocks regions, domains, and daily commissions.
  • Do Daily Commissions every day — 4 commissions = 60 Primogems/day. Over 7 days = 420 Primogems (~2.6 pulls). This is your most reliable income.
  • Unlock Teleport Waypoints — 2 seconds each, saves enormous travel time later.
  • Upgrade Statues of the Seven — increases stamina cap and gives AR EXP. Prioritize the Statue in each new region you enter.
  • Don't touch Resin yet — before AR 45, artifact domains drop terrible 5★ pieces. Spend Resin on Ley Line Blossoms (Mora and EXP books) only.

Phase 2: Days 8–14 (AR 20–30) — Build Your Core Team

Goal: Lock in exactly 1 Main DPS and assemble the National team (Bennett + Xiangling + Xingqiu + 4th slot).

The #1 mistake new players make: spreading resources across 10+ characters. Pick 4–6 characters max and pour 80% of your resources into them.

Phase 3: Days 15–21 (AR 30–40) — Prepare for Endgame

Goal: Reach Inazuma story, farm talent books, and get your Main DPS to level 80/90.

  • Push the Archon Quest to unlock Sumeru (and eventually Natlan / Snezhnaya).
  • Farm talent book domains on the days those books are available (each region has 2 fixed weekdays per book cluster; Sunday unlocks all clusters).
  • Craft your first forgeable weapon — The Stringless (Xiao/Fischl), Dragon's Bane (Xiangling), or Iron Sting (Kazuha/Alhaitham) are free and often outperform early 5★ weapons.
  • Start doing 2–3 Artifact domains per week just to get a feel for substats. Don't go all-in yet — save your real farming for AR 45+.
  • Spend Resin on Weekly Bosses — they drop Northlander Billets (for forging) and ascension materials for your Main DPS.

Phase 4: Days 22–30 (AR 40–45) — The Milestone

Goal: Reach AR 45, then start serious artifact farming.

  • AR 45 is the single most important milestone — it unlocks guaranteed 5★ drops from artifact domains (one per 20 Resin run).
  • Before AR 45: spend Resin on Ley Line Blossoms (Mora / EXP books) and Weekly Bosses only. Artifact domains at lower AR have terrible 5★ rates — don't waste Resin.
  • At AR 45: pick your primary artifact domain (based on your Main DPS element) and start running it with Condensed Resin (40 Resin = 2 runs).
  • Start attempting Spiral Abyss Floor 9 with your built team. Even a 24-star clear is a huge accomplishment for a new player.

Biggest Beginner Mistakes

  1. Farming artifacts before AR 45 — the 5★ drop rate is abysmal. Save your resin for Mora and EXP books until you hit the milestone.
  2. Spreading resources too thin — leveling 10 characters to level 40 is worse than leveling 4 characters to level 80. Commit to 1 Main DPS first.
  3. Spending Primogems on the Standard Banner — only use Acquaint Fates (blue) on Standard. Intertwined Fates (pink) are for Limited Character banners only.
  4. Ignoring Daily Commissions — 60 Primogems/day sounds small, but that's 1,800/month = ~11 free pulls. Over a year that's 130+ pulls.
  5. Not upgrading Teleport Waypoints — each waypoint gives 5 Primogems and saves minutes of travel time per run.

Realistic target: 6,000–8,000 Primogems/month as a casual F2P player. That's enough for ~25–30 pulls/month, or one limited character every 3–4 months.

When to Pull (and When Not To)

  • DO pull on: Limited Character Event banners featuring versatile supports (Kazuha, Furina, Neuvillette) or a Main DPS you genuinely want to play.
  • DO NOT pull on: Standard banner with Intertwined Fates (use Acquaint Fates instead). Weapon banner unless you're a whale navigating Epitomized Path. Any banner that doesn't match your team plan.
  • Save at least 160 pulls (25,600 Primogems) before committing to a new character — this covers worst-case 50/50 loss + hard pity twice.

Quick Answers

How long does it take to reach AR45 as a new player?

About 25–45 days for a casual player (1–2 hours/day). Speedrunners who focus purely on quests can hit AR45 in ~15 days. The roadmap assumes a balanced pace.

Can I clear Spiral Abyss Floor 12 with F2P characters?

Yes. National team (Bennett + Xiangling + Xingqiu + any DPS) clears Floor 12 consistently when supports hit ~160–200% Energy Recharge. You don't need 5★ characters to 3-star clear.

Should I spend money on the Battle Pass?

The 980 Primogem Battle Pass is the best value in Genshin. It gives EXP books, Mora, weapons, and sometimes a free 5★ weapon. Highly recommended for F2P players who can afford it.

What if I miss Daily Commissions for a few days?

Nothing bad happens. Just log in and complete them when you can. They don't carry over, but missing a few days won't derail your progress. Consistency matters more than perfection.

Is it worth rerolling for a specific starting character?

No. Rerolling takes 30–40 minutes per account and the Beginner's Wish banner guarantees Noelle anyway. Your early progress is identical regardless of which 4★ you pull. Focus on playing, not rerolling.
