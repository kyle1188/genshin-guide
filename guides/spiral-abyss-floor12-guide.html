快速问答

What characters do I need for Floor 12?

Recommend at least 3 C6+ main DPS with well-built supports. Raiden + Xiangling + Xingqiu + Bennett covers 80% of scenarios.

How do I handle No Constellar Electro phase?

Use Raiden National or Thoma Yoimiyo teams. The 50% Electro DMG bonus significantly reduces clear time.

What is the time limit per chamber?

60 seconds per chamber, 180 seconds total. Practice to complete each in 45-50 seconds for safety buffer.

How should I prioritize artifact stats?

Main DPS: CR/CD/EM priority. Supports: ER/HP/Healing. Don't chase perfect rolls—70+ main stat score is sufficient.
