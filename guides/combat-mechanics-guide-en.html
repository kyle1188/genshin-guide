Dodge and Block

Dodge Mechanic

Dodge damage reduction:

  • Perfect dodge: 80% reduction + brief i-frames
  • Normal dodge: 50% reduction
  • Failed dodge: Full damage

Dodge timing:

Enemy ActionBest Dodge Timing
---------------------------------
Charge attackBefore attack ends wind-up
Combo first hitContinuous dodge
AoE attackEdge of attack range

Block Mechanic

Block conditions:

  • Shield-bearing characters (Zhongli, Noelle, Diona)
  • Block damage reduction only works when shield is active

Shield damage reduction:

Shield TypeReduction %
--------------------------
Zhongli Geo shield75%
Noelle Geo shield60%
Diona Cryo shield50%

FAQ

Q: Do elemental reactions have priority?

A: Yes. In Vaporize and Melt, the second-applied element has higher multiplier (2.0x vs 1.5x).

Q: How long is dodge i-frame?

A: Perfect dodge is about 0.3 seconds i-frames, enough to dodge most attacks.

Q: Can shields block elemental application?

A: No. Shields only reduce damage, elemental application still transfers.

Q: Do combo attacks have DMG bonus?

A: No direct bonus, but ensures stable elemental reaction triggering.
