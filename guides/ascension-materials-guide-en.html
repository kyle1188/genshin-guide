Genshin Impact Ascension Materials Guide: Elemental Gems, Boss Drops & Full Checklist

Ascension materials are the first wall every new character hits, and the most underestimated resource sink in the game: a single character needs 168 local specialties and 420,000 Mora to reach level 90. This guide breaks the materials into four types, with a full six-stage requirement table and a seven-element gem reference.

  • How many material types? Four: elemental gems, common enemy drops, local specialties, and world boss materials, plus Mora.
  • What does a full 1-90 ascension cost? Gems at 1/9/9/6, 168 specialties, 84 enemy drops across three tiers, 46 boss materials, and 420,000 Mora.
  • What are the four gem tiers? Sliver, Fragment, Chunk, and Gemstone - the total is always 1/9/9/6.
  • Where do gems come from? World bosses and weekly bosses. Pick the boss that drops both the gem and the boss material you still need.

How to Farm Each Material Efficiently

  • Elemental gems: farm world bosses. Each boss drops one gem type plus its own exclusive material, and weekly bosses also drop gems. Pick the boss that covers both gaps.
  • World boss materials: 2-3 per kill at 40 resin each, so 46 needs roughly 15-23 runs.
  • Common enemy drops: use the Adventurer Handbook tracker and chain enemies of the same series in one route.
  • Local specialties: gather in the wild on a 48-hour respawn. No resin required.
  • Mora: the Blossom of Wealth is the most reliable source when you are short.

Do Not Forget Talent Materials

Beyond ascension, talents need books (Teachings, Guides, Philosophies), enemy drops, weekly boss materials, and Crowns of Insight. One talent to level 10 costs about 1.65 million Mora, and all three talents run close to 5 million plus 3 Crowns.

So when you pick a weekly boss, choose the one whose talent material and ascension gem you both still need - one resin spend, two payoffs.

Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting Mora: 420,000 is not trivial. Farm Blossoms of Wealth along the way.
  • Discarding low-tier gems: Slivers and Fragments are required early on. Do not use them up.
  • Expecting daily specialty respawns: it is a 48-hour cycle, so a next-day run is wasted.
  • Farming random bosses: without matching your actual gaps, resin goes down the drain.
  • Ignoring talents: ascension raises the ceiling, but talents are where the damage actually comes from.

Bottom Line

Ascension comes down to one checklist and one priority order. Four material types plus Mora, with a fixed total of 1/9/9/6 gems, 84 enemy drops, 168 specialties, 46 boss materials, and 420,000 Mora. Decide which character you are building, farm only the gaps, pick weekly bosses that cover both talent and gem needs, and treat the final 80-90 ascension as optional depending on your resources.
