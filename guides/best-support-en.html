Quick Answers

Most universal support?

Bennett (ATK buff + heal, fits almost any team) and Kazuha (shred + grouping, all elements) are the universality ceiling; Zhongli's shield is the top pick for survival. These three are highest priority.

Do supports need levels?

Their stats differ: Bennett/Baizhu want HP and ER, Zhongli wants HP, Kazuha wants EM and ER, Nahida wants EM. Level to 80 ascended, and raise the key talents.

Still worth pulling?

Bennett/Zhongli/Kazuha are long-term core supports needed every version; Xilonen/Nahida are mandatory for their systems. Fill based on the teams you actually play.
