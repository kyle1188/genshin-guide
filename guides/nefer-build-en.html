Genshin Nefer Build: 5★ Dendro Catalyst Lunar-Bloom Sub-DPS

Nefer is a V6.0 (Luna II) 5-star Dendro Catalyst from Nod-Krai's Curatorium of Secrets. She is an on-field Lunar-Bloom sub-DPS that scales off elemental mastery — Night of the Sky's Unveiling 4pc + Reliquary of Truth (Dendro signature) + EM sands, paired with Lauma to amplify Lunar-Bloom.

Quick Answer

  • Role: On-field Lunar-Bloom sub-DPS, EM scaling.
  • Weapon: Reliquary of Truth (Dendro signature) > Nightweaver's Looking Glass > Dawning Frost (4-star).
  • Artifacts:Night of the Sky's Unveiling 4pc (first) / Gilded Dreams / Deepwood Memories.
  • Main stats: EM sands + EM cup + CRIT rate / CRIT DMG circlet.
  • Team: Nefer + Lauma + Columbina + Aino.

Who Nefer Is

Nefer is a 5-Star Dendro Catalyst released in Version 6.0 (Luna II, 2025-10-22). Owner of the Nod-Krai Curatorium of Secrets and agent of the Northern Intelligence Network. She is an on-field Lunar-Bloom sub-DPS whose damage scales off elemental mastery, with built-in RES shred and reaction-base-DMG uplift.

Is Nefer Worth Pulling

Skip if you already own Lauma + Columbina and don't need a Lunar-Bloom main; if you lack a Dendro on-field sub-DPS and want to run Lunar-Bloom, Nefer is the 5-Star answer. Fully functional at C0; C1/C2 widen the Lunar-Bloom window.
