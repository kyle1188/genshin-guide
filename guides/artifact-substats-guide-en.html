Complete Artifact Substat Guide: From Beginner to Endgame

Artifact substats determine whether your character is truly endgame-ready. This guide covers the 1:2 CRIT ratio, Elemental Mastery soft caps, Energy Recharge thresholds, and substat priorities for DPS/support/reaction teams.

The 1:2 CRIT Golden Ratio

Principle: CRIT Rate (CV) and CRIT DMG (CD) contribute equally to damage when the ratio is 1:2.

  • CV (Crit Value) = CRIT Rate% + CRIT DMG% × 0.5
  • Ideal target: 60–70% CR rate, 120–160% CR dmg
  • The closer to 1:2, the higher the expected damage

Elemental Mastery Soft Caps

Elemental Mastery (EM) benefits transformation reactions (Swirl/Overload/Shatter/Electro-Charged/Burning) but has soft caps:

EM RangeBenefit CurveRecommendation
----------------------------------------
0–100Linear growth, highest ROIMust stack
100–200Starting to decayCan continue stacking
200–300Significant decaySituational
300+Nearly uselessDon't stack

> Amplifying reactions (Vaporize/Melt) do NOT benefit from EM. Only base damage × multiplier matters.

FAQ

Q: Should I prioritize CRIT or ER first?

A: Meet ER threshold first (ensure burst rotation), then fill remaining slots with CRIT.

Q: Is 1:2 CRIT ratio a hard requirement?

A: No, it's optimal. 70% CR + 140% CD ≈ same as 100% CR + 70% CD in expected damage.

Q: Does Elemental Mastery help Vaporize/Melt?

A: No. Amplifying reactions only scale with base damage × multiplier — EM only helps transformation reactions.

Q: What if my artifacts have bad substats?

A: Re-roll. Each refinement slot is independently random — there's no way to fix bad substats after the fact.
