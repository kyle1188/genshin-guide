Elemental Reaction Mechanics Detailed

Elemental reactions are the core of Genshin combat. Understanding the fundamental difference between Amplified and Transformative reactions will help you make better team building and combat decisions.

Reaction Categories

Genshin elemental reactions are divided into two main categories:

CategoryReactionsCharacteristics
--------------------------------------
**Amplified**Vaporize, MeltMultiplier-based, scale with ATK and EM
**Transformative**Electro-Charged, Overloaded, Swirl, Burgeon, Hyperbloom, Aggravate/SpreadFixed base damage, primarily scale with EM

---

Amplified Reactions

Vaporize

Trigger: Hydro + Pyro

Damage Formula:


Vaporize\ DMG\ =\ Base\ DMG\ ×\ \(1\ \+\ DMG\ Bonus\)\ ×\ Vaporize\ Multiplier\ \(1\.5\ or\ 2\.0\)

Key Mechanics:

  • Hydro → Pyro = 1.5x
  • Pyro → Hydro = 2.0x (Reverse Vaporize)
  • Scales with ATK bonus

Recommended Characters:

  • Pyro: Hu Tao, Diluc, Bennett
  • Hydro: Xingqiu, Yelan, Kokomi

Melt

Trigger: Cryo + Pyro

Damage Formula:


Melt\ DMG\ =\ Base\ DMG\ ×\ \(1\ \+\ DMG\ Bonus\)\ ×\ Melt\ Multiplier\ \(1\.5\ or\ 2\.0\)

Key Mechanics:

  • Cryo → Pyro = 1.5x
  • Pyro → Cryo = 2.0x (Reverse Melt)
  • Scales with ATK bonus

Recommended Characters:

  • Pyro: Hu Tao, Diluc, Bennett
  • Cryo: Ayaka, Ganyu, Shenhe

---

Transformative Reactions

Electro-Charged

Trigger: Hydro + Electro

Characteristics:

  • Continuous Electro DMG over time
  • DMG scales with Elemental Mastery
  • Can spread to nearby enemies

Recommended Characters:

  • Electro: Fischl, Razor
  • Hydro: Xingqiu, Kokomi

Overloaded

Trigger: Pyro + Electro

Characteristics:

  • Pyro AoE DMG
  • Creates knockback effect
  • DMG scales with Elemental Mastery

Recommended Characters:

  • Pyro: Bennett, Diluc
  • Electro: Fischl, Razor

Swirl

Trigger: Anemo + Any Element

Characteristics:

  • Spreads element to nearby enemies
  • Can shred resistance (with Kazuha/Sucrose)
  • DMG scales with Elemental Mastery

Recommended Characters:

  • Anemo: Kazuha, Sucrose, Venti

Aggravate/Spread

Trigger: Electro + Dendro

Characteristics:

  • Aggravate: Electro + Dendro, boosts subsequent Electro DMG
  • Superaggravate: Electro + Dendro + Electro, higher DMG
  • Spread: Dendro + Electro, boosts subsequent Dendro DMG
  • Burbling: Dendro + Electro + Dendro, higher Dendro DMG

Recommended Characters:

  • Electro: Fischl, Yae Miko, Raiden Shogun
  • Dendro: Nahida, Tighnari

Burgeon

Trigger: Dendro + Electro (with Dendro Core)

Characteristics:

  • Core explodes on Electro trigger
  • DMG scales with Elemental Mastery
  • Can auto-track enemies

Recommended Characters:

  • Dendro: Nahida, Collei
  • Electro: Fischl, Razor

Hyperbloom

Trigger: Dendro + Hydro (with Dendro Core)

Characteristics:

  • Core becomes homing projectile on Hydro trigger
  • DMG scales with Elemental Mastery
  • High mobility, auto-tracking

Recommended Characters:

  • Dendro: Nahida, Collei
  • Hydro: Xingqiu, Nilou

Amplified vs Transformative: Core Differences

PropertyAmplifiedTransformative
-------------------------------------
DMG SourceATK + EMPrimarily EM
DMG CeilingHigh (can stack CRIT)Medium (EM has soft cap)
Team ComplexityMedium-High (needs precise application)Low (auto-triggers)
Representative TeamsMelt teams, Vaporize teamsAggravate, Bloom teams

Team Building Strategies

Amplified Reaction Teams

Core Strategy:

1. Ensure main DPS can reliably trigger amplified reactions

2. Supports provide frequent elemental application

3. Stack ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG

Example Teams:

  • Hu Tao + Xingqiu + Bennett + Zhongli (Melt)
  • Diluc + Xingqiu + Bennett + Zhongli (Vaporize)

Transformative Reaction Teams

Core Strategy:

1. Stack Elemental Mastery

2. Ensure consistent seed/swirl triggers

3. Leverage auto-tracking for mob clearing

Example Teams:

  • Nahida + Fischl + Kuki + Kazuha (Aggravate)
  • Nahida + Xingqiu + Kuki + Kazuha (Bloom)

---

One-Sentence Summary

> Amplified scales with ATK and CRIT, Transformative scales with EM; amplified has higher ceiling but needs precision, transformative is stable but lower ceiling.

FAQ

Q: Are Amplified or Transformative reactions better?

A: Neither is absolutely better. Amplified has higher ceiling, Transformative is more stable. Choose based on your roster and artifact situation.

Q: Does Elemental Mastery help Amplified reactions?

A: Yes, as a secondary bonus, but not the primary source. Amplified reactions primarily scale with ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG.

Q: Do Transformative reactions benefit from CRIT?

A: No. Transformative reaction DMG only scales with Elemental Mastery, independent of CRIT.

Q: Is Aggravate an Amplified or Transformative reaction?

A: Aggravate is a special Transformative reaction, but it provides DMG bonus to subsequent same-type reactions.
