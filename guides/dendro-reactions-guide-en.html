Complete Dendro Reaction Guide: Mechanisms, Triggers & Team Building

Dendro is the most complex elemental system in Genshin. Mastering Bloom, Aggravate, and Burning reactions dramatically improves your team damage.

Bloom Reaction

Dendro + Hydro creates a seed (Blooming Core) that exists for 15 seconds. When it touches an enemy, it explodes dealing Dendro DMG.

Aggravate & Spread

Dendro + Electro triggers Aggravate, providing a DMG bonus to subsequent Dendro/Electro hits.

ReactionEffect
------------------

Aggravate + DendroSubsequent Dendro DMG +20%

Aggravate team comp:

  • Main DPS: Tighnari/Collei/Nahida (Dendro)
  • Sub DPS: Fischl/Razor/Raiden (Electro)
  • Support: Kazuha (group + RES shred)
  • Survival: Zhongli/Kokomi

FAQ

Q: Hyperbloom or Burgeon — which deals more DMG?

A: Depends on team. Hyperbloom is stable and frequent; Burgeon has higher single-hit burst. Kuki Hyperbloom teams are currently the most consistent Dendro reaction DPS.

Q: Does Aggravate affect subsequent damage?

A: Yes. After Aggravate triggers, subsequent Dendro/Electro DMG increases by 20% for 20s. That's why Aggravate teams trigger the initial reaction first.

Q: Can enemies destroy seeds?

A: No. Seeds can only be triggered by elemental reactions.
