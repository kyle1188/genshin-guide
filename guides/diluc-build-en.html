Genshin Diluc Build & Teams: 5-Star Pyro Claymore & Crimson Witch of Flames

Quick Answer

  • Diluc is a 5★ Pyro claymore from Mondstadt, an on-field main DPS whose loop is his Burst (Phoenix infusion) + 3× Skill hits + plunge attacks.
  • Core set: Crimson Witch of Flames 4pc (2pc +15% Pyro DMG; 4pc adds +37.5% Pyro DMG after the Skill, 3 stacks / 10s — near-permanent uptime).
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Pyro DMG Goblet / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: Crits > ATK > EM (1–2 lines for Vaporize teams) > ER (120–130% without Bennett, 100–115% with).
  • Key: while the Burst infusion is up, all Normal/Charged/Plunge attacks deal Pyro; Vaporize teams > Melt; the plunge combo (Dragonstrike) scales with Kazuha/Ians buffs.
  • Best teams: Diluc + Xingqiu/Yelan + Bennett + Kazuha (Vaporize); Diluc + Kaeya/Rosaria + Bennett + Zhongli (Melt).
  • Weapon: Wolf's Gravestone is the most stable F2P-adjacent pick; craft Prototype Archaic, 4★ Serpent Spine (needs a shielder to hold stacks).

How Diluc Works

His loop is "Burst for the Pyro infusion, then high-DMG window":

  • Burst (Dawn): AoE Pyro + an infusion that A4 extends by 4s and adds +20% Pyro DMG; every Normal/Charged/Plunge inside the window becomes Pyro.
  • Skill (Searing Onslaught): usable 3 times in a row before going on CD — the stack engine of Crimson Witch 4pc (+12.5% Pyro per use, up to 3).
  • Plunging Attack / Dragonstrike (Normal/Skill hit → sprint → jump → plunge): big single-target bonus, amplified by Kazuha's shred or Ians' plunge scaling — a major part of his modern damage.
  • So he runs ATK + Crits + a Pyro Goblet; Vaporize teams keep just 80–120 EM.

Team Comps

Vaporize Team (most consistent)

Diluc + Xingqiu/Yelan (Hydro) + Bennett (ATK + heal) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Xingqiu's Burst keeps Hydro up; Diluc's 3× Skill + plunges trigger Vaporize on every hit; Kazuha shreds Pyro/Hydro RES.
  • Bennett's Burst zone gives the ATK buff that Diluc's whole window sits inside.

Melt Team (single-target ceiling)

Diluc + Kaeya/Rosaria (Cryo) + Bennett + Zhongli (shield / Physical RES shred)

  • Kaeya's off-field Cryo + Diluc's Pyro → Melt; Zhongli's shield keeps him safe on-field.
  • Best against specific Spiral Abyss rooms or single-target bosses.

Constellation Priority

  • C1: +15% Pyro DMG against foes above 50% HP — flat, always-on value, top pick.
  • C4: 25% chance for the Skill to deal extra Pyro DMG — conditional, not worth chasing.
  • C6: faster Normal Attacks and amplified plunge/Normal-Attack damage after the Skill — the true scaling ceiling.

FAQ

Is Diluc still relevant?

Yes. Vaporize is a T0-stable archetype; the plunge (Dragonstrike) scales even harder with Kazuha or Ians, and his kit is straightforward.

What is the best-in-slot weapon?

Wolf's Gravestone. Without 5-stars: craft Prototype Archaic, or take Serpent Spine (with Zhongli's shield to hold its stacks).

Vaporize or Melt?

Vaporize is more consistent (Xingqiu or Yelan keep Hydro up); Melt has a higher multiplier but relies on off-field Cryo from Kaeya or Rosaria.

How much Energy Recharge does Diluc need?

His Burst costs 80. Around 100–115% ER with Bennett, 120–130% without.

Is C1 worth chasing?

Yes — C1's flat +15% Pyro DMG against foes above 50% HP is cleaner value than C4's conditional bonus.
