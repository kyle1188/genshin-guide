Genshin Resin Efficiency 2026: How to Spend 160 Daily Resin & When to Use Fragile

Resin is your most valuable daily resource — 160 cap, 1 per 8 min, wasted if it overflows. This guide gives a stage-based spending plan.

Resin is your most valuable daily resource: 160 cap, 1 point per 8 minutes, ~21 hours to refill. Spend it at least once a day — overflow is free progress thrown away.

AR45 is the dividing line

Below AR45, artifact domains don't guarantee a 5-star — every run is a gamble. After 45 they do. Pre-45 priority: character/weapon ascension mats > talent books > Mora (Ley Lines). The free Gladiator/Wanderer sets are enough to bridge.

Fragile Resin: save for AR45+

Each Fragile Resin = +60. The most-regretted move is using it pre-45 on artifacts. Correct use: stockpile to 45+, then dump during double-drop events or when grinding one set.

Condensed Resin: time-saver

Craft at the bench: 40 resin + 1 Crystal Core → one unit that doubles a domain/Ley Line reward. Bag holds max 5 — stock Crystal Cores from Anemo crystalflies early. Does not work on bosses (weekly/world bosses get no double).

Buying resin with Primogems

The cost rises each purchase. The first 1–3 are acceptable; beyond that, be careful. Don't buy resin at the expense of saving for characters — a new character adds more than a few runs.

Common traps

  • Capping and overflowing: spend at least daily; if near full, run a no-resin domain to pad.
  • Farming artifacts pre-45: no 5-star guarantee, pure waste.
  • Early Fragile Resin: using it pre-45 is the most regretted decision.
  • Skipping weekly bosses: first 3 at 30 each drop rare mats you can't recover.
  • Ignoring double-drop events: during resin-half/double weeks, push the event domains first.
