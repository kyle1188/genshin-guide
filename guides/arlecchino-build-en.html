Genshin Arlecchino Build Guide 2026: Weapons, Artifacts, Teams & Bond of Life Mechanic

Arlecchino — the Fatui Harbinger "The Knave" — is the most reliable Pyro on-field DPS. She converts her Normal Attacks to Pyro via Bond of Life, self-heals with her burst, and needs almost no healer. She is complete at C0. This guide covers weapons, artifacts, stat targets, team comps, and her unique Bond of Life mechanic.

Note: Arlecchino's kit, Bond of Life conversion and artifact choice are long-term stable core gameplay and do not change much across versions. Constellations and banner schedules shift, but the build below holds. In-game data is authoritative.

Is Arlecchino worth building?

Arlecchino stands out as the benchmark of Pyro on-field teams for four reasons:

  • Stable Pyro on-field: once she has Bond of Life, her NA/CA/Plunge all become Pyro, sustaining damage on single and group targets.
  • Self-sustain: her burst heals based on Bond of Life and ATK, so survivability is easy and you can skip a healer. But while Bond of Life is active she cannot be normally healed — watch the tempo.
  • ATK pays off twice: her Ascension passive turns ATK above 1000 into all-element and physical RES (up to 20%), so stacking ATK boosts both damage and durability.
  • Flexible teams: she works as the core DPS in Vaporize, Mono-Pyro or Overload — extremely versatile.
Arlecchino is complete at C0 and very cost-effective. C2 makes marks start as "Due" and grants an extra 900% ATK AoE hit on absorb — a breakpoint constellation; C6 further boosts burst and dual-CRIT. High constellations are not mandatory.

Talent priority

Arlecchino's Normal Attack is her main damage source (fully Pyro after infusion) — level it first:

  1. Normal Attack (Invitation to a Beheading) → 9 or crown. Post-infusion NA is most of her damage and consumes Bond of Life.
  2. Elemental Burst (Balemoon Rising) → 8-9. Absorbs marks, clears Skill CD and self-heals — key to sustain and burst.
  3. Elemental Skill (All Is Ash) → 8. Applies Blood-Debt Directive and grants Bond of Life — the loop starter, but its own multiplier is secondary.

Bond of Life & RES mechanic

Arlecchino's core: casting Skill or defeating marked enemies grants Bond of Life; at ≥30% Max HP her NA/CA/Plunge convert to Pyro with an extra scaling bonus. Understand this and damage jumps.

  • Elemental Skill (All Is Ash): applies "Blood-Debt Directive"; absorbing it via CA or burst grants Bond of Life (65% per mark, up to 145% cap within 35s), starting the loop.
  • Masque of the Red Death (infusion): at Bond of Life ≥30%, NA/CA/Plunge become Pyro, dealing extra DMG scaling with Bond of Life % whileconsuming it and reducing Skill CD.
  • Ascension passive "Agony Alone May Be Repaid": every 100 ATK above 1000 gives +1% all-element and physical RES, up to 20% — so ATK pays off twice.
Key rule: while Arlecchino has Bond of Life she cannot be normally healed — only her burst heals her. Keep the burst ready for survival. Also, aim ATK 2200+; over-investing in RES squeezes out ATK substats, so balance it.

Common mistakes

  • Stacking dual-CRIT but not ATK. Arlecchino scales on ATK%; 2200+ is the baseline or late-game damage feels flat, and the RES bonus also suffers.
  • Forcing a healer to top her up. Normal healing is ineffective while she holds Bond of Life — it disrupts tempo. Either rely on her burst or drop the sustain slot for more damage.
  • Ignoring the Bond of Life loop. Without Bond of Life her output drops sharply — use Skill to mark, then CA/burst to absorb and maintain infusion.
  • Skipping EM in reaction teams. ~200 EM clearly lifts reaction damage in Vape/Bloom; pure Pyro teams can ignore it.
  • Treating her as pure ATK. ATK matters, but dual-CRIT and the Bond loop are equally key; ATK alone leaves infusion bonuses and CRIT unused.

No Arlecchino yet?

Arlecchino is a limited 5-star. To try a Pyro main DPS first, Hu Tao is the burst benchmark, Bennett is the universal Pyro support, and Xiangling gives off-field Pyro everyone wants — build the Pyro framework now and add Arlecchino on rerun. For another carry see Lyney.

Quick Answers

What is the best build for Arlecchino?

Run 4-piece Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (ATK sands, Pyro DMG goblet, CRIT rate/DMG circlet); Crimson Moon's Semblance is best, with Deathmatch or White Tassel as 4-stars. Target 2200+ ATK, ~70% CRIT Rate, 140%+ CRIT DMG.

How does Arlecchino's Bond of Life work?

She gains Bond of Life from her Skill or by defeating marked enemies. At ≥30% of Max HP it converts her attacks to Pyro DMG with an extra bonus. Her passive also turns ATK above 1000 into all-element RES (up to 20%), so ATK helps both damage and survivability.

What stats should I prioritize on Arlecchino?

Prioritize 2200+ ATK, ~70% CRIT Rate, 140%+ CRIT DMG, and ~200 Elemental Mastery in reaction teams (Vaporize/Bloom). Most damage scales on ATK%, so build ATK first.
