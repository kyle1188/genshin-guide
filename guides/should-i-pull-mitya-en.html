Genshin: Should I Pull Mitya? 7.2 Constellation & F2P Verdict

Verdict: pull him if you own a Cryo character; otherwise save and wait for a rerun. Mitya is a 5-star Electro Catalyst main DPS released in 7.2. His damage runs entirely on the Stellar-Conduct reaction (Cryo + Electro), so without a Cryo teammate his output roughly halves. C0 is fully usable and clears the Abyss; C1 is the quality jump (Gradient Beacons 4 to 5, +40% on-field CRIT DMG); C2 unlocks his off-field role. One copy averages about 12,160 Primogems; worst case is 28,800 (roughly $360).

Quick Decisions

  • Worth it? Cryo on team (Odette / Yae Miko / Cryo Traveler) → pull; none → save and wait, C0 is not wasted.
  • Constellations? C1 is the upgrade point for on-field DPS, C2 for off-field; stay C0 on a tight budget.
  • F2P? Yes — a near-zero-cost team Mitya + Cryo Traveler + Alyosha + Sucrose works at C0.
  • Whom to pick? Lack of a main DPS → Mitya; lack of an off-field buffer / Hydro applier → Furina (rerun, shared pity).

Worth it: Mitya vs Furina (same pool)

In 7.2 Phase 1, Mitya (new) and Furina (rerun) share one pity counter and the 50/50 guarantee, so your pulls carry between them. Choose by what your account is missing:

In one line: when both line up and resources are tight, pull the one you are missing — Mitya if you own Cryo (new characters are stronger), Furina if you lack an off-field buffer (rerun but extremely flexible).

Should F2P pull him

Yes — Mitya's F2P team is almost free:
