Xilonen Build Guide 2026 — Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Xilonen is the Geo blacksmith of Natlan who quietly became one of the best universal supports in the game: she shreds enemy Elemental RES, heals the team, and buffs everyone's damage — all off-field. Here's how to build her in 2026 without wasting resin.

Sources: GameWith, Tacter, genshin-builds, and KQM theorycrafting. Patch numbers shift — in-game values are final.

The verdict in one line

4-piece Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City, DEF% main stats, Peak Patrol Song if you have it (Favonius Sword is the F2P pick), and slot her into a Neuvillette / Furina / Arlecchino team as the off-field RES-shredder + healer. She's a flexible S-tier support in 2026.

First, understand her role (she's not a DPS)

Xilonen is an off-field Geo support. Her value comes from two things:

  • RES Shred: her Elemental Skill drops "Sourcewater Samples" that, after she Normal Attacks twice, convert to your team's elements and lower nearby enemies' Elemental RES (up to 3 of Pyro / Hydro / Electro / Cryo).
  • Healing + Buff: her Burst is a strong single-target heal (great for offsetting Furina's HP drain), and her Scroll set hands the whole team a big Elemental DMG bonus.
The one rule that trips people: Xilonen needs Pyro / Hydro / Electro / Cryo teammates to convert her samples. A mono-Geo team wastes her. Pair her with those elements and she becomes a near-universal buffer.

Do you need her signature? No. Favonius Sword carries her just fine — the only thing you lose is a little personal buff value, and you gain team energy. Spend primogems on characters first.

Best artifacts — the Scroll set is her identity

Every major build resource converges on 4-piece Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City:

  • 2pc: +20% DEF — directly strengthens her DEF-scaling buffs.
  • 4pc: after a nearby Nightsoul Burst or a reaction she triggers, the whole party gets +12% (and up to +28% more in Nightsoul's Blessing) Elemental DMG for the involved elements. This is her core team-wide buff.
Conflict to avoid: only ONE Scroll holder per team. If someone else already runs Scroll, put Xilonen on Archaic Petra (Geo support) or Instructor (EM + team EM) instead — don't stack two Scroll sets, the 4pc bonus won't double.

Temporary fallback: 2pc Archaic Petra + 2pc DEF% until you finish Scroll. Don't farm Gilded Dreams or Husk of Opulent Dreams for her support role.

Talent priority

Elemental Skill > Elemental Burst > Normal Attack. Her Skill creates the samples that drive everything; crown it first. Burst is your team heal. Normal Attack stays at 1 (you only tap it twice to fill Nightsoul). Aim for roughly 9 / 9 / 1 (Skill / Burst / NA).

Best team comps

Neuvillette Hypercarry

Neuvillette + Furina + Xilonen + Kazuha

The premium Hydro core. Xilonen's heal offsets Furina's HP drain, her Geo RES-shred stacks with Kazuha's VV shred, and Scroll buffs the whole team. This is where she shines brightest.

Arlecchino Pure Pyro

Arlecchino + Xilonen + Kazuha + Bennett

Xilonen + Kazuha + Bennett layer Pyro RES-shred, DMG buffs, and healing on top of Arlecchino's already-huge Pyro damage. An S-tier staple team.

Melt (Chasca / Citlali)

Chasca + Xilonen + Citlali + Bennett

Xilonen provides Geo RES-shred + healing while Citlali adds Cryo and a shield; Bennett tops up ATK. Built for consistent Melt reactions.

Generalist flex

Any carry + Xilonen + 2 PECH units

Because she buffs whichever elements her samples convert to, she slots into almost any team with Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo carries. Extreme flexibility is her trademark.

The Nightsoul trick (why she's the universal buffer)

Xilonen's power loops through her Nightsoul mechanic: use her Skill to drop samples → tap Normal Attack twice to fill her Nightsoul gauge → the samples convert to your team's Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo and shred those RES types on nearby enemies. Meanwhile Scroll's 4pc fires off a team-wide Elemental DMG buff. She does all of this off-field, which is why she competes with (and often replaces) Kazuha or Zhongli in modern teams — and why pairing her with Neuvillette or Furina feels like free damage.

5 common mistakes

  1. Running two Scroll holders — if a teammate already has Scroll, Xilonen's 4pc won't stack. Switch her to Archaic Petra or Instructor.
  2. Too little ER — without ~180%+ she can't Burst every rotation, and you lose the heal that offsets Furina's drain.
  3. Locking her in a mono-Geo team — no PECH teammates means her samples never convert, so she shreds nothing. Always bring Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo.
  4. Ignoring DEF% — her signature and Scroll buffs both scale off DEF; a DEF-heavy build makes her whole team stronger.
  5. Skipping her because "she's just a support" — in 2026 she's among the highest-value units to pull; her buffs are team-wide and element-agnostic.

Don't have Xilonen yet?

She's a limited 5★. While you wait, similar off-field utility comes from a shielder like Zhongli (Tenacity of the Millelith) or Kazuha (Viridescent Venerer shred) — but neither heals and shreds *and* buffs like she does. Pre-farming DEF% artifacts and a Favonius Sword is worth it either way.
