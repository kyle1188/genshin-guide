Genshin Gacha & Primogem Strategy 2026: Pity, Planning & What to Pull

Gacha is not about luck, it is about planning. Read the pity, count your Primogems, skip weak banners — and F2P accounts still lock in the characters they want.

Planning beats impulse: Genshin rewards goal-driven saving, not pulling on every banner. Pick your target, count the pulls, skip limited characters you do not need — that is the steadiest account growth.

The Pity System

ItemRule
Hard pityCharacter banner guarantees a 5-star by 90 wishes
Soft pity5-star rate spikes from ~wish 74 (~33% per pull); most get it between 75-85
50/50First 5-star is 50% featured; lose it and the next is guaranteed — worst case 180 wishes
4-starAt least one 4-star every 10 wishes

Pity carries over between same-type banners: 60 pity this banner continues next banner, so saving for a specific character is viable.

Where Primogems Come From

Daily commissions

60 Primogems / day, ~1,800 / month — the baseline

Events

420-1,000 each, 2-3 per patch — the big chunk

Spiral Abyss

1,200 / cycle (every 2 weeks) once your build is ready

Chests / Achievements

Open-world chests, 5-20 per achievement — adds up

A diligent F2P gathers about 8,000 Primogems (~50 pulls) per patch (~6 weeks); the Welkin Moon (daily 90) speeds saving noticeably.

Planning Rules

Four don'ts: don't pull the weapon banner (needs ~240 pulls plus Epitomized Path unless you overflow), don't pull the standard banner (use free Acquaint Fates), don't break for a weak limited, don't trickle 10-pulls. Save at least 160 pulls (25,600 Primogems) before a target so a lost 50/50 still lands it.

Beginner Order

  • Clear story with free units first: Traveler / Amber / Kaeya / Lisa clear all story — no need to pull early.
  • Main DPS before supports: one usable carry (or a 4-star National team) beats scattering pulls on solo units.
  • Fill team gaps: already have a Pyro DPS? Don't pull another — take a limited that fills a missing element or role.
  • Trade Stardust for Fates: monthly shop exchange for Intertwined Fates — never waste it.

Common Mistakes

  • Pulling on every banner: resources thin out, you are always one short, and nobody reaches constellation.
  • Forcing the weapon banner: separate pity plus Epitomized Path; terrible F2P value when refined 4-stars already suffice.
  • Ignoring the Welkin: 90 Primogems daily compounds; long-term better than occasional top-ups.

Quick Answers

How does Genshin pity work?

Character banner hard-pities a 5-star at 90 wishes; soft pity spikes the rate from ~74; the 50/50 gives a 50% featured chance first, then guarantees it next — worst case 180 wishes. Pity carries across same-type banners.

How many Primogems guarantee a character?

Save at least 160 pulls (25,600 Primogems) before a target so a lost 50/50 still lands it. A diligent F2P reaches that in about 1.5-3 patches.

Is the weapon banner worth it?

Strongly no. Separate pity plus Epitomized Path needs ~240 pulls to secure; most F2P players are fine with refined 4-star weapons — keep Primogems for character banners.

Which banner should a beginner pull first?

None at first. Clear story with free units, then save for a limited character that fills a real team gap; use free Acquaint Fates only on the standard banner.
