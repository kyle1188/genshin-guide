Kaveh Build & Teams: Dendro Core Detonator & Support Healer

Quick Answer

  • Kaveh is a 4★ Dendro claymore, an on-field Bloom/Hyperbloom support who generates Dendro Cores with attacks, then detonates them with his Burst.
  • Core sets: Flower of Paradise Lost 4pc (+40% Bloom/Hyperbloom DMG) or Deepwood Memories 4pc (30% Dendro RES shred).
  • Main stats: EM Sands / EM Cup / EM or Crit Circlet.
  • Best teams: Nilou + Xingqiu + Kaveh + Baizhu (Bloom); Kaveh + Fischl + Raiden + Xingqiu (Hyperbloom).
  • C4 is the value pick: Dendro Core burst DMG +60%.

How Kaveh Works

Kaveh's value comes from a two-step "generate → detonate" loop:

  • Normal Attacks and his Skill apply Dendro; hitting a wet target creates a Dendro Core.
  • His Burst "Painted Dome" detonates Dendro Cores in a radius, dealing Dendro AoE and healing himself (he is healed by the Core, not hurt by it).
  • His passive "An Architect's Undertaking" prevents his own Dendro Cores from damaging him on-field.

Team Comps

Bloom Team

Kaveh + Nilou (Hydro applicator) + Xingqiu (Hydro/Sub-DPS) + Baizhu (Healer/Dendro)

  • Nilou maintains Hydro + Xingqiu adds wet hits → Kaveh detonates Dendro Cores.
  • Kaveh heals from Cores; Baizhu's shield keeps him safe on-field.

Hyperbloom Team

Kaveh + Raiden Shogun (Sub-DPS) + Nahida (Dendro/EM) + Xingqiu

  • Raiden applies Electro → Cores turn Hyperbloom → Kaveh detonates for a big Dendro burst.
  • Nahida converts her EM to the whole team; more EM on Kaveh = more Hyperbloom DMG for everyone.

Constellation Priority

  • C4: Dendro Cores detonated by Kaveh deal 60% more DMG — the best value investment.
  • C6: During Painted Dome, his Normal/Skill hits auto-detonate nearby Cores, a big on-field DPS upgrade.

FAQ

Is Kaveh a main DPS or support?

He's an on-field sub-DPS / core support. He detonates Dendro Cores and heals, so he fits teams that want Dendro reactions without a pure carry.

Kaveh or Alhaitham?

Alhaitham is a pure Dendro carry with a higher ceiling; Kaveh is more of a support / multi-tool. With limited resources, Alhaitham scales harder, but Kaveh is more beginner-friendly (self-heals).

Can Kaveh be a healer?

Yes, but his healing only triggers when he detonates Cores, which needs steady Hydro application. Pure healing is weaker than Baizhu/Kokomi, but his secondary healing is very useful.

How to build a Bloom team without Nilou?

Use Xingqiu (Hydro + mitigation) + Kaveh + Nahida + Zhongli/Kokomi. Xingqiu is the best F2P Hydro applicator.

Should Kaveh prioritize EM or Energy Recharge?

Main stat goes to EM (amplifies reactions and healing); ER comes from substats to keep his Burst cycling. Prioritize EM on main slots, ER on subs.
