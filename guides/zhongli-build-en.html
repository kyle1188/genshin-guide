Genshin Zhongli Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Zhongli — the Geo Archon of Liyue — gives Genshin Impact its strongest shield. His Jade Shield scales off his Max HP, grants near-total interruption immunity, and on expiry drops a universal 20% RES shred on every element. This guide covers weapons, the Tenacity of the Millelith set, HP stacking, team comps, and why he is the safest "plug him in anywhere" unit in the game.

Note: Zhongli's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Zhongli Is Worth Building

His value is the freedom to ignore enemy mechanics entirely:

  • Near-unbreakable shield: the Jade Shield scales off his Max HP and absorbs all damage types, plus 100% interruption resistance — your carry never gets knocked out of their animation.
  • Universal 20% RES shred: when the shield breaks or he casts his Burst, enemies take -20% to ALL elemental and physical RES. That buff applies to the whole team's damage, every rotation.
  • Petrify control: his Burst stuns most enemies and deals burst AoE Geo damage — great for interrupting dangerous attacks.
  • Zero rebuild: he does his job at C0 with a 3-star weapon. The most F2P-friendly top-tier unit in the game.
Because his shield scales off HP and his RES shred is universal, a single Black Tassel + Tenacity build makes almost every carry ~20% stronger and far safer. That is why he is on nearly every account's bench.

Plug-and-play

Talent Priority

Level in this order — his shield is everything, his burst is utility:

  1. Elemental Skill (Dominus Lapidis) → 9–10. The Jade Shield's strength scales with talent level and his HP. This is his core value; max it.
  2. Elemental Burst (Planet Befall) → 8–9. Petrify + the 20% RES shred on cast. Level for comfort and burst damage.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. Only level if you run a Geo DPS build.

How the Shield + Shred Works

Zhongli's kit is two buttons that make the rest of the fight trivial:

  • Cast Skill (hold): creates the Jade Shield on the active character. Shield HP = base + a large chunk of his Max HP. While it holds, the team has 100% interruption immunity.
  • Shield expiry / Burst: when the Jade Shield breaks (or he casts Planet Befall), enemies in range get -20% RES to all elements and physical for 20s. This is a team-wide damage buff, not just his.
  • The rotation: Zhongli E (shield up, +20% team ATK from Tenacity) → swap to carry and unload → the shield naturally expires mid-fight, re-applying the RES shred. Re-cast as needed.
Key rule: his RES shred is universal — it boosts Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, Electro, Anemo, Geo, and physical alike. That is why he slots into literally any team.

Common Mistakes

  • Building ATK instead of HP. His shield scales off HP. An ATK-stack Zhongli has a paper shield and wastes his kit.
  • Skipping the Skill levels. The shield strength scales with talent level — leaving it at 1-6 leaves real mitigation on the table. Level it to 9-10.
  • Forgetting the RES shred. It only applies when the shield breaks or he bursts. Make sure the shield actually expires (or use his Burst) each rotation to keep the -20% up.
  • Over-investing in a 5-star weapon. Black Tassel (3-star, free) already gives more HP than most 5-stars for shielding. Spend primogems elsewhere.
  • Running him as a damage dealer by default. His support value (shield + shred + ATK buff) almost always outweighs his personal damage.

Don't Have Zhongli Yet?

He is a limited 5-star, so you may not own him. For shields and interruption immunity, Noelle (a 4-star Geo on the beginner list) is a solid F2P shielder, and Diona or Layla cover Cryo shielding. They don't bring his universal RES shred, but they keep your carry alive. Build them while you wait for his banner.
