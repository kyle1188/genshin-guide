Genshin Impact Weekly Bosses Guide 2026 – Strategies, Drops & Farming Routes

Weekly bosses drop the ascension materials for your 5★ characters and Northlander Billets for forging. This guide covers every weekly boss, their attack patterns, recommended elemental counters, and the most efficient farming routes.

⚡ Version Note: Weekly bosses updated for 7.0 Snezhnaya. New bosses may be added in future patches — check back for updates.

Stormterror Dvalin (Mondstadt)

Recommended elements: Pyro, Cryo, Geo (break shield) | AR requirement: 25+

Phase 1 — Flight: You'll fly behind Dvalin shooting cursed wounds. Use updrafts to catch up. Destroy two wounds to land for Phase 2.

Phase 2 — Platform battle:

  • Breath attack: Dvalin sweeps left-to-right or right-to-left. Attack the exposed claw during the wind-up.
  • Bite attack: Dvalin lunges forward — his claws are exposed. Switch to heavy DPS characters.
  • Platform cracks: Blue glowing areas deal Anemo DMG over time. Use wind currents to glide between safe platforms.
  • Meteor fall: Run outside the blue circle indicator before it lands.

F2P team: Xiangling + Kaeya + Barbara + Amber (Anemo resonance from Amber's talent helps with flight).

Premium team: Diluc + Xiangling + Qiqi + Venti (Venti's burst breaks claws fast).

Andrius, Wolf of the North (Dragonspine)

Recommended elements: Cryo, Pyro, Electro | AR requirement: 30+

Key mechanics:

  • Phase 2: Andrius enters a Cryo state — attacks faster and more aggressively. Keep HP high.
  • Charge attacks sweep the platform. Dodge sideways, not backward.
  • A healer is mandatory — the multi-hit combos can one-shot unshielded characters.

F2P team: Kaeya + Amber + Barbara + any DPS. Pyro resonance from Amber boosts damage.

Azhdaha (Sumeru)

Recommended elements: Pyro, Electro, Cryo | AR requirement: 40+

Key mechanics:

  • Phase transition: Azhdaha burrows underground — wait for him to re-emerge.
  • Geo shields can be broken with claymore or Geo attacks.
  • The Seismic Hammer attack has a wide AoE — dodge sideways, not away.
  • Phase 2: Azhdaha gains a Geo core on his back. Destroy it to enter vulnerability phase.

F2P team: Xiangling + Bennett + Xingqiu + ANY geo/geo-free DPS. The National team works exceptionally well here.

La Signora (Inazuma)

Recommended elements: Cryo (Phase 1) / Pyro (Phase 2) | AR requirement: 40+

Key mechanics:

  • Phase 1 (Pyro form): Use Cryo characters or Hydro to trigger evaporate. The heat gauge builds — stay mobile.
  • Phase 2 (Cryo form): Use Pyro characters. Find and activate Pyro devices on the arena to warm up and break her ice shield.
  • She has high defense when her shield is up — wait for openings.

Tip: Bring a dedicated heater character (Diluc, Bennett) for Phase 2. Using the wrong element wastes time and risks a wipe.

Raiden Shogun (Musou Shinseki)

Recommended elements: Cryo, Pyro, Geo | AR requirement: 45+

Key mechanics:

  • Her Musou no Hitotachi is an unavoidable massive slash — bring a healer/shielder.
  • She has very high Electro RES — avoid using Electro DPS (it will do almost nothing).
  • Destroy the Electro Eye that appears during her burst to enter vulnerability.

F2P team: Xiangling + Bennett + Xingqiu + any non-Electro DPS. National team is ideal here.

Scaramouche (Wandering Soul)

Recommended elements: Multiple elements needed | AR requirement: 45+

Key mechanics:

  • Use elemental matrices on the arena to buff yourself and debuff Scaramouche.
  • Hydro matrix → increases your Hydro DMG. Pyro → Pyro DMG. etc.
  • Knock him off the platform during his wind attack to trigger a vulnerability window.

Tip: This fight rewards elemental awareness. Activate the matrix matching your main DPS element before engaging.

All-Devouring Narwhal (Fontaine)

Recommended elements: Pyro, Cryo, Electro | AR requirement: 50+

Key mechanics:

  • The Narwhal has a parallel dimension phase — you must defeat the Devouring Energy clone to damage the main boss.
  • Bring high survivability characters (Zhongli, Furina) for the AoE-heavy phases.
  • Dodge the massive water wave attacks — they cover most of the arena.

F2P team: Xiangling + Bennett + Xingqiu + Dendro Traveler (for Hyperbloom reactions during the clone phase).

F2P Farming Strategy

You can only claim weekly boss rewards once per week (Monday 4:00 AM server time). Plan your runs:

  • Week 1: Stormterror (most accessible, good for beginners)
  • Week 2: Azhdaha (drops all billet types — best efficiency)
  • Week 3: Andrius (claymore/polearm billets)
  • Week 4: La Signora or Raiden Shogun (depending on which weapon you need)

Cost-saving tip: The first 3 weekly boss claims per week cost only 30 Resin instead of 60. Prioritize your most-needed bosses within that window.

Common Mistakes

  • Using the wrong element: Signora requires Cryo in Phase 1 and Pyro in Phase 2. Using Pyro in Phase 1 is almost useless.
  • Ignoring the healer: Weekly bosses hit hard. A dead DPS deals zero damage. Bring a healer or shielder.
  • Running bosses without armor upgrades: Equip the right artifact sets (Gladiator, Noblesse, etc.) before attempting weekly bosses — undergeared characters will struggle.
  • Skipping the vulnerability window: Every boss has a period where they're stunned or exposed. Save your bursts for that window, not before.

Quick Answers

What weekly boss should I farm first as a new player?

Stormterror Dvalin — it's in Mondstadt (the first region), has the lowest AR requirement (25+), and drops valuable swords/bows/catalysts for early-game characters.

Can F2P players defeat all weekly bosses?

Yes. While 5★ characters make fights easier, every weekly boss is cleable with well-built 4★ characters and proper elemental counters. National team (Xiangling+Bennett+Xingqiu) plus any DPS handles most bosses.

Why do some weekly bosses cost 30 resin and others 60?

The first 3 weekly boss claims each week cost 30 resin. After that, it jumps to 60. Plan to run your highest-priority bosses within the 30-resin window.

How often do weekly bosses reset?

Every Monday at 4:00 AM server time. You can challenge them as many times as you want, but rewards are only given once per reset cycle.

Which weekly boss drops the most useful materials?

Azhdaha — he's the only boss that drops ALL Northlander Billet types (Sword, Bow, Claymore, Polearm, Catalyst), making him the most efficient for forgeable weapon farming.
