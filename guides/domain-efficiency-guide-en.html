Time Management Tips

- Single run: Normal ~1-2 min, Hard ~2-3 min

- Daily resin: Prioritize hard mode for better quality

- Weekly reset: Mon/Thu, plan routes ahead

- Batch farming: Multiple domains per session > spread out

FAQ

Q: What should I farm before AR45?

A: Before AR45, focus on talent mats and local boss materials. Artifacts can be farmed systematically after AR45.

Q: What's the difference between Normal and Hard domains?

A: Hard mode has a chance to drop golden artifacts. Normal only drops purple. Always choose Hard.

Q: How much resin should I farm daily?

A: Daily recommendation is 160 resin (8 domains). Weekend extra purchases can supplement.
