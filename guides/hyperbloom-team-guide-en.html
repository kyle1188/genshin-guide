Genshin Hyperbloom Team Guide: Mechanics, Core Units & F2P Teams

Hyperbloom is the friendliest damage archetype added with Dendro: no CRIT, no ATK stacking, no demanding rotations, and Kuki Shinobu single-handedly covers both triggering and healing. For low-investment and low-spender accounts it is one of the best returns in the game. This guide covers the reaction logic, damage scaling, unit roles, and two ready-to-copy teams.

Quick Answers

  • How it triggers: Dendro + Hydro make seeds, Electro hits a seed, a homing shot hits the enemy.
  • Damage source: only the Electro trigger's level and EM — ATK and CRIT do nothing.
  • Cheapest team: Kuki Shinobu + Xingqiu + Dendro Traveler + Collei, all 4-star.

Hyperbloom Mechanics in Brief

The reaction chain:

  1. An enemy under Dendro + Hydro triggers Bloom and spawns Dendro Cores (seeds);
  2. Up to 5 seeds can exist at once; the oldest detonates when the cap is hit, dealing small AoE Dendro damage to everyone nearby;
  3. Hitting a seed with Electro triggers Hyperbloom: the seed turns into a Sprawling Shot that homes in on the nearest enemy.

Hyperbloom damage has three properties that define how you build:

  • Scales only with the trigger's level and Elemental Mastery — base reaction damage grows with character level, multiplied by an EM factor;
  • Ignores ATK, CRIT Rate/DMG and Electro DMG Bonus, and cannot crit;
  • Ignores enemy DEF but is reduced by the target's Dendro RES.

The takeaway: pour every resource into the Electro trigger — level 90, EM 800+ — and stop. Everyone else just applies Dendro, applies Hydro, and keeps the team alive.

The Three Team Roles

Hyperbloom teams follow a strict three-part structure:

RoleJobRecommended units
Dendro applierKeep Dendro on enemiesNahida (best), Dendro Traveler, Collei, Baizhu (survivability)
Hydro applierMake seeds with DendroXingqiu (most reliable), Yelan, Kokomi (AoE + heal), Barbara (free), Ayato
Electro triggerZap the seedsKuki Shinobu (best 4★), Raiden Shogun (auto E), Fischl, Dori, Cyno (on-field)
Flex slotShield / buff / extra auraZhongli, shielder, second Hydro or Dendro, Kazuha/Sucrose (CC)

Key point: the Electro unit is the one whose EM matters, because seed damage belongs to whoever triggers it. With two Electro units, make sure the high-EM one goes first (e.g., Raiden's coordinated attacks usually win the trigger).

Ready-to-Copy F2P Teams

Formula: Electro trigger + Hydro applier + double Dendro + survival.

Team 1: Ultra-budget (all 4-star)

  • Kuki Shinobu (trigger + heals), Xingqiu (Hydro), Dendro Traveler + Collei (Dendro)
  • Double-Dendro resonance adds EM; put 4-piece Deepwood Memories on Traveler/Collei to shred Dendro RES and push seed damage;
  • Works for both single-target and multi-wave fights — the classic budget Abyss team.

Team 2: Premium Hyperbloom (5-star core)

  • Kuki Shinobu + Xingqiu/Yelan + Nahida + Zhongli/Kokomi
  • Nahida's application and teamwide EM buff far outclass Dendro Traveler;
  • For an on-field driver style, swap Kuki for EM Raiden — her E coordinated attack triggers roughly every 0.9s for much higher seed frequency.

Rotation (Team 1 example):

  1. Apply Dendro with Nahida/Traveler's Skill or Burst;
  2. Cast Xingqiu's Burst — rain swords keep applying Hydro and spawn seeds;
  3. Activate Kuki's Skill — the ring converts seeds into Sprawling Shots;
  4. Swap back to the Hydro driver and normal-attack to keep the aura going.

The mantra: Dendro first, Hydro second, Electro last — never let Electro hit the enemy body first (that becomes Quicken/Overloaded instead of Hyperbloom). Save the Electro for the seeds.

Artifacts & Weapons

Kuki Shinobu (trigger) — all EM, all day:

  • Artifacts: 4-piece Gilded Dreams (consistent) or 4-piece Flower of Paradise Lost (highest ceiling for Bloom-line reactions, but needs ramp-up);
  • Main stats: EM / EM / EM on Sands, Goblet, Circlet;
  • Weapon: Iron Sting (craftable) is the best budget pick; Sapwood Blade works as an alternative;
  • Substats: EM > ER > HP.

Nahida — 4-piece Deepwood Memories (−30% Dendro RES helps both seeds and her own damage). No Nahida? Let Dendro Traveler or Collei wear the Deepwood set.

Xingqiu / Yelan — normal damage build; Xingqiu wants Sacrificial Sword for burst uptime. No EM needed here.

Common Questions

  • Do the seeds hurt me? Naturally detonated seeds damage everyone. Converting them with Electro on time turns them into homing shots — damage dealt, self-damage avoided.
  • How does the Raiden driver version play? Full-EM Raiden triggers seeds via her Skill's coordinated attacks while you normal-attack on field — stable and strong in Abyss.
  • Why is my Hyperbloom damage low? Check three things: is the trigger level 90, is EM above 800, and are enough seeds actually being hit by Electro?

Summary

Hyperbloom's biggest advantage is that it needs one well-built character, not a whole hyper-invested team: a level-90 full-EM Kuki Shinobu with Xingqiu, Dendro Traveler and Collei clears Abyss-ready content. When resources are tight, maxing Kuki first is one of the highest-return investments you can make.
