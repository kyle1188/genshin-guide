Genshin Beginner Mistakes: 10 Costly Errors and How to Avoid Them

Quick answer
  • The three most expensive mistakes: farming artifacts before AR45, using Fragile Resin while your Resin is near cap, and splitting resources across too many characters. Each one can cost you a month or two of progress.
  • Upgrade order: character level → weapon level → talents → artifacts. The first three are guaranteed value; artifacts are a gamble.
  • Resin regenerates 1 point every 8 minutes, caps at 200, and produces about 180 per day. Overflow is pure loss — a day offline throws away nine domain runs.
  • Before AR45, artifacts have exactly one job: get the right main stats on your main DPS and stop at +8 to +12.

2. Resin: the three ways you lose it

Do not let it overflow

Resin regenerates 1 point every 8 minutes, caps at 200, and takes 26 hours 40 minutes to fill from zero. Normal daily play generates about 180 points. A day and a half offline permanently deletes a full bar — at 20 Resin per domain run, that is nine runs you will never get back.

Do not pop Fragile Resin at cap

Fragile Resin restores Original Resin, but anything above the cap simply disappears. Rule: spend down below 100 Resin first, then use it.

Do not burn all your Fragile Resin before AR45

AR45 is where artifact domains start guaranteeing 5-star drops. Every Fragile Resin spent earlier buys you 4-star pieces you will feed away two weeks later. Hold them until you know which set you actually need.

3. Upgrade order: why artifacts come last

Character level, weapon level and talents are guaranteed value — every Resin spent converts into stats. Artifacts are probabilistic; two weeks can produce zero improvement.

PriorityItemWhy
1Character level and ascensionBase ATK, reaction damage, and whether you can even enter content
2Weapon level and refinementWeapon base ATK multiplies into total ATK — best value per Resin
3Key talentsTalents multiply damage directly; every level is real percentage
4Artifact main statsGet the main stats right before worrying about substats
5Artifact substatsThe actual endgame; take this seriously after AR50

When a character feels weak, check in this order: level → weapon at 90 → key talent levels → Energy Recharge → artifact main stats → substats. Nine out of ten problems are in the first three.

4. Artifacts: five frequent mistakes

Follow the level caps

Before AR45 you replace artifacts faster than you can level them. Set a hard rule: 3-star max +8, 4-star max +12, 5-star max +16. Only break it when that one piece is what lets you clear a boss or an Abyss floor.

Main stat beats set bonus

A set bonus is worth roughly 15–25% damage. A wrong main stat costs 40–50% of that slot. "Four-piece set with a DEF sands" loses to "two-piece with the correct main stat" every time.

Do not level everything you pick up

Check three things first: right slot, right main stat, at least two useful starting substats. All three pass, level it. Otherwise it is fodder.

Do not hoard 3-star pieces

Past AR25, 3-star artifacts have exactly one use: feeding better pieces. Hoarding them just leaves you short on fodder.

Do not trash bad 5-stars

After AR45 the Artifact Strongbox lets you trade three 5-stars for a chosen set. Bad 5-star pieces are raw material.

5. Wishes: three decisions people regret

  • Pulling every banner. Pity carries across banners, but abandoning a half-built pity count wastes everything invested. Pick a target and save enough to guarantee it.
  • Chasing the weapon banner early. Its worst-case cost is far higher than a character banner, and weapons cannot carry a team. Characters first.
  • Pulling a banner you do not want for a 4-star. There is no guarantee for a specific 4-star, and you may wreck your pity count doing it.

6. Teams: why "a bit of everything" means "nothing works"

The classic beginner state: eight characters at level 60, talents at 3, weapons at 40, and nothing can clear a boss. The cause is diluted resources.

Build one team of four with this structure:

  • One on-field main DPS — the damage source, upgrade first.
  • One sub-DPS / off-field — deals damage without field time.
  • One buffer or shredder — an Anemo unit in 4VV, or an EM support.
  • One sustain — healer or shielder, so you can actually finish the fight.

Two hidden traps: not enough Energy Recharge (burst always just short) and stacking only ATK (no CRIT, so damage plateaus). See the Energy Recharge guide and stat priority.

FAQ

When should I actually use Fragile Resin?

At AR45 or later, once you know which artifact set you are farming. If either condition is missing, keep saving.

Should I farm zero artifacts before AR45?

Do not spend Resin on artifact domains. Quest, chest, weekly boss and event artifacts are enough to get by as long as the main stats are right. Levels, weapons and talents matter far more.

What level should I stop leveling an artifact at?

Before AR45: +8 for 3-star, +12 for 4-star, +16 for 5-star. Only go past that when the piece is what clears a wall.

I built the wrong character. Is it ruined?

No. Weapons and artifacts move to other characters; only talent books and ascension materials are locked to that character. Cut your losses and refocus on the main team.

What if I cannot spend all my Resin each day?

Weekly boss discounts first, then whatever ascension, weapon or talent material you are shortest on, then Ley Lines to empty the bar. Ley Lines are better than overflow.

Do I need a 5-star to progress?

No. Four-star teams clear all story and overworld content, and there are proven all-4-star Abyss clears. Five-stars buy efficiency, not permission.
