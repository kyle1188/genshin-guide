Genshin Aino Build: 4★ Hydro Claymore Lunar-Charged Sub-DPS

Aino is a V6.0 (Luna I) 4-star Hydro Claymore, a Nod-Krai genius mechanic. She's a Lunar-Charged Hydro sub-DPS — Q applies off-field Hydro + team-wide Elemental Mastery, Silken Moon's Serenade 4pc + Flame-Forged Insight (Claymore signature) + ER sands. She's a free 4-star alternative to Columbina.

Quick Answer

  • Role: Lunar-Charged Hydro sub-DPS + team EM enabler.
  • Weapon: Flame-Forged Insight (Claymore signature) > Favonius Greatsword > Makhaira Aquamarine (4-star).
  • Artifacts:Silken Moon's Serenade 4pc (first) / Noblesse Oblige / Cinder City Scroll.
  • Main stats: ER sands + EM cup + EM circlet.
  • Team: Zibai + Aino + Gorou + Linnea (Lunar-Crystallize).

Who Aino Is

Aino is a 4-Star Hydro Claymore released in Version 6.0 (Luna I). A genius mechanic from Nod-Krai and creator of Ineffa. She is a Lunar-Charged Hydro sub-DPS — Q applies off-field Hydro + team-wide Elemental Mastery, and she is a free 4-star alternative to Columbina for Lunar Hydro application.

Talent Priority

Elemental Burst (Q) > Elemental Skill (E) > Normal Attack. Q is the core Hydro sub-DPS + team EM buff (Structured Power Booster makes Q DMG scale off 50% of EM); E generates + restores; NA is minor.

Constellation Value (gcsim)

Baseline (C0 + Favonius Greatsword) 137,264 DPS. C1 +3.1%, C2 +4%, C3/C4 +0%, C6 +10.1% (+6% over C5). C6 is the big jump; C1–C4 have low marginal value — F2P should pull C0, chase C6 if surplus.

Is Aino Worth Pulling

Skip if you already own Columbina; if you lack a Lunar Hydro applicator + team EM enabler and want to save 5-star resources, Aino is a cheap, high-value 4-star, essential for Zibai / Flins Lunar teams.
