Complete Domain Farming Guide: From Priority to Resin Efficiency

Resin is Genshin's most precious resource. This guide teaches you how to prioritize talent domains, boss runs, and EXP domains to maximize every point of resin spent.

Domain Types Overview

Domains (副本) are the primary way to spend Resin to obtain character EXP and talent materials. They fall into these categories:

--------------------------------
Adventure EXP Domain (阅历本)20Character EXP books
Mora Domain (摩拉本)20Mora
Talent Material Domain (天赋本)20Character talent materials
Boss Domain (周本)40Character ascension materials + elite materials
Weapon Domain (武器本)20Weapon ascension materials

Domain Priority Ranking

F2P/micro-spend players should prioritize Resin in this order:

Priority 1: Talent Material Domains

  • Fixed weekly drops — do not miss
  • Saturday/Sunday: 4-star talent mats; weekdays: 5-star
  • Strategy: Choose based on your main DPS character this week

Priority 2: Adventure EXP Domains

  • Heavy demand early game, reduced later
  • Low resin cost (20), extremely cost-effective
  • Strategy: Farm heavily before AR 60, then only as needed

Priority 3: Boss Domains

  • Produce rare character ascension materials
  • Refresh every Monday/Thursday, 3 runs per week
  • High resin cost (40), but has pity system

Priority 4: Mora Domains

  • Only needed before AR 45 (character ascension)
  • Mora can be obtained through daily commissions afterwards

FAQ

Q: What domains should F2P players prioritize weekly?

A: Priority 1 — Talent domains (60 resin) + Boss domains (120 resin) = 180 resin total. Save remaining resin or spend on EXP domains.

Q: Should I farm talent domains on Saturday or Sunday?

A: Weekends drop 4-star talent mats; weekdays drop 5-star. Choose based on which character you're maining.

Q: How long do I need to keep farming Mora domains?

A: Until AR 45 (character ascension). After that, use resin for talent domains — Mora comes freely from dailies.

Q: What is the fastest way to increase AR?

A: Complete world quests, daily commissions, and resin domains every day. You can reach AR 45 in 2–3 weeks of consistent play.
