Genshin Serenitea Pot Guide: Trust Rank, Realm Currency & Free Primogems

Most players treat the Serenitea Pot as a decorating toy and leave its real value on the table: weekly Transient Resin, a steady Mora drip, free companionship EXP, and 20 Primogems per character from companion gifts. This guide skips aesthetics and squeezes every reward out of your realm.

  • Unlock? Meet the AR requirement, finish the Liyue Archon quest, then do 'A Teapot to Call Home' to get the gadget.
  • Level Trust Rank? Craft each furnishing for the first time (buy blueprints, make one of each). Max 10; cap 2,400 currency at TR10.
  • Max currency rate? Push one realm to 20,000 Adeptal Energy — furnishing sets are the fastest way — for 30/hour.
  • Free primogems? Place each character's favorite set, invite them, and talk to them for 20 Primogems each — over 1,000 total.

How to Unlock the Serenitea Pot

The housing system arrived in 1.5. To unlock it: meet the Adventure Rank requirement, complete Archon Quest Chapter I Act III "A New Star Approaches", then pick up the world quest "A Teapot to Call Home" in Liyue. Finishing it earns you the Serenitea Pot gadget from Madame Ping — equip it from your inventory and step into your own realm anytime.

First visit: choose one of the three starter realm layouts — Floating Abode, Emerald Peak or Cool Isle (more unlock later; you can switch anytime, so do not overthink it). Tubby, the teapot spirit, runs the shop, crafting, realm switching and reward claiming.

The Two Numbers That Matter: Trust Rank & Adeptal Energy

About 90% of the teapot's value is gated behind two stats. Get these right and decorating is optional.

  • Trust Rank: raised only by crafting each furnishing for the first time; max 10. It gates purchasable blueprints, craft queue slots, realm areas, companion slots and your Realm Currency cap (2,400 at TR10). Fastest path: buy cheap blueprints and craft one of each.
  • Adeptal Energy: raised by placing more and better furnishings; it sets your Realm Currency income tier. Only the highest-energy realm counts, so pile one realm to 20,000 energy and you earn the full 30 currency/hour — no need to decorate every realm.

The fastest energy jump is furnishing sets: placing every piece of a matched set (a full tea house, a full courtyard) grants a large bonus on top of the pieces — the cheapest way to leap from mid-tier to top-tier income.

Spending Realm Currency: Weekly Must-Buys

Currency accrues passively until the cap, so claim it often from Tubby. Best value first:

  • Transient Resin (Trust Rank 6+): one per week from the Realm Depot — a Fragile Resin with an expiry date. The single best teapot purchase.
  • Mora: weekly limited stock from Tubby's shop; one of the strongest passive Mora sources in the game.
  • Character EXP / weapon ore: handy top-ups when resin is tight.
  • Furnishing & set blueprints: prioritize blueprints that complete sets — they raise both energy and trust.

Companions: Friendship EXP & Free Primogems

  • Friendship EXP: companions placed in your realm gain companionship EXP passively over time; claim it from the Trust Rank menu (weekly cap). A zero-resin friendship farm — park characters you rarely field here.
  • Companion gifts (the real primogem payout): every character has favorite furnishing sets. Place their set, invite the character, talk to them and collect 20 Primogems + ascension/talent materials per character. Across the roster this is over a thousand free Primogems.

Chubby the Traveling Salesman & Replication

  • Chubby visits every Friday 04:00 to Monday 04:00, selling pets, rocks and rare Spincrystals; on weekends your friends can trade with him in your realm too.
  • Replication: once enabled in settings, you can share your layout as a Replica ID or paste someone else's to copy their whole build — provided you own every furnishing it uses.

Common Mistakes

  • Currency stops accruing at the cap — claim from Tubby every login.
  • Do not spread furnishings across realms; one realm at 20k energy maxes your income.
  • Dozens of cheap dupes beat one finished set? No — finish sets first.
  • Do not ignore the fields: three field types × 4 crops cover scarce materials like Silk Flowers.

Summary

The teapot is one of the few systems that pays you back forever. Spend a few minutes reaching 20k energy, grab the weekly resin and Mora, collect every companion gift, and park unused characters inside for free friendship. Set it up once, collect forever.
