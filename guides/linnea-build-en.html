Genshin Linnea Build: 6.5 Geo Bow Support

Quick Answer

  • Role: Off-field support/sub-DPS, core of Lunar-Crystallize system.
  • Weapons: Golden Frostbound Oath (5★ signature) > Favonius Warbow / Snare Hook (4★) > Slingshot (F2P).
  • Artifacts: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon 4pc (best) / Silken Moon's Serenade / Husk of Opulent Dreams.
  • Stats: DEF sands + DEF/Geo DMG goblet + CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: Zibai + Linnea + Columbina + Illuga (best) or Navia + Linnea + Columbina + Furina.

Who Is Linnea?

Linnea is a 5★ Geo bow character released in Version 6.5, serving as the core enabler of the Lunar-Crystallize reaction system. Her damage and healing scale off DEF, converting normal Crystallize into Lunar-Crystallize for RES shred, EM buffs, and team-wide healing.
