Genshin Gadget Guide: Must-Have Gadgets, How to Get Them & Usage Tips

Gadgets are Genshin Impact's craft-once, benefit-forever tools: the NRE for eating mid-combat, the Portable Waypoint for custom fast travel, the Red Feather Fan for faster gliding — all free, gated only by city Reputation levels. This guide ranks the must-craft list, how to unlock each, and the details that make them click.

  • What's the #1 gadget? NRE (Menu 30) — Mondstadt Reputation 5. Eat healing/revival food with one button mid-combat, everywhere except the Spiral Abyss.
  • Exploration must-haves? Portable Waypoint (Mondstadt Rep 6, lasts 7 days), Red Feather Fan (Inazuma Rep 5, +30% gliding), and each region's Treasure Compass.
  • What's the Parametric Transformer? A weekly "trash to treasure" machine: dump up to 150 quality points of spare materials, charge it with elemental attacks, receive random EXP books, Mora and ascension materials. From the AR31 "Tianqiu Treasure Trail" quest.
  • How do I equip gadgets? Inventory → Gadgets tab → Equip; activate with Z on PC. Reputation blueprints are crafted at the blacksmith/alchemy bench; most are permanent, resonance stones are consumables.

What is the gadget system

Gadgets live in a dedicated equipment slot — Z on PC activates the equipped one, freely swappable. Three sources:

  • Reputation rewards (the bulk): city Reputation levels unlock blueprints → craft at the blacksmith or alchemy bench.
  • World quests: e.g. Memento Lens (Inazuma's Sacred Sakura Cleansing), Parametric Transformer (Liyue's Tianqiu Treasure Trail).
  • Events/shops: limited-time or special-currency exchanges, sometimes rerun later.

Key fact: most gadgets are permanent after one craft with cheap materials — the real gate is Reputation level, so grinding reputation IS collecting gadgets. Speed that up with our Reputation guide.

Combat & survival picks

  • NRE (Menu 30): two slots — healing dish plus revival dish; auto-switches to revival when someone drops. The lifesaver for weekly bosses and overworld wipes (banned in Spiral Abyss and Imaginarium Theater).
  • Warming Bottle (Dragonspine): Sheer Cold protection for Dragonspine expeditions, unlocked via Frostbearing Tree levels.
  • Parametric Transformer: weekly. Feed up to 150 quality points of spare junk (failed dishes, mints, mob drops), charge with elemental attacks, receive random EXP books, Mora, talent or ascension materials. Set a weekly reminder.

Exploration picks

  • Portable Waypoint: drop it at farming loops or event zones for 7 days; placing a new one destroys the old — don't misclick.
  • Red Feather Fan: +30% glide speed, short cooldown — covers most long glides and gliding events.
  • Wind Catcher: bank 5 wind charges, spawn a current when you need vertical lift (high oculi and chests); complements Venti/Wanderer-style mobility.
  • Treasure Compass: points to the nearest chest; 30s cooldown on a find, 5s when clear. Mandatory for 100% exploration.
  • Resonance Stone: circles one uncollected oculus (consumable — craft a batch of 5 to keep stocked).

Combo play: compass points to chests → open them for Encounter Points → resonance stone sweeps oculi — one loop, two progress bars.

Tips & common mistakes

  • One slot at a time: only the equipped gadget works — keep the compass on while exploring, swap to NRE before boss fights.
  • Don't waste the waypoint on domain entrances: domains already teleport; farm spots and event zones pay far better.
  • Transformer: feed junk only: outputs are random — failed dishes, mints and mob drops are the intended fuel, never ascension materials.
  • Resonance stones are consumables: one use each; craft in batches. Compasses are permanent — prioritize them.
  • Refill the NRE: food doesn't restock itself — check both slots before hard content.

Summary

Gadgets are the "Reputation levels buy lifelong convenience" trade: Mondstadt Rep 5's NRE and Rep 6's Portable Waypoint are the two best unlocks in the game, the Parametric Transformer prints free loot weekly, and every region then hands you a fan, compass and stones as you level its Reputation. Materials are cheap — the only real job is clearing weekly reputation tasks. Once the kit is in, daily life plays like a different game.
