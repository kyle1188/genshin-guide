Genshin Lunar Reactions Explained: Nodkrai Moon Phases & Elemental Interactions

Quick Answer

  • What are Lunar Reactions: A unique elemental interaction system introduced in Nodkrai (6.0), where Hydro/Cryo combined with Electro/Dendro produce extra effects.
  • Core mechanic: With characters like Columbina on field, a "Moon Phase" state triggers, boosting team output and restoring Nightsoul.
  • Moon Phase system: Cycles through four forms — new moon, first quarter, full moon, last quarter — each granting a different buff.
  • Build impact: Core Lunar Reaction characters should prioritize EM; teams need to cover both Hydro and Electro to meet trigger conditions.

Lunar Reaction Base Mechanics

Lunar Reactions are a unique elemental interaction system of the Nodkrai region, implemented starting with version 6.0. Unlike traditional elemental reactions, it is a compound system driven by Moon Phases, and requires specific character combinations to activate.

Types of Lunar Reactions

  • Hydro + Electro → Lunar Ripple: Generates a zone that continuously heals
  • Cryo + Dendro → Crystal Symbiosis: Produces a shield while applying elemental attachments
  • Electro + Hydro → Current Resonance: Amplifies Elemental Burst damage and shortens CD
  • Dendro + Cryo → Lunar Bloom: AoE heal and cleanse negative states

Moon Phase System in Detail

The Moon Phase is the core driver of Lunar Reactions and rotates automatically with in-game time:

Lunar Reactions & Character Builds

Columbina Build Essentials

  • Weapon: Best choice is a 5-star Hydro DMG or ER catalyst
  • Artifacts: Center on 4pc Pale Vessel, pursuing dual EM + ER stats
  • Stat priority: EM > ER > Crit Rate > HP

Team-Building Ideas

  • Core lineup: Columbina (Hydro) + Yae Miko (Electro) + Nahida (Dendro) + Zhongli (Geo)
  • Alternative: Swap some slots with Xingqiu or Shenhe, keeping Hydro/Electro/Dendro coverage

Common Misconceptions

  • Misconception 1: Lunar Reactions need no specific characters — A Lunar-resonance character must be on field to activate the full mechanic
  • Misconception 2: Moon Phases can be switched manually — Moon Phases are determined entirely by in-game time and cannot be controlled
  • Misconception 3: Every element can trigger Lunar Reactions — Only the four Hydro/Cryo-with-Electro/Dendro pairings work

Related Questions

How are Lunar Reactions triggered?

Your team must include a Lunar-resonance character (such as Columbina) and have two complementary elements present (Hydro+Electro, Cryo+Dendro, etc.) for it to trigger.

Do Moon Phases affect combat?

Yes. Different Moon Phases grant different buffs, so run high-intensity combat within the skill-loop window that matches your team.

Which characters suit Lunar Reaction teams?

Columbina is the core, with Yae Miko, Nahida, and Xingqiu as strong secondary picks.

Is the Lunar Reaction related to the Nightsoul system?

Indirectly. Both are region-exclusive systems, but with different triggers and effects, and their buffs can stack simultaneously.
