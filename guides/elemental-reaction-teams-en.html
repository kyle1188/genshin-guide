Complete Elemental Reaction Team Guide: From F2P to Endgame

Elemental reactions are the core of Genshin Impact damage. This guide covers all seven reaction types (Vaporize/Melt/Hyperbloom/Freeze/Electro-Charged/Overload/Swirl) with team templates, key characters, and rotation tips — from F2P builds to endgame completion.

Elemental Reaction Team Composition Guide

Genshin Impact's core gameplay revolves around elemental reactions. Mastering reaction-based team compositions lets you deal massive damage with limited characters. This guide breaks down teams by reaction type, with templates, key characters, and playstyle tips.

FAQ

Q: What's the best reaction team for F2P?

A: Hyperbloom (Nahida + Kuki + Xingqiu + Zhongli) or Vaporize (Hu Tao + Xingqiu + Bennett) — both need only 1 five-star.

Q: Which characters are universal reaction enablers?

A: Xingqiu (Hydro), Bennett (Pyro + heal + ATK buff), Kazuha (Swirl + shred), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom trigger).

Q: Is Vaporize or Melt higher damage?

A: Vaporize (sub-DPS triggers 2x) is slightly higher on average, but Melt has nuclear burst potential when main DPS triggers 2x.

Q: Do transformation reactions scale with EM or Character Level?

A: Transformation reactions (Bloom/Overload/Electro-Charged/Shatter/Swirl) scale ONLY with the triggering character's EM and level — not the target's DEF or resistance.
