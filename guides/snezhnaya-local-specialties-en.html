Snezhnaya Local Specialties: 5 Materials, 48-Hour Respawn & Farm Routes

Snezhnaya, the Cryo nation opened in Version 7.0, ships with five new local specialties: Golden Fern, Flockingweed, Frostfairy Flower, Kristallfold and Fairy Wormwood. They are the required ascension materials for the region's characters (168 of each per character to reach level 90), and several can now be grown in the Serenitea Pot. This guide covers locations, the character mapping, farming tips and respawn mechanics.

  • Which characters need them? Golden Fern → Vesna, Flockingweed → Alyosha, Frostfairy Flower → Odette / Vodyanitsa, Kristallfold → Mitya, Fairy Wormwood → Valeriy. One full ascension needs 168.
  • Fastest way to find them? Keep Odette or Alyosha in your party (works off-field) to mark nearby specialties on the minimap; use the Item Tracker's "Go to Collect" for the big clusters.
  • Respawn & cultivation? 48-hour respawn per node (independent of the daily reset). Golden Fern, Flockingweed and Frostfairy Flower became Serenitea Pot–cultivable in the 7.2 official notes.

Zone-by-Zone Farming Notes

Golden Fern

Clusters in the warmer western zones — Flamefeather Valley and Rankova Zorya hold the main ~80 open-world spawns. The small reddish-gold leaves get lost in autumn grass; bring Odette so the minimap marks them for you.

Flockingweed

Around Fellfrost Peak, roughly 74 spawns. It is Alyosha's own ascension material, and he is one of the two marking characters — farming your own supply is the most convenient loop.

Frostfairy Flower

The coldest areas: Snezhnograd's gardens, the road to the Sanctuary of Grief, the abandoned station south of Pale Crown Palace and White Birch Snowgrave. The pale flower is nearly the color of snow, so without a marker character you are basically walking blind.

Kristallfold

Mitya's ascension material, clustered around Novokitezhgrad. As a 7.2 character, demand only exists after his banner — no rush to grind it early.

Fairy Wormwood

Valeriy's material, concentrated at Teplo Oasis. Small zone, dense spawns — you can clear most of it in a single loop.

Farming Tips: Two Marker Characters + the Train

Marker characters: Odette and Alyosha both have a passive that shows nearby Snezhnaya specialties on the minimap, and it works while they are off-field — just keep one in the party. Note that the 7.2 characters Mitya and Valeriy do not have this passive, so you will need Odette/Alyosha to find Kristallfold and Fairy Wormwood for them.

Item Tracker: open any specialty in your inventory → "Go to Collect" — blue rings on the map mark the large clusters with live counts.

Train system: the rail network links the major stations; transferring between zones by train is far faster than overland, and each car has a crafting bench.

Sheer Cold: Snezhnaya's cold meter is harsher than Dragonspine's and applies to the whole party. Warm up at kristall outcrops or heat sources; a Pyro character in the party helps.

48-Hour Respawn, Co-Op and Serenitea Pot

Respawn: every node regrows 48 hours after you pick it — independent of the 4 AM daily reset. Hitting the 168 goal = ~70-80 open-world nodes × 2-3 respawn cycles + co-op and pot top-ups.

Co-op free-lunch: join other players' worlds and pick their already-grown nodes (owner permission required). It is the fastest way to close the gap; plan around the 48-hour clock.

Serenitea Pot: the 7.2 official notes added Golden Fern, Flockingweed and Frostfairy Flower as pot-cultivable crops (Golden Fern has a seed entry). Kristallfold and Fairy Wormwood are not yet in the pot catalog.

Purchasing: there is no confirmed permanent merchant for Snezhnaya specialties — open-world gathering, co-op and cultivation are the reliable channels.

FAQ

What are the five Snezhnaya local specialties?

Golden Fern, Flockingweed, Frostfairy Flower, Kristallfold and Fairy Wormwood.

Which characters use Snezhnaya specialties for ascension?

Golden Fern → Vesna; Flockingweed → Alyosha; Frostfairy Flower → Odette and Vodyanitsa; Kristallfold → Mitya; Fairy Wormwood → Valeriy. Each character needs 168 total from level 1 to 90.

How long do local specialties take to respawn?

48 hours per node, counted from when you picked it — not tied to the daily 4 AM server reset.

How do I find them quickly?

Keep Odette or Alyosha in your party (the passive works off-field) so nearby specialties are marked on the minimap, then use the Item Tracker's "Go to Collect" to jump to big clusters.

Can Snezhnaya specialties be grown in the Serenitea Pot?

Golden Fern, Flockingweed and Frostfairy Flower are pot-cultivable since the 7.2 official update (Golden Fern has a seed). Kristallfold and Fairy Wormwood are not yet.
