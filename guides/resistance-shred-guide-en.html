Genshin Resistance Shred Guide: RES Formula, Sources & Damage Gain

Why does 4-piece Viridescent Venerer say “−40% RES” yet only add around 28% damage? And why does the same shred suddenly double your numbers against a Ruin Guard? The answer is the three-tier resistance multiplier. Here is the math, every source, and how to use it.

Quick answer
  • Every enemy has an independent base RES per damage type; the standard value is 10%
  • Three-tier multiplier: RES < 0 → 1 − RES/2 (half effect); 0 ≤ RES < 75% → 1 − RES; RES ≥ 75% → 1 ÷ (4 × RES + 1)
  • Because negative RES only counts at half strength, −40% against a 10%-RES enemy ends at −30% and gives roughly +27.8% damage, not 40%
  • Main sources: VV 4pc 40%, Zhongli Jade Shield 20% (all elements + Physical), Xilonen Skill 36% (PECH + Geo), Superconduct 40% (Physical), Deepwood Memories 30% (Dendro)
  • Same-name effects do not stack — only the highest applies; different sources do stack
  • RES shred and DEF shred are two independent brackets and can both be maxed out

How RES converts to damage

Resistance does not subtract damage directly — it converts into a damage multiplier, and the formula depends on which tier the final RES lands in:

Final RESDamage multiplierNote
< 0% (negative)1 − RES / 2Still helps, but at half efficiency
0% – 75%1 − RESLinear zone, where shred is most valuable
≥ 75%1 ÷ (4 × RES + 1)High-RES zone, sharply diminishing

Final RES = base RES + bonus RES − shred. In other words, shred subtracts from the number; it is not a proportional discount.

Once RES is pushed below zero it enters the half-effect zone. Take the standard 10% base RES:

Shred scales with enemy RES

The higher the enemy’s RES, the more a shred is worth. Against a 70% Physical RES enemy:

That is why against high-RES bosses a shred support beats stacking more ATK — a printed 40% can double your damage on a 70%-RES target.

What does not stack

  • Same-name effects do not stack; only the highest applies. Two VV holders still give one −40%.
  • Deepwood Memories does not stack with itself — multiple wearers still give one −30%.
  • Different sources do stack, added into the same final-RES pool (e.g. VV 40 + Xilonen 36 = −76%).
  • Watch the element limits: VV cannot shred Geo / Anemo / Physical, and Xilonen cannot shred Anemo / Dendro. Match your shred to your main DPS’s damage type.

Using shred in practice

  1. Match the shred to your main DPS. A Pyro carry wants VV swirling Pyro — VV will never shred Geo.
  2. Prioritise shred against high-RES enemies. Far better value on bosses and tanky Abyss floors than another ATK layer.
  3. Do not double up on the same effect. One VV is enough; use the second slot for DMG bonus, Energy, or healing.
