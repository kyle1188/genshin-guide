Is the Genshin Battle Pass Worth It? Rewards, Time to Max & Weapon Picks

The Battle Pass is one of Genshin’s most debated purchases: some call it a progress accelerator, others say the Welkin Moon is strictly better. The real answer depends on how you play. This guide breaks down the free track, Gnostic Hymn and Gnostic Chorus, the math behind the value, how long maxing takes, and which of the ten BP weapons to pick at level 30.

Quick Answers

  • Worth it? Yes if you reach level 50 each patch and need Mora, EXP books or ore; choose Welkin instead if you only want pulls.
  • Time to max? Weekly EXP is capped near 10,000; full dailies plus weeklies take roughly 6 weeks.
  • Weapon pick? Serpent Spine for claymore carries, Black Sword/Wolf-Fang for sword, Deathmatch for polearm, Solar Pearl for catalyst, Viridescent Hunt for bow.

The Three Tracks

The Battle Pass resets every patch and unlocks at Adventure Rank 20. The three tracks serve very different players:

TrackPriceCore contentsBest for
Sojourner (free)FreeSmall Primogems, Acquaint Fates, ore & MoraEveryone
Gnostic Hymn~$9.99Big Mora/EXP/ore, talent books, Primogems, one 4★ CRIT weapon at Lv30Active low spenders
Gnostic Chorus~$19.99All Hymn rewards + 10 instant levels, namecard, blueprint, 5 Fragile ResinCosmetic lovers / catching up

Key mechanic: buying a paid track is retroactive — every paid reward for the levels you already earned unlocks at once, so buying late never wastes progress.

Is Gnostic Hymn Worth It? Two Player Profiles

Buy it if you:

  • Log in daily and reliably finish the pass (level 50) each patch;
  • Constantly lack Mora, Hero’s Wit or weapon enhancement ore — the resin-equivalent value of one patch’s materials is huge;
  • Need a dependable CRIT-rate 4★ weapon (CRIT weapons are hard to replace before your artifacts are built).

Skip it if you:

  • Only care about pulls — the Welkin Moon (~$5) gives far more Primogems per dollar;
  • Play irregularly and often finish below level 50, wasting paid rewards;
  • Already have your weapon roster and material stockpile covered.

One line summary: Welkin for pulls, Battle Pass for materials and weapons.

How Long to Max: EXP and Mission Planning

  • The pass caps at level 50 and resets each version;
  • EXP comes from daily and weekly missions, with a weekly cap of about 10,000 ("This BP Period" missions bypass the cap);
  • Clearing dailies plus weeklies takes roughly 6 weeks, so most players finish near patch end;
  • Unclaimed rewards expire when the patch ends — the safest purchase moment is the day you hit 50, then claim everything in one go.

The Level-30 Weapon Bounty: How to Pick

All BP weapons carry a CRIT Rate substat — a rare and valuable source of crit on 4-star gear. The pool has grown over versions and now offers ten weapons (the original five plus a second set). Choose by your main DPS’s weapon type:

WeaponTypeBest forNotes
Serpent SpineClaymoreDiluc, Eula, Beidou, Arataki IttoWidely considered the best 4★ claymore
The Black SwordSwordKamisato Ayaka, Keqing (NA/CA sword DPS)Self-healing plus solid offense
Wolf-FangSwordSkill/Burst-focused sword usersStrong on sub-DPS too
Solar PearlCatalystNingguang, Klee on-field DPSBalanced ATK/CRIT catalyst
DeathmatchPolearmHu Tao, Cyno, RosariaOne of the most useful crit polearms
The Viridescent HuntBowVenti, TartagliaPull-in cyclone helps vs groups

Simple logic: look at your current carry’s weapon type and crit needs first. If your main team is already geared, pick for the next character you plan to build. Serpent Spine tops most tier lists for claymores — but never take the “best” weapon you cannot use.

Should You Upgrade to Gnostic Chorus?

Gnostic Chorus adds only: 10 instant levels, the patch namecard, a furnishing blueprint and 5 Fragile Resin. The core rewards are identical to Gnostic Hymn while the price roughly doubles. Unless you collect cosmetics or desperately need to finish before the patch ends, it is almost never worth it. The community consensus: buy Gnostic Hymn, skip Gnostic Chorus.

Summary

Bottom line: active players who hit 50 and need materials get one of the best single purchases in the game from Gnostic Hymn; pull-focused players should buy Welkin; value seekers always skip Gnostic Chorus. And always reach level 50 before paying, so every dollar lands on rewards you are guaranteed to collect.
