Planning Recommendations

For F2P Players

Focus on collecting free primogems—you can reach 2000–3000 per month through daily commissions and events. Save up to 160 pulls (25,600 primogems) before committing to any banner.

For Low-Spenders

If a featured character is one you truly want, buy the Battle Pass first (it includes fragments), then supplement with a Monthly Card if needed. Don't buy both unless you're certain about the upcoming banner.

For Whales

Consider purchasing the Omnirange Blessing offer whenever available—it provides an excellent return on investment. Also, use primogems to stockpile extra resources for Spiral Abyss rewards.

FAQ

Q: Can I buy both Monthly Card and Battle Pass at the same time?

A: Yes, but new players should prioritize. Buy Battle Pass when a desired character is featured, otherwise start with the Monthly Card for steady income.

Q: Is there a cap on stored primogems?

A: No. You can accumulate indefinitely—saving for 160+ pulls is recommended before wishing on a featured banner.

Q: When is the best time to buy Battle Pass?

A: When the current Banner features a strong 5-star character you want. It guarantees fragments toward that character plus exclusive weapons and some primogems.
