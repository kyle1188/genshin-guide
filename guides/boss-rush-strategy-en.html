Complete Boss Speedrun Guide: From Beginner to 1-Minute Clears

Mastering boss speedrun techniques dramatically reduces farming time. Understanding mechanics, picking the right team, and proper rotation are essential skills.

Core Speedrun Principles

PrincipleDescription
------------------------
Mechanic KnowledgeKnow Boss attack patterns, dodge proactively
Team OptimizationRight elemental reactions and DPS timing
Resource ManagementSkill CD and energy allocation
PositioningMinimize damage taken, maximize DPS windows

Weekly Boss Speedruns

Azhdaha

  • Weakness: Dendro/Electro (pierces Geo shield)
  • Key mechanic: Geo pulse phase needs quick breaks
  • Recommended: Nahida + Fischl + Xingqiu + Zhongli
  • Target: Under 90 seconds

Wolf of the North

  • Weakness: Cryo (high DMG after shield break)
  • Key mechanic: Shield phase needs Cryo break
  • Recommended: Ayaka + Xingqiu + Bennett + Sucrose
  • Target: Under 60 seconds

Cryo Hypostasis

  • Weakness: Pyro (Phase 2 requires Pyro)
  • Key mechanic: Ignition phase needs fire resistance
  • Recommended: Diluc + Xingqiu + Bennett + Sucrose
  • Target: Under 50 seconds

Pyro Hypostasis

  • Weakness: Hydro
  • Key mechanic: Hydro phase needs consistent application
  • Recommended: Furina + Xingqiu + Bennett + Kazuha
  • Target: Under 55 seconds

Maguu Kenki

  • Weakness: Electro (Susano no Okami phase)
  • Key mechanic: Break Susano first, then main body
  • Recommended: Raiden + Xingqiu + Xiangling + Bennett
  • Target: Under 100 seconds

Speedrun Team Templates

Rapid Strike (Universal): Main DPS + Applicator + Buffer + Survival

  • Example: Hu Tao + Xingqiu + Bennett + Zhongli

Reaction Team (Transform): Dual trigger + Group + Survival

  • Example: Kuki + Xingqiu + Kazuha + Kokomi

Switch Team (Burst): Multi-switch + ER + Buff

  • Example: Raiden + Xingqiu + Xiangling + Bennett

FAQ

Q: Do I need constellations for speedruns?

A: No. Proficiency matters more than constellations. With mechanic knowledge, C0 can speedrun.

Q: Do weapons matter for speedruns?

A: They help but aren't decisive. 4-star weapons are sufficient. Key is team comp and rotation.

Q: Not enough weekly materials?

A: Focus on the boss drops for your current character. Don't spread resin too thin.
