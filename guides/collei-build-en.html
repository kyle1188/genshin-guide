Collei Build & Teams: 4-Star Dendro Bow Off-Field Applicator & Shred

Quick Answer

  • Collei is a 4★ Dendro bow, an off-field Dendro applicator who casts a boomerang Skill and a sustained Dendro Burst zone.
  • Core set: Deepwood Memories 4pc (30% Dendro RES shred on hit, works off-field, near-permanent uptime).
  • Main stats: ER Sands (she is very energy-hungry, 200% threshold), Dendro/EM Cup, Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: ER (to threshold) > EM > Crits > ATK.
  • Best teams: Collei + Fischl + Raiden + Kazuha (Aggravate); Collei + Xingqiu + Yae/Raiden + Baizhu (Hyperbloom).

How Collei Works

Collei's value is off-field Dendro application plus shred, not personal damage:

  • Skill: throws a boomerang that applies Dendro on the way out and the way back, 12s CD.
  • Burst: creates a "Cuilein-Anbar" zone dealing Dendro for 3s; every Dendro reaction inside extends it 1s (up to +3s).
  • Deepwood 4pc shreds 30% Dendro RES after her Skill/Burst hit, off-field, so application + shred stay online almost constantly.

Team Comps

Aggravate Team (optimal)

Collei (Dendro/shred) + Fischl (off-field Electro) + Raiden Shogun (DPS/Electro) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Collei applies Dendro + Raiden applies Electro → target stays Quicken'd.
  • Fischl's Oz triggers Aggravate at high frequency; Raiden's Burst detonates.
  • Collei's Deepwood 30% Dendro RES shred amplifies the whole team's Dendro reactions.

Hyperbloom Team

Collei (Dendro/shred) + Xingqiu (Hydro) + Yae/Raiden (Electro) + Baizhu (healer/Dendro)

  • Xingqiu applies Hydro + Collei applies Dendro → Dendro Cores spawn.
  • Raiden/Yae apply Electro → Cores turn Hyperbloom.
  • Collei's Deepwood shred; Baizhu's shield keeps the on-field carry safe.

Constellation Priority

  • C4: Each Dendro reaction in the zone extends it 1s (up to +3s), lengthening shred uptime — best value.
  • C1: smoother Energy Recharge, lowers the ER requirement.

FAQ

Is Collei worth building as a 4-star?

Yes — she's the backbone of F2P Aggravate/Hyperbloom teams. Her Deepwood shred stays online almost permanently, pairing well with an Electro carry.

Collei or Nahida?

Nahida is the stronger 5★ EM-convert main support but requires a pull. Collei is the free, easy-to-get Dendro applicator. Use Nahida if you have her; otherwise Collei is the answer.

Should Collei prioritize ER or EM?

Main stat goes to ER (her Burst costs 60 energy); substats add EM. Push ER to 200%, then fill remaining substats with EM/Crits.

Does Collei need Deepwood?

No. For a pure applicator, Deepwood gives the best shred. As a sub-DPS, use Gilded Dreams or Tenacity. Without full Deepwood, 2pc + any 2pc works as a starter.

What teams fit Collei?

Teams with an Electro carry (Raiden, Keqing, Fischl) for Aggravate, or a Hydro applicator for Hyperbloom. Core idea: she applies Dendro, others apply Electro/Hydro.
