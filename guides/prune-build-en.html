Genshin Prune Build: 4★ Anemo Catalyst CC & Mastery Support

Prune is a V6.6 (Luna VII) 4-star Anemo Catalyst from Nod-Krai, an early-leak Prydwen. She's a CC + elemental-mastery utility: Viridescent Venerer for sub-DPS, Instructor for support, Song of the Lonely Moon is best value.

Quick Answer

  • Role: Anemo CC + mastery support / sub-DPS.
  • Weapon: Song of the Lonely Moon > Favonius Codex > Skyward Atlas.
  • Artifacts:Viridescent Venerer 4pc (sub-DPS) / Instructor 4pc (mastery support).
  • Main stats: EM sands + Anemo DMG cup / EM cup + EM circlet.
  • Team: Prune + Xiangling + Bennett + Zhongli/Furina.

Who Prune Is

Prune is a 4-Star Anemo Catalyst released in Version 6.6 (Luna VII). Hailing from Nod-Krai as a witch-hunter, she was known under the earlier leak name Prydwen. She is an Anemo support / sub-DPS built around crowd control and elemental mastery — Anemo DMG plus Swirl resistance shred.

Is Prune Worth Pulling

Skip if you already have Sucrose or Kazuha; if you lack a CC + mastery enabler and would rather not spend on a 5-Star support, Prune is a cheap, high-value 4-Star solution. Fully functional at C0, no constellation dependency.
