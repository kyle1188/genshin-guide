Genshin Expedition Guide: Rewards, Limits & Best Dispatch Setup

Expeditions are one of the few free resource streams in Genshin that keeps producing while you are logged out. They cost no Resin, do not occupy a party slot, and take about 30 seconds a day, yet they steadily feed you Mora, ore and cooking ingredients. This guide covers the unlock requirement, the slot limit, exact rewards for every duration, region locations and the characters that cut your timer.

Quick Answer
  • Unlock: Reach Adventure Rank 14, then talk to Katheryne in any major city and pick “Dispatch Character on Expedition”.
  • Slot limit: AR14 = 2 slots → AR26 = 3 → AR31 = 4 → AR36 = 5 (the cap).
  • Duration vs Mora: 4h = 625, 8h = 1,575, 12h = 2,500, 20h = 5,000. Per hour that is 156 / 197 / 208 / 250 — the 20-hour option wins.
  • One-login-per-day setup: send five 20-hour Mora expeditions before bed, claim at the same time tomorrow for a steady 25,000 Mora per day.
  • Two easy mistakes: dispatched characters can still fight (only the Traveler cannot be dispatched); rewards are never delivered automatically — claim them from Katheryne.

1. How to Unlock Expeditions

Push your Adventure Rank to 14, then visit the Adventurers’ Guild in any major city and talk to Katheryne. Every Katheryne across Teyvat shares one dispatch board, so you can send a character to a Liyue spot while standing in Mondstadt.

Each region has six dispatch points, broadly “one or two Mora spots, one ore spot and the rest local ingredients”. The three original regions are the most stable:

If you are building a specific character, prioritise the region that supplies their ascension material.

Newer regions such as Nod-Krai also have such characters — the dispatch screen flags them for you.

5. The Optimal 30-Second Routine

  1. Pick a fixed claim time. Choose the moment you always log in (bedtime works well) and treat it as your collection slot. If you only log in once a day, always pick 20 hours.
  2. Send every slot to a Mora spot. Five Mora expeditions × 5,000 = 25,000 Mora per day, 175,000 per week. Switch to ore spots only when you need forging materials (7–8 Crystal Chunks per 20-hour run).
  3. Swap one or two slots as needed. Short on a region’s specialty for an ascension? Point a slot at that region’s ingredient spot.
  4. Do not forget to claim. Rewards never expire, but they never land in your inventory on their own. Make “talk to Katheryne first” a habit.
  5. A bad dispatch is not fatal. You can recall a character, but the elapsed time is wasted and yields nothing.

6. FAQ

Do expeditions affect combat or the Spiral Abyss?

No. Since Version 1.1 dispatched characters can still join your party and enter domains or the Abyss. The Traveler is the only character excluded from dispatch.

Can expeditions give local specialties?

Yes. Mondstadt’s Sweet Flowers and Bird Eggs, Liyue’s Lotus Heads and Matsutake, Inazuma’s Lavender Melons and Seagrass and more are all obtainable — a 20-hour run returns 8–12 of them.

How do I unlock expeditions in newer regions?

Dispatch points follow Statues of the Seven: unlock the statue and that region’s expeditions appear on the board. A few Inazuma points additionally require specific world quests.

Summary

Expeditions come down to two numbers: 20 hours and five slots. Send all five to Mora spots before you log off, claim at the same time the next day, and you bank 175,000 Mora a week for almost no effort. Swap a slot or two when you need ore or ascension specialties. It is one of the highest-value habits in the game — skipping it is throwing away free Mora.
