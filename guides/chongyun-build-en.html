Genshin Chongyun Build & Teams: 4-Star Cryo Claymore & Frost Field Mechanic

Quick Answer

  • Chongyun is a 4★ Cryo claymore (Liyue exorcist, "Frozen Ardor"), a sub-DPS / Superconduct–Freeze enabler whose core mechanic is his Frost Field.
  • Core set: Blizzard Strayer 4pc (2pc +15% Cryo DMG; 4pc adds 3 × 7.5% Cryo DMG vs. Frozen/Cryo-affected foes) or Bloodstained Chivalry 4pc (physical-support build).
  • Main stats: ER or ATK% Sands / Cryo DMG or ATK% Goblet / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: ER > ATK > Crits > HP (Burst is only 40 energy, so ER is the whole job).
  • Key: the Skill field turns Sword / Claymore / Polearm Normal Attacks into Cryo and adds +8% ATK SPD; C2 cuts E/Q CD 15% inside the field.
  • Best teams: Chongyun + Eula + Yelan/Fischl + Kazuha (Superconduct Physical); Chongyun + Ayaka + Xingqiu + Jean (Freeze).
  • Weapon: Wolf's Gravestone is the top pick; F2P uses Favonius Greatsword for ER or Prototype Archaic for free ATK.

How Chongyun Works

His value = "applies Cryo for the team + turns physical DPS into Cryo":

  • Skill (Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost): a 10s Frost Field converts Sword / Claymore / Polearm Normal Attacks inside it to Cryo, and his "Steady Breathing" passive adds +8% Normal-Attack SPD.
  • Burst (Spirit Blade: Cloud-Parting Star): a 3-hit Cryo AoE at just 40 energy — a cheap, repeatable Freeze / Superconduct window.
  • So a physical carry (Eula, a claymore / polearm user) standing in the field deals Cryo and can trigger Superconduct (25% Physical RES shred) with an Electro partner.
  • He himself stacks ATK + ER, and the Cryo Goblet scales his AoE nuke.

Team Comps

Superconduct Physical Team (classic)

Eula (physical carry) + Chongyun (Cryo / converts her attacks) + Yelan/Fischl (Electro) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Chongyun's field turns Eula's Normal Attacks into Cryo; Yelan or Fischl applies Electro → Superconduct shreds 25% Physical RES for her Lightfall Sword nuke.
  • Kazuha groups + shreds RES; C2 cuts the whole team's E/Q CD by 15%.

Freeze Team (Ayaka core)

Ayaka (carry) + Chongyun (sub-DPS) + Xingqiu (off-field Hydro) + Jean (heal/shred)

  • Ayaka + Chongyun give the Cryo Resonance bonus (Freeze Crit +15%); Xingqiu keeps Hydro up → Freeze lock.
  • Jean heals + Anemo shred; C2 extends the whole rotation.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: E/Q CD −15% inside the Frost Field — the team-wide loop game-changer, top value.
  • C4: Energy refund on Cryo-affected hits (1 energy / 2s) — eases the team's ER pressure.
  • C6: a 4th Burst hit + 15% extra DMG to low-HP foes — a boss-damage finisher.

FAQ

Can Chongyun replace Zhongli?

Not directly. Zhongli gives a shield, Physical RES shred and Geo damage; Chongyun's job is turning physical attacks into Cryo to trigger Superconduct. They can coexist, but the physical carry then deals a Cryo reaction, not physical damage.

Why pair Chongyun with Eula instead of Klee?

Eula is a physical carry — Superconduct's 25% Physical RES shred scales directly into her Lightfall Sword. Klee scales a Pyro DMG Goblet, so converting her attacks to Cryo is low-value (she wants Melt instead).

Is Chongyun's ER demand high?

No — his Burst is only 40 energy. A Favonius Greatsword plus a few ER substats gets him to 150%+, castable every rotation.

Is Chongyun worth building?

Yes. Free 4-star, works in both Superconduct and Freeze teams, and C2 turns him into a team-wide loop upgrade — a zero-investment, high-ROI sub-DPS.

What exactly does C2 change?

Inside the Frost Field, every party member's E/Q CD is reduced by 15%. Paired with Yelan or Fischl's frequent Electro, Superconduct stays up near-permanently.
