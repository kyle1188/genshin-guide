Genshin Odette Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Stellar Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: 5-star Cryo sword off-field sub-DPS and enabler for the new Stellar reactions. She does not want field time.
  • Best weapon: Whitelake Frostfeather (signature). Craftable fallback: Emberwell, or Finale of the Deep.
  • Best artifacts: 4-piece Heart of the Furnace. Only one copy per team — the buff does not stack.
  • Stat targets: ATK 2400–2600, CRIT Rate 60–75%, CRIT DMG 150–175%+, ER 100–130%.
  • Talent order: Skill > Burst >> Normal Attack. Stop pulling constellations at C2.

What Odette Actually Does

Odette is the first 5-star built around Stellar Glimmer, the reaction layer that arrived with Snezhnaya in Version 7.0. She is not a Cryo main DPS. Her job is to sit off-field, keep Cryo applied, and convert two existing reactions into their Stellar versions:

  • Superconduct → Stellar-Conduct (Cryo + Electro)
  • Cryo Swirl → Stellar Swirl (Cryo + Anemo)

Both conversions scale off her ATK, which is why every part of her build pushes ATK and CRIT rather than Energy Recharge. She also buffs the team’s Stellar reaction damage, so a large share of her value never shows up in her own damage numbers.

Practical consequence: build her like a damage dealer, not like a support. ATK sands and ATK goblet beat an ER sands almost every time.

Best Artifacts and Stat Targets

4-piece Heart of the Furnace (the 7.0 set) is correct in nearly every team. 2-piece gives ATK; 4-piece grants ATK after Stellar triggers and raises nearby party members’ Stellar damage by 50%. It works while she is off-field, which is exactly her job.

Critical: the team-buff portion does not stack. Run exactly one Heart of the Furnace user. If Odette holds it, give Alyosha Noblesse Oblige instead.

Talent Priority and How Far to Pull

Skill > Burst >> Normal Attack. The Skill carries both her off-field damage and her Dance Double uptime; the Burst adds the Stellar damage buff and a second summon. Normal Attack can stay at level 1 in every standard setup.

Constellations: C2 is the practical stopping point for most accounts. Beyond that the gains are team-DPS oriented and expensive — better spent on the main DPS of your Stellar team.

Best Teams

Stellar-Conduct (Cryo + Electro)

  • Odette + Sandrone + Yae Miko + Alyosha — the reference composition. Sandrone carries, Odette and Yae Miko cover off-field, Alyosha heals and buffs.
  • Odette + Wriothesley + Yae Miko + Alyosha — works, but note Alyosha’s healing fights Wriothesley’s HP-loss mechanic.
  • Odette + Cyno + Yae Miko + Alyosha — Cyno benefits from her long off-field window.

Stellar Swirl (Cryo + Anemo)

  • Odette + Mizuki + Sucrose + Qiqi
  • Odette + Mizuki + Cryo Traveler + Faruzan
  • Odette + Mizuki + Cryo Traveler + Sucrose — the cheapest version.

If both Stellar types are active at once, Stellar-Conduct takes priority for Odette’s own Stellar damage. Decide which reaction you are building around before you build the team.

Three Mistakes Worth Avoiding

  • Two Heart of the Furnace users. The team buff does not stack — the second copy wastes a set slot that could hold Noblesse Oblige.
  • Stacking Energy Recharge. Her value is the Skill summon, not the Burst. 100–130% ER is enough; ER sands is almost always a mistake.
  • Judging her by her own damage numbers. A big chunk of her contribution is the Stellar damage she enables in teammates. Compare team clear times, not personal DPS.

FAQ

Is Odette a main DPS?

No. She is an off-field Cryo sub-DPS and Stellar reaction enabler. She wants minimal field time: cast the Skill, drop the Dance Double, swap out. Her damage and buffs both scale off ATK.

What is Odette's best weapon if I don't have her signature?

Emberwell, the Snezhnaya craftable sword, is the best free option for reaction builds. Finale of the Deep and high-refinement Harbinger of Dawn are solid budget picks. Avoid pure Energy Recharge swords.

Why do guides say only one character should wear Heart of the Furnace?

The 4-piece team buff to Stellar Glimmer damage does not stack. Running a second copy on Alyosha wastes the set - give him 4pc Noblesse Oblige instead so the team gets both effects.

Does Odette need Energy Recharge?

Barely. 100-130% is enough because the Dance Double comes from her Skill, and the Skill has a 15s cooldown with no energy cost. ER sands is a common build mistake on her.

Which Stellar reaction should I build around?

Pick one. Stellar-Conduct (Cryo + Electro) has the deeper roster right now - Sandrone, Yae Miko, Cyno, Alyosha. Stellar Swirl (Cryo + Anemo) is cheaper to assemble but has fewer enablers. If both are active, Stellar-Conduct takes priority for her own Stellar damage.
