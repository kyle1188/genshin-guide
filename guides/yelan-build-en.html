Genshin Yelan Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Yelan — the mysterious intelligence officer of Liyue — is one of the best off-field Hydro units in Genshin Impact. Her Elemental Burst drops a coordinated "Exquisite Throw" that follows your on-field character and fires Hydro attacks scaling off her Max HP, while her Skill marks enemies for bonus Hydro application. This guide covers weapons, the Emblem of Severed Fate set, HP stacking, her Vape / Neuvillette / Furina team comps, and the HP-scaling engine that makes her a top-tier sub-DPS in almost any team.

Note: Yelan's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Yelan Is Worth Building

She is a premier Hydro enabler whose value comes from applying Hydro without being on field, and whose damage scales off a stat most units ignore — HP:

  • Off-field Hydro at range: her Burst's Exquisite Throw trails the active character and fires Hydro attacks on every Normal Attack — she keeps your team wet while you play someone else.
  • HP scaling: both her coordinated attack and her Burst's damage scale off her Max HP, so building HP directly raises her personal damage.
  • Burst utility — "Adapts": her unique mechanic grants up to +50% coordinated-attack damage based on how many different elements are in the party. Pair her with a varied team for a big spike.
  • Universal fit: she drops into Vape, Mono Hydro, Neuvillette, and Furina teams with almost no rebuild — the definition of evergreen value for a Hydro account.
In a Vape or double-Hydro team, Yelan's HP-scaled coordinated attacks plus her "Adapts" buff typically add +20–35% team DPS while also being the team's Hydro driver. That is why she sits on almost every Hydro wishlist.

Best Weapons

Yelan wants HP% and a bow. Because her damage scales off HP, an HP weapon often beats a higher-base-ATK one for most builds.

5★ BiSAqua Simulacra

Signature · HP% + DMG

Her tailor-made weapon: grants a large HP% bonus and a flat DMG boost to all attacks. The clear best-in-slot, but the gap to F2P is small enough that you can skip it.

Obtain: weapon banner

5★ altPolar Star / Skyward Harp

CRIT-focused alternatives

Polar Star rewards her coordinated attacks with CRIT and DMG. Skyward Harp is a simple, strong CRIT bow you may already own. Both work if you lack the signature.

Obtain: weapon banner / standard banner

F2P ★Favonius Warbow

Top free-to-play pick

A 4-star bow that gives Energy Recharge and, on CRIT, produces a party-wide energy particle. It smooths her Burst rotation and helps the whole team — the default recommendation for a F2P Yelan.

Obtain: shop (starglitter)

F2PPrototype Crescent / Mouun's Moon

Craftable / 4★ alternatives

Prototype Crescent (craftable) gives a big ATK spike on charged hits. Mouun's Moon converts your ER into team-wide Burst DMG — strong if you over-stack ER. Both are free and easy to refine.

Obtain: blacksmith (crafting) / banner

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Normal Attack is the least of her worries:

  1. Elemental Burst (Depth-Clarion Dice) → 8–10. The Exquisite Throw and its HP-scaled damage are her entire kit; leveling it raises both personal damage and the "Adapts" scaling.
  2. Elemental Skill (Lingering Lifeline) → 8. The mark and bonus Hydro application matter, but the Burst matters more. Level for comfort.
  3. Normal Attack → 1 (or 6 if you ever run her on-field). Ignore otherwise.

How Her Coordinated Attack Works

Yelan's value is in the Exquisite Throw, not in the hit itself. Master this and she out-values most 5-stars:

  • Cast Burst: she drops an Exquisite Throw that follows the active character. Every Normal Attack they land fires a Hydro coordinated strike scaling off her Max HP and Talent level.
  • "Adapts" stacks: the more different elements in your party (up to 3 stacks), the higher the coordinated-attack damage — up to +50%. Build a varied team, not a mirror match.
  • Skill mark: her Elemental Skill tags an enemy so the next coordinated attack (and her own) hit harder and apply extra Hydro — use it to open the rotation.
Key rule: a Yelan whose Burst is down contributes almost nothing. Lock in her ER (and a Favonius Warbow if needed) so her Q is up every rotation.

Common Mistakes

  • Building ATK instead of HP. Her coordinated attacks scale off Max HP. An ATK-sponge Yelan deals a fraction of her potential.
  • Letting her Burst fall off. Without ER tuned (≈120–140%, or Favonius), her Q drops and she loses most of her value. ER is non-negotiable.
  • Running a mono-element team. Her "Adapts" buff needs different elements. A full-Hydro party throws away up to +50% of her damage.
  • Using an Elemental Mastery weapon. She does not consume reactions herself — EM bows like The Stringless do almost nothing for her.
  • Over-leveling her Normal Attack. It barely matters off-field. Spend the books on Burst/Skill.

Don't Have Yelan Yet?

She is a limited 5-star, so you may not own her. For off-field Hydro, Xingqiu (a free 4-star from Story Quests and the shop) is her best stand-in — he applies Hydro on-field and off, and pairs with the same Vape / Neuvillette teams. Candace (a 4-star on the beginner radar) is a weaker alternative who buffers Normal Attack Hydro DMG. Build either while you wait for her banner; they cover most Hydro teams until she arrives.
