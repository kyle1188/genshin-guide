Genshin Valeriy Build: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: Shield support + off-field Electro Sub-DPS, core component of Stellar-Conduct teams.
  • Best Weapon: Summit Shaper; alternatives include Skyward Blade / Calamity of Eshu.
  • Artifacts: 4pc A Day Carved from Rising Winds (highest ATK) or 4pc Heart of the Furnace (Stellar-Conduct specific).
  • Main Stats: ATK% Sands / ATK% Goblet / ATK% Circlet (full ATK build).
  • Key mechanic: Special Charged Attack at max Impact Momentum triggers Offensive Directive, granting team-wide Electro DMG bonus.

Character Overview

Valeriy is a 4-star Electro sword character from Snezhnaya, released in Version 7.2 (November 4, 2026). His core value lies in the trinity of shield + off-field Electro application + team-wide Electro DMG buff, making him an essential component of Stellar-Conduct teams.

Both his shield absorption and Offensive Directive buff scale with his ATK, making full ATK investment his only priority.

The Special Charged Attack "Direct-Fire Mortar" is Valeriy's core damage source and buff provider—level it first.

Constellation Analysis

  • C0: Fully functional; standard Stellar-Conduct configuration
  • C1: Special Charged Attack restores 6 Energy; first E after entering combat grants +150% Impact Momentum → 手感质变
  • C2 ★Key: Party gains +100 EM for 18s after using E; excess Impact Momentum converts to shield absorption → best stop point for most players
  • C4: Impact Momentum cap +30, charge efficiency +30%
  • C6: Special Charged Attack and Shell DMG +100%, enemies hit take +10% Electro CRIT Rate / +40% Stellar-Conduct CRIT DMG → whale endgame

Pull recommendation: C0 functional → C1 convenience → C2 quality of life (stop here) → C6 if budget allows

Team Compositions

Valeriy synergizes deepest with Mitya—he provides shield + Electro DMG bonus + off-field Electro application, allowing Mitya to stand and deal Stellar-Conduct damage uninterrupted.

Playstyle Tips

  • Open with E skill to deploy shield and accumulate Impact Momentum
  • At 40 Momentum, use Special Charged Attack to trigger Offensive Directive buff
  • Switch to your main DPS while Valeriy applies off-field Burst lightning
  • C2 party-wide EM buff stacks with Mitya's EM buffer for maximum Stellar-Conduct damage
