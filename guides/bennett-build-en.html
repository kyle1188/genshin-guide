Genshin Bennett Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 6.7 Phase 2 (live through Aug 11, 2026)

Raiden Shogun is rerunning now — and Bennett is the irreplaceable core of Raiden National (Raiden + Bennett + Xiangling + Xingqiu), the team that clears Spiral Abyss far above its cost. If you just pulled or plan to pull Raiden, building Bennett correctly is what makes the whole shell actually click. This guide covers exactly that: his weapon, artifacts, the ER breakpoint, and the constellations that matter.

Bennett — the down-on-his-luck adventurer from Mondstadt — is the single highest-usage support in Genshin Impact's history. He is a Pyro support who, with one tap of his Burst, provides a massive ATK buff, a healing circle, Pyro resonance, and a party battery all at once. He is also free (clear Spiral Abyss floor 3 to own him forever). This guide covers his best weapon (Aquila Favonia vs Favonius/Sapwood), the 4pc Noblesse Oblige set, the ~200% Energy Recharge breakpoint, why C1 is the real upgrade, the C6 Pyro-infusion caveat, and teams with Raiden / Ganyu / Hu Tao / Xiangling.

Note: Bennett's kit, weapon ranking, the 4pc Noblesse Oblige default, and the ~200% ER requirement are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 6.7 Raiden rerun above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of banner.

Why Bennett Is Worth Building

No other unit delivers this much team value from a single skill, at zero wish cost. If you build only one support, make it him.

  • Free and permanent: clear Spiral Abyss floor 3-3 and Bennett is yours at C0. You can then stack constellations from the Starglitter shop.
  • All-in-one Burst: his Q (Fantastic Voyage) drops a field that buffs ATK (scales with Bennett's base ATK) and heals anyone above 70% HP. One button fixes your team's damage and survival.
  • Pyro resonance + battery: standing in his circle fuels his own Burst and enables 2-Pyro teams, which is why he props up Xiangling, Hu Tao, and every Pyro carry.
  • Universally slottable: he fits Melt, Vape, Physical, and mono-element teams alike. His buff does not care about your main DPS's element.
A properly built Bennett's ATK buff alone can add 800–1,200+ flat ATK to your whole team — the single biggest raw damage multiplier most accounts ever get, for free.

Best Weapons

Bennett wants Base ATK (for the buff) and Energy Recharge (to Burst every rotation). He uses a sword.

BiS ★Aquila Favonia

Best-in-slot · 5★ sword

The premier choice: huge base ATK pumps his buff, and it self-heals on taking damage — perfect for a circle-tank. If you already own it, it is his default.

Obtain: weapon banner / standard

4★Sapwood Blade / Favonius Sword

ER tools · craftable / farmable

Sapwood Blade (Sumeru craft) grants ER and refunds energy to the team on Burst — excellent when Bennett is your battery. Favonius Sword generates CRIT particles to fix anyone's energy. Both are top F2P picks.

Obtain: forge / wishes

F2PPrototype Rancour / Harbinger of Dawn

Free damage / free crit

Prototype Rancour (forge) gives ATK% and a small on-hit buff. Harbinger of Dawn (3★) grants CRIT Rate + DMG while above 90% HP — and Bennett sits in his own heal circle at full HP, so the passive stays live. A classic budget build.

Obtain: forge / starter

Alt 5★Skyward Blade

ER-heavy option

If energy is your bottleneck and you have no Favonius, Skyward Blade's ER% plus on-Burst CRIT/energy helps him Burst off cooldown. Use only when you over-need recharge.

Obtain: standard banner

For essentially every Bennett, 4pc Noblesse Oblige is mandatory — its 2-piece already gives him ER, and the 4-piece adds a team-wide +20% ATK on Burst, stacking directly with his own buff.

Stat Targets — ER First, Then Base ATK

Bennett's two priorities are contradictory on paper, so target them in order: get enough ER to Burst every rotation, then pile Base ATK (from weapon + sands/circlet) to grow the buff.

SandsER% (or ATK% if ER covered)ER% (to breakpoint) > ATK% (base ATK) > HP% (for healing) > CRIT (for Favonius)
GobletHP% (or ATK% / Pyro DMG%)
CircletHP% / ATK% ( Healing% only if struggling)
ER breakpoint by team: with another Pyro (2-Pyro) or a battery like Raiden ≈ 180–200% ER; solo Pyro, no battery ≈ 220%+ ER; with Favonius/Sapwood + 2-Pyro you can dip to ~160%. Hit the breakpoint first — a Bennett who misses his Burst loses both the buff and the heal.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — his value is almost entirely the Burst:

  1. Elemental Burst (Fantastic Voyage) → 8–13. The ATK buff and heal scale directly with Burst level. Crown this first; it is his whole kit.
  2. Elemental Skill (Passion Overload) → 6–8. Mostly used to generate energy and for the occasional Nuke build. Level it second.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. He rarely swings his sword outside meme Nuke comps. Leave it low.

Constellations — C1 Is the Real Upgrade

Bennett's constellation curve is unusual: one constellation changes everything, and another is a trap.

  • C1 (Grand Expectation): removes the 70% HP gate on his ATK buff, so everyone gets the full buff regardless of HP. This is a massive, account-wide damage jump — the single best low-cost constellation in the game. Prioritize it.
  • C2–C4: small QoL (more Burst stats, longer circle, more ER). Nice but not essential.
  • C5: raises Burst talent level by 3 — a real buff-number bump.
  • C6 (Fire Ventures): infuses melee characters in the circle with Pyro. This can ruin Physical or Anemo carries (Xiao, Razor, Heizou) and break Normal-Attack-focused teams. Only unlock C6 if you know you'll never need those, or keep it at C5. Many players stop at C5 on purpose.
Key rule: Build toward C1, celebrate C5, and think twice before C6. A C6 Bennett that Pyro-locks your Xiao or Razor is a regret, not an upgrade.

Common Mistakes

  • Too little Energy Recharge. The #1 Bennett killer. Sub-180% ER without a battery means missed Bursts and a team that loses both buff and heal.
  • Building him as a DPS. His personal damage is tiny; his job is the circle. Stacking CRIT/DMG on him wastes the slot.
  • Skipping C1. Without C1, low-HP carries (Hu Tao at half HP, low-HP Ganyu) miss the buff entirely. C1 is where he becomes broken.
  • Blindly unlocking C6. The Pyro infusion sabotages Physical/Anemo on-fielders. Stop at C5 unless you're sure.
  • Letting someone else hog Noblesse. If a teammate already runs 4pc Noblesse, Bennett's own set is wasted. Coordinate the set across the team.

Don't Have Bennett Yet?

He is free: clear Spiral Abyss floor 3 (any half-decent team) and he is permanently yours. While you get there, Xiangling (free from the same floors) covers off-field Pyro, and Thoma / Dehya can substitute a Pyro circle in a pinch — but neither matches Bennett's ATK buff + heal combo. Build Bennett and Xiangling together; they are the foundation of the National shell that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they're the backbone of every Raiden National clear during the 6.7 rerun.

Quick Answers

What is Bennett's best build?

Bennett is the premier Pyro attack-buffer/healer. Use 4-piece Noblesse Oblige (HP%/ER sands, HP goblet, HP/CRIT circlet) - his burst buff scales on Base ATK, so weapon Base ATK matters more than substats. Aim Energy Recharge 200-250%. Signature is Aquila Favonia; best F2P is Sapwood Blade or Favonius Sword.

Why does Bennett need so much Energy Recharge?

His burst is the core of nearly every team, but the ATK buff scales on Base ATK while ER drives uptime. At 200-250% ER he can burst every rotation even as the sole Pyro.

What weapon Base ATK should I look for?

The ATK buff from Fantastic Voyage scales on Bennett's total Base ATK (weapon + character). High-Base-ATK swords like Aquila Favonia or Skyward Blade give bigger buffs; F2P Sapwood Blade and Favonius are efficient.
