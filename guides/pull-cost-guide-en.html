Genshin C6 Cost: Constellation & R5 USD Cost Tables

"How much does a max constellation actually cost?" answered in one line: C0 averages 12,160 Primogems (~$152); C6 worst case is 201,600 Primogems (~$2,520, no first-purchase double). Each constellation step is a full pity cycle (its own 50/50 + hard pity), so C6 = seven 5-stars; a signature-weapon R5 = five 5-star weapons. This page puts every constellation and weapon USD cost matrix, the F2P/whale payback schedules, and the "should you spend it" decision table on one screen. All figures use the site-wide 160 Primogems = 1 Intertwined basis (matching the Pity System guide). The USD cost matrix, the three-tier F2P payback schedule, and the "should you spend it" decision table on this page are original calculations compiled by Teyvat Starter Guide, not copied from any single data source.

  • Verdict: 90% of players need C0 only (~80% of the C6 experience). Spend on artifacts and talents first, not constellations.
  • C0 cost: average 12,160 Primogems ~ $152; worst case 28,800 ~ $360.
  • C6 cost: worst case 201,600 Primogems ~ $2,520 (no 2x).
  • Payback: pure F2P saves C0 in 4-6 months; with Welkin Moon, 2-3.

The C0 to C6 Constellation USD Cost Matrix

A constellation is "pulling the same limited 5-star again". Each step is a full pity cycle (its own 50/50 + 90-pull hard pity), so C6 is seven of them. Reading it: one limited at C0 is enough — average 12,160 Primogems is about 4-6 months of pure F2P saving. From C2 you enter the heavy-spend tier; C6 worst case is near $2,500. Constellations are polish, not a step change; only characters that genuinely leap at C1/C2 (see the Constellation Value guide) justify chasing C6.

How Much Is a Signature Weapon, R1 to R5

A signature-weapon R5 means owning five copies of that 5-star weapon. The weapon banner's 5-star expectation is ~125 pulls each with an 80-pull hard pity (separate from the character banner), so R5 is ~625 pulls ≈ $1,250 (no 2x). But R1 already captures 85-95% of the weapon's value, and R1 to R5 adds only 8-18% real DPS — most accounts stop at R1. For the full refinement curve and 4-star fallbacks, see the Signature Weapon Worth It guide.

The takeaway is blunt: everyone can save for C0, C2 needs light spending or a long wait, and C6 is whale territory. To hit C6 without a year-long grind, combine the light tier (~$15/month) with the first-purchase double.

Three Ways to Spend Less (Same Budget, +1-2 Stars)

  1. First-purchase double: every top-up is doubled, saving up to ~34% (C6 worst case drops from $2,520 to $1,663).
  2. Welkin Moon $5/mo: +3,000 Primogems, the best-value spend in the game (see the Welkin guide).
  3. Single-pull past 85: once you are in soft pity, single-pull and stop the moment a 5-star hits; ten-pulls waste the leftover pulls.

Bottom Line

In one line: sink money into C0 and artifacts, do not chase C6. C0 averages ~$152 and worst ~$360, saved in a few months F2P; C6 worst ~$2,520 is whale-only. Stop a signature weapon at R1 (R5 is a ~$1,250 chase only worth it for the free-routes). Add Welkin + first-purchase double and the same budget buys 1-2 extra 5-stars.
