Genshin Lyney Build Guide 2026: Weapons, Artifacts, Teams & Mono-Pyro Mechanic

Lyney — Fontaine's star magician — is a Pyro charged-attack bow DPS. His Level-2 aimed shot summons a Grin-Malkin Hat and stacks Prop Surplus, which powers up his Elemental Skill. Paired with two more Pyro teammates, his passive maxes out at +100% DMG. This guide covers weapons, artifacts, stat targets, team comps, and his unique Mono-Pyro mechanic.

Note: Lyney's kit, Mono-Pyro damage logic and artifact choice are long-term stable core gameplay and do not change much across versions. Constellations and banner schedules shift, but the build below holds. In-game data is authoritative.

Is Lyney worth building?

Lyney stands out as the benchmark of Pyro charged-attack teams for four reasons:

  • Highest direct Pyro DMG: with three Pyro teammates his passive stacks to +100%, dealing huge single-target and AoE burst.
  • Smooth charged-attack loop: the Level-2 shot summons a taunting hat and stacks Prop Surplus for Skill DMG, with a comfortable rotation.
  • Built-in HP management: charged shots and the Skill cost HP but also heal, so with Bennett survival is rarely an issue.
  • Strong without reactions: he relies on direct DMG, so he does not need a Hydro/Cryo applier — team building stays flexible.
Lyney is complete at C0 and very cost-effective. C2 adds +20% Skill DMG per Prop Surplus stack (capped +100%) and is the best value investment; C6 resets Skill cooldown on kill and adds +50% CRIT DMG to charged shots for a feel-changing spike. High constellations are not mandatory.

Talent priority

Lyney's Level-2 charged shot is his main damage source — level it first:

  1. Normal Attack (Card Force Translocation) → 9 or crown. The Level-2 shot summons the hat and stacks Prop Surplus — most of his damage.
  2. Elemental Skill (Bewildering Lights) → 8-9. Consumes Prop Surplus for AoE Pyro DMG and heals — key to burst and sustain.
  3. Elemental Burst (Wondrous Trick: Miracle Parade) → 8. Mobility and extra Pyro DMG; not the rotation core.

Prop Surplus & Mono-Pyro mechanic

Lyney's core: the Level-2 shot summons a Grin-Malkin Hat and stacks Prop Surplus (max 5); the Skill consumes stacks for bonus DMG. Understand this and damage jumps.

  • Level-2 charged shot: summons a taunting hat and adds 1 Prop Surplus stack. Stacks only build above 60% HP, and HP never drops below 60% this way.
  • Passive "Finale of the Dawnsight": +60% DMG vs Pyro-affected enemies, plus +20% per extra Pyro teammate (capped 100% at three Pyro). This is why Mono-Pyro is so strong.
  • HP management: the Skill consumes Prop Surplus to heal; with Bennett survival is trivial. Keep HP above 60% to keep stacking.
Key rule: Lyney's Skill is weak without Prop Surplus — keep stacking via charged shots and don't waste the Skill empty. Also, Mono-Pyro wants three Pyro characters (including Lyney); don't slot a Hydro/Cryo applier unless you deliberately switch to Vaporize/Melt.

Common mistakes

  • Stacking CRIT DMG but not ATK. Lyney scales on ATK%; 2000+ is the baseline or late-game damage feels flat.
  • Breaking the Mono-Pyro bonus with reactions. Fewer than three Pyro leaves the passive uncapped. If you want Vape, drop a Pyro and use Yelan/Xingqiu with a clear plan.
  • Skipping Bennett / Xiangling. Double Pyro is what maxes his passive; without them Lyney is just an average Pyro carry.
  • Letting HP drop too low. Below 60% you stop gaining Prop Surplus, hurting both Skill DMG and healing — watch the HP line.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge. The burst costs 80 energy; single-Pyro teams strain, so aim ~120% or use Pyro resonance.

No Lyney yet?

Lyney is a limited 5-star. To try a Pyro main DPS first, Hu Tao is the burst benchmark, Bennett is the universal Pyro support, and Xiangling gives off-field Pyro everyone wants — build the Pyro framework now and add Lyney on rerun. For another carry see Arlecchino.

Quick Answers

What is Lyney's best build?

Run 4-piece Marechaussee Hunter (ATK sands, Pyro DMG goblet, CRIT rate/DMG circlet); The First Great Magic is best, with Scion of the Blazing Sun or Song of Stillness as 4-stars. Target 60–70% CRIT Rate, 150%+ CRIT DMG, 2000+ ATK.

Why does Lyney fit a Mono-Pyro team?

His talent gives 60% base DMG bonus to Pyro-affected enemies, +20% per extra Pyro teammate (100% cap at three Pyro). A Bennett + Xiangling two-Pyro core is his highest-ceiling team.

What stats should I farm for Lyney?

Prioritize 2000+ ATK, 60–70% CRIT Rate, 150%+ CRIT DMG, ~120% Energy Recharge. He deals direct damage and ignores reactions, so Elemental Mastery is a minor stat.
