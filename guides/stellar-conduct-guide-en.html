Genshin Stellar Conduct Explained: Snezhnaya Electro-Cryo Resonance & Star Energy Layers

Quick Answer

  • What is Stellar Conduct: A unique elemental resonance mechanic introduced in Snezhnaya (7.0), where Electro combined with Cryo produces a special buff.
  • Core mechanic: When a Stellar Conduct resonance character is in the party, the Electro-Cryo reaction not only deals Superconduct damage but also stacks "Star Energy Layers."
  • Star Energy Layer effects: Each layer grants different EM, recharge, or damage bonuses; it stacks up to 3 layers.
  • Build impact: Characters like Mitya / Valeriy should prioritize Electro DMG and Elemental Burst DMG; teams also need a Cryo support.

Stellar Conduct Base Mechanics

Stellar Conduct is a combat system unique to the Snezhnaya region, implemented starting with version 7.0. On top of the traditional Superconduct reaction (Electro + Cryo), it adds a stacking buff mechanic, making the Electro-Cryo pairing a core strategic choice in Snezhnaya.

The Three-Layer Structure of Star Energy Layers

  • Layer 1: On trigger, raises team-wide Elemental Mastery by +20 points
  • Layer 2: On consecutive triggers, restores 15% Elemental Energy
  • Layer 3: At full stack, the next Elemental Burst deals +30% damage

Star Energy Layers decay over time (~0.5 layer per second), so keep applying alternating Electro / Cryo attachments in sustained combat.

Stellar Conduct & Character Builds

Mitya Build Essentials

  • Weapon: Best is Raiden Shogun's signature weapon, or a general Electro-DMG catalyst
  • Artifacts: Center on 4pc Raiden Shogun Legacy, pursuing Electro DMG bonus + Crit Rate
  • Stat priority: Electro DMG > Crit Rate > EM > ER

Valeriy Build Essentials

  • Role: Shield support; her main job is to keep Mitya alive so the Star Energy Layers hold
  • Artifacts: Either "Emblem of Severed Fate" or "Wanderer's Troupe" work, emphasizing HP and DEF
  • Key talent: Prioritize upgrading the Elemental Skill to guarantee shield strength and duration

Odette Build Advice

  • Function: Off-field Cryo application, pairing with Mitya's on-field Electro to continuously trigger Stellar Conduct
  • Weapon: A generic 4-star claymore such as Favonius Greatsword is fine

Common Misconceptions

  • Misconception 1: Stellar Conduct is the same as normal Superconduct — Stellar Conduct adds stacking buffs; normal Superconduct only shreds Physical RES
  • Misconception 2: Star Energy Layers can stack infinitely — Capped at 3 layers, and they naturally decay over time
  • Misconception 3: Any Electro/Cryo character can trigger the full Stellar Conduct — A Snezhnaya-resonance character must be on field to activate all effects

Related Questions

How is Stellar Conduct different from normal Superconduct?

Normal Superconduct only reduces the enemy's Physical RES; Stellar Conduct additionally grants stacking Star Energy Layer buffs, with richer effects.

Do Star Energy Layers disappear?

Yes. Each layer decays by about 0.5 per second, so keep triggering Electro/Cryo reactions in combat to maintain it.

Do Snezhnaya characters have to be on field together?

Not required, but at least one Snezhnaya-resonance character must be on field to activate the full Stellar Conduct mechanic.

Can Stellar Conduct and the Nightsoul system work at the same time?

Yes. They belong to different regional systems; meeting both conditions in a team grants a double buff.
