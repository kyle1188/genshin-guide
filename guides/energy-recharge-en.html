Genshin Energy Recharge Guide 2026: Burst uptime & How Much ER You Need

Energy Recharge decides whether your Burst comes up on cooldown. Same team, built right — smooth rotation; built wrong — dead Burst.

ER multiplies the energy you catch: an on-field same-element character gets 3 energy per particle, a different-element on-field gets 1, off-field same ~1.8, off-field different ~0.6. So funneling — support drops Skill then you swap to the DPS to catch particles — triples charge efficiency.

Emblem of Severed Fate: ER into Damage

4-pc Emblem turns ER into output: 2-pc gives +20% ER; 4-pc adds Burst DMG equal to (total ER - 100%) x 25%, capped at 75% (~300% ER). Xiangling, Xingqiu, Yelan, Raiden and Beidou all convert stacked ER into damage — one of the best F2P sets to farm.

Batteries and Funneling

  • Raiden Shogun: the ultimate battery; her Burst grants flat team energy that mostly ignores each unit's ER, slashing everyone else's requirement.
  • Bennett funneling: Bennett's E drops Pyro particles, then swap to Xiangling to catch them (same-element x3) — far faster than Xiangling self-generating.
  • Favonius / Sacrificial: Favonius crits spawn clear particles (team-wide); Sacrificial lets a unit cast Skill twice, doubling its own particle output.

Common Mistakes

  • Over-stacking ER: past the need it is diminishing returns. Abyss drops fewer particles than overworld, so +20-30% over the table is enough — don't sacrifice Crit for ER.
  • Ignoring on-field catching: a different-element catch yields only 1 energy; random swapping wastes it.
  • Treating off-field like on-field: Xiangling and Beidou take a ~40% off-field energy tax, so their need is naturally higher — give them what the table says.

Quick Answers

What is Energy Recharge?

ER multiplies the energy you gain from particles and orbs. An on-field same-element catch gives 3 energy, a different-element catch only 1; off-field catches are also discounted. More ER = Burst comes up faster.

How much ER does Xiangling need?

With a Bennett double-Pyro core, about 180-220%; solo Pyro without a fire battery she needs 260% or more. Pair The Catch + Emblem and funnel Bennett's Pyro particles to her.

Why is Raiden the best battery?

Her Burst grants flat team energy that mostly ignores each unit's ER, so everyone else's requirement drops sharply when she is on the team. She herself wants 200-250% ER (her damage also scales with it).

Is the Emblem set worth farming?

Absolutely. Its 4-pc turns Burst DMG into a function of ER (up to 75% at ~300% ER), converting recharge directly into output. Xiangling, Xingqiu, Yelan, Raiden and Beidou all use it — among the best F2P sets.
