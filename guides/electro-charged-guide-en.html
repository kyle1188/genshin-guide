Genshin Electro-Charged Guide: 0.4U Ticks and Taser Team Setup

Quick answer
  • Electro-Charged = Hydro + Electro, either order. Both auras coexist on the target — the only regular dual-aura state in the game.
  • Damage = 2 × level multiplier, about 2,894 at level 90 with 0 EM. It ticks once per second until either aura runs out.
  • Each tick drains 0.4U of Hydro and 0.4U of Electro while natural decay continues — so duration is decided by whichever aura is weaker.
  • A third element can trigger two reactions at once (Pyro → Overload + Vaporize; Cryo → Superconduct + Freeze), with the Electro reaction resolved first.

1. Why Hydro and Electro coexist

Most elements either react away or overwrite each other. Electro-Charged is the exception: once triggered, Hydro and Electro both remain as auras. Neither is consumed as "the trigger". The state persists until one gauge empties.

Because both elements are present, an Electro-Charged target can benefit from both damage types at once and can still receive a third element. That is the mechanical foundation of Taser teams.

2. Damage formula and EM scaling

Electro-Charged DMG = 2 × Level Multiplier × (1 + EM Bonus) × Electro RES Multiplier, with the EM bonus at 16 × EM ÷ (EM + 2000).

The key is that this is per tick. At 2,894 a tick over eight seconds you are looking at roughly 23,000 damage, in an area. That makes Electro-Charged excellent in multi-target fights and unremarkable on a single boss.

Whichever character triggers the reaction owns the numbers — the one who applies the second element. That is why Taser teams put EM on the Electro or Hydro unit that closes the reaction.

3. What 0.4U per tick actually means

Every tick drains 0.4U from each aura while natural decay keeps running. Consequences:

  • Duration is set by the weaker aura. 1U Hydro with 2U Electro ends when the Hydro does, roughly two ticks.
  • Strong application extends it. 2U Hydro plus continuous off-field Electro reliably holds four to six ticks.
  • Frequent weak application beats one huge application. A 1U refresh every two seconds wastes less to decay than a single 4U dump.

Two details people miss:

  • If Electro-Charged ends because an aura expired and more than 0.5 s has passed since the last tick, one extra tick is dealt.
  • Switching characters pauses Electro-Charged on that character; it resumes if they return while both auras remain. Off-field characters cannot be killed by it.

4. Third element: two reactions in one hit

Because both auras are present, a third element can resolve two reactions simultaneously. This is Electro-Charged's most distinctive property.

Priority rule: the Electro reaction resolves first. The Hydro reaction only happens if gauge remains after the Electro aura is consumed. So Pyro on an Electro-Charged target always gets Overload; whether Vaporize also lands depends on leftover gauge.

5. The arc mechanic

Electro-Charged damage also arcs to one Wet target within 5 m, calculated against that target's own Electro RES. Two limits:

  • It does not apply Electro to the arced target, only damage. Electro-Charged does not spread the element the way Swirl does.
  • It does not consume the target's Wet status, so a wet enemy can keep getting zapped.

Practical takeaway: wet the field first, then apply Electro. High-frequency Hydro from Xingqiu, Yelan or Kokomi is the foundation of any Taser team.

FAQ

Whose damage is Electro-Charged?

The character who triggers the reaction. Build EM on whoever applies the second element, usually the off-field Electro or Hydro unit.

Why does my Electro-Charged only tick once or twice?

One of the auras is too weak. Each tick drains 0.4U from both, so a weak application cannot sustain many ticks. Apply Hydro more often or open with a strong application.

Can I run Electro-Charged and Hyperbloom together?

You can, but it is not recommended. Dendro competes for both Bloom and Quicken, the reaction chain becomes unpredictable, and the Electro-Charged window shrinks.

How much EM does a Taser team need?

600 to 1000 EM on the trigger unit. At level 90, 0 EM ticks for 2,894 while 1000 EM ticks for 18,327 — more than six times the damage.

Does a shield block Electro-Charged?

No. Transformative reactions bypass shields entirely, but enemy elemental RES still applies. High Electro RES noticeably reduces the damage.

Can I play Taser without Raiden?

Yes. Beidou, Fischl, Xingqiu and Kazuha is a well-established budget version. Damage is lower but the mechanics are intact — the key is uninterrupted Hydro and Electro coverage.
