Best F2P 4-Star Weapons in Genshin 2026: Craftable, BP & Event Picks

A refined 4-star often beats a base 5-star, and it costs almost nothing. Stack a few by type and your early game doubles in comfort.

R5 4-star ≥ base 5-star: 4-stars are cheap and easy to refine (R5), and for burst-focused users a refined Catch on Xiangling matches a base 5-star in both uptime and damage. Refine the useful 4-stars first — far better value than wishing on the weapon banner.

Best by Type

Swords

Amaurayuu Hagiri (craft): Ayaka's F2P bis, refund solves ER. Sapwood Blade (craft): Bennett's bis, free. Iron Sting (craft): Anemo EM sword for Kazuha / Sucrose.

Claymores

Prototype Archaic (craft): enough for Noelle / Razor. Snow-Tombed Starsilver (craft): physical claymore. Serpent Spine (paid BP): top claymore, R5 beats base 5-star.

Bows

Rust (paid BP): Yoimiya's F2P bis, tiny gap at R5. Stringless (free BP): Fischl / Venti reaction god-tier. Favonius Warbow: ER support for Collei / Diona.

Catalysts

Mappa Mare (craft): EM catalyst. Prototype Amber (craft): healer catalyst with ER. Hakushin Ring (craft): Electro-reaction buffer.

Polearms

The Catch (free fishing): Xiangling / Raiden bis, R5 smooths rotation. Kitain Cross Spear (paid BP): Hu Tao / Xingqiu. Favonius Lance: support ER.

Refinement Advice

Refine your mains to R5 first: one refined 4-star far outclasses a base 5-star. Weekly bosses drop billets — push your staple weapons to R5. If you bought the paid BP, grab Serpent Spine / Rust / Kitain Cross Spear first; skip niche ones you cannot use yet.

Common Mistakes

  • Chasing 5-star weapons: in most cases R5 4-star ≥ base 5-star; wishing on the weapon banner is terrible value.
  • Wasting BP tokens: paid-BP exchanges are precious — only take the three universal weapons, never a niche you cannot use.
  • Ignoring craftables: the blacksmith's free weapons are often the character's bis — refine those first.

Quick Answers

How big is the gap between 4-star and 5-star weapons?

In most cases a refined (R5) 4-star equals or beats a base (R1) 5-star, especially for burst-focused users. The Catch at R5 on Xiangling matches a base 5-star in both uptime and damage.

Which weapons should a beginner refine first?

Start with craftables: Sapwood Blade (Bennett), Amaurayuu Hagiri (Ayaka), The Catch (Xiangling / Raiden), Prototype Archaic (Noelle / Razor). They are free and often bis.

Which paid-BP weapon is the best value?

Serpent Spine, Rust and Kitain Cross Spear are the most universal and stay relevant for patches. Skip niche ones you cannot use yet.

Is the weapon banner worth it?

Strongly no. It has its own pity plus Epitomized Path and needs ~240 pulls to secure. F2P players should spend Primogems on character banners instead.
