Is the Welkin Moon Worth It? Value Math, Stacking Rules & Spending Priority

Quick Answer
  • Price and contents: $4.99 grants 300 Genesis Crystals instantly plus 90 Primogems per day for 30 days, a total of 3,000 Primogem-equivalents, or about 18.75 wishes.
  • Efficiency: roughly 601 Primogems per dollar, the best rate in the game and about 7 to 10 times better than regular Genesis Crystals.
  • Stacking cap is 180 days (six passes). Purchases beyond the cap are refunded as 330 Genesis Crystals. It does not auto-renew.
  • The daily reset is 4:00 AM server time, and a missed day is gone forever.

1. What Blessing of the Welkin Moon Actually Is

The Blessing of the Welkin Moon is the only time-based, two-part bundle in Genshin Impact. On purchase you receive 300 Genesis Crystals immediately, then 90 Primogems per day from your mail for 30 days. It is not a subscription: nothing auto-charges, and after 30 days you must buy it again manually. The only requirement is Adventure Rank 5. You will find it under Paimon Menu, Shop, Gift Shop, Recommended.

The important detail is how you collect it. The 90 daily Primogems must be claimed in-game, and the server reset is 4:00 AM server time (Asia GMT+8, Europe GMT+1, America GMT-5). Miss that window and the day's 90 Primogems are permanently forfeited.

2. The Value Math: 3,000 Primogems

  • Upfront: 300 Genesis Crystals (1 Genesis Crystal converts to 1 Primogem, and crystals also buy outfits and the Battle Pass)
  • Over 30 days: 90 x 30 = 2,700 Primogems
  • Total: 3,000 Primogem-equivalents = 18.75 wishes (160 Primogems per wish)
  • Unit cost: about 601 Primogems per dollar, roughly $0.00166 per Primogem

For reference, over a 42-day version cycle a free-to-play player earns roughly 110 wishes, while a Welkin player earns about 134. That 24-wish gap is bigger than one four-star guarantee step. That is the real argument for the Welkin Moon: it does not create a burst of currency, it raises your monthly baseline from roughly 20 wishes to roughly 38.

The classic mistake is the reverse: buying the largest crystal pack to chase a character immediately, when splitting that money across a Welkin pass and unused double-crystal tiers would have produced several times the pulls.

5. Stacking Rules and the Cost of Missing a Day

  • 180-day stacking cap: you can hold up to six Welkin passes at once. The shop blocks further purchases once 179 or more days remain.
  • Excess is refunded: if a purchase pushes you past 180 days, the extra does not add days; it is returned as 330 Genesis Crystals instead.
  • Missed days are measurable: missing 5 days cuts the yield from 18.75 wishes to about 16. Missing 8 to 10 days raises your unit cost from $0.00166 to roughly $0.0024 per Primogem. Still better than any crystal pack, but a self-inflicted loss of 30 to 40 percent of the value.
  • Buy early: purchase at least a month before your target banner so the 30 collection days span both banner phases.

6. When Genesis Crystals Are the Right Choice

The only advantage crystals have is instant delivery, so they make sense when time matters more than efficiency:

  • You are at 60 to 70 pity with a guarantee, and the banner ends in a day or two while you are a few pulls short.
  • You want an outfit, which costs 1,680 Genesis Crystals and cannot be bought with Primogems.
  • You cannot log in every day, so most of the Welkin collection window would be wasted.
  • You need to top up a weapon banner quickly; weapon banners need 60 to 80 pulls to matter, and a guarantee costs about 240 wishes.

7. Common Misconceptions

  • "Welkin auto-renews." It does not. It is a one-time 30-day pass you must repurchase.
  • "Buy the big crystal pack first, then add Welkin." Reverse it: fill Welkin to the 180-day cap, then spend on first-time doubles, and only then consider regular crystals.
  • "Primogems and crystals are fully interchangeable." 1 Genesis Crystal converts to 1 Primogem, but Primogems cannot convert back, and outfits and the Battle Pass are crystal-only. Crystals are strictly more flexible.
  • "Skipping a claim is fine." A skipped day is a permanent loss with no make-up option.

Bottom Line

On a limited budget, buy the Welkin Moon first and fill it to the 180-day cap before touching anything else. Remember three numbers: $4.99 for 3,000 Primogem-equivalents, 90 Primogems that must be claimed manually each day, and a 180-day stacking limit. The Welkin Moon is not exciting, it simply raises your per-version pull baseline, which is exactly the most efficient way to save for a five-star long term.
