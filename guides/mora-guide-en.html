Genshin Mora Guide: Best Farming Methods, Ley Line Rates & Spending Plan

Mora is the one currency that runs through every part of progression - character levels, ascensions, talents, weapons, and artifacts all cost it. Fully building one character to level 90 with triple-crowned talents takes about 7 million Mora. This guide covers where Mora comes from, how Blossom of Wealth scales with World Level, and how to spend it wisely.

  • Most reliable source? Blossom of Wealth (unlocked at AR 12), which gives 60,000 Mora per 20 resin at World Level 6 and above.
  • What does a full build cost? About 7 million: 1.67M leveling, 420K ascension, and 4.96M for three talents.
  • What can you earn without resin? Weekly reputation work is worth about 150K, the Serenitea Pot adds 100K weekly, and Stardust exchange gives 300K+ monthly.
  • Is 80 to 90 worth it? Pure ATK-scaling characters gain under 5% damage, so skip the last ten levels unless you need them.

Where Mora Actually Goes

The takeaway is blunt: talents are the biggest Mora sink, roughly 70% of the total, while leveling and ascension are comparatively cheap. Running dry usually happens when you upgrade talents back to back.

At World Level 6 and above, every resin returns 3,000 Mora, the highest rate in the game. The flip side: do not spend Fragile Resin on ley lines early. Wait until the rate is maxed.

Mora Sources That Cost No Resin

  • Daily commissions and Katheryne's bonus: steady daily income for minimal effort.
  • Reputation bounties and requests: three bounties worth about 90,000 plus three requests worth about 60,000, totaling around 150,000 per week for almost no cost.
  • Serenitea Pot Realm Depot: driven by Trust Rank and Adeptal Energy, worth up to 100,000 Mora per week as passive income.
  • Stardust exchange: buy Mora with Stardust for over 300,000 per month.
  • Souvenir Shops: trade Anemo and Geo Sigils - 60 Mondstadt Sigils is about 96,000, and 120 Liyue Sigils is about 192,000.
  • Event shops: most patches stock 500,000+ Mora for event currency, usually better value than ley lines.
  • Chests, quests, and exploration: one-time income that is significant early on.

Spending Plan: Aim for the Highest Value

  • Main DPS first: sub-DPS and supports only need to be functional, not maxed.
  • Reserve 80 to 90 for characters that need it: HP- or DEF-scaling units and transformative reaction triggers such as Zhongli, Nilou, and Kuki Shinobu. Pure ATK characters are better left at ascension 80.
  • Focus your talents: level the main damage skill instead of maxing all three.
  • Be careful with artifacts: taking a placeholder artifact all the way to +20 is a hidden Mora pit. Only invest in worthwhile pieces.

When to Farm Mora on Purpose

  • World Level 6+ with spare resin: the highest ley line efficiency.
  • During Ley Line Overflow: claim the three daily double runs every day.
  • When an event shop has Mora: buy it with event currency before spending resin.
  • When you are only tens of thousands short: use the Realm Depot or Sigil shops instead of a dedicated ley line run.

Common Mistakes

  • Spending Fragile Resin on low World Level ley lines: you get roughly half the value.
  • Ignoring resin-free sources: reputation, the Serenitea Pot, and Stardust are the most forgotten steady income.
  • Building everyone at once: spreading too thin means Mora never lasts.
  • Upgrading artifacts blindly: pouring Mora into a bottomless pit.
  • Farming ley lines during events: wasting resin when the event shop already sells Mora.

Bottom Line

Mora management comes down to prioritizing resin-free income and farming ley lines only at high World Levels. Weekly reputation work is worth about 150K, the Serenitea Pot adds 100K, and Stardust gives 300K+ monthly, forming a steady baseline. When you run short, farm Blossom of Wealth at World Level 6+ for 60,000 per 20 resin. On the spending side, treat talents as the priority and reserve 80-90 for characters that truly need it, and your Mora will never dry up.
