Genshin Ley Line Guide: Blossom of Wealth vs Blossom of Revelation

Quick Answer
  • Two types: Blossom of Wealth (gold) gives Mora, Blossom of Revelation (blue) gives Character EXP materials.
  • Each claim costs 20 Original Resin, or 1 Condensed Resin for double rewards in one go.
  • At World Level 6+ the payout caps at 60,000 Mora or about 122,500 Character EXP.
  • Unlock: Mondstadt/Liyue at AR8 (Revelation) and AR12 (Wealth); other regions at AR18 plus an activated Statue of The Seven.
  • Every clear also gives 100 Adventure EXP and 20 Companionship EXP; the Battle Pass mission only needs the fight, not the claim.

What Ley Line Outcrops Are

Ley Line Outcrops are the glowing challenge points scattered across every nation. Touch one, clear the enemies that spawn, and a blossom appears — revitalize it with 20 Original Resin to claim the reward. Outcrops do not drop artifacts or ascension materials. They do exactly two things: convert Resin into Mora or Character EXP materials. That is their entire job.

Unlock Requirements

  • Mondstadt and Liyue: Blossoms of Revelation at Adventure Rank 8, Blossoms of Wealth at Adventure Rank 12.
  • Every other region (Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, Nod-Krai): both types require Adventure Rank 18 plus at least one Statue of The Seven already activated in that region.

Practical takeaway: activate a statue in a new nation and Ley Lines are immediately usable — the fastest way to top up Mora and EXP.

Two details matter. Hero's Wit (20,000 EXP each) only starts dropping reliably at World Level 4 (around AR 35); before that, Blossoms of Revelation mostly hand out Adventurer's Experience (5,000 EXP each) and are poor value. So in the early game, push Adventure Rank rather than grinding Ley Lines.

Respawn Rules: Claim It or It Gets Stuck

This is the most misunderstood rule in the system:

  • Each nation has exactly two outcrops at a time — one gold, one blue — moving through a fixed sequence of spawn points.
  • After you claim one, the next outcrop for that nation spawns at the next point in the sequence. That is why you can farm the same area repeatedly.
  • If you defeat the enemies but do not claim the reward, the blossom stays where it is and no new outcrop spawns until the daily reset at 4:00 AM server time.

So: if you want to chain-farm, you must claim every time. Leaving blossoms unclaimed to "save Resin" simply locks the system.

EXP or Mora — Which Should You Farm?

On the community-standard Stardust-equivalent metric, Blossoms of Revelation are slightly more Resin-efficient: about 65.3 Stardust value per Resin versus about 60 for Blossoms of Wealth — roughly a 9% edge. But there is a hidden cost: using EXP materials to level a character also consumes Mora (about 1 Mora per 5 EXP). A max-efficiency Revelation run gives ~122,500 EXP, which carries roughly 24,500 Mora in leveling cost — and that largely erases the gap.

A reasonable overall Resin priority: first three weekly bosses (30 Resin discount) → urgent World Boss materials → talent / weapon domains → Ley Lines → artifact domains. Ley Lines rank above artifacts because their payout is deterministic; artifact farming is pure gambling.

Condensed Resin and the Ley Line Overflow Event

Condensed Resin costs 40 Original Resin plus 1 Crystal Core, and you can hold at most 5. Used on an outcrop it grants double rewards in a single interaction — note that it creates no extra value; it merges two runs into one, saving time rather than Resin. It cannot be used on weekly bosses.

Ley Line Overflow is the recurring double-drop event. The rules matter:

  • During the event, the first 3 claims each day give double rewards for the normal 20 Original Resin — effectively half price.
  • Those 3 claims only count Original Resin. Claiming with Condensed Resin neither uses one of the 3 nor counts toward the limit.
  • Beyond 3 claims the reward returns to normal, so stop at 3 per day during the event and spend the rest of your Resin elsewhere.

Efficiency Tips

  • The Battle Pass weekly mission "Complete Ley Line deposit challenges 15 times" only requires clearing the fight — claiming is not needed. Free progress when you are short.
  • Enemy level scales with World Level, but outcrop mobs are easy. Bring one fast AoE character (any Pyro, Electro, or Anemo main DPS) and clear in under 30 seconds.
  • The moment you claim, teleport to the next spawn point. Standing in place does nothing — outcrops do not refresh on their own.
  • Resin regenerates 1 per 8 minutes, caps at 160, and yields about 180 per day. Never let it sit at 160 — that is pure waste.
  • At low World Level, spending Fragile Resin on Ley Lines is one of the worst deals in the game. Save them until World Level 6.
