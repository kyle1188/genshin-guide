Free-fire-pool (Standard Banner) 5★ Weapons

These 5★ weapons rotate in the Standard Banner (wish history never resets). If you're saving primogems for a 5★ character, you can also land one of these over time:

  • Primordial Jade Cutter (sword) — CRIT rate substat; best for any HP-scaling sword user.
  • Staff of Homa (polearm) — HP → ATK; great for Hu Tao when her banner isn't running.
  • Elegy for the End (bow) — EM/ATK shred; Kazuha/Xingqiu friendly.
  • Amos' Bow (bow) — charged-attack DMG boost; Ganyu/Ayaka option.
  • Wolf's Gravestone (claymore) — ATK buff to team; Itto/Diluc pick.
  • Skyward Atlas (catalyst) — EM/CRIT; support catalyst option.

Tip: standard banner 5★ weapons share the same pity counter as characters. If you're close to a 5★ character guarantee, keep wishing — you'll likely land a weapon too.

Craftable 5★ Weapons (event/quest rewards)

  • Key of Khaj-Nisut — obtained through Sumeru questline; best on Nilou/Furina.
  • Staff of Scarlet Sands — available via event; excellent on Cyno and Xiangling.

These are free forever (one-time acquisition) and compete with limited-banner weapons — no need to waste pulls.

When NOT to pull for 5★ weapons

  • Your 4★ is already doing the job: The Stringless (4★ bow) is often good enough for Xiao/Fischl. Don't force a 5★ just because.
  • You have no team for the DPS: Pulling Staff of Homa without Hu Tao is a wasted investment. Build the team first.
  • Standard banner weapons are accessible: Jade Cutter / Wolf's Gravestone are always available — no need to spend premium currency.

快速问答

What is the best 5★ weapon in Genshin Impact 2026?

There isn't one universal best — it depends on role. For support: Freedom-Sworn (Kazuha) and Elegy for the End. For DPS: Mistsplitter Reforged (Ayaka), Staff of Homa (Hu Tao), Engulfing Lightning (Raiden).

Can I get 5★ weapons without spending primogems?

Yes. Craftable weapons (Stringless, Dragon's Bane, Whiteblind) cost materials only. Key of Khaj-Nisut and Staff of Scarlet Sands are quest/event rewards — one-time free acquisitions.

Are standard banner 5★ weapons worth pulling for?

Yes, if you need a specific weapon for a character you're using. Primordial Jade Cutter (CRIT rate) and Wolf's Gravestone (ATK buff) are always useful on the right characters.

Should I buy weapons from the shop?

The shop offers 4★ weapons and occasionally 5★ weapon fragments. Focus your spending on characters first; weapons come naturally through wishes.

How do I know which weapon to forge first?

Forge the weapon that matches your most-played DPS. If you main Xiao → Stringless. If you main Hu Tao → Dragon's Bane (4★) until you get Staff of Homa. Check each character's build page for a weapon recommendation.
