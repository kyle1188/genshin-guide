Genshin Raiden Shogun vs Yae Miko: Which to Build? 2026

Raiden Shogun (Burst DPS / Battery) and Yae Miko (Off-field Sub-DPS) are often compared. Before you wish, which is the better investment? This guide contrasts them across element, weapon, role, best weapon and difficulty, then gives beginner advice and a team-building verdict.

Note: this comparison is based on the two characters' long-term stable roles and mechanics, not version-specific. In-game data is final.

Who Is More Worth Building?

Both are Inazuma Electro but different roles. Raiden is a battery + burst DPS and the linchpin of Raiden National — she fixes team energy and bursts huge. Yae Miko is an off-field totem DPS great in Aggravate but slow to ramp. Get Raiden if you need an energy engine; get Yae if you already run a National core.

Which for Beginners?

Raiden is more systemic: pair with Xingqiu/Xiangling/Bennett and she's done — simple loop. Yae wants careful placement and totem stacks, ramping slower, trickier for beginners.

Can They Team Up?

They co-team well in Aggravate/Spread comps: Yae supplies off-field Electro, Raiden supplies battery and burst — complementary. But cost and build investment are high.
