Genshin Impact Terms and Abbreviations: AR, BiS, CV, ICD Explained

Guides say things like "a CV of 40 is a solid piece, BiS is the Emblem set, and mind your ICD" - and new players are left guessing. Here is every common term, grouped into five sections, each with a one-line explanation. Once you know these, you can read any guide without translation.

Quick answer - the five most-asked

  • AR: Adventure Rank. Raising it also raises your World Level.
  • BiS: Best in Slot - the optimal weapon or artifact set for that slot.
  • CV: Crit Value = Crit Rate x2 + Crit DMG, used to score artifact substats.
  • ICD: Internal Cooldown. The same source cannot trigger an aura or effect more often than once per interval.
  • ER: Energy Recharge. Not to be confused with your stamina bar.

FAQ

What CV counts as a good artifact?

Around 40 Crit Value on a single 5-star piece is strong; anything above 45 is rare. There is no need to chase 40 on every piece across a whole roster.

Does CD mean crit damage or cooldown?

It depends on context. On a stat panel it usually means Crit Damage; when discussing skill uptime it means cooldown. To avoid ambiguity, many guides write CR/CD for crit stats and spell out "cooldown".

Is BiS always the strongest option?

BiS usually means the best overall pick, but team composition, constellations, weapon refinement and playstyle can all change it. For some characters the best-in-slot set changes with the team.

Why does ICD matter?

It caps how often auras and effects can trigger. Get the rhythm wrong and the later part of a rotation stops triggering reactions, which cuts damage directly.

What is Energy Recharge versus stamina?

Different systems. ER affects how fast your Burst charges; stamina is the bar drained by sprinting, climbing and swimming. Original Resin is a third, separate resource used to claim rewards.

Why does the community call a whole team "National Team"?

It refers to the classic Xiangling, Xingqiu, Bennett plus an Anemo flex composition built on Pyro resonance and Vaporize. Its appeal is high damage without needing 5-star characters.
