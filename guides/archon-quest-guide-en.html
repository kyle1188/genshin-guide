Genshin Archon Quest Guide: Full Chapter Order and AR Requirements

Quick Answer
  • Archon Quests are the main story — the only quest line that unlocks new regions and systems.
  • Prologue AR1/10/18 → Chapter I Liyue AR23-28 → Chapter II Inazuma AR30 → Interlude AR30/28.
  • Chapter III Sumeru AR30-35 → Chapter IV Fontaine AR30-40 → Chapter V Natlan AR40-28.
  • The story has reached Snezhnaya Chapter VII, with four acts released.
  • The usual blockers: an unfinished Interlude, a missing prerequisite chapter, or a character reserved by another quest.

What an Archon Quest Is

Archon Quests are Genshin's main story. The distinction from the other two quest types is easy to remember:

TypeContentHow it unlocks
Archon QuestMain story — the nations and the SevenAdventure Rank + previous chapters
Story QuestA single character's own story, gives a trial of that characterStory Keys (1 per 8 daily commissions)
World QuestSide stories and regional lore on the mapTriggered by location or conditions

The main story is the only quest line that unlocks new regions, new systems and some weekly bosses. If a new player feels stuck, nine times out of ten the main story has not been pushed far enough.

Chapter Highlights

Prologue · Mondstadt (AR 1 → 18)

Waking up near Windrise, meeting Paimon, and ultimately facing Stormterror. Prologue Act III, "Song of the Dragon and Freedom", is a key milestone — it is one of the unlock requirements for Genius Invokation TCG.

Chapter I · Liyue (AR 23 → 28)

Traveling with Zhongli, witnessing the Rite of Descension, and confronting the Overlord of the Vortex in "A New Star Approaches". Act IV, "We Will Be Reunited", introduces Dainsleif.

Chapter II · Inazuma (AR 30)

Entering the closed nation through the "Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves" prologue, living through the Vision Hunt Decree, and facing Raiden Shogun and Signora in "Omnipresence Over Mortals". Act IV, "Requiem of the Echoing Depths", pairs you with Dainsleif again.

Interlude (AR 30 / 28)

Four cross-region acts: "The Crane Returns on the Wind", "Perilous Trail", "Inversion of Genesis" and "Paralogism". Interludes sit between chapters and are mandatory — you cannot start Sumeru's main story without finishing "The Crane Returns on the Wind".

Chapter III · Sumeru (AR 30 → 35)

From "Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark" to "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises", closing with "Caribert". Sumeru introduces Dendro and a batch of new systems.

Chapter IV · Fontaine (AR 30 → 40)

Opening with "Prelude of Blancheur and Noirceur", moving through "Masquerade of the Guilty", ending in "Bedtime Story". Fontaine's main story has the densest concentration of puzzle and trial gameplay.

Chapter V · Natlan (AR 40 → 28)

"Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn" opens it, six acts plus an interlude follow, and it closes with "A Space and Time for You". Natlan is tightly bound to the Abyss war storyline and is essential to understanding the later plot.

Snezhnaya and the newest chapters

The main story has now advanced to Chapter VII in Snezhnaya, with the first four acts released. New chapters typically unlock their first act on the day a version goes live, with later acts rolled out across subsequent versions. To play them on day one, make sure both your AR and your prerequisite chapters are in order.

The Three Unlock Conditions

  1. Adventure Rank: see the table above. AR comes from quests, opening chests, offering to statues and Ley Line Outcrops.
  2. All prerequisite chapters complete: the main story is a chain — skipping any act blocks what comes after. Interludes count as prerequisites too.
  3. Occasional extra conditions: a few acts additionally require a specific world quest or a certain exploration threshold.

Why the Main Story Is Worth Rushing

  • Regions: Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Snezhnaya all unlock through the main story. Without them you have no access to their materials or domains.
  • Systems: the Serenitea Pot, fishing and the reputation system all have main story prerequisites.
  • Steady income: every act pays Primogems, Adventure EXP, Mora and EXP books. Over a full playthrough this adds up to a serious Primogem total — a major source for free-to-play players.
  • Bosses and characters: some weekly bosses and story quests depend on main story progress.

Suggested Order

  1. Finish the Mondstadt prologue first — it unlocks core systems and Genius Invokation TCG.
  2. Push Liyue far enough to open Inazuma, and build Liyue reputation for the Condensed Resin blueprint along the way.
  3. Push Sumeru, Fontaine and Natlan in parallel with your character building — the regions they unlock are where ascension materials come from.
  4. Never skip an interlude. They are short, but they gate everything after them.
  5. When AR is the blocker, open chests and run daily commissions rather than grinding Ley Lines.
