Genshin Elemental Mastery Guide 2026: What EM Does, Soft Caps & Who Should Build It

Elemental Mastery only boosts reaction damage, not raw hit damage. Build it on the wrong unit and it's wasted. This guide covers the EM formula, per-reaction soft caps, and who should stack it.

EM only amplifies reaction damage — zero effect on normal attack or skill damage. Transformative reactions (Bloom/Hyperbloom/Overload) love EM; amplifying reactions (Vape/Melt) want only a little; a pure raw-DPS carry gets nothing from it.

How EM scales reaction damage

The reaction multiplier follows (2.78×EM)/(EM+1400) — a fixed multiplier that rises with EM but with diminishing returns. No hard cap, but past 1000 EM the main-stat you give up (Crit/ATK) costs more than the EM gains.

Who should build EM

  • Transformative triggers: Hyperbloom (Kuki/Raiden), Bloom (Nilou/Kokomi), Burgeon (Thoma). These don't Crit — pure level + EM.
  • Anemo VV supports: Kazuha converts each EM into 0.04% team elemental DMG (1000 EM = +40%); Sucrose shares 20% of her EM with the team. Both run full EM Sands/Goblet/Circlet.
  • Nahida: her damage and team EM buff scale with her own EM; pushing to 1000 EM feeds both herself and the team.

Who should NOT build EM

  • Amplifying carries (Vape Hu Tao, Melt Ganyu): damage comes from Crit and ATK; an EM Sands loses ATK and Crit. Keep 100-200 from substats only.
  • Pure raw-DPS carries (no reactions): EM gives literally zero — skip it.

Two key details

  • 2nd-applier rule: amplifying reaction damage uses the EM of the unit that applies the SECOND element. Make your Main DPS the second applier so it gets the bonus.
  • Level-90 bonus: transformative reaction damage scales with character level — L90 vs L80 is about +34.3%. Level the trigger to 90 before farming artifacts; it's a bigger gain.

Common traps

  • EM Sands on a Vape carry: loses ATK and Crit, damage flattens.
  • ATK Sands on a Hyperbloom trigger: seed damage only reads level + EM; ATK/Crit are wasted.
  • EM past 1000: main-stat opportunity cost, marginal returns drop fast.
  • Forgetting L90: a trigger stuck at 80 throws away ~34% damage.

Quick Answers

What does Elemental Mastery do?

It only amplifies elemental reaction damage (Vaporize/Melt/Electro-charged/Bloom etc.) — zero effect on normal attack or skill damage. So EM is a reaction-only stat; a raw-DPS carry gets nothing from it.

What is the EM soft cap?

By reaction: amplifying (Vape/Melt) 100-300, Quicken (Aggravate/Spread) 400-600, transformative (Bloom/Hyperbloom/Overload) 800-1000+. Beyond that returns diminish; past 1000 it's better to give the main-stat to Crit/ATK.

Who should build EM hardest?

Transformative reaction triggers (Hyperbloom Kuki, Bloom Nilou/Kokomi) and Anemo VV supports (Kazuha, Sucrose) — the former scale reactions deep on EM, the latter convert EM into team-wide damage. Nahida also wants ~1000 EM.

Why shouldn't a Vape carry build EM?

A Vape carry (e.g. Hu Tao) gets damage from Crit and ATK; an EM Sands sacrifices ATK and Crit for little gain. Keep only 100-200 EM from weapon/substats; don't force an EM Sands.

What is the EM formula?

The reaction multiplier = (2.78×EM)/(EM+1400), a fixed ratio that rises with EM but with diminishing returns. No hard ceiling, but each point past 1000 clearly loses value.
