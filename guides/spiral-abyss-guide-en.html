Genshin Spiral Abyss Guide 2026: Structure, Teams & Floor 12 Strategy

The Abyss is the ultimate test of team strength, not early-game content. Read the structure, read the Moon, build two teams — and F2P accounts can stable 36-star too.

The Abyss is a final exam, not early-game content: the Spiral Abyss refreshes every two weeks with timed 3-star chambers — the ultimate test of team strength. While leveling, focus on the open world and character build; don't stress over one or two stars.

How the Abyss Works

ItemRule
Floors12 floors total; first 8 are easy, difficulty ramps from 9, Floor 12 is the ceiling
ChambersEach has an upper and lower half, 90s timer, speed clears for stars
Stars3 stars per chamber (time gate); all 12 floors maxed = 36 stars
Abyssal MoonChanges each cycle, boosts a reaction / attack type — your team-building compass

Stars only measure leftover time: faster clears = more stars. So DPS threshold and rotation skill matter more than raw stats.

Build Two Teams

One team for single-target bosses, one for mobs. The upper half is often a tanky solo enemy; the lower half is usually a pack. National / Aggravate suit single-target; Hyperbloom / Freeze suit groups. Keep two teams that don't share key units.

Floor 12 General Strategy

  • Read the Moon blessing and enemy list first: each cycle boosts a different reaction — build around it and you save half the effort.
  • Shield break & elements: Pyro breaks Cryo shields, Hydro/Dendro break Pyro shields. Bring the right element to pop shields in seconds.
  • Single-target up, mobs down: don't force one comp; swap by environment.
  • ER and rotation: Floor 12 enemies are tanky; a dropped burst costs stars. Max sub-DPS Energy Recharge.

Pacing Advice

Don't grind Floor 12 on day one. Lock 9 stars on Floors 8-11 first; push 12 once build and artifacts are ready. The Moon changes each cycle — last cycle's 36 stars don't guarantee this one. If two cycles stall, go back and build characters.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring the Moon: forcing a countered reaction both cuts damage and overruns the timer.
  • One team for everything: the Abyss forces two teams — a strong solo team only covers the upper half.
  • Rushing before artifacts: main DPS without crit / ER thresholds breaks the loop; finish gear before climbing.

Quick Answers

How often does the Spiral Abyss refresh?

Every two weeks (~15 days) a new cycle launches with a fresh Abyssal Moon blessing and enemy lineup. Miss the stars and you wait for the next cycle.

Can F2P players reach 36 stars?

Yes, but it needs two built teams and finished artifacts. All-4-star teams like National or Hyperbloom can push Floor 12 — it just demands tighter rotation skill.

Stuck on Floor 11 — what now?

Check the Moon blessing and shield elements first; then rebuild your main DPS crit and sub-DPS Energy Recharge. Floor 11 usually fails on ER or shield-breaking, not on missing 5-stars.

Does the Abyssal Moon blessing matter?

A lot. It directly boosts a reaction / attack type; building around it saves half the effort. Forcing a countered reaction overruns the timer and drops stars.
