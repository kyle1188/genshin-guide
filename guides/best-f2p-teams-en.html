Best F2P Teams in Genshin 2026: National, Hyperbloom, Freeze & Taser

The real gate in Genshin is team-building understanding, not 5-star count. All four teams below use only 4-stars from the standard banner, events or shop — build them and Floor 12 still 36-stars.

F2P can full-clear the Abyss: the real gate is team-building understanding, not 5-star count. All four teams below use only 4-stars from the standard banner, events or shop — build them and Floor 12 still 36-stars.

The Four Slots

Main DPS

On-field damage dealer; stacks crit and matching elemental DMG.

Sub-DPS

Off-field skills keep dealing damage after swap (Xiangling, Fischl).

Support

Buff / RES-shred / grouping (Bennett, Sucrose).

Survival

Healer or shielder that keeps the rotation unbroken.

National Team (the bedrock)

Bennett + Xiangling + Xingqiu + Sucrose. Bennett's burst buffs ATK and heals; Xiangling's Pyronado snapshots the buff for big Vaporize; Xingqiu applies Hydro off-field; Sucrose groups, shreds RES and shares Elemental Mastery. Universally useful and the top F2P priority.

Hyperbloom (budget Dendro core)

Dendro Traveler + Xingqiu + Kuki Shinobu + Fischl. Hydro + Dendro make Dendro Cores; Kuki's Electro triggers homing Hyperblooms; Fischl adds off-field Electro. Low cost, high payoff, excellent against groups.

Freeze (crowd control)

Kaeya + Xingqiu + Sucrose + Diona. Hydro + Cryo permanently freeze trash mobs; Diona brings shield, heal and energy. Low investment and very newbie-friendly.

Taser (Electro-Charged, multi-target)

Sucrose + Fischl + Beidou + Xingqiu. Hydro + Electro chains Electro-Charged; Sucrose swirls both for RES shred. Explosive against 2-3 enemies.

Where to Spend Resources

Supports before DPS: Bennett (C1 is a leap) → Xingqiu / Xiangling (stack ER) → Fischl (C6 is a leap) → then system pillars. Most support combat talents are fine at level 1 — save talent books for the main DPS.

Common Mistakes

  • Forcing a 4-star main DPS: in early game, off-field sub-DPS / supports pay off far more than a 4-star carry.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge: Xiangling and Xingqiu live on bursts; low ER breaks the rotation.
  • One team for everything: the Abyss needs two teams — single-target boss vs multi-target mobs. Don't force one comp.

Quick Answers

Can you 36-star the Abyss without 5-stars?

Yes. All-4-star teams like National, Hyperbloom and Freeze full-clear Floor 12. 5-stars raise the ceiling and comfort, not the gate.

Which F2P team is strongest?

The National Team (Bennett + Xiangling + Xingqiu + Sucrose) is the most versatile and easiest to build — the top F2P priority. Hyperbloom is best against groups.

What does each of the four slots do?

Main DPS deals on-field damage, sub-DPS adds off-field, support buffs / shreds RES, and survival keeps the rotation alive with heal or shield. Drop any one and the loop breaks.

Which team should a beginner build first?

National first: Bennett and Xingqiu are near-free, Xiangling comes from the Abyss, Sucrose from events. One built team clears most content.
