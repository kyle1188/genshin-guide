Genshin Artifact Leveling Guide: EXP Costs, Fodder Efficiency & Enhancement Tips

Quick Answer
  • A 5-star artifact needs 270,475 EXP to reach +20, consuming an equal amount of Mora (1 EXP = 1 Mora).
  • Breakpoints: +4 needs 3,300, +8 needs 11,425, +12 needs 34,625, +16 needs 100,675. The final +16 to +20 stretch alone costs 169,800.
  • Fodder base EXP: 1-star 420 / 2-star 840 / 3-star 1,260 / 4-star 2,520 / 5-star 3,780. Enhancement materials: Sanctifying Unction 2,500, Sanctifying Essence 10,000.
  • Enhancement rolls: 1x at 90%, 2x at 9%, 5x at 1%, averaging about 1.13x with no extra cost.
  • Feeding a leveled artifact returns its base EXP plus 80% of the invested EXP, and Mora is only charged on the base value.

1. What It Costs to Max One Artifact

The cost structure is simple and brutal: EXP and Mora are consumed in lockstep at a 1:1 ratio. Every point of EXP you feed in also drains a point of Mora. That is why maxing one artifact often empties half your wallet.

By that math, maxing a 5-star artifact takes roughly 72 unleveled 5-star pieces, or a mix of 5-star fodder and lower-tier artifacts. Low-star artifacts give little individually, but they are plentiful, so in practice they carry most of the load.

3. Multipliers and Chunked Enhancing

Every enhancement click can roll an EXP multiplier: 1x with 90% chance, 2x with 9%, 5x with 1%, for a long-run average near 1.13x, with no extra Mora.

The side effect is overflow waste. Any EXP beyond the current level cap simply evaporates. Feed a +16 piece into a fresh artifact and trigger 5x, and everything past +20 is lost. So enhance in chunks:

  • Feed to +4 first to reveal the fourth substat. If it is bad, stop, do not push to 20.
  • When chasing +20, stop around +12 and top up in two or three smaller steps to limit overflow from an unlucky 5x roll.
  • Near max level, switch to small materials such as Sanctifying Unction for fine control.

4. Recycling Leveled Artifacts: the 80% Rule

A leveled artifact consumed as fodder returns: base EXP + 80% of invested EXP. A 5-star at +16 (100,675 invested) therefore returns 3,780 + 80,560, about 84,340 EXP, losing only around 20 percent.

The Mora rule matters just as much: enhancement charges Mora only on the base value, and the returned 80 percent is not charged again. Recycling a leveled artifact into a new one is thus cheaper in Mora than feeding many loose low-star pieces. That makes a failed investment far less punishing than it feels.

The best return comes from clearing investigation spots daily: no Resin at all, yet about 60,000 artifact EXP per day, the equivalent of four free domain runs. Over months, that habit outperforms simply farming a few extra domains.

The Realm Depot requires Trust Rank 8 to unlock enhancement materials. The weekly Realm Currency cap is 5,040, so buy the Transient Resin first (1,200) and spend the rest on Unction and Essence.

6. Mora and Destruction

Unleveled 1-star to 4-star artifacts can be destroyed for Mora: 420 / 840 / 1,260 / 2,520 respectively, up to 100 items at once. Two caveats:

  • 5-star artifacts cannot be destroyed, so they can only be used as fodder or for artifact transmutation.
  • Destruction gives far less value than feeding them as fodder. Only do it if you are desperate for Mora and already drowning in fodder.

7. Strategy by Progression Stage

  • Early game: leave 5-star artifacts alone. Take a 4-star Berserker or Instructor set to +12 and spend your resources on character levels and talents.
  • Mid game: push both teams to a full +16 set. Substats matter more than star rating at this stage, and bad 5-star pieces belong in transmutation, not in the enhancing slot.
  • End game: only invest in pieces with double-crit potential. Level to +4 or +8 and, if both substat upgrades miss, recycle the piece into the next candidate. This chained approach sifts hundreds of artifacts quickly.

8. Common Mistakes

  • Dumping all materials in one go: a 5x roll then wastes the overflow, worst of all near max level.
  • Ignoring the substat rhythm: pushing straight to 20 without checking the +4 and +8 reveals pours fodder into dead ends.
  • Destroying 5-star artifacts: they cannot be destroyed anyway, and their fodder value beats their Mora value.
  • Relying on Resin only: skipping daily investigation spots throws away roughly 80 Resin of value every day.

Bottom Line

Artifact leveling comes down to chunking and filtering. Remember three numbers: 270,475 total EXP, 169,800 in the final stretch, and an 80 percent recycle return. Gather EXP from daily investigation spots, only invest in double-crit pieces, and you will stay far ahead of players who simply grind domains.
