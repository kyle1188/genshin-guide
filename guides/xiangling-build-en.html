Genshin Xiangling Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Xiangling — the chef of Liyue's Wanmin Restaurant — is the most-built 4-star in Genshin Impact and the backbone of more top-tier teams than almost any 5-star. She is an off-field Pyro Sub-DPS whose summoned panda Guoba and spinning Pyronado Burst blanket the field in Pyro, enabling Vape, Melt, and the legendary Raiden National team. She is also free (clear Spiral Abyss floor 3 to own her forever). This guide covers her best weapon (The Catch vs Staff of Homa), the Emblem of Severed Fate set, the Energy Recharge breakpoint that keeps her Pyronado spinning, and teams with Raiden / Ganyu / Hu Tao / Neuvillette / Yelan.

Note: Xiangling's kit, weapon ranking (The Catch), artifact set (Emblem of Severed Fate), and the ~180–220% ER requirement are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner/event availability shifts, but the build below holds. In-game data is always final.

Why Xiangling Is Worth Building

She is the single best free damage engine in the game, and she slots into more team archetypes than any other unit. If you only build one 4-star, make it her.

  • Free and permanent: clear Spiral Abyss floor 3-3 and she is yours at C0 — no wishes, no RNG. You can then crown her constellations from Starglitter shop.
  • Off-field Pyro uptime: her Burst (Pyronado) is a homing, field-wide Pyro applicator that follows your active character. It enables Vaporize/Melt on your entire team's damage.
  • Raiden National core: Xiangling + Bennett + Xingqiu + Raiden is one of the highest-clearing, lowest-cost teams in the game's history — it beats content far above its investment.
  • Scales with the team: because her damage rides on reactions and Burst uptime, she gets stronger as you add better carries and shredders (Kazuha, Zhongli, Furina).
A well-built Xiangling's Pyronado ticks can hit 20k–40k+ per spin in Vape/Melt setups — comparable to many 5-star Sub-DPS, at zero wish cost.

Best Weapons

Xiangling wants Energy Recharge and CRIT/ATK on a polearm. Her signature is a fishing reward, not a banner weapon.

BiS ★The Catch (R5)

Best-in-slot · free from fishing

Her tailor-made weapon: ER% plus a Burst CRIT Rate and DMG bonus at Refinement 5. It is free (catch the required fish in any region) and strictly better than most paid polearms for her. The clear default.

Obtain: fishing (exchange at the rod NPC)

5★Staff of Homa

Premium damage option

Huge HP→ATK conversion and CRIT DMG. Pairs well if you funnel a Homa into her, but you'll still need ER from substats/team to keep her Burst up. Best when you already own one.

Obtain: weapon banner

F2PDeathmatch / Dragon's Bane

Battle Pass / forge picks

Deathmatch (BP) gives CRIT Rate and ATK and is a stable damage upgrade. Dragon's Bane (forge) gives EM — excellent in Vape/Melt because her Pyronado procs reactions constantly. Both are free-ish and strong.

Obtain: BP / blacksmith

ER toolsFavonius Lance / Kitain Cross Spear

Fixes energy problems

Favonius Lance generates team energy via CRIT hits — great if your Xiangling struggles to Burst. Kitain Cross Spear (Inazuma forge) gives ER and a Burst buff. Use these when ER is your bottleneck.

Obtain: shop / forge

Stat Targets — ER Is the Headline

Unlike most DPS, Xiangling's #1 stat is Energy Recharge. Her Burst costs 80 and she generates almost no energy herself, so she needs ER from weapon + substats to spin every rotation.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — almost all her damage is the Burst:

  1. Elemental Burst (Pyronado) → 8–10. The homing Pyro field is ~90% of her output. Crown this first if you can.
  2. Elemental Skill (Guoba) → 8–9. Guoba's Pyro application sets up reactions and adds decent off-field damage. Level it second.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. She rarely auto-attacks; leave it low unless you on-field her (not recommended).

Pyronado & Energy (Her Core)

Xiangling's power lives in a perfectly-timed Burst loop. Master these:

  • Hit the ER breakpoint first. A Xiangling who can't Burst every rotation does ~30% of her value. Weapon (The Catch) + Emblem + Bennett usually gets you there.
  • Snapshot timing. Pyronado "snapshots" her stats at cast — buff Bennett's ATK field and any DMG bonus (Noblesse, Furina) before you pop her Burst.
  • Guoba placement. Guoba waddles to a spot and breathes Pyro; position it on the enemy cluster so its breaths actually connect (it has a short range).
  • Use Bennett's burst as the battery. Standing in Bennett's circle both buffs her ATK and refunds energy — the reason 2-Pyro teams fix her energy.
Key rule: Xiangling is an off-field engine, not an on-field carry. Build her for Burst uptime (ER) and reaction damage (CRIT/EM), then let your main DPS eat the Vape/Melt. Don't swap her in to auto-attack.

Common Mistakes

  • Too little Energy Recharge. The #1 Xiangling killer. Sub-180% ER without a battery means missed Bursts and collapsed damage.
  • Building her on-field as a main DPS. She wants to throw Guoba + Burst and swap out. Auto-attacking wastes her kit.
  • Skipping Bennett. Bennett is ~half her value (battery + buff + heal). A Xiangling without Bennett needs way more ER.
  • Ignoring Elemental Mastery in Vape/Melt. ~80–160 EM noticeably lifts reaction damage. Zero EM leaves free damage on the table.
  • Crimson Witch without enough ER. 4pc CW is only better than Emblem when energy is already solved — otherwise Emblem wins on both fronts.

Don't Have Xiangling Yet?

She is free: clear Spiral Abyss floor 3 (any half-decent team) and she is permanently yours. While you get there, Bennett (also free from the same Abyss floors) covers Pyro buffing, and Yanfei / Thoma / Dehya can substitute off-field Pyro application in a pinch — but none match her free, Burst-based uptime. Build Bennett and Xiangling together; they are the foundation of the National shell that carries most accounts through endgame.
