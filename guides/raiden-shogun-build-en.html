Genshin Raiden Shogun Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Raiden Shogun — the Electro Archon of Inazuma — is the best battery in Genshin Impact. Her Elemental Skill fuels the whole party's bursts, and her own Burst turns stacked Energy Recharge into massive Electro damage. This guide covers weapons, the Emblem of Severed Fate set, ER stacking, the famous Raiden National team, and how her Engine of All-Afairs keeps every rotation online.

Note: Raiden's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner and constellation availability shift over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Raiden Is Worth Building

She solves the two problems every team eventually hits — burst uptime and sustained damage:

  • Party-wide energy: her Skill's Coordinated Attack snapshots and refunds Energy to every off-field character based on their cost — suddenly your whole team's bursts come up every rotation.
  • ER-to-damage conversion: her Passive turns excess Energy Recharge into ATK, so stacking ER makes her both more reliable and harder hitting.
  • Self-sustaining Burst: her Musou no Hitotachi ignores 60% of enemy DEF and scales with ER, so she stays relevant deep into endgame.
  • Universal fit: she slots into National, Hypercarry, Taser, and Aggravate teams — one of the highest-account-value 5-stars ever released.
In Raiden National, her burst alone adds so much coordinated damage and uptime that the team clears Abyss floors at C0 with mostly 4-star gear. That is why she is a top pull for any account.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her value is split between Skill (battery) and Burst (damage):

  1. Elemental Skill (Transcendence) → 9. The Coordinated Attack is what refunds energy to the team; leveling it raises both the refund and its damage.
  2. Elemental Burst (Musou Shinsetsu) → 9 or Crown. Her main damage source; the DEF-ignore and ER scaling live here.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. Only level if you lean on her infused Normal Attacks in a Hypercarry setup.

How the Burst Engine Works

Raiden's whole kit is about resolving Energy into action. Understand this and she never sits idle:

  • Cast Skill (E): leaves the Eye of Stormy Judgment, which attacks with your active character and refunds Energy to the party when their bursts fire. This is the battery.
  • Cast Burst (Q): she enters the Musou state — Normal/Charged/Plunge attacks become Electro and ignore 60% DEF. Her burst damage scales with her ER.
  • Resolve stacks: her Skill also stores "resolve" from team bursts, which converts into burst damage and duration when she ultimates. More team bursts = bigger Raiden ult.
Key rule: Raiden's Burst should fire after the rest of the team has spent their bursts, so she banks maximum resolve. Never ult with her first in the rotation.

Common Mistakes

  • Too little Energy Recharge. Under ~250% her burst weakens and the team battery stalls. ER is her #1 stat, not ATK.
  • Building ATK% on Sands. ER Sands is almost always correct — her passive makes ER into ATK anyway. An ATK Sands wastes the conversion.
  • Ultimating first. She banks resolve from teammates' bursts; popping hers first throws away most of her damage.
  • Skipping The Catch. It is free from fishing and reaches ~90% of Engulfing Lightning. Not farming it leaves real damage on the table.
  • Running her alone as a "main DPS" with no battery context. Her value is the team engine; pair her where bursts matter.

Don't Have Raiden Yet?

She is a limited 5-star, so you may not own her. For Electro application and a budget battery, Fischl (a 4-star on the beginner list) is an excellent off-field Electro and Aggravate enabler. Yae Miko or Keqing cover different Electro niches. Build them while you wait for her banner.
