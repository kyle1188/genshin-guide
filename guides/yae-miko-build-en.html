Genshin Yae Miko Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Hot right now · Genshin 7.0 Snezhnaya (launches Aug 12, 2026)

Version 7.0 Snezhnaya reworks Cryo and adds the free Electro unit Alyosha plus the new Stellar Conduct reactions — so Electro teams are squarely back in the spotlight. Yae Miko is the premier Electro sub-DPS: her three off-field Sesshou Sakura turrets blanket the field with Electro damage while you run your carry, and she slots into Aggravate, Taser and Raiden cores without stealing field time. With 7.0 pushing Electro back to the meta, building Yae now pays off immediately. This guide covers exactly that.

Yae Miko is the game's best off-field Electro turret. Her Elemental Skill plants up to three Sesshou Sakura that automatically attack nearby enemies with Electro — zero field time, permanent background Electro application and damage. She is the Electro answer to a set-and-forget sub-DPS, and with Raiden she forms a devastating double-Electro core. This guide covers her best weapon (Kagura's Verity vs The Widsith / Hakushin Ring), the 4pc Golden Troupe / Flowers of Paradise Lost sets, the ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT / EM stat plan, why her Skill turrets are the whole kit, and teams with Nahida / Raiden / Fischl / Kazuha.

Note: Yae Miko's kit, her Golden Troupe / Paradise Lost preference, and her turret playstyle are long-term stable meta and do not shift with patches. The 7.0 banner note above is a traffic note, not a build change — the guide below holds regardless of version.

Why Yae Miko Is Worth Building

Among Electro units, only Yae gives you a deployable, multi-target off-field Electro engine that needs no on-field time to contribute.

  • Off-field Electro turrets: three Sesshou Sakura auto-attack, feeding Aggravate, Electro-Charged and Quicken without a single swap.
  • Massive team flexibility: she fits Aggravate, Taser, Raiden double-Electro, Mono Electro and even Hyperbloom shells.
  • Premium Electro synergy: with Raiden her turrets and Raiden's skill stack off-field Electro for huge clears, and Raiden's Burst cleans up the turrets for a refund.
  • Quiet DPS: her turrets hit hard and persist, turning her from "enabler" into a real damage source.
A correctly built Yae Miko is the Electro version of a premium off-field sub-DPS: drop three turrets, swap to your carry, and let the field do the work. If you want one Electro sub-DPS that works everywhere, make it her.

Best Weapons

Yae wants ATK%, CRIT and (in Aggravate) some EM. She uses a catalyst.

BiS ★Kagura's Verity

Best-in-slot · 5★ catalyst

Her signature: a passive that stacks Elemental Skill damage each time she uses her Skill — and her whole kit is Skill turrets. The clear best if you have it.

Obtain: weapon banner

4★The Widsith (R5)

Best budget · random buff

On cast it randomly grants a big ATK% / Elemental DMG / EM buff — at R5 the uptime is strong and the spike is enormous. The best f2p-friendly 4★ for her.

Obtain: gacha / shop

4★Hakushin Ring

Craftable Electro buffer

After her Burst, it buffs nearby Electro and Dendro DMG — a perfect support pick for Aggravate / Bloom teams, and it is forgeable for free.

Obtain: forge

5★Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds

High CRIT, ramping DMG

Big CRIT Rate and a stacking DMG passive the longer she stays on field (relevant if you pocket her snapshots). Strong generalist 5★.

Obtain: weapon banner

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Skill drives the turrets, her Burst the reset, her NA is minor:

  1. Elemental Skill (Yakan Evocation: Sesshou Sakura) → 8–13. The turrets' damage and count live here. Crown this first.
  2. Elemental Burst (Great Secret Art: Tenko Kenshin) → 8–10. Detonates turrets for a refund and deals burst damage; level it after the Skill.
  3. Normal Attack → 1–6. She barely attacks outside meme on-field builds. Leave it low.

Constellations — C1 and C2 Are the Sweet Spot

Yae's constellation curve is front-loaded: early cons fix her rotation and damage.

  • C1 (Karma of the Sakura Shrine): she can plant a fourth turret and her turrets gain +20% CRIT DMG — a real damage and uptime boost.
  • C2 (Gift of the Mikina's Wisdom): turrets ignore 60% DEF and her Burst's turret-detonation damage rises — a big spike.
  • C4 (Tale of the Dappled Moon): after her Burst, Skill DMG +20% for the team — a clean team buff.
  • C6 (Mysteries of the Miyobi's Work): turrets gain extra attacks and Crit Rate — turns her into a sustained damage monster. The whale stop.
Key rule: C0 is already a top-tier off-field Electro; C1/C2 are the realistic sweet spot if you main her. Because she is a limited 5★, stop at C2 unless Yae is your favorite.

Common Mistakes

  • Not planting all three turrets. Yae's damage scales with turret count — dropping one or two wastes most of her kit. Always place three before swapping.
  • Wrong artifact set. Random 2pc+2pc without Golden Troupe (or Paradise Lost in Aggravate) leaves the +70% Skill damage on the table. The set matters more for her than most.
  • Dumping Energy Recharge. She refunds Burst energy by detonating turrets; over-investing ER steals substats from CRIT/ATK%. Keep ER modest.
  • Building full EM without CRIT. In Aggravate EM helps, but her turrets still need CRIT to actually crit. Balance EM with CRIT/ATK%.

Don't Have Yae Miko Yet?

She is a limited 5★ who reruns periodically, so pull her when she returns — and with 7.0 Snezhnaya spotlighting Electro via the free Alyosha and Stellar Conduct, building your Electro core now pays off immediately. While you wait, Fischl or Raiden can supply off-field Electro in a pinch, but neither gives Yae's three-turret field control. Build her alongside Nahida and Raiden; together they are the Electro-Dendro core that carries most accounts through endgame, and right now they are the safest evergreen pickups before the 7.0 shift.
