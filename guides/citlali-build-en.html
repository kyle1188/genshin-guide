Genshin Citlali Build: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Quick Answer

  • Role: Off-field Cryo support/shielder with team-wide DMG bonus.
  • Best Weapon: Signature Starcaller Watch (EM sub), F2P option is Sacrificial Fragments.
  • Artifacts: 4pc Scroll of Hero of Cinder City (best) or Tenacity of the Millelith (if healing needed).
  • Main Stats: Full EM set (Sands/Goblet/Circlet all EM).
  • Key mechanic: Shield scales with EM; A4 adds bonus team DMG.
