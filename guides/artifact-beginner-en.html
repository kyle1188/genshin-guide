Genshin Artifacts for Beginners: Main Stats & Enhancement Guide

Artifacts are the most impactful gear system in Genshin. This guide covers everything from selecting the right main stats to optimizing substats—so you stop wasting resin on bad rolls.

⚠️ Golden Rule: Know what main stat your character needs BEFORE farming. The wrong set choice can cost hundreds of Resin attempts to fix later.

Enhancement Priority Checklist

Step 1: Confirm the 4-piece set bonus matches your build goal (e.g., "Emblem of Severed Fate" for burst mages).

Step 2: Verify all three variable slots have correct main stats—the biggest time-sink is fixing the wrong combination.

Step 3: Substat priority ranking: CRIT > ER/EM > ATK% > HP%/DEF%

Common Beginner Mistakes

❌ Using cheap artifacts as placeholders

Wrong! Start with correctly-statted 3-star pieces—they save you time refining later.

❌ Caring only about sets, ignoring stats

Wrong! A mismatched 4-piece like "Vermillion Hereafter" is less useful than a well-built 2+2 hybrid set.

❌ Perfecting one piece while neglecting the rest

Wrong! A slightly imperfect but coherent set beats five individual perfect pieces that don't work together.

FAQ

Q: Which substats should I prioritize?

A: Aim for ~14% combined CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG first, then Energy Recharge (140% minimum), Elemental Mastery (if the team benefits), and finally flat ATK.

Q: Which domain should new players farm first?

A: It depends on your main DPS. Hu Tao needs "Tower of Aosta" (Crimson Witch) or "Shadow of the Serpentines". Klee prefers "Golden House" (Blizzard Strayer). Identify your carry, then farm accordingly.
