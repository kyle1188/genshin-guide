Genshin Superconduct Guide: 40% Physical RES Shred and Physical Teams

Quick answer
  • Superconduct = Cryo + Electro, in either order. It deals Cryo DMG in a 5 m radius and lowers the Physical RES of every enemy in that radius by 40% for 12 s.
  • Damage = 1.5 × level multiplier, about 2,170 at level 90 with 0 EM. It scales only with the trigger's level and EM — no Cryo DMG bonus, no crit.
  • Superconduct does not apply Cryo, so it cannot chain into further reactions. That is the big difference from Swirl.
  • The shred does not stack, only refreshes. Rotating two Cryo/Electro units still gives −40%.

1. Trigger rules and radius

Superconduct triggers whenever Cryo and Electro meet, with no order requirement. It deals one instance of Cryo DMG within 5 m and applies a Physical RES −40% status to every enemy in that radius.

"No Cryo application" matters. Superconduct cannot chain: it hits, it ends. It will not spread Cryo to neighbours, and it cannot set up Freeze or Melt. Its role is narrow and clear — it is a physical resistance switch.

2. Damage formula and level table

Superconduct DMG = 1.5 × Level Multiplier × (1 + EM Bonus) × Cryo RES Multiplier, where the EM bonus is 16 × EM ÷ (EM + 2000).

Do not build EM for this. The Superconduct hit itself is typically only 3–8% of a physical team's total damage; EM investment comes straight out of your carry's CRIT and ATK. Your trigger unit can sit at 0 EM and lose almost nothing.

3. What −40% Physical RES is actually worth

This is the most commonly miscalculated number in the game. Once RES goes negative it enters a half-effectiveness zone, so "−40% RES" is not "+40% damage".

The pattern is simple: the tankier the enemy, the more Superconduct is worth. Against a 70% RES Ruin Guard it more than doubles your damage (2.33×); against a 0% RES target it gives only 20%. This is exactly why physical teams are locked to Cryo plus Electro in the Abyss.

Common physical DMG bonus sources:

  • Goblet main stat: 58.3% Physical DMG Bonus at 5-star level 20.
  • Pale Flame 2-piece: +25% Physical DMG; the 4-piece adds +50% ATK at two stacks.
  • Bloodstained Chivalry 2-piece: +25% Physical DMG.
  • Mika: party Physical DMG Bonus plus ATK speed, the dedicated physical support.
  • Character kits: Eula, Razor and Xinyan carry physical bonuses in their ascension or talents.

Two practical rules:

  • The shred window is 12 s, so compress your carry's burst inside it. Eula's burst takes about 7 s from cast to detonation — re-apply Superconduct 1–2 s before you start.
  • You need off-field coverage on both elements, or the window lapses. Fischl's Oz is the lowest-maintenance Electro source for physical teams.

FAQ

Whose stats does Superconduct use?

The character who triggers the reaction second. Their level and EM are used, regardless of who applied the first element.

Why does damage spike against Ruin Guards?

Autonomous machines sit around 70% Physical RES. Shredding to 30% is roughly a 133% damage gain, so the difference is far more visible than against normal enemies.

Do physical builds want elemental DMG bonus?

No. Physical and elemental DMG bonuses are separate buckets; physical characters only use the former. A Physical DMG goblet is the only correct main stat.

Does Superconduct work on frozen enemies?

It can trigger, but it breaks the Freeze. Permanent Freeze teams should avoid mixing Cryo and Electro for that reason.

Can I play physical without Eula?

Yes. Freminet, Razor and physical Zhongli all work, with Mika as the key support. The ceiling is lower than elemental teams, but it clears the overworld and early Abyss floors comfortably.

Do RES shred and DEF shred stack?

Yes, and they should. They are independent multipliers, so their effects multiply rather than add.
