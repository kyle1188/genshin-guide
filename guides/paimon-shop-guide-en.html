Genshin Starglitter Shop Guide: Monthly Rotation & Best Things to Buy

Paimon's Bargains is one of the few places in Genshin Impact where you can trade deterministic currency for a specific character or weapon: duplicate pulls auto-convert into Starglitter and Stardust, and a fixed character/weapon pair goes on sale every month. This guide covers both currencies, who is on rotation, and what is actually worth buying.

  • What is the best buy? Bennett C1 (on sale May/November, 34 Starglitter) is the community's biggest value spike, then Xingqiu (March/September) if you lack him, plus Xiangling C4 / Fischl C6 for your teams.
  • How do I earn Starglitter? Duplicates auto-convert on pulls: a 4★ character/weapon gives 2 Starglitter (5 once C6), a 5★ gives 10 (25 at C6). F2P players earn roughly 10–20 per month.
  • Are Blackcliff weapons worth it? At 24 Starglitter with a CRIT DMG substat they are decent transitional picks, but the passive needs kills to stack and is near-useless vs single bosses — lower priority than character constellations.
  • When does the shop reset? On the 1st of every month at 4:00 (server time). The character/weapon rotation follows a fixed 6-month cycle.

What is Paimon's Bargains?

Open Paimon Menu → Shop → Paimon's Bargains. It sells fixed items plus rotating characters and weapons, all refreshing on the 1st of each month at 4:00 server time:

  • Rotating characters: two 4★ characters per month at 34 Masterless Starglitter each, on a fixed 6-month cycle.
  • Rotating weapons: one full Blackcliff or Royal set (one of each type) per month at 24 Starglitter per weapon, alternating by month.
  • Fixed Starglitter items: Intertwined Fate and Acquaint Fate (5 Starglitter each) plus Crown of Insight (50, monthly limit 1).
  • Stardust items: Fates (75 Stardust each), Mora, EXP books, enhancement ore and materials with monthly caps.

The two currencies never mix: Masterless Starglitter comes from high-rarity duplicates and buys characters/weapons; Masterless Stardust comes from 3★ items and buys pulls plus ascension materials.

Monthly fixed items worth grabbing

  • Fates with Stardust: Intertwined and Acquaint Fates are 75 Stardust each with a monthly cap — free pulls, always max them out (750 Stardust budget/month).
  • Intertwined Fate with spare Starglitter: 5 each, the safest sink; keep rolling until the banner you want arrives.
  • Crown of Insight: 50 Starglitter, limit 1 per month — buy only when weekly-boss materials and Mora are ready, do not hoard.
  • Ascension materials and Mora: spend excess Stardust on whatever you are currently building.

Common mistakes

  • Pulling on banners you do not want just to farm Starglitter: 34 Starglitter equals ~6.8 pulls of value — never spend dozens of pulls to earn it.
  • Buying a maxed character: the shop blocks C6 characters, so check constellations first.
  • Blindly buying Blackcliff: the kill-stack passive does nothing against a single boss; skip it if your DPS already has a 5-star weapon.
  • Hoarding Stardust: the Fate exchange resets monthly with a cap — clear it before the month ends.
  • Forgetting the reset: new banners on the 1st usually bring a Starglitter windfall — handle that month's character and caps the same day.

Summary

The winning play is “check the calendar, then strike”: before each 1st-of-month reset, look up that month's duo — Bennett C1 and Xingqiu months are your two most important spending windows of the year. Keep buying monthly Fates with Stardust, never burn Starglitter carelessly, and this little shop becomes a reliable constellation source even at zero spend.
