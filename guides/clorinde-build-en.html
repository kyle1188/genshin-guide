Clorinde Build & Teams: 5-Star Geo Claymore & the Bond of Life Mechanic

Quick Answer

  • Clorinde is a 5★ Geo claymore (Fontaine Duelist), an on-field carry whose damage is calculated as Electro; her signature mechanic is Bond of Life.
  • Core set: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy 4pc (+18% DMG on every Bond of Life change, stacks to 3 / 6s — near-permanent uptime) or Emblem of Reminiscence 4pc (Normal-attack focus).
  • Main stats: ATK% Sands / Electro Cup / Crit Circlet.
  • Sub-stat priority: Crits > ATK > HP > ER.
  • Key quirk: normal healing does not restore her HP — healers top up her Bond of Life instead.
  • Best teams: Clorinde + Nahida + Baizhu/Charlotte + Yae/Raiden (Aggravate); Clorinde + Thoma + Fischl + Kazuha (Overload).
  • Signature weapon: Uyuyuki (crafted "Calamity of Eshu" is the F2P best-in-place).

How Clorinde Works

Her "Bond of Life" is the most unusual part of her kit:

  • Her HP is pinned to the Bond of Life gauge, so standard healing doesn't refill it; a healer adds Bond of Life value instead.
  • Empowered Normal/Charged Attacks apply Electro at high frequency — great for triggering Charged/Aggravate/Overload/Superconduct.
  • Every Bond of Life change fires her "Dark-Shattering Flame" passive (+20% ATK as damage, 3 stacks) and Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy 4pc (+18% DMG × 3 stacks) — effectively near-permanent uptime.
  • So she runs ATK + Crits primarily, with HP substats to feed the Bond of Life scale.

Team Comps

Aggravate / Hyperbloom Team (most common)

Clorinde (DPS) + Nahida (Dendro/EM) + Baizhu/Charlotte (heal) + Yae Miko/Raiden (Electro/Sub-DPS)

  • Nahida applies Dendro + Clorinde's empowered attacks apply Electro → Aggravate.
  • Baizhu/Charlotte keep her alive (topping up Bond of Life, not HP); Yae/Raiden add off-field Electro.
  • Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy amplifies the whole rotation.

Overload Blaster Team (crowd control)

Clorinde + Thoma (shield/Pyro) + Fischl (off-field Electro) + Kazuha (grouping/shred)

  • Thoma's Burst applies Pyro + Clorinde applies Electro → Overload clears crowds.
  • Kazuha groups + shreds Pyro/Electro RES; Clorinde's Skill finishes multi-target waves.

Constellation Priority

  • C2: at three talent stacks she gains near-immunity to interruption — solves her on-field survival, top value.
  • C6: 80% damage reduction + strong anti-interruption during the Skill + auto-tracking summoned hit — the on-field game-changer.

FAQ

Is Clorinde Geo or Electro?

Her element is Geo (Geo Resonance / Crystallize), but her empowered Normal/Charged Attacks deal Electro damage — so she uses an Electro Cup and runs Electro reaction teams. This is the most common confusion.

Why can't Clorinde be healed?

Her HP is pinned to the Bond of Life gauge, so standard healing doesn't refill it. Healers add Bond of Life value instead; she survives mostly through her own Bond of Life loop.

Can Clorinde team with Eula?

Yes. Clorinde applies Electro + Eula as the physical carry + a Cryo applicator → Superconduct shreds Physical RES. A strong physical-team variant.

No signature weapon — what next?

F2P: forge Calamity of Eshu (ATK substat, strongest free). Transitions: Mistsplitter / Royal Longsword or a 4★ Crit claymore.

Is Clorinde worth pulling?

Yes — she's one of the top on-field carries; Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy keeps her DMG buff nearly permanent, and she scales hard in Electro reaction teams. The cost is learning the Bond of Life mechanic and using non-standard healing.
