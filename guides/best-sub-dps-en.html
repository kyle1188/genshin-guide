How to Choose / Team Up

Priority: Xiangling and Xingqiu top the list — one off-field Pyro, one off-field Hydro, essential to almost every National/Vaporize team, and easy to get. Yelan and Fischl are advanced upgrades.

Quick Answers

Which off-field sub-DPS are must-builds?

Xiangling and Xingqiu top the list — one off-field Pyro, one off-field Hydro, essential to almost every National/Vaporize team, and easy to get. Yelan and Fischl are advanced upgrades.

Do sub-DPS need levels and talents?

Yes. Level them to survive (80+ ascended), take the key off-field skill to 8-9, and build ER plus the right CRIT/ATK. Only with enough Energy Recharge do they cast reliably.

Can an off-field sub-DPS be a main DPS?

Usually no — they're built to supplement, not carry. But Fischl/Yae in Aggravate or a low-investment Xiangling can guest-star; still build a proper main DPS separately.
