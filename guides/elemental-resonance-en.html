Genshin Elemental Resonance Guide 2026: Which Team Buff to Pick

Elemental Resonance is a free, always-on team buff: just two same-element units in your 4-man party and the whole team benefits. Picking the right one beats building another character.

Resonance is free, always-on team buff: two characters sharing an element in your 4-man party grants a passive team-wide bonus — no trigger, no on-field requirement. Four different elements instead trigger Protective Canopy, not an element resonance.

Each resonance also shortens a specific element's aura duration on your characters, but the combat value is in the numbers above.

Four Different Elements: Protective Canopy

With four distinct elements and no pair, you get Protective Canopy: +15% Elemental RES and +15% Physical RES for the whole team. Sturdy but trades away damage — mostly a survival pick.

How to Pick

Pyro is most universal

Flat +25% ATK fits physical and reaction teams alike; slot a second Pyro (Bennett / Xiangling) into almost any DPS team.

Hydro for HP-scalers

Neuvillette, Yelan, Hu Tao, Kokomi all gain from Max HP — double Hydro is a real bump.

Cryo for Freeze

Ayaka / Ganyu Freeze teams get a free 15% CRIT, slashing Crit Rate substat needs.

Electro for energy

Raiden / Beidou and other burst-heavy units loop smoother with the extra particles.

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking it needs on-field: benched characters still count as long as they are in the 4 slots.
  • Chasing Pyro blindly: Dendro teams want Dendro resonance for EM, Freeze teams want Cryo — don't sacrifice the archetype for ATK.
  • Treating four-unique as default: Protective Canopy only adds RES; damage teams skip it.

Quick Answers

How do you trigger Elemental Resonance?

Two or more characters of the same element in your 4-man party trigger it automatically as a permanent team buff — no on-field requirement. Four distinct elements trigger Protective Canopy instead.

Which resonance is strongest?

Pyro (+25% ATK) is the most universal, fitting both physical and reaction teams. But pick by what the team lacks: Hydro for HP-scalers, Cryo for Freeze, Dendro for reaction teams, Electro for energy-hungry teams.

Is double Hydro worth it?

Yes — especially for Neuvillette, Yelan, Hu Tao and Kokomi who scale with Max HP; double Hydro is a flat +25% HP, like a free HP sands.

What does four different elements do?

Four distinct elements trigger Protective Canopy: +15% Elemental RES and +15% Physical RES for the team. Survival-oriented; damage teams usually avoid it.
