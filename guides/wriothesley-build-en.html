Genshin Wriothesley Build Guide 2026: Weapons, Artifacts, Teams & Chilling Penalty Mechanic

Wriothesley — the Duke of the Fortress of Meropide — is a Cryo Normal/Charged Attack catalyst DPS. His Skill enters the Chilling Penalty state, empowering his fists with Cryo, and he self-heals via an enhanced Charged Attack. This guide covers weapons, artifacts, stat targets, team comps, and his unique Chilling Penalty & Gracious Rebuke mechanic.

Note: Wriothesley's kit, Chilling Penalty logic and artifact choice are long-term stable core gameplay and do not change much across versions. Constellations and banner schedules shift, but the build below holds. In-game data is authoritative.

Is Wriothesley worth building?

Wriothesley stands out as the benchmark of Cryo on-field teams for four reasons:

  • Stable Cryo on-field: the Skill's Chilling Penalty state empowers his Normal/Charged Attacks with Cryo, sustaining damage on single and group targets.
  • Built-in sustain: below 60% HP he gains an enhanced Charged Attack that heals 30% Max HP — survivability is easy.
  • ATK pays off: his passive grants +6% ATK per stack (up to 5 = 30%) whenever his HP changes, so frequent self-damage actually raises his ATK.
  • Flexible teams: he works as the core DPS in Melt or Freeze — highly versatile.
Wriothesley is complete at C0 and very cost-effective. C1 lets every NA string reliably grant the enhanced Charged Attack and extends the Skill state — the core constellation for feel and damage; C2 adds up to +200% burst DMG by Prosecution Edict stacks, and C6 boosts CA dual-CRIT and splits it into two hits for a spike. High constellations are not mandatory.

Talent priority

Wriothesley's Normal Attack is his main damage source (the Skill empowers his NA) — level it first:

  1. Normal Attack (Forceful Fists of Frost) → 9 or crown. The empowered Repelling Fists / Charged Attack after Skill are most of his damage.
  2. Elemental Skill (Icefang Rush) → 8-9. Enters Chilling Penalty, empowers NA and stacks ATK — the loop starter.
  3. Elemental Burst (Darkgold Wolfbite) → 8. AoE Cryo finisher plus Ousia follow-up; not the rotation core.

Chilling Penalty & Gracious Rebuke mechanic

Wriothesley's core: the Skill enters Chilling Penalty, empowering his fists; below 60% HP he gains Gracious Rebuke for an enhanced Charged Attack. Understand this and damage jumps.

  • Elemental Skill (Chilling Penalty): boosts interruption resistance and, above 50% HP, empowers Repelling Fists and consumes HP (once per 0.1s).
  • Gracious Rebuke (enhanced CA): below 60% HP it grants a no-stamina, +50% DMG Charged Attack that heals 30% Max HP — key to both sustain and burst.
  • Passive "There Shall Be a Reckoning for Sin": in Chilling Penalty, each HP change adds +6% ATK (up to 5 stacks = 30%). Frequent self-damage actually raises ATK — an important damage multiplier.
Key rule: keep Wriothesley's HP in the 50%-60% band — above 60% he can't get Gracious Rebuke healing, too low risks death. Use Bennett/Zhongli and the enhanced CA to hold that band. Also, Marechaussee needs HP changes to trigger, and his self-damage perfectly fills it, so it's the default set.

Common mistakes

  • Stacking CRIT DMG but not ATK. Wriothesley scales on ATK%; 2000+ is the baseline or late-game damage feels flat.
  • Letting HP sit too high. Above 60% you lose Gracious Rebuke healing — sustain and tempo both suffer; hold 50%-60%.
  • Skipping Bennett / Zhongli. Bennett buffs ATK and Zhongli shields — both are key to holding his HP band and output; without them tolerance drops sharply.
  • Ignoring Energy Recharge. The burst costs 60 energy; single-Cryo teams strain, so secure the rotation or use Cryo resonance.
  • Treating him as pure ATK. ATK matters, but dual-CRIT and the Penalty loop are equally key; ATK alone leaves CA and CRIT unused.

No Wriothesley yet?

Wriothesley is a limited 5-star. To try a Cryo main DPS first, Ganyu is the charged-shot benchmark, Ayaka is the burst Cryo carry, and Hu Tao shows the on-field DPS mindset — build the Cryo framework now and add Wriothesley on rerun.

Quick Answers

What is the best build for Wriothesley?

Run 4-piece Marechaussee Hunter (ATK sands, Cryo DMG goblet, CRIT rate/DMG circlet); Cashflow Supervision is best, with Flowing Purity or The Widsith as 4-stars. Target 2000+ ATK, 50%+ CRIT Rate, 150%+ CRIT DMG.

How does Wriothesley's Chilling Penalty work?

His Skill enters Chilling Penalty: above 50% HP, consuming HP boosts damage; below 60% HP he gains a Gracious Rebuke-enhanced Charged Attack (Rebuke: Vaulting Fist) that deals +50% DMG and heals 30% Max HP. His passive grants +6% ATK per HP-change stack (up to 5 = 30%).

What stats should I prioritize on Wriothesley?

Prioritize 2000+ ATK, 50%+ CRIT Rate, 150%+ CRIT DMG, and 100–200 Elemental Mastery in Melt teams. He deals direct DMG; Energy Recharge just needs to keep his burst on rotation.
