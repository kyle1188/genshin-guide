Genshin Alhaitham vs Nahida: Which to Build? 2026

Alhaitham (On-field DPS) and Nahida (Off-field Dendro) are often compared. Before you wish, which is the better investment? This guide contrasts them across element, weapon, role, best weapon and difficulty, then gives beginner advice and a team-building verdict.

Note: this comparison is based on the two characters' long-term stable roles and mechanics, not version-specific. In-game data is final.

Who Is More Worth Building?

Both are Dendro cornerstones, but one is a DPS, one the applicator. Alhaitham is the on-field DPS dealing Aggravate via EM — demanding on rotation. Nahida is the off-field Dendro + EM buffer, simple. Pick Alhaitham for an Aggravate carry, Nahida for the Dendro system; they often run double-Dendro.

Which for Beginners?

Nahida is clearly simpler (apply Dendro off-field); Alhaitham needs swapping for Aggravate and managing seeds/mirror, demanding more skill.

Can They Team Up?

Strongly recommend together — Alhaitham + Nahida double-Dendro Aggravate is standard; one applies Dendro, one deals damage — extremely complementary.
