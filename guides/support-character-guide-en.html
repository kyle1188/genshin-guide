Complete Support Character Guide

A good support character can turn an average main DPS into a top-tier damage dealer. This guide breaks down the mechanics and builds of core supports.

Core Support Types

TypeRoleExamples
----------------------
**DMG Buffer**Provides elemental DMG bonusBennett, Fischl
**Resistance Shredder**Reduces enemy element RESKazuha, Sucrose
**Elemental Applicator**Sustained elemental applicationXingqiu, Kuki Shinobu, Fischl
**Healer**Maintains team survivalKokomi, Barbara, Zhongli
**Energy Battery**Accelerates burst rotationBennett, Xingqiu

Core Supports Breakdown

Kazuha (Anemo)

Role: Resistance shred + crowd control + DMG boost

Core Mechanics:

  • Elemental Skill『Thousand Winds』pulls nearby enemies
  • Elemental Burst『Wind's Grand Conduct』provides Anemo DMG bonus
  • C1 unlocks Elemental Mastery transfer on swirl

Recommended Build:

ArtifactsMain StatsSubstat Priority
-----------------------------------------
Viridescent Venerer (4pc)ER Sands, Anemo DMG, CRIT/ER circletER > EM > CRIT
Glint n' Swirl (2+2)ER Sands, EM Goblet, CRIT circletER > EM

  • Signature: Favonius Codex (ATK/EM dual property)
  • Free alternative: Festering Desire (ER + CD reduction)
  • Forged: Rainslasher (passive synergizes with swirl)

Bennett (Pyro)

Role: DMG buffer + healer + energy battery

Core Mechanics:

  • Elemental Burst『Good Luck!』provides ATK bonus and HP recovery
  • Passive『Well-Rounded』stops healing when HP is below 70%
  • Constellation upgrades burst DMG and duration

Recommended Build:

ArtifactsMain StatsSubstat Priority
-----------------------------------------
Ember-Smeared (2pc)ER Sands, Pyro DMG, Healing Bonus GobletER > Healing
Inscribed (4pc)ER Sands, ATK Goblet, Healing GobletER > ATK

Weapon Recommendations:

  • Signature: Amos Bow (ATK bonus)
  • Free alternative: Iron Sting (ER + crit)
  • Forged: Whiteblind (ER + ATK)

Xingqiu (Hydro)

Role: Hydro application + damage reduction + interrupt resistance

Core Mechanics:

  • Elemental Skill『Guhua Sword: Fatal Raze』provides Hydro application and interrupt resistance
  • Elemental Burst『Rivet Kit: Drifting Tears』sustained Hydro application
  • Constellation upgrades burst DMG and healing

Recommended Build:

ArtifactsMain StatsSubstat Priority
-----------------------------------------
Noble Elegance (2pc)ER Sands, Hydro DMG, CRIT circletER > CRIT
Glint n' Swirl (4pc)ER Sands, Hydro DMG, CRIT circletER > CRIT

Weapon Recommendations:

  • Signature: Tidehunter's Will (off-field Hydro DMG)
  • Free alternative: Festering Desire (ER + CD)
  • Forged: Favonius Sword (generates orbs)

  • Constellation upgrades transformative reaction DMG

Support Team Formulas

Formula 1: National Team (Classic)

Lineup: Raiden + Xingqiu + Xiangling + Bennett

Core Strategy:

  • Raiden on-field, Xingqiu/Xiangling/Bennett off-field
  • Elemental reactions generate massive energy orbs
  • Bennett provides ATK bonus, Xingqiu provides damage reduction

Formula 2: Dendro Reaction Team

Lineup: Nahida + Kuki + Kazuha + Zhongli

Core Strategy:

  • Nahida provides Dendro application and DMG bonus
  • Kuki triggers Aggravate/Spread reactions
  • Kazuha swirls and shreds resistance
  • Zhongli provides shield protection

Formula 3: Freeze Team

Lineup: Ayaka + Xingqiu + Bennett + Shenhe

Core Strategy:

  • Xingqiu applies Hydro, Ayaka triggers Melt
  • Shenhe provides Cryo DMG bonus
  • Bennett provides ATK bonus

FAQ

Q: Do supports need maxed talents?

A: Yes. Support skill multipliers and effects directly determine team strength. Recommend at least Lv.8.

Q: What ER percentage is optimal?

A: Core supports should aim for 180%+. Raiden 180-220%, Xingqiu 160-180%, Bennett 200-220%.

Q: Can supports use 5-star weapons?

A: Yes, but 4-star weapons are usually sufficient. Prioritize 5-star weapons for main DPS.

Q: How to judge if a support is adequate?

A: Check if bursts rotate consistently, skill coverage is complete, and damage contribution is stable.
