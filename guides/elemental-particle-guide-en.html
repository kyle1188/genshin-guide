Genshin Elemental Particles and Energy Guide: Orbs, On-Field vs Off-Field, ER Math

Why does someone else's Burst always come back on cooldown while yours lags behind? Stacking Energy Recharge (ER) is only half the story. The deeper reason is where particles come from, who catches them, and whether they match the element. Three particles can be worth 9 energy on-field on the same element, or 1.8 energy off-field on a different element - a five-fold difference.

Quick answer

  • Base values: 1 elemental particle = 1 base energy, 1 elemental orb = 3 (three times a particle).
  • Element match: same element pays 300% of base, a clear (elementless) particle pays 200%, a different element pays only 100%.
  • On-field vs off-field: off-field characters only receive 60% in a 4-character party, 70% in a 3-character party and 80% in a 2-character party.
  • What ER does: it multiplies the energy you receive - it never makes you generate more particles. Tested up to 300% with no soft cap.
  • Cheapest fix: keep your battery on the same element as your carry, and let the carry be on-field to catch the particles.

Particles, orbs and flat energy are three different things

  • Elemental particles: generated when an Elemental Skill hits an enemy; 1 base energy each.
  • Elemental orbs: only dropped when an enemy crosses an HP threshold; 3 base energy each, three times a particle.
  • Flat energy: energy injected straight into the Burst meter, unaffected by Energy Recharge. Sources include certain weapon passives, the Scholar and Exile artifact sets, and Raiden Shogun's Burst.

Colour matters too. Characters can only generate particles of their own element, so skill particles always match the caster. Clear (elementless) particles come mainly from the Favonius weapon passive and some enemy drops, and they pay 200% to everyone regardless of element.

Three rules that save a lot of ER

  1. Funnel to your carry. The same particle is worth 1.67x more on-field than off-field. Cast a support's skill, then swap to your carry so the particles land on them.
  2. Match the battery's element to your carry's. Same element is 300% versus 100% for a mismatch. This is exactly why Xiangling pairs with Bennett, Xiao with Sucrose, and Eula with Diona.
  3. Smaller parties refund more per character. Off-field returns are 60% at four members but 80% at two - which is why duo runs often have no energy problems while full four-man teams stall.

One key point: flat energy ignores Energy Recharge. Teams built around Prototype Amber or Raiden get less value from pushing ER further than teams that live on particles.

Where Energy Recharge comes from

  • Sands main stat: about 51.8% ER at +20 on a 5-star piece - the single largest, most reliable source.
  • Substats: roughly 4.5%-6.5% per roll depending on quality.
  • Weapons: several weapons carry ER as a substat or in their passive (Sacrificial and Favonius lines, The Catch, and others).
  • Artifact sets: Emblem of Severed Fate converts excess ER into damage, making it the best home for overflow.
  • Ascension and passives: some characters gain ER on ascension or refund energy through their kit.
  • Elemental resonance: matching resonance can improve cooldowns and energy flow.

There is no known ER soft cap - community testing shows it still scaling linearly at 300%. But ER competes with crit and attack rolls, so the goal is "just enough", not maximum.

Energy problems are really about who generates, who catches, and whether the element matches. Matching your battery's element to your carry and funneling particles on-field beats adding another 20% ER.

FAQ

What is the difference between an elemental particle and an orb?

An orb is worth three times a particle (3 base energy versus 1). Particles come mainly from Elemental Skills, while orbs only drop when an enemy crosses specific HP thresholds.

Why is my carry always short on energy?

Check whether it is catching particles off-field. Off-field characters receive only 60% in a four-man party, so swapping your carry in to catch them is worth 1.67x.

How much ER should I build?

It depends on the character and the team. Rough brackets: zero-generation sustained damage dealers often need 200% or more, carries with a same-element battery usually sit at 120%-150%, and cheap-Burst supports are often fine at 100%-120%.

What is flat energy?

Energy that goes straight into the Burst meter and is not affected by Energy Recharge. Common sources are certain weapon passives, the Scholar and Exile sets, and Raiden Shogun's Burst.

Does party size affect energy gain?

Yes. Off-field returns scale from 60% in a four-character party to 70% at three and 80% at two.

Why funnel particles to your carry?

Because on-field is worth 1.67x off-field. Casting a support skill and immediately swapping to your carry is the cheapest way to reduce ER requirements.
