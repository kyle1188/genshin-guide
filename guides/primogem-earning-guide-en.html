Complete Primogem Guide: From F2P to Full Pity

Primogems are Genshin's most valuable currency. This guide covers every source — daily commissions, Spiral Abyss, world quests, events — so you can plan your pulls strategically.

Daily Primogems (Steady Income)

Daily Commissions: Complete 4 commissions daily for 40 primogems + adventure EXP. That's 6,000/month — the most reliable source for F2P players.

Serenitea Pot: 3 deliveries per day, random primogem rewards (10/20/40). ~900/month on average.

Interpretation Tokens: Exchange at AR 45+ for 160 primogems each (3 total). Worth prioritizing.

Events & Story

Limited Events: Each version typically has 2–4 events, contributing 200–1,600 primogems each. ~8,000–12,000/year from events alone.

Archon Quests: Each act rewards 300–800 primogems. Complete all ~4,000–6,000.

World Quests: Total ~3,000–5,000 primogems across all regions. Do these during early game.

FAQ

Q: How many primogems can F2P earn per month?

A: Steady sources give ~14,000–18,000/month (dailies + abyss + events + quests).

Q: What if I can't clear Spiral Abyss?

A: Even 1★ on floors 9–12 gives ~2,400/month. Better than skipping it entirely.

Q: Is the Battle Pass worth buying?

A: 680 RMB/year for ~6,000 extra primogems. Decent but not essential for F2P.

Q: How fast can I save for a hard pity?

A: With daily commissions + abyss + events, F2P players can reach 16,000 primogems in about 1 month.
