Crown of Insight Guide: When to Crown a Talent (Lv.9 to Lv.10)

Quick Answer

  • One use only: a combat talent from base Lv.9 → Lv.10. Nothing else in the game consumes crowns.
  • Per crown: 700,000 Mora + 16 four-star talent books + 12 three-star drops + 2 weekly boss materials + 1 crown, and Ascension Phase 6.
  • Triple crown = 3 crowns and 2.1M Mora in the final three steps alone.
  • Constellations do not help: a C3 talent showing Lv.12 is still base Lv.9 and still needs the crown.
  • Supply is mostly finite: about 30 from region offerings, 1/month from Paimon's Bargains, roughly 1 per version from events.

What One Crown Actually Buys

A Crown of Insight does exactly one thing: it takes a single combat talent from base Lv.9 to base Lv.10. It is not used by weapons, artifacts, furnishings or anything else. That final step is usually a 7%–10% multiplier gain on the talent, which on a carry's main damage talent is one of the largest single upgrades left in the game — and on a support's utility talent is close to nothing.

Talent you are consideringValue of the 9 → 10 stepCrown it?
Carry's main damage talent (Elemental Skill or Burst)Highest in the kit — it is the multiplier your whole rotation multipliesYes, first
On-field carry's Normal AttackHigh, but only if the kit actually attacks with itYes, if it is where the damage lives
Healer's or shielder's scaling talentModest — a few percent of a number that is already comfortableOnly if you main the character
A 4-star main DPS you plan to replaceLow long-term returnUsually no

The honest framing: crowns are not a power spike you chase, they are a tiebreaker for the character you have already decided to invest in.

Every Source of Crowns

The supply is deliberately tight and mostly finite. Miss an event crown and it is gone forever.

The permanent offering crowns sit at fixed reward tiers. The ones most players can still reach:

  • Frostbearing Tree (Dragonspine) - Lv.11 gives 1.
  • Sacred Sakura's Favor (Inazuma) - Lv.5, 15, 25, 35 and 45 give 5 total.
  • Lumenstone Adjuvant (The Chasm) - Lv.4 gives 1.
  • Tree of Dreams (Sumeru) - 5 total across the Lv.5/15/25/35/45 tiers.
  • Fountain of Lucine (Fontaine) - 5 total across the same tiers.
  • Tablet of Tona (Natlan) - 5 total across the same tiers.

Later regions add more tiers, which is why the total keeps climbing with each version. Check your region trees first: they are the only crowns that require nothing but exploration.

Who Deserves Your First Crown

Three questions, in order:

  1. Is this talent the source of the damage or healing I actually use? If the answer is "the character's damage comes from the Burst", crown the Burst and ignore the Normal Attack.
  2. Will I still be playing this character in three months? Crowns cannot be refunded or moved. A crown on a temporarily meta pick is a crown burned.
  3. Is there a cheaper upgrade left? If the weapon is at Lv.80 or the artifacts are still +12, that Mora buys more damage than the crown does. Finish those first.

Common consensus picks that hold value across versions: a carry you actively play every Abyss cycle, or a Bennett-style support whose Burst is the reason the team exists.

The Constellation Trap

Constellations that raise talent levels do not reduce the crown cost. A character at C3 can display a talent at Lv.12 while its base level is still 9 — the crown is what moves the base. Conversely, once you have crowned a talent, a later constellation pushing the display to Lv.13 does not refund anything.

Practical rule: if the talent screen says Lv.12 with a "+3" from constellations, the base is still 9 and the crown is still unspent.

When Not to Crown

  • You have not finished the build. Weapon level and artifact main stats are guaranteed gains; a crown is a percentage on top of them.
  • You are still raising several characters. One crown finishes one talent; spreading crowns across five characters finishes none.
  • You are crowning a talent you do not press. Check the rotation before the talent screen.
What does a Crown of Insight do in Genshin Impact?

It upgrades a single combat talent from base Lv.9 to base Lv.10, and it has no other use in the game. Talent levels gained from constellations do not count toward the base level, so the crown is still required.

How much does it cost to crown a talent?

700,000 Mora, 16 four-star talent books, 12 three-star enemy drops, 2 weekly boss materials and 1 Crown of Insight, with the character at Ascension Phase 6. A triple crown costs 3 crowns and 2.1 million Mora in the final steps alone.

Where do you get Crowns of Insight?

About 30 come from regional offering systems such as the Sacred Sakura, Tree of Dreams, Fountain of Lucine and Tablet of Tona. One more is purchasable each month from Paimon's Bargains for 50 Masterless Starglitter, roughly one per version comes from limited-time events, and Imaginarium Theater Act 10 gives one on first clear.

Is crowning a talent worth it?

Only after the rest of the build is finished. The 9 to 10 step is typically a 7 to 10 percent multiplier gain, which is large on a carry's main damage talent and negligible on a support's utility talent. Crown the talent your damage actually comes from.
