Quick Answers

Who should F2P build first?

The three gods Xiangling, Bennett, Xingqiu first — from fishing/Liyue story/shop, they form Raiden National or Vaporize teams. Fischl is a free off-field Electro, Kaeya bridges early.

Do these need wishes?

Mostly no. Xiangling (fishing), Xingqiu (Liyue story + shop), Bennett (standard/starter), Fischl (standard) are all free; Kazuha is limited but ultra-versatile — pull when he reruns.

Can F2P clear the Abyss?

Yes. Raiden National and Freeze (Ganyu/Ayaka + Xingqiu + Kazuha + Diona) are F2P-friendly and can full-clear with investment. Pour resources into those cores.
