Genshin Ganyu Build 2026: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Ganyu — the half-qilin emissary of Liyue's Cloud Retainer — is the iconic Cryo bow DPS in Genshin Impact, famous for her Charged Shot "Frostflake Arrow" that drops a blooming AoE Cryo explosion. She excels at both Melt (massive single-target bursts) and Freeze (comfortable AoE control) teams. This guide covers bows (Amos' Bow to F2P picks), the Wanderer's Troupe set, her Charged Shot engine, the Melt team with Bennett / Xiangling / Zhongli, and the Freeze variant with Mona / Kazuha.

Note: Ganyu's kit, stat priorities, and artifact/weapon ranking are long-term stable meta and do not change with patches. Banner availability shifts over time, but the builds below hold. In-game data is always final.

Why Ganyu Is Worth Building

She is the benchmark ranged Cryo carry and one of the few units that scales beautifully into both Melt and Freeze. Her kit rewards patient Charged Shot aiming over spammy rotations.

  • Frostflake Arrow (her signature): her charged shot splits into a bloom that deals AoE Cryo damage. This is her core damage and the trigger for Melt/Freeze reactions.
  • Massive Melt ceiling: a well-built Melt Ganyu can delete bosses — her Frostflake bloom procs Melt for a 2× Cryo multiplier, among the highest single-hit numbers in the game.
  • Comfortable Freeze: in a Freeze team she stays safe while dealing steady AoE, great for Spiral Abyss and open-world clearing.
  • Off-field value too: her Elemental Burst (Celestial Shower) drops an AoE Cryo field that enables both reactions and light crowd control.
A Melt Ganyu's Frostflake bloom can hit 50k–100k+ per shot in endgame — the gold standard for Cryo burst damage, at the cost of aiming discipline.

Talent Priority

Level in this order — her Frostflake Arrow comes from her Charged Attack, which is governed by Normal Attack:

  1. Normal Attack (Liutian Archery) → 8–10. Her Charged Shot / Frostflake bloom scales off NA level — this is nearly all of her damage.
  2. Elemental Burst (Celestial Shower) → 8–9. The AoE Cryo field enables reactions and adds meaningful burst damage.
  3. Elemental Skill (Floral Brush) → 6–8. Mostly a taunt (Ice Lotus) to distract enemies while you aim; low damage priority.

Frostflake Arrow & Aiming (Her Core)

Ganyu's damage lives in the charged shot. Master the loop on purpose:

  • Always charge to Level 2: the Frostflake bloom (the second hit, AoE Cryo) is her real damage. A Level-1 charge wastes most of her kit.
  • Aim for weak spots / group centers: Prototype Crescent and headshots reward precise aiming — a stationary target (thanks to a shield) makes this consistent.
  • Time reactions: in Melt, let Pyro linger on the enemy, then drop the Frostflake so Cryo triggers Melt (not the reverse — Pyro on Cryo is only 1.5×, Cryo on Pyro is 2×).
  • Use the taunt: her Skill's Ice Lotus draws aggro so she can aim undisturbed. Place it between you and the enemy.
Key rule: Ganyu is an aiming DPS, not a button-masher. A shielded, stationary Ganyu firing precise Level-2 Frostflake blooms will out-damage a panicking one by a mile.

Common Mistakes

  • Releasing at Level 1 charge. The Frostflake bloom (AoE) only appears on a full Level-2 charge. Short-charging throws away most of her damage.
  • Wrong reaction order in Melt. Ganyu must apply Cryo onto existing Pyro (2×). If Pyro hits her Cryo first (1.5×), she loses a third of the multiplier.
  • Building ER instead of ATK/CRIT. She is not Burst-spam reliant; over-investing in Energy Recharge starves her main stats.
  • No shielder. Without Zhongli/Diona she gets interrupted mid-aim and dies to melee — her damage collapses.
  • Ignoring Elemental Mastery in Melt. ~80–160 EM noticeably lifts Melt damage. Zero EM leaves burst damage on the table.

Don't Have Ganyu Yet?

She is a limited 5-star. For Cryo ranged DPS, Rosaria (a 4-star from banners and story) is a strong polearm Cryo carry and crit-support, and Kaeya (a free starter) covers off-field Cryo application. For Freeze enablement, Mona / Xingqiu and a shielder (Zhongli / Diona) cover most of Ganyu's shells. Build any of these while you wait for her banner; they slot into the same teams until she arrives.
