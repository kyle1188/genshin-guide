Genshin Healer Guide: Healing Formula and Best Healers

Healing looks simple until you ask why your healer does nothing. The answer is almost never investment — it is the scaling stat: HP on Jean or ATK on Kokomi will gut your output. This guide covers the healing formula, each healer’s scaling stat, gear choices, and the specific logic of healing for Furina.

  • What stat does healing scale with? It depends on the character: Kokomi, Baizhu and Sigewinne scale on HP, Jean, Qiqi, Xianyun and Charlotte on ATK, and Xilonen and Noelle on DEF. Building the wrong stat halves your output.
  • HP or Healing Bonus circlet? Almost always Healing Bonus. It is a separate multiplier from HP%, so once you already stack 100% HP the extra 46.6% HP is diluted, while 35.9% Healing Bonus is pure gain.
  • Who are the best healers? Baizhu (party heal plus shield, HP-scaled), Kokomi (constant party healing) and Sigewinne (party heal on a Skill) lead the pack; Bennett from C1 is the strongest single-target healer.
  • Which healer fits Furina? Pick an instant party healer such as Jean or Charlotte. Furina’s Fanfare stacks need the whole party topped up, and single-target healers force you to swap through every character, costing real DPS.

How healing is calculated

Healing has fewer multipliers than damage. The core formula simplifies to:

Final healing = base healing × (1 + Healing Bonus) × (1 + Incoming Healing Bonus) × scaling stat

Three things follow from this:

  • Healing Bonus is its own multiplier, coming from the circlet main stat, 2-piece set bonuses, weapons and some passives.
  • Incoming Healing Bonus belongs to the person being healed. It only helps that one character and is a poor investment for a team-wide healer.
  • The scaling stat is fixed per character, and building the wrong one is the most common cause of terrible healing numbers.

Gear: the circlet is the dividing line

"HP or Healing Bonus circlet" is the most searched support-build question, and the answer is almost always Healing Bonus:

  • If you already have 100% HP bonus, adding 46.6% more HP is only about a 23% relative gain.
  • 35.9% Healing Bonus is a completely separate multiplier, so it multiplies straight through.
  • Exceptions: Nilou wants pure HP for her passive, and Zhongli wants HP for shields — neither should run a Healing Bonus circlet.

Main stats in practice: Energy Recharge (or the scaling stat) on the sands, the scaling stat on the goblet, Healing Bonus on the circlet. Energy Recharge is the hidden stat that matters — an 80-cost Burst at 100% ER means zero healing for half the fight.

Healing for Furina specifically

Furina’s Fanfare scales with party HP changes and needs cumulative healing equal to 100% of the party’s combined HP to max out. That dictates who you bring:

  • Best: instant party healers such as Jean and Charlotte — one Burst takes everyone from 50% to 100% and grants max stacks immediately.
  • Good: continuous party healers such as Kokomi, Baizhu and Sigewinne.
  • Worst: active-character-only healers such as Bennett and Kuki, which force you to cycle every character through the field and bleed DPS.

Practical checklist

  1. Do not blindly stack HP — confirm the scaling stat first; HP on Jean does nothing.
  2. Energy Recharge beats raw healing: a healer who cannot cast is not a healer.
  3. In co-op only the host can use food, so teammates depend entirely on character healing — bringing one matters more there.
  4. Overflow is not wasted with Ocean-Hued Clam, which is exactly how Kokomi contributes damage.
  5. Statues of The Seven heal you, but not during combat, so never count on them as an emergency button.

Bottom line

Three things decide a healer: the right scaling stat, a Healing Bonus circlet, and enough Energy Recharge. The rest is matchup choice — Baizhu for comfort, Kokomi for sustained party healing, Jean or Charlotte for instant party heals that max Furina. Raw healing numbers are not the goal; healing when it matters is.
