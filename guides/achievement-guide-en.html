Genshin Achievement Guide: Categories, Primogem Totals & Priority Checklist

Achievements are the most underrated Primogem reservoir in Genshin Impact: over 1,800 achievements worth roughly 14,000 Primogems, with more added every patch. Most complete themselves along the way — the real questions are how the categories work, which ones deserve dedicated farming, and where to find hidden ones. This guide answers all three.

  • How many Primogems total? All achievements together are worth ~14,000 Primogems (1,800+ achievements and growing). Individual achievements pay 5, 10 or 20 Primogems.
  • Where do I view them? Paimon Menu → Achievements, grouped by category. Note that Wonders of the World hides many achievements until you complete them.
  • Which categories pay best? Wonders of the World (exploration + world quests, 900+ entries), Memories of the Heart (hangout endings), Challenger series (combat challenges), and the commission-linked series.
  • How do I find hidden ones? Their conditions are invisible until unlocked. Use community achievement trackers filtered by region/version, or follow a guide map as you explore.

What is the achievement system

Achievements are an ever-growing reward system paying 5 / 10 / 20 Primogems per entry by difficulty. Three reasons they matter:

  • Sheer volume: 1,800+ achievements worth ~14,000 Primogems — nearly a full pity's worth of pulls.
  • Evergreen: every patch adds dozens tied to new maps, quests and events.
  • Ride-along nature: a huge share attach to exploration, world quests and combat tricks you'll do anyway — half the pool is free.

Completing every entry in a category also awards an exclusive namecard for your profile — the completionist's trophy shelf.

Handling hidden achievements

Nearly half of Wonders of the World is hidden — conditions invisible until triggered, so you can miss them without knowing. Three fixes:

  • Achievement trackers: community sites list every entry filterable by patch, region, and done/undone — the efficient way to patch holes.
  • Interactive maps: official/community maps flag location-based easter eggs (summit climbs, special seats, feeding spots).
  • Patch achievement lists: communities compile every new achievement per version — follow along and nothing slips.

Easter-egg achievements (sit in the statue's hands, get flattened by a boar) are meme culture — looking them up beats guessing for hours.

Efficient farming route

  • One region at a time: clear a region's exploration + books + world quests in one campaign instead of zig-zagging.
  • Double-dip with chests: exploration achievements cluster with chests and oculi — run Treasure Compass and Resonance Stones together.
  • Batch combat entries: stack Challenger-type tasks, sort by boss, and sweep them in one session with one team.
  • Weekly checkpoint: fold an achievement-progress check into your weekly boss routine for commission-series counts.
  • Day-one new patches: fresh regions guide you through their own achievements — strike while motivation is hot.

Common mistakes

  • Watching only the total: the menu shows per-category progress — finishing almost-complete categories snaps up namecards fastest.
  • Guessing at hidden entries: 30 seconds with a tracker beats two hours of trial and error.
  • Assuming auto-claim: achievements record themselves, but check the menu after odd actions to confirm.
  • Brute-forcing combat entries: attempting speedkills underleveled is misery; come back with a maxed team.
  • Ignoring namecards: category-completion namecards are permanent profile trophies — worth grabbing.

Summary

Achievements are the game's best-hidden free pool — 14,000 Primogems parked in "ride-along" content. Follow new patches, patch hidden entries with trackers, sweep combat challenges once your account is built, and treat hangouts and world quests as mainline. No dedicated grinding required; the count and the Primogems climb on their own.
