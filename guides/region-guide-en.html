Mondstadt: Freedom's Beginning

Features: Knights of Favonius, Dandelion Sea, Statue of The Seven

Must-Visit Spots:

  • The Great Tree at Whispering Woods
  • Starfell Lake at sunset
  • Starfell Valley's stars
  • Jueyun Cliff's peak

    • Collection Tips:

  • Anemo Slimes: Mountains and caves
  • Secret Scripture: Reduces stamina consumption
  • Recipes: Apple Pie, Mushroom Soup

    • Inazuma: Land of Eternity

    Features: Grand Narukami Shrine, Tatarasuna, Watatsumi Island

    Must-Visit Spots:

  • Grand Narukami Shrine's cherry trees
  • Sangonomiya Shrine on Watatsumi
  • Mt. Yougou's hot springs
  • The Tenryou Commission compound

    • Collection Tips:

  • Electro Sigils: Islands and caves
  • Sacred Sakura Favor: Defense bonus
  • Recipes: Unagi, Chawanmushi

    • FAQ

    Q: Which nation should I explore first?

    A: Follow the story order: Mondstadt → Liyue → Inazuma → Sumeru → Fontaine → Natlan.

    Q: Which nations are hardest for Sigils?

    A: Inazuma (needs island unlocking) and Sumeru (complex rainforest canopy) are relatively harder.

    Q: When will Snezhnaya release?

    A: No official date announced. Watch for version update announcements.
