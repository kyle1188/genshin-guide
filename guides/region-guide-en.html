Complete Guide to Exploring All Seven Nations in Genshin Impact

Teyvat has seven distinct nations, each with unique culture, elements, and secrets. This guide covers everything you need to know about exploring each nation.

  • How many nations are there? Seven: Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Snezhnaya. Snezhnaya is not playable yet.
  • What order do I unlock them? Story order: Mondstadt, then Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan. Inazuma only opens after the Liyue Archon Quest and the Ritou Escape Plan quest chain.
  • Which Sigil does each nation drop? Each region drops its own: Anemo Sigils in Mondstadt, Geo in Liyue, Electro in Inazuma, Dendro in Sumeru, Hydro in Fontaine and Pyro in Natlan.
  • Who is the Archon of each nation? Barbatos (Anemo), Morax (Geo), Ei (Electro), Buer (Dendro), Focalors (Hydro), Mavuika (Pyro) and the Tsaritsa (Cryo).
  • Which regions are hardest to fully explore? Inazuma, because its islands unlock one at a time, and Sumeru, because the rainforest canopy and the desert underground layers hide separate collectibles.

Mondstadt: Freedom's Beginning

Features: Knights of Favonius HQ, Windrise oak, Dandelion Sea

Must-Visit Spots:

  • The giant oak tree at Windrise
  • Starfell Lake at sunset
  • Starfell Valley under the stars
  • Dragonspine summit in clear weather

Collection Tips:

  • Anemoculus: mountains and caves
  • Wind glider stamina reduced by exploration rewards
  • Cooking recipes: Apple Pie, Mushroom Soup

Liyue: The Harbor of Contracts

Features: Liyue Harbor, Jueyun Karst, The Chasm

Must-Visit Spots:

  • Liyue Harbor at night
  • Guyun Stone Forest offshore pillars
  • Luhua Pool's mineral hot springs
  • Mt. Aocang, Cloud Retainer's abode

Collection Tips:

  • Geoculus: cliffs and caves
  • Luxurious chests: quest rewards
  • Local specialties: Glaze Lily, Qingxin

Inazuma: Land of Eternity

Features: Grand Narukami Shrine, Kannazuka, Watatsumi Island

Must-Visit Spots:

  • Sacred Sakura at Grand Narukami Shrine
  • Sangonomiya Shrine on Watatsumi Island
  • Mt. Yougou's summit at dawn
  • Ritou's harbour district

Collection Tips:

  • Electroculus: islands and caves
  • Sacred Sakura's Favor: rewards scale with offering level
  • Cooking recipes: Grilled Unagi Fillet, Chawanmushi

Sumeru: Land of Wisdom

Features: Sumeru City and its divine tree, rainforest canopy, desert ruins

Must-Visit Spots:

  • Valley of Dahri at sunset
  • The divine tree above Sumeru City
  • Mausoleum of King Deshret
  • Aranara world quest chain

Collection Tips:

  • Dendroculus: canopy and underground
  • Character EXP materials: run Ley Line Blossoms
  • Local specialties: Mint, Lotus Head

Fontaine: Land of Justice

Features: Court of Fontaine, underwater exploration, mechanical puzzles

Must-Visit Spots:

  • Opera Epiclese
  • Palais Mermonia rooftop
  • Underwater ruins off the Beryl Region
  • Erinnyes Forest windmills

Collection Tips:

  • Hydroculus: underwater and on the surface
  • Fontaine specialties: Lakelight Lily, Romaritime Flower
  • Cooking recipes: Poisson Seafood Soup, Fontaine dishes

Natlan: Land of Burning Essence

Features: Six tribes, Nightsoul and Phlogiston, volcanic terrain

Must-Visit Spots:

  • Stadium of the Sacred Flame
  • Basin of Unnumbered Flames
  • People of the Springs hot springs at Toyac Springs
  • Scions of the Canopy rope bridges

Collection Tips:

  • Pyroculus: volcanoes and caves
  • Phlogiston: Natlan-only resource for exploration skills
  • Local specialties: Cacahuatl, Grainfruit

Snezhnaya: The Frozen End

Status: Not yet

Known so far:

  • Headquarters of the Fatui
  • Seat of the Cryo Archon
  • Tied to the conflict with the Heavenly Principles

FAQ

Q: Which nation should I explore first?

A: Follow the story order: Mondstadt → Liyue → Inazuma → Sumeru → Fontaine → Natlan.

Q: Which nations are hardest for Sigils?

A: Inazuma (needs island unlocking) and Sumeru (complex rainforest canopy) are relatively harder.

Q: When will Snezhnaya release?

A: No official date announced. Watch for version update announcements.
