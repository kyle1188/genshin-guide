元E七元素与反应Elements & Reactions

火 / 水 / 冰 / 雷 / 草 / 岩 / 风。不同元素交互触发反应是战斗灵魂。Pyro / Hydro / Cryo / Electro / Dendro / Geo / Anemo. Combining elements to trigger reactions is the core of combat.

  • 蒸发(火+水)、融化(火+冰)：按倍率增伤，前期主增伤。Vaporize (Pyro+Hydro), Melt (Pyro+Cryo): multiplier damage, main early damage boost.
  • 超载(火+雷)：范围火伤，破岩盾。Overload (Pyro+Electro): AoE Pyro, breaks Geo shields.
  • 感电(水+雷)：持续雷伤传递。Electro-Charged (Hydro+Electro): persistent Electro spread.
  • 冻结(水+冰)：强控。Frozen (Hydro+Cryo): strong crowd control.

遗A圣遗物与武器基础Artifacts & Weapons

  • 主词条：主 C 攻风暴(攻击沙/元素杯/双暴头)；副 C 看机制(充能/精通/双暴)。Main stats: Main DPS wants ATK%/Element DMG/Crit; Sub-DPS depends on kit (ER/EM/Crit).
  • 副词条：双暴 > 百分比攻击 > 元素充能 > 元素精通。Substats: Crit > %ATK > ER > EM.

索X探索技巧Exploration Tips

  • 优先升级神像提升体力上限；跑图善用双风共鸣、夜兰/流浪者位移。Upgrade statues first for stamina; use Dual Anemo resonance and Yelan/Wanderer for mobility.
  • 宝箱优先级：精致 > 普通；用寻宝罗盘定位未收集宝箱。Chest priority: exquisite > common; use Treasure Compass to find uncollected chests.

  • 元素视野 + 实时地图标记，避免神瞳漏收集。Elemental Sight + live map markers prevent missing oculi.

30 天里程碑路线（按冒险等级）30-Day Milestone Route (by Adventure Rank)

阶段 1：冒险等级 1–20（约第 1–5 天）Stage 1: AR 1–20 (≈ Day 1–5)
目标：过完蒙德主线，解锁核心功能Goal: finish Mondstadt story, unlock core systems
  • 推进魔神任务，序章做完解锁风之翼。Push the Archon quest; finish prologue to unlock Glider.
  • 新手池必抽完两次十连（保底得诺艾尔）。Use both beginner 10-pulls (guaranteed Noelle).
  • 12 级解锁每日委托后立即每天做。Do commissions daily once unlocked at AR12.
  • 起步阵容：旅行者+香菱(邀约免费)+芭芭拉(20级奖励)+凯亚。Starter team: Traveler + Xiangling (free) + Barbara (AR20 reward) + Kaeya.
  • 神像、锚点沿途全开；雪山地图前期可跳过。Open all statues/waypoints along the way; skip Dragonspine early.
阶段 2：冒险等级 20–28（约第 6–12 天）Stage 2: AR 20–28 (≈ Day 6–12)
目标：拿关键免费角色，进入璃月Goal: get key free characters, enter Liyue
  • 20 级拿芭芭拉，优先拉到 40 级突破保生存。Get Barbara at 20, push to 40 ascension for survival.
  • 通关深境螺旋 3-3 拿香菱。Clear Spiral Abyss 3-3 for Xiangling.
  • 推进主线到璃月，解锁声望系统。Advance story to Liyue, unlock Reputation.
  • 培养顺序：奶妈→主 C→辅助核心→工具人。Build order: healer → Main DPS → core support → utility.
  • 小技巧：所有已拥有角色先拉到 20 级突破拿成就原石，再集中投资主 C。Tip: ascend every owned character to 20 first for achievement primos, then invest in Main DPS.
阶段 3：冒险等级 28–35（约第 13–20 天）Stage 3: AR 28–35 (≈ Day 13–20)
目标：完成璃月主线，世界等级冲 4Goal: finish Liyue story, push World Level to 4
  • 28 级可放慢享受探索，继续主线至璃月结束。At 28 you can slow down and enjoy exploration until Liyue ends.
  • 世界等级 4 开放周本与高阶材料；别卡世界等级。World Level 4 opens weeklies and high-tier mats; don't freeze your WL.
  • 开始攒原石：限时活动(约 400+)、前瞻码(约 300)、米游社签到。Start saving: events (~400+), livestream codes (~300), daily check-in.
  • 主 C 拉到 70 级、核心天赋点满；辅助 60–80 突破即可。Main DPS to 70, core talents maxed; supports just 60–80 ascension.
阶段 4：冒险等级 35–45（约第 21–30 天）Stage 4: AR 35–45 (≈ Day 21–30)
目标：冲刺 45 级，为圣遗物毕业准备Goal: sprint to 45, prep for artifact farming
  • 主 C 突破 80 级，核心天赋点到 8 级。Main DPS ascends 80, core talents to level 8.
  • 树脂只刷突破材料 + 天赋本 + 少量地脉花，不碰圣遗物本。Resin only for ascension + talent mats + few ley lines, no artifact domains.
  • 尘歌壶解锁后种突破材料、换树脂。After Serenitea Pot, grow ascension mats and convert resin.
  • 攒够脆弱树脂（建议 20+ 个），等 45 突破后刷主 C 金色圣遗物。Stock Fragile Resin (20+ recommended), farm gold artifacts after 45.
45 级突破后（满 30 天之后）After AR 45 breakthrough (beyond day 30)
目标：圣遗物毕业，凑两队打深渊Goal: artifact graduation, two teams for Abyss
  • 圣遗物副本最高难度必掉五星，用囤的脆弱树脂 + 浓缩树脂集中刷主 C 毕业套。Top artifact domains guarantee 5-star; use stocked Fragile + Condensed Resin to farm the main set.
  • 主词条对的上就先用，强化到 +20 停，别死磕副词条。Use correct main stats first, enhance to +20, don't chase substats.
  • 凑出两队能打深渊的阵容，拿满深渊稳定原石（长期正循环）。Build two Abyss-capable teams for steady Abyss primos (long-term loop).
